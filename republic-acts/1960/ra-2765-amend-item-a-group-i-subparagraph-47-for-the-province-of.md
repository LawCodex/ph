# Republic Act No. 2765

**An Act to Amend Item (a), Group I, Subparagraph (47), for the Province of Sorsogon, Page 118, Paragraph F, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2765**

**An Act to Amend Item (a), Group I, Subparagraph (47), for the Province of Sorsogon, Page 118, Paragraph F, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (a), Group I, subparagraph (47), for the Province of Sorsogon, page 118, paragraph f, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"(a) Gubat Public Market—Purchase and improvement of additional site, including construction of market tiendas ₱50,000,00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
