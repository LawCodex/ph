# Republic Act No. 2205

**An Act to Constitute the Sitios of Cambuñgatod and Solop Both in the Municipality of Talalora, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Juan of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2205**

**An Act to Constitute the Sitios of Cambuñgatod and Solop Both in the Municipality of Talalora, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Juan of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Cambuñgatod and Solop in the Municipality of Talalora Province of Samar, are hereby constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio San Juan of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
