# Act No. 1254

**An Act Annexing the Barrio of Eguia, of the Municipality of San Isidro, to the Municipality of Infanta, Both in the Province of Pangasinan.**

[ Act No. 1254, October 26, 1904 ]

AN ACT ANNEXING THE BARRIO OF EGUIA, OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN ISIDRO, TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF INFANTA, BOTH IN THE PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The barrio of Eguia, now a part of the municipality of San Isidro, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby annexed to and consolidated with the municipality of Infanta, in the same province.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, October 26, 1904.
