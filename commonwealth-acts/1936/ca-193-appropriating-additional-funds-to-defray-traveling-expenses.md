# Commonwealth Act No. 193

**An Act Appropriating Additional Funds to Defray Traveling Expenses to Homeseekers.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 193, November 14, 1936 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING ADDITIONAL FUNDS TO DEFRAY TRAVELING EXPENSES TO HOMESEEKERS.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The additional sum of one hundred thousand pesos or so much thereof as may be respectively necessary, are hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Philippine Treasury not appropriated for other purposes, to be disbursed by the Secretary of Labor, during the rest of the present year and during the year nineteen hundred and thirty-seven, for traveling expenses of homeseekers, including freight of working animals and agricultural tools.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 14, 1936.
