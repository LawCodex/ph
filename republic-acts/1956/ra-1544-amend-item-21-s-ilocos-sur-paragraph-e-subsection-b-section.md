# Republic Act No. 1544

**An Act to Amend Item 21(s), Ilocos Sur, Paragraph (e), Subsection B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1544**

**An Act to Amend Item 21(s), Ilocos Sur, Paragraph (e), Subsection B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 21 (s), Ilocos Sur, paragraph (e), subsection B, section one of Republic Act Numbered Nine hundred twenty, is amended to read as follows:

| "(s) Pudoc Barrio School | ₱1,000.00 |
|---|---|
| "(s-1) Becques Home Economics | 1,000.00 |
| "(s-2) Libtong Barrio School | 1,000.00" |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
