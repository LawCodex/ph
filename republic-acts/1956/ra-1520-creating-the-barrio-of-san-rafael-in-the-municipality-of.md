# Republic Act No. 1520

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Rafael in the Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1520**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Rafael in the Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Duck Island in the Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, is hereby constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, which shall be known as the barrio of San Rafael.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
