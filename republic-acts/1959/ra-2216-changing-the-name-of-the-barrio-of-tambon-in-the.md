# Republic Act No. 2216

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tambon in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to Barrio Tandiong Muslim**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2216**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tambon in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to Barrio Tandiong Muslim**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Tambon in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is changed to barrio Tandiong Muslim.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
