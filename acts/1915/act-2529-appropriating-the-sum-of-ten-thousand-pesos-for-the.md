# Act No. 2529

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Ten thousand Pesos for the Agricultural and Industrial Fair to Be Held At Baguio.**

[ Act No. 2529, September 04, 1915 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TEN THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE AGRICULTURAL AND INDUSTRIAL FAIR TO BE HELD AT BAGUIO.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The sum of ten thousand pesos, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be expended under the direction of the Secretary of the Interior for the purposes of an agricultural and industrial fair to be held at Baguio, Mountain Province, in December, nineteen hundred and fifteen, and January, nineteen hundred and sixteen.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, September 4, 1915.
