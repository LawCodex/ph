# Batas Pambansa Blg. 507

**An Act Converting the Gen. Tinio Provincial High School in the Municipality of Gen. Tinio, Province of Nueva Ecija, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Gen. Tinio National High School, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 507**

An Act Converting the Gen. Tinio Provincial High School in the Municipality of Gen. Tinio, Province of Nueva Ecija, into a National High School to be Known as the Gen. Tinio National High School, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor

## Section 1

The Gen. Tinio Provincial High School in the Municipality of Gen. Tinio, Province of Nueva Ecija, is converted into a national high school to be known as the Gen. Tinio National High School.

## Section 2

The operation of said school for the current fiscal year shall continue to be funded out of local funds and from its share of national aid to local schools. The requirements for succeeding fiscal years shall be provided in the forthcoming General Appropriations Acts.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1983
