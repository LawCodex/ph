# Republic Act No. 5065

**An Act Creating Four Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Samar, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5065**

An Act Creating Four Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Samar, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Samar, is further amended to read as follows:

"Samar, fourteen assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
