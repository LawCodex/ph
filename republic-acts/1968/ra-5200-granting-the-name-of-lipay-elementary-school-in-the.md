# Republic Act No. 5200

**An Act Granting the Name of Lipay Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Nalipasan Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5200**

An Act Granting the Name of Lipay Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Nalipasan Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lipay Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Nalipasan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1968.
