# Republic Act No. 4252

**An Act to Repeal Section One Hundred Forty-Five, Chapter Fourteen of the Code of Mindanao and Sulu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4252**

AN ACT TO REPEAL SECTION ONE HUNDRED FORTY-FIVE, CHAPTER FOURTEEN OF THE CODE OF MINDANAO AND SULU

## Section 1

Section one hundred forty-five, Chapter Fourteen, of the Code of Mindanao and Sulu is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965
