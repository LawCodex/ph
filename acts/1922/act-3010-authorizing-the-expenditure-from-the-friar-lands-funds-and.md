# Act No. 3010

**An Act Authorizing the Expenditure From the Friar Lands Funds and the San Lazaro Estate Fund of the Necessary Amounts for the Acquisition of Equipment and Public Works During the Calendar Year Nineteen Hundred and Twenty-Two**

[ Act No. 3010, March 08, 1922 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE EXPENDITURE FROM THE FRIAR LANDS FUNDS AND THE SAN LAZARO ESTATE FUND OF THE NECESSARY AMOUNTS FOR THE ACQUISITION OF EQUIPMENT AND PUBLIC WORKS DURING THE CALENDAR YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The expenditures of the following amounts are hereby authorized from the Friar Lands Funds and the San Lazaro Estate Fund for the calendar year nineteen hundred and twenty-two, in addition to the appropriation contained in sections 1-FF and GG of Act Numbered Twenty-nine hundred and ninety-seven:
