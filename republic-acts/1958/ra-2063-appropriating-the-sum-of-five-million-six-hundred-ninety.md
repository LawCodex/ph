# Republic Act No. 2063

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Five Million Six Hundred Ninety-Four Thousand Eight Hundred Fifty-Four Pesos for the Implementation of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Eight Hundred Thirty-One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2063**

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Five Million Six Hundred Ninety-Four Thousand Eight Hundred Fifty-Four Pesos for the Implementation of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Eight Hundred Thirty-One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sum of five million six hundred ninety-four thousand eight hundred fifty-four pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to implement the provisions of Republic Act Numbered One thousand eight hundred thirty-one from the date of its effectivity.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 13, 1958.
