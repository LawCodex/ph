# Batas Pambansa Blg. 834

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barat Community School in the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Ablan Community School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 834**

An Act Changing the Name of the Barat Community School in the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Ablan Community School

## Section 1

The name of the Barat Community School in the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Norte, is changed to Ablan Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
