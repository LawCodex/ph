# Republic Act No. 8010

**An Act Converting the Vicente Madrigal Municipal High School Into a National High School to Be Known as the Vicente Madrigal National High School in the Municipality of Binangonan, Province of Rizal, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8010, May 25, 1995 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE VICENTE MADRIGAL MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE VICENTE MADRIGAL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BINANGONAN, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Vicente Madrigal Municipal High School is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as the Vicente Madrigal National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.1aшphi1

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on May 25, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
