# Republic Act No. 9426

**An Act Declaring March 8 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Entire Province of Compostela Valley to Commemorate its Founding Anniversary**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9426 April 10, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH 8 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE ENTIRE PROVINCE OF COMPOSTELA VALLEY TO COMMEMORATE ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

To commemorate its founding anniversary as a province, March 8 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the entire Province of Compostela Valley.

## SEC. 2

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
