# Republic Act No. 2785

**An Act Declaring the Sitios of Ocini, Octawan and Maylocban in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur, as Part of the Barrio of Tinorognan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2785**

**An Act Declaring the Sitios of Ocini, Octawan and Maylocban in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur, as Part of the Barrio of Tinorognan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Ocini, Octawan and Maylocban in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur, are declared as part of the barrio of Tinorognan.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
