# Batas Pambansa Blg. 683

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Bongog in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Northern Samar, to Barangay Domingo Mercader**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 683**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Bongog in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Northern Samar, to Barangay Domingo Mercader

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Bongog in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Northern Samar, is changed to Barangay Domingo Mercader.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
