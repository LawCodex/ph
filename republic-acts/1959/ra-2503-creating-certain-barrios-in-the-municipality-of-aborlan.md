# Republic Act No. 2503

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2503**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Tinagong-dagat, Apoaporawan and Malatgao in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan, are converted into barrios of said municipality to be known as the barrios of Tinagong-dagat, Sandoval and Malatgao, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
