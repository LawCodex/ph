# Republic Act No. 5619

**An Act Changing the Name of Alabaan Elementary School in the Municipality of Marcos Province of Ilocos Norte, to Fortunato Daquioag Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5619, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ALABAAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARCOS PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO FORTUNATO DAQUIOAG MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Alabaan Elementary School in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Fortunato Daquiaog Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
