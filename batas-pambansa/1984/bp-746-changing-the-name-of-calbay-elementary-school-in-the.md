# Batas Pambansa Blg. 746

**An Act Changing the Name of Calbay Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Santiago Mier Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 746**

An Act Changing the Name of Calbay Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Santiago Mier Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Calbay Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, shall be changed to Santiago Mier Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
