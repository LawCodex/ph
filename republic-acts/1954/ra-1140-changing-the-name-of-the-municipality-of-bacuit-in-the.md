# Republic Act No. 1140

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bacuit in the Province of Palawan to El Nido**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1140**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bacuit in the Province of Palawan to El Nido**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Bacuit, Province of Palawan is hereby changed to El Nido.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1954.
