# Republic Act No. 2531

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Boho in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Sorsogon, to San Antonio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2531**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Boho in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Sorsogon, to San Antonio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Boho in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Sorsogon, is hereby changed to San Antonio.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
