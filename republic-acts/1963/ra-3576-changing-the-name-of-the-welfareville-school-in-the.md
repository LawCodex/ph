# Republic Act No. 3576

**An Act Changing the Name of the Welfareville School in the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Province of Rizal, to Jose Fabella Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3576**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Welfareville School in the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Province of Rizal, to Jose Fabella Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Welfareville School in the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Province of Rizal, is hereby changed to Jose Fabella Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
