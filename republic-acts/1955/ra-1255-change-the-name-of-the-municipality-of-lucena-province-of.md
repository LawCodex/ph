# Republic Act No. 1255

**An Act to Change the Name of the Municipality of Lucena, Province of Iloilo, to that of “New Lucena”**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1255**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Municipality of Lucena, Province of Iloilo, to that of "New Lucena"**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Lucena, Province of Iloilo, is changed to that of "New Lucena".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
