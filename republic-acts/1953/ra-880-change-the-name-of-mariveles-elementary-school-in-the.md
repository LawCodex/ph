# Republic Act No. 880

**An Act to Change the Name of Mariveles Elementary School in the Municipality of Mariveles, Province of Bataan, to "Antonio G. Llamas Elementary School."**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 880, June 19, 1953 ]

AN ACT TO CHANGE THE NAME OF MARIVELES ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARIVELES, PROVINCE OF BATAAN, TO "ANTONIO G. LLAMAS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mariveles Elementary School in the municipality of Mariveles, Province of Bataan, is hereby changed to "Antonio G. Llamas Elementary School."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1953.
