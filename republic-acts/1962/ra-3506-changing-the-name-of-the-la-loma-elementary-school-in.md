# Republic Act No. 3506

**An Act Changing the Name of the La Loma Elementary School in Quezon City to Magsaysay Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3506**

**An Act Changing the Name of the La Loma Elementary School in Quezon City to Magsaysay Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the La Loma Elementary School in Quezon City is changed to Magsaysay Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
