# Republic Act No. 8949

**An Act Amending Republic Act Number 8734 entitled "An Act Separating the Pasil Municipal High School also known as the Doña Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School - Pasil Annex in the Municipality of Pasil, Province of Kalinga, from the DoÑa Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School in the Municipality of Pasil, and Converting it into a separate and independent National High School to be known as the Pasil National High School, and appropriating funds therefor"**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8949 August 31, 2000**

AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NUMBER 8734 ENTITLED "AN ACT SEPARATING THE PASIL MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL ALSO KNOWN AS THE DOÑA EUFRONIA M. PUZON MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL - PASIL ANNEX IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PASIL, PROVINCE OF KALINGA, FROM THE DOÑA EUFRONIA M. PUZON MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PASIL, AND CONVERTING IT INTO A SEPARATE AND INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE PASIL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR"

## SECTION 1

Section 1 of Republic Act No. 8734 is hereby amended to read as follows:

"SECTION 1. The Pasil Municipal High School, also known as Doña Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School - Pasil Annex in the Municipality of Pasil, Province of Kalinga, is hereby separated from the Doña Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School in the Municipality of Tabuk, and converted into a separate and independent national high school to be known as Pasil National High School."

## Section 2

The title of Republic Act No. 8734 shall now read as follows:

"An Act Separating The Pasil Municipal High School Also Known As The Doña Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School - Pasil Annex In The Municipality Of Pasil, Province Of Kalinga, From The Doña Eufronia M. Puzon Memorial High School In The Municipality Of Tabuk, And Converting It Into A Separate And Independent National High School To Be Known As The Pasil National High School, And Appropriating Funds Therefor."

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **August 31, 2000**
