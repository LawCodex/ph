# Act No. 3127

**An Act to Change the Name of the Municipality of Salasa, Pangasinan, Philippine Islands, to Bugallon**

[ Act No. 3127, February 27, 1924 ]

AN ACT TO CHANGE THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF SALASA, PANGASINAN, PHILIPPINE ISLANDS, TO BUGALLON

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of Salasa, Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Bugallon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, February 27, 1924.
