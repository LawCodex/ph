# Batas Pambansa Blg. 654

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tampakan Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Hadji Akbar Ulama Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 654**

An Act Changing the Name of the Tampakan Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Hadji Akbar Ulama Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Tampakan Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is changed to Hadji Akbar Ulama Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
