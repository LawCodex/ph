# Republic Act No. 2031

**An Act Amending Subsection (c), Section Fourteen, of Commonwealth Act Numbered One Hundred and Forty-Six, as Amended, by Removing from the Jurisdiction, Supervision and Control of the Public Service Commission Tugboats and Lighters**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2031**

AN ACT AMENDING SUBSECTION (C), SECTION FOURTEEN, OF COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED ONE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIX, AS AMENDED, BY REMOVING FROM THE JURISDICTION, SUPERVISION AND CONTROL OF THE PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION TUGBOATS AND LIGHTERS.

## Section 1

Subsection (c), section fourteen, of Commonwealth Act Numbered One hundred and forty-six, as amended by Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred and fifty-four, is further amended to read as follows:

"(c) Vehicles drawn by animals and bancas moved by oar or sail, and tugboats and lighters."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Enacted, without executive approval, June 23, 1957.
