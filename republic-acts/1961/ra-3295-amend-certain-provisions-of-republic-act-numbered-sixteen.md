# Republic Act No. 3295

**An Act to Amend Certain Provisions of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen Regarding Public Works Projects for the Province of Iloilo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3295**

**An Act to Amend Certain Provisions of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen Regarding Public Works Projects for the Province of Iloilo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Priority No. 23, page 43-B and Priorities Nos. 30 and 33, page 44, group III, for the Province of Iloilo, Appendix B, Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen, are hereby amended to read as follows:

"23. Janiuay (Elementary) Tolarucan Completion ₱3,000.00

"30. Janiuay Poblacion Repair 4,000.00

"3. Leon (High School) Poblacion Repair 6,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
