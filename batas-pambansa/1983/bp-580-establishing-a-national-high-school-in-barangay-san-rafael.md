# Batas Pambansa Blg. 580

**An Act Establishing a National High School in Barangay San Rafael Municipality of Tigaon, Province of Camarines Sur, to Be Known as the San Rafael National High School, and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 580**

An Act Establishing a National High School in Barangay San Rafael, Municipality of Tigaon, Province of Camarines Sur, to be Known as the San Rafael National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

There is hereby established a national high school in Barangay San Rafael, Municipality of Tigaon, Province of Camarines Sur, to be known as the San Rafael National High School.

## Section 2

Such sum as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of said school shall be provided in subsequent General Appropriations Acts, until when time the operation of the institution as a national high school shall be deferred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
