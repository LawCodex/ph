# Republic Act No. 1640

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Canaway in the Municipality of Malilipot, Province of Albay, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1640**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Canaway in the Municipality of Malilipot, Province of Albay, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Canaway in the Municipality of Malilipot, Province of Albay, is hereby converted into a barrio of the said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
