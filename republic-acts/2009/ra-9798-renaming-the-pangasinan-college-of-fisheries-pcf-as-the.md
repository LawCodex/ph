# Republic Act No. 9798

**An Act Renaming the Pangasinan College of Fisheries (PCF) as The Pangasinan Technological Institute (PTI)**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9798**

AN ACT RENAMING THE PANGASINAN COLLEGE OF FISHERIES (PCF) AS THE PANGASINAN TECHNOLOGICAL INSTITUTE (PTI)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Pangasinan College of Fisheries (PCF) is hereby renamed as the **_Pangasinan Technological Institute (PTI)._**

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

| JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | PROSPERO C. NOGRALES Speaker of the House of Representatives |
