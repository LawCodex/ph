# Batas Pambansa Blg. 165

**An Act Converting the Lolomboy Barangay High School in the Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Lolomboy National High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 165**

An Act Converting the Lolomboy Barangay High School in the Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, into a National High School to be Known as the Lolomboy National High School

## Section 1

The Lolomboy Barangay High School in Barangay Lolomboy, Municipality of Bocaue, Province of Bulacan, is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as the Lolomboy National High School.

## Section 2

The amount of two hundred thousand pesos for the operation of the school shall be charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 80 for the Ministry of Education and Culture intended for new national schools. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operations and maintenance shall be provided for in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 8, 1982
