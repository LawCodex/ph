# Act No. 2322

**An Act Amending Section three, Subsection (B), of Act Numbered Twenty-Two Hundred and Sixty-Four, Substituting for the Word "Masaplud" the Word "Quinayan."**

[ Act No. 2322, February 03, 1914 ]

AN ACT AMENDING SECTION THREE, SUBSECTION (B), OF ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FOUR, SUBSTITUTING FOR THE WORD "MASAPLUD" THE WORD "QUINAYAN."

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

Section three, subsection (b), of Act Numbered Twenty-two hundred and sixty-tour is hereby amended by striking out the word "Masaplud" and inserting in lieu thereof the word "Quinayan," of the municipality of Guihulngan, Oriental Negros.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the same shall take effect on its passage, in accordance with section one of Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and forty-five of the Philippine Legislature.

Enacted, February 3, 1914.
