# Republic Act No. 5561

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Oco in the Municipality of Viga, Province of Catanduanes, to San Jose**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5561, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BARRIO OF OCO IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF VIGA, PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES, TO SAN JOSE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Barrio of Oco in the Municipality of Viga, Province of Catanduanes, is hereby changed to San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
