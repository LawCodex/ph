# Republic Act No. 930

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of "Alabang-baybay" in the City of Legaspi to "San Francisco"**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 930, June 20, 1953 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BARRIO OF "ALABANG-BAYBAY" IN THE CITY OF LEGASPI TO "SAN FRANCISCO".

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of "Alabang-Baybay" in the City of Legaspi shall hereafter be known as "San Francisco".1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 20, 1953.
