# Republic Act No. 2024

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tapul, Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan, to Barrio Salvacion**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2024**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tapul, Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan, to Barrio Salvacion**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Tapul in the Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan, is changed to barrio Salvacion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
