# Republic Act No. 2799

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Almeria, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2799**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Almeria, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following places in the Municipality of Almeria, Province of Leyte, are declared as barrios of said municipality, to wit:

1\. Tamarindo as Barrio Tamarindo;

2\. Sampao as Barrio Sampao;

3\. Eyusan as Barrio Eyusan;

4\. Salangi as Barrio Salangi; and

5\. Lo’ok as Barrio Lo’ok.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
