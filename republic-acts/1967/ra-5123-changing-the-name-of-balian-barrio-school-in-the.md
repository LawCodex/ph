# Republic Act No. 5123

**An Act Changing the Name of Balian Barrio School in the Municipality of Pangil, Province of Laguna, to Victor Oteyza De Guia, Jr. Memorial Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5123**

An Act Changing the Name of Balian Barrio School in the Municipality of Pangil, Province of Laguna, to Victor Oteyza De Guia, Jr. Memorial Barrio School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Balian Barrio School in the Municipality of Pangil, Province of Laguna, is hereby changed to Victor Oteyza de Guia, Jr. Memorial Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
