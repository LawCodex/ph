# Batas Pambansa Blg. 588

**An Act Converting the Bula Barangay High School in the Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Bula National High School and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 588**

An Act Converting the Bula Barangay High School in the Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur, into a National High School to be Known as the Bula National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

The Bula Barangay High School in the Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur, is converted into a national high school to be known as the Bula National High School.

## Section 2

The operation of said school for the current fiscal year shall continue to be funded out of local funds and from its share of national aid to local schools. The requirements for succeeding fiscal years shall be provided in the forthcoming General Appropriations Acts.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
