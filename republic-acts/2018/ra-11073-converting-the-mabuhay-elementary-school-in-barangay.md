# Republic Act No. 11073

**An Act Converting the Mabuhay Elementary School in Barangay Mabuhay. Municipality of Sultan Naga Dimaporo, Province of Lanao Del Norte Into An Integrated School to Be Known as Mabuhay...**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11073**

**AN ACT CONVERTING THE MABUHAY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MABUHAY, MUNICIPALITY OF SULTAN NAGA DIMAPORO, PROVINCE OF LANAO DEL NORTE INTO AN INTEGRATED SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS MABUHAY INTEGRATED SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Mabuhay Elementary School in Barangay Mabuhay, Municipality of Sultan Naga Dimaporo, Province of Lanao del Norte is hereby converted into an integrated school to be known as Mabuhay Integrated School.

## Section 2

All personnel, assets, liabilities, and records of the Mabuhay Elementary School are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Mabuhay Integrated School.

## Section 3

The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department’s program the operationalization of the Mabuhay Integrated School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 4

The Secretary of Education shall formulate the rules and regulations implementing the provisions of this Act within one hundred twenty (120) days after its effectivity.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
