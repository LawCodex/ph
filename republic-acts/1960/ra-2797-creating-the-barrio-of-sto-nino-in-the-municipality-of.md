# Republic Act No. 2797

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Sto. Niño in the Municipality of Magalang, Province of Pampanga**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2797**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Sto. Niño in the Municipality of Magalang, Province of Pampanga**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Balas in the Municipality of Magalang, Province of Pampanga, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Sto. Niño.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
