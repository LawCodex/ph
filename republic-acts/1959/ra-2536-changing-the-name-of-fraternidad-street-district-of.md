# Republic Act No. 2536

**An Act Changing the Name of Fraternidad Street, District of Pandacan, City of Manila, to Teodora San Luis Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2536**

**An Act Changing the Name of Fraternidad Street, District of Pandacan, City of Manila, to Teodora San Luis Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Fraternidad Street in the District of Pandacan, City of Manila, is changed to Teodora San Luis Street.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
