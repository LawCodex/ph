# Republic Act No. 1668

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Dampigan in the Municipality of Santa Rita, Province of Samar, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1668**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Dampigan in the Municipality of Santa Rita, Province of Samar, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Dampigan in the Municipality of Santa Rita, Province of Samar, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Dampigan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
