# Act No. 4228

**An Act to Amend Paragraph (C), Subsection a of Section One of Act Numbered Forty-One Hundred and Eighty.**

[ Acts No. 4228, August 10, 1935 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND PARAGRAPH (C), SUBSECTION A OF SECTION ONE OF ACT NUMBERED FORTY-ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY.

Be it enacted by the Senate and. House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Paragraph (c), subsection A of section one of Act Numbered Forty-one hundred and eighty, otherwise known as the Public Works Act of nineteen hundred and thirty-five, is hereby amended so that item 15, under caption Palawan, shall read as follows:

"15. For the Balala Elementary School, Culion ..................... P1,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on the date of its approval.

Approved, August 10, 1935.
