# Republic Act No. 1263

**An Act Changing the Name of Mindanao Street in the City of Cagayan De Oro to Tiano Brothers Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1263**

**An Act Changing the Name of Mindanao Street in the City of Cagayan De Oro to Tiano Brothers Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Mindanao Street in the City of Cagayan de Oro is hereby changed to Tiano Brothers Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
