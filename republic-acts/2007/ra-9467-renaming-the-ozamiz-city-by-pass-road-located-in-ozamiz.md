# Republic Act No. 9467

**An Act Renaming the Ozamiz City By-Pass Road Located in Ozamiz City as Congressman Hilarion J. Ramiro By-Pass Road**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9467 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT RENAMING THE OZAMIZ CITY BY-PASS ROAD LOCATED IN OZAMIZ CITY AS CONGRESSMAN HILARION J. RAMIRO BY-PASS ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Ozamiz City By-pass Road in Ozamiz City is hereby renamed as Congressman Hilarion J. Ramiro By-Pass Road.

## Sec. 2

The Department of Public Works and Highways is hereby directed to implement the provision of this Act.

## Sec. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
