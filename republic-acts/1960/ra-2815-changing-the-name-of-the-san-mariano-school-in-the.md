# Republic Act No. 2815

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Mariano School in the Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, to Malaking Pook Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2815**

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Mariano School in the Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, to Malaking Pook Barrio School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Mariano School in the barrio of Malaking Pook, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is changed to Malaking Pook Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
