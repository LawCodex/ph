# Batas Pambansa Blg. 115

**An Act Changing the Name of Lanao General Hospital in Marawi City to Amai Pakpak General Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 115**

An Act Changing the Name of Lanao General Hospital in Marawi City to Amai Pakpak General Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Lanao General Hospital in Marawi City is hereby changed to Amai Pakpak General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 21, 1981
