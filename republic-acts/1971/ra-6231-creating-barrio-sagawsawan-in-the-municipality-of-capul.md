# Republic Act No. 6231

**An Act Creating Barrio Sagawsawan in the Municipality of Capul, Province of Northern Samar.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6231, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO SAGAWSAWAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAPUL, PROVINCE OF NORTHERN SAMAR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Sagawsawan in the Municipality of Capul, Province of Northern Samar, is hereby converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as Barrio Sagawsawan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
