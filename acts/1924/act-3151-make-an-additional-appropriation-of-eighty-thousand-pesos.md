# Act No. 3151

**An Act to Make An Additional Appropriation of Eighty thousand Pesos for the Campaign for the Eradication of Anthrax Under the Provisions of Act Numbered thirty-One Hundred and Nineteen, and for Other Purposes**

[ Act No. 3151, March 06, 1924 ]

AN ACT TO MAKE AN ADDITIONAL APPROPRIATION OF EIGHTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE CAMPAIGN FOR THE ERADICATION OF ANTHRAX UNDER THE PROVISIONS OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-ONE HUNDRED AND NINETEEN, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

For the purpose of continuing and conducting the campaign for the eradication of anthrax in the Philippine Islands, undertaken under the provisions of Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and nineteen, the sum of eighty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the necessary expense of the anthrax campaign. This sum shall be disbursed in the manner and for the purposes provided for in said Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and nineteen.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 6, 1924.
