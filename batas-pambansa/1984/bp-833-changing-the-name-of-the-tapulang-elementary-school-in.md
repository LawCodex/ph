# Batas Pambansa Blg. 833

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tapulang Elementary School in Barangay Tapulang, in the Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, to Don Mariano Marcos Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 833**

An Act Changing the Name of the Tapulang Elementary School in Barangay Tapulang, in the Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, to Don Mariano Marcos Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Tapulang Elementary School in Barangay Tapulang, Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, is changed to Don Mariano Marcos Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
