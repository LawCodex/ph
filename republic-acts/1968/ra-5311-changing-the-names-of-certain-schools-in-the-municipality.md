# Republic Act No. 5311

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Dingle, Province of Iloilo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5311**

An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Dingle, Province of Iloilo

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Licu-an Elementary School in the Municipality of Dingle, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to San Matias Elementary School.

## Section 2

The name of Agtatacay Elementary School also in the Municipality of Dingle, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Agtatacay-Gutao Elementary School.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
