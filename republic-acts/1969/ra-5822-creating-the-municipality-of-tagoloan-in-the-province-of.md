# Republic Act No. 5822

**An Act Creating the Municipality of Tagoloan in the Province of Lanao Del Norte**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5822, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING THE MUNICIPALITY OF TAGOLOAN IN THE PROVINCE OF LANAO DEL NORTE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Barrios Kiasar, Darimbang, Dimayon, Ina-gongan, Malibato, Dalamas, and Panalawan, all of the Municipality of Baioi, Province of Lanao del Norte, are separated from said municipality and constituted into a distinct and independent municipality, to be known as the Municipality of Tagoloan, same province. The seat of government of the new municipality shall be in the poblacion of Barrio Darimbang.

## Section 2

The first mayor, vice-mayor and councilors of the new municipality shall be elected in the next general elections in November 1969.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 21, 1969.
