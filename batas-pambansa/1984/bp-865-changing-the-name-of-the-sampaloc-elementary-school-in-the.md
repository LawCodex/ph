# Batas Pambansa Blg. 865

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sampaloc Elementary School in the Municipality of Sampaloc, Province of Quezon, to Medardo L. Tumagay Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 865**

An Act Changing the Name of the Sampaloc Elementary School in the Municipality of Sampaloc, Province of Quezon, to Medardo L. Tumagay Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Sampaloc Elementary School located in the Municipality of Sampaloc, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Medardo L. Tumagay Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
