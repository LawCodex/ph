# Republic Act No. 1967

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pulong Buhangin Elementary School, Barrio of Pulong Buhangin, Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Bulacan, to Cornelia M. De Jesus Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1967**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pulong Buhangin Elementary School, Barrio of Pulong Buhangin, Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Bulacan, to Cornelia M. De Jesus Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pulong Buhangin Elementary School, barrio of Pulong Buhangin, Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Bulacan, is changed to Cornelia M. de Jesus Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
