# Republic Act No. 7345

**An Act Changing the Name of the Hernani Provincial High School in the Municipality of Hernani, Province of Eastern Samar, to Hernani High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7345, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE HERNANI PROVINCIAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HERNANI, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO HERNANI HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Hernani Provincial High School in the Municipality of Hernani, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Hernani High School.
