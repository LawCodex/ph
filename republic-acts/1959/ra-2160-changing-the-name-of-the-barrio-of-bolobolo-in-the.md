# Republic Act No. 2160

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bolobolo in the Municipality of Jabonga, Province of Agusan, to San Vicente**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2160**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bolobolo in the Municipality of Jabonga, Province of Agusan, to San Vicente**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Bolobolo in the Municipality of Jabonga, Province of Agusan, is changed to San Vicente.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
