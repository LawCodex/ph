# Republic Act No. 7703

**An Act Changing the Name of the Benigno S. Aquino Memorial High School in Concepcion, Tarlac, to Benigno S. Aquino National High School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7703, May 05, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BENIGNO S. AQUINO MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL IN CONCEPCION, TARLAC, TO BENIGNO S. AQUINO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Benigno S.Aquino Memorial High School in Concepcion, Tarlac, is hereby changed to Benigno S. Aquino National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved,

Lapsed into law on May 5, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
