# Republic Act No. 1014

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Yabawon in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1014**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Yabawon in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitios known as Mahaba, Angomon, Solocan, Kapanranan and Yabawon in the municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon, are hereby constituted into a separate and independent barrio to be known as the barrio of Yabawon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
