# Batas Pambansa Blg. 235

**An Act Changing the Name of the Coron School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Coron, Province of Palawan, to Assemblyman Claudio R. Sandoval School of Fisheries.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 235**

An Act Changing the Name of the Coron School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Coron, Province of Palawan, to Assemblyman Claudio R. Sandoval School of Fisheries

## Section 1

The name of the Coron School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Coron, Province of Palawan, is hereby changed to Assemblyman Claudio R. Sandoval School of fisheries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: September 12, 1982
