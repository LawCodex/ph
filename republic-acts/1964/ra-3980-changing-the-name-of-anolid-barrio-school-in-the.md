# Republic Act No. 3980

**An Act Changing the Name of Anolid Barrio School in the Municipality of Malasiqui, Province of Pangasinan, to Genaro Armas Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3980**

**An Act Changing the Name of Anolid Barrio School in the Municipality of Malasiqui, Province of Pangasinan, to Genaro Armas Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Anolid Barrio School in the Municipality of Malasiqui, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Genaro Armas Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
