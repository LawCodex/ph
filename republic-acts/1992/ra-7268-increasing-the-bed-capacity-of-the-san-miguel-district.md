# Republic Act No. 7268

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the San Miguel District Hospital in San Miguel, Bulacan, From Fifty (50) to Seventy-five (75) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7268, March 20, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE SAN MIGUEL DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN SAN MIGUEL, BULACAN, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assemble:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the San Miguel District Hospital in San Miguel, Bulacan, is hereby increased from fifty (50) to seventy-five (75) beds.1âшphi1

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
