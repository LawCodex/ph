# Republic Act No. 2880

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Sominot in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to San Antonio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2880**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Sominot in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to San Antonio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Sominot in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is changed to San Antonio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
