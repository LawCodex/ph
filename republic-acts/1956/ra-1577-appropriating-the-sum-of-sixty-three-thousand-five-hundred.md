# Republic Act No. 1577

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Sixty-Three Thousand Five Hundred Seventy-Two Pesos to Enable the Government of the Philippines to Meet its Commitments with the South East Asia Treaty Organization**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1577**

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Sixty-Three Thousand Five Hundred Seventy-Two Pesos to Enable the Government of the Philippines to Meet its Commitments with the South East Asia Treaty Organization**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sums are appropriated out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated for the period from July first, nineteen hundred fifty-six to June thirtieth, nineteen hundred fifty-seven, for the purpose specified hereunder:

<table>
<tr>
<td>1. For Philippine contribution to the SEATO Secretariat</td>
<td>₱42,512.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2">2. For the SEATO Division, Home Office, Department of Foreign Affairs:</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(a) One Chief of Division, Foreign Affairs Officer Class II</td>
<td>7,200.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(b) One Economist</td>
<td>6,000.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(c) One Political and Cultural Assistant</td>
<td>5,100.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(d) One Stenographer-clerk</td>
<td>2,760.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>TOTAL</td>
<td><u>₱63,572.00</u></td>
</tr>
</table>

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
