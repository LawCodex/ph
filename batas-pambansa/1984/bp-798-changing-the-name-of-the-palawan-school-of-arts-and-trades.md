# Batas Pambansa Blg. 798

**An Act Changing the Name of the Palawan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, to Palawan College of Arts and Trades**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 798**

An Act Changing the Name of the Palawan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, to Palawan College of Arts and Trades

## Section 1

The name of the Palawan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, is hereby changed to Palawan College of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

The Palawan College of Arts and Trades is authorized to continue offering the present collegiate courses of the Palawan School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
