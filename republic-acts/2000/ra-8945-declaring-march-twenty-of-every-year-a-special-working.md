# Republic Act No. 8945

**An Act Declaring March Twenty of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the City of San Fernando, Province of La Union**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8945 August 24, 2000**

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH TWENTY OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF SAN FERNANDO, PROVINCE OF LA UNION

## SECTION 1

March twenty of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the City of San Fernando, Province of La Union to commemorate the creation of said city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **August 24, 2000**
