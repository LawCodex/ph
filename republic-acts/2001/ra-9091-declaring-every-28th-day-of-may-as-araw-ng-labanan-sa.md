# Republic Act No. 9091

**An Act Declaring Every 28th Day of May as Araw Ng Labanan Sa Alapan, a Special Working Public Holiday in the Municipality of Imus, Cavite**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9091 April 08, 2001**

AN ACT DECLARING EVERY 28th DAY OF MAY AS ARAW NG LABANAN SA ALAPAN, A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF IMUS, CAVITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

May 28 of every year is hereby declared as Araw ng Labanan sa Alapan a special working public holiday in the Municipality of Imus, Cavite, in commemoration of the historic "Labanan sa Alapan."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately after its publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) AQUILINO Q. PIMENTEL, JR. President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE, JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on February 6, 2001 and October 2, 2000, respectively.
