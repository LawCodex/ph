# Republic Act No. 1987

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Paloma in the Municipality of Valladolid, Province of Negros Occidental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1987**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Paloma in the Municipality of Valladolid, Province of Negros Occidental**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Paloma in the Municipality of Valladolid, Province of Negros Occidental, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Paloma.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
