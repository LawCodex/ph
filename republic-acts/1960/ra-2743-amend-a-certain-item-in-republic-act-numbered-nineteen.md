# Republic Act No. 2743

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred for the Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2743**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred for the Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 67, page 470 of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"67. Municipal building, Hinundayan ₱10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
