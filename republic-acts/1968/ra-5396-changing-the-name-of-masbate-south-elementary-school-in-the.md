# Republic Act No. 5396

**An Act Changing the Name of Masbate South Elementary School in the Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, to Jose Zurbito, Sr. Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5396**

An Act Changing the Name of Masbate South Elementary School in the Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, to Jose Zurbito, Sr. Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Masbate South Elementary School in the Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, is hereby changed to Jose Zurbito, Sr. Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
