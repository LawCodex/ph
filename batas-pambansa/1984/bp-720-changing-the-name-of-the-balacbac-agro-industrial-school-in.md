# Batas Pambansa Blg. 720

**An Act Changing the Name of the Balacbac Agro-Industrial School in the Municipality of Kapangan, Province of Benguet, to Governor Bado Dangwa Agro-Industrial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 720**

An Act Changing the Name of the Balacbac Agro-Industrial School in the Municipality of Kapangan, Province of Benguet, to Governor Bado Dangwa Agro-Industrial School

## Section 1

The name of the Balacbac Agro-Industrial School in the Municipality of Kapangan, Province of Benguet, is changed to Governor Bado Dangwa Agro-Industrial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
