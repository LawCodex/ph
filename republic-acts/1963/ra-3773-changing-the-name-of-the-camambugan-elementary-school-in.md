# Republic Act No. 3773

**An Act Changing the Name of the Camambugan Elementary School in the Barrio of Camambugan, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, to Francisca Baldovino Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3773**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Camambugan Elementary School in the Barrio of Camambugan, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, to Francisca Baldovino Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Camambugan Elementary School in the barrio of Camambugan, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Francisca Baldovino Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
