# Republic Act No. 2833

**An Act Changing the Name of Alegria Street in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, to Juan Luna Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2833**

**An Act Changing the Name of Alegria Street in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, to Juan Luna Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Alegria Street in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, is hereby changed to Juan Luna Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
