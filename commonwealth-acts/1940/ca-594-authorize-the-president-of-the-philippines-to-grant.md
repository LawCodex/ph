# Commonwealth Act No. 594

**An Act to Authorize the President of the Philippines to Grant Retirement Gratuity to the Estate of Lorenzo Palileo.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 594, August 19, 1940 ]

AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES TO GRANT RETIREMENT GRATUITY TO THE ESTATE OF LORENZO PALILEO.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The President of the Philippines is authorized to grant retirement gratuity to the estate of Lorenzo Pelileo, former Provincial Treasurer of Davao, under the provisions of Act Numbered Two thousand five hundred eighty-nine, as an ended.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, August 19, 1940.
