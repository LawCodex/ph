# Republic Act No. 7326

**An Act Repealing Republic Acts Numbered Sixty-one Hundred Forty-five and Fifty-four Hundred Seventy-four (Re: Prohibition to Catch Fish Called "Ipon" During Certain Months of the Year, Etc.)**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7326, March 31, 1992 ]

AN ACT REPEALING REPUBLIC ACTS NUMBERED SIXTY-ONE HUNDRED FORTY-FIVE AND FIFTY-FOUR HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR (RE: PROHIBITION TO CATCH FISH CALLED "IPON" DURING CERTAIN MONTHS OF THE YEAR, ETC.)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Acts Numbered Sixty-one hundred forty-five and Fifty-four hundred seventy-four are hereby repealed by this Act.

## Section 2

All provisions of existing laws, orders, decrees, rules and regulations inconsistent herewith are likewise repealed.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
