# Republic Act No. 5319

**An Act Changing the Name of Pasig-Natividad Elementary School in the Municipality of Lambunao, Province of Iloilo, to Filemon Lebero Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5319**

An Act Changing the Name of Pasig-Natividad Elementary School in the Municipality of Lambunao, Province of Iloilo, to Filemon Lebero Memorial Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Pasig-Natividad Elementary School in the Municipality of Lambunao, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Filemon Lebero Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
