# Republic Act No. 2771

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Bug-O and Molobolo in the Municipalities of Asturias and Tuburan, Respectively, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2771**

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Bug-O and Molobolo in the Municipalities of Asturias and Tuburan, Respectively, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bug-o in the Municipality of Asturias, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Bug-o.

## Section 2

The sitio of Molobolo in the Municipality of Tuburan, same province, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Molobolo.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
