# Republic Act No. 3454

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Miguel Agorilla**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3454**

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Miguel Agorilla**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Miguel Agorilla is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto: _Provided,_ That this grant shall become effective upon Miguel Agorilla taking an oath of allegiance to the Republic of the Philippines and executing a renunciation of his former citizenship, copies of which shall be recorded in the civil registrar’s office in the locality of his residence: _Provided, further,_ That the citizenship herein granted carries with it all the duties and responsibilities incident to such citizenship.
