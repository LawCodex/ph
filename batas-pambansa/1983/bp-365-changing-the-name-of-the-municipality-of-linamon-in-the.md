# Batas Pambansa Blg. 365

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Linamon in the Province of Lanao Del Norte to Tomas Cabili.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 365**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Linamon in the Province of Lanao Del Norte to Tomas Cabili

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Linamon in the Province of Lanao del Norte is changed to Tomas Cabili.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1983
