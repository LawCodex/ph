# Republic Act No. 1730

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1730**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item four, Group I, under aw. Tarlac, page six hundred forty-three, section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is amended to read as follows:

| "4. Malacampa Elementary School Building, Camiling Repair and completion | ₱10,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
