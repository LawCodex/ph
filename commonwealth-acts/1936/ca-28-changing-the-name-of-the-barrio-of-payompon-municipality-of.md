# Commonwealth Act No. 28

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Payompon, Municipality of Sablayan, Province of Mindoro, to that of Buenavista.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 28, September 08, 1936 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BARRIO OF PAYOMPON, MUNICIPALITY OF SABLAYAN, PROVINCE OF MINDORO, TO THAT OF BUENAVISTA.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Payompon, municipality of Sablayan, Province of Mindoro, is hereby changed to that of Buenavista.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted, without Executive approval, September 8, 1936.
