# Republic Act No. 1973

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Llavac in the Municipality of Infanta, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1973**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Llavac in the Municipality of Infanta, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Biga, Pandaruan, Bubuyog, Maragundong, Riao, Mahabang Salongan, Llavac, Calauang, Tipuan, Malangam and Tulay-bato in the Municipality of Infanta, Province of Quezon, are hereby constituted into a new barrio in the said municipality to be known as barrio Llavac.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
