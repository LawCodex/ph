# Republic Act No. 2738

**An Act Amending Group III, Paragraph “Ah,” for Mountain Province, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven, by Adding Thereto a New Item to be Known as Item (49)**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2738**

**An Act Amending Group III, Paragraph "ah," for Mountain Province, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven, by Adding Thereto a New Item to be Known as Item (49)**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Group III, paragraph "ah," for Mountain Province, Section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is amended by adding thereto the following new item:

"(49) Namilangan Barrio School building, potia—Construction of three rooms ₱6,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
