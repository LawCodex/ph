# Republic Act No. 7253

**An Act Declaring the 24th Day of November of Every Year as a Special Holiday for City of Dumaguete, Province of Negros Oriental**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7253, March 20, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE 24TH DAY OF NOVEMBER OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL HOLIDAY FOR CITY OF DUMAGUETE, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The 24th day of November of every year is hereby declared as a special holiday for the City of Dumaguete, Province of Negros Oriental, in observance of its charter day.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, March 20, 1992.
