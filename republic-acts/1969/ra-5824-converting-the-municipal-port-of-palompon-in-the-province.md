# Republic Act No. 5824

**An Act Converting the Municipal Port of Palompon in the Province of Leyte Into a National Port**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5824, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE MUNICIPAL PORT OF PALOMPON IN THE PROVINCE OF LEYTE INTO A NATIONAL PORT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The municipal port of Palompon in the Province of Leyte is hereby converted into a national port.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Enacted without Executive approval, June 21, 1969.
