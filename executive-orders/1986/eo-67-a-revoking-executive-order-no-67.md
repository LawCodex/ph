# Executive Order No. 67-A

**Revoking Executive Order No. 67**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 67-A November 26, 1986**

REVOKING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 67

WHEREAS, Executive Order No. 67 was issued by the President to reorganize the Civil Service Commission;

WHEREAS, in view of the fact that the proposed Constitution was to be submitted to a plebiscite and it contained provisions on the Civil Service, Executive Order No. 67 must be revoked.

NOW, THEREFORE, I, CORAZON C. AQUINO, President of the Philippines, do hereby order:

## Sec. 1

Executive Order No. 67 is hereby revoked.

## Sec. 2

All laws, orders, issuances, rules, regulations or parts thereof inconsistent with this Executive Order are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

## Sec. 3

This Executive Order shall take effect immediately.

DONE in the City of Manila, Philippines, this 26th day of November, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and eighty-six.
