# Republic Act No. 1442

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Soledad in the Municipality of Binalbagan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Barrio Payao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1442**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Soledad in the Municipality of Binalbagan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Barrio Payao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Soledad in the Municipality of Binalbagan, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Barrio Payao.1âшphi1
