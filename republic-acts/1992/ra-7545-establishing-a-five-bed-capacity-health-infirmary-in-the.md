# Republic Act No. 7545

**An Act Establishing A Five-bed Capacity Health Infirmary in the Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, To Be Known As the Angat Health Infirmary, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7545, May 22, 1992 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A FIVE-BED CAPACITY HEALTH INFIRMARY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ANGAT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO BE KNOWN AS THE ANGAT HEALTH INFIRMARY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established in the Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, a five-bed capacity health infirmary, to be known as the Angat Health Infirmary.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
