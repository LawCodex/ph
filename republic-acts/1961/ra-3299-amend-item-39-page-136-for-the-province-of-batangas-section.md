# Republic Act No. 3299

**An Act to Amend Item 39, Page 136, for the Province of Batangas, Section Two-G of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3299**

**An Act to Amend Item 39, Page 136, for the Province of Batangas, Section Two-G of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 39, page 136, for the Province of Batangas, Section two-g of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is hereby amended to read as follows:

"39. Malaking Pulo Barrio School, Tanauan ₱10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
