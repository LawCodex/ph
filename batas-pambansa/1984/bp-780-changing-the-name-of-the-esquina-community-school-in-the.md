# Batas Pambansa Blg. 780

**An Act Changing the Name of the Esquina Community School in the Municipality of Baybay, Province of Leyte, to President Carlos P. Garcia Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 780**

An Act Changing the Name of the Esquina Community School in the Municipality of Baybay, Province of Leyte, to President Carlos P. Garcia Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Esquina Community School in the Municipality of Baybay, Province of Leyte, is changed to President Carlos P. Garcia Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
