# Republic Act No. 9430

**An Act Declaring July 23 of Each Year a Special Working Holiday in the City of Tanauan, Province of Batangas to be known as "Apolinario Mabini Day"**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9430 April 10, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING JULY 23 OF EACH YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF TANAUAN, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS TO BE KNOWN AS "APOLINARIO MABINI DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

To commemorate the birth anniversary of Apolinario Mabini, national hero and illustrious son of the City of Tanauan, Province of Batangas, July 23 of each year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the said city to be known as "Apolinario Mabini Day".

## SEC. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
