# Batas Pambansa Blg. 743

**An Act Changing the Name of the Onat-Bagacay Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Braulio Manikan Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 743**

An Act Changing the Name of the Onat-Bagacay Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Braulio Manikan Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Onat-Bagacay Elementary School located in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, is hereby changed to Braulio Manikan Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
