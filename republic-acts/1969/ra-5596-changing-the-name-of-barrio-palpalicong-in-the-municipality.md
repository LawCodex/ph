# Republic Act No. 5596

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Palpalicong in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Franco**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5596, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO PALPALICONG IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BATAC, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO BARRIO FRANCO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Palpalicong in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Franco,

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
