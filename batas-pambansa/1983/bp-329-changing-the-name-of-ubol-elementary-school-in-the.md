# Batas Pambansa Blg. 329

**An Act Changing the Name of Ubol Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Datu Amilbangsa Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 329**

An Act Changing the Name of Ubol Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Datu Amilbangsa Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Ubol Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is changed to Datu Amilbangsa Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
