# Republic Act No. 5437

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Patricia B. Rosal**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5437**

An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Patricia B. Rosal

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Patricia V. Rosal is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: September 9, 1968.
