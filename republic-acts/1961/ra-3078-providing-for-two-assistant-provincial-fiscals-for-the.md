# Republic Act No. 3078

**An Act Providing for Two Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Southern Leyte, by Further Amending Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3078**

**An Act Providing for Two Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Southern Leyte, by Further Amending Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to assistant provincial fiscals, is further amended so that the Province of Southern Leyte shall have two assistant provincial fiscals.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1961.
