# Republic Act No. 4149

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Langangan in the Province of Cagayan, to Santa Praxedes**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4149**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Langangan in the Province of Cagayan, to Santa Praxedes**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Langangan in the Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to Santa Praxedes.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
