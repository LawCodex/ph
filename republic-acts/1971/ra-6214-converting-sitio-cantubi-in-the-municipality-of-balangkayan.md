# Republic Act No. 6214

**An Act Converting Sitio Cantubi in the Municipality of Balangkayan in the Province of Eastern Samar Into a Regular Barrio to Be Known as Barrio Cantubi.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6214, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING SITIO CANTUBI IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BALANGKAYAN IN THE PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR INTO A REGULAR BARRIO TO BE KNOWN AS BARRIO CANTUBI.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cantubi in the Municipality of Balangkayan in the Province of Eastern Samar is hereby converted into a regular barrio to be known as Barrio Cantubi.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
