# Republic Act No. 1688

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Taug Norte, Barrio of Taug, Municipality of Lila, Province of Bohol, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of La Fortuna**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1688**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Taug Norte, Barrio of Taug, Municipality of Lila, Province of Bohol, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of La Fortuna**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Taug Norte, barrio of Taug, Municipality of Lila, Province of Bohol, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of La Fortuna.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
