# Republic Act No. 1680

**An Act Amending Item (b), Group III, Paragraph Thirty-Five, for the Mountain Province, Subsection f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Four Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1680**

**An Act Amending Item (b), Group III, Paragraph Thirty-Five, for the Mountain Province, Subsection f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Four Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (b), group III, paragraph thirty-five, for the Mountain Province, subsection f, section two of Republic Act Numbered One thousand four hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "(b) Besao Municipal Building Construction | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
