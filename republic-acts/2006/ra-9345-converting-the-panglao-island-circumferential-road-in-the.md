# Republic Act No. 9345

**An Act Converting the Panglao Island Circumferential Road in the Municipalities of Panglao and Dauis, including the Ambassador Pablo Suarez Bridge in the Sitio Ubos-Totulan, Tagbilaran City, Province of Bohol into a National Road**

Thirteenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fifth day of July, two thousand five.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9345 June 5, 2006**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE PANGLAO ISLAND CIRCUMFERENTIAL ROAD IN THE MUNICIPALITIES OF PANGLAO AND DAUIS, INCLUDING THE AMBASSADOR PABLO SUAREZ BRIDGE IN THE SITIO UBOS-TOTULAN, TAGBILARAN CITY, PROVINCE OF BOHOL INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

The Panglao Island Circumferential Road in the Municipalities of Panglao and Dauis, including the Ambassador Pablo Suarez Bridge in the Sitio Ubos-Totulan, Tagbilaran City, Province of Bohol, consisting of 37 kilometers, more or less, is hereby converted into a national road.

## SEC. 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## SEC. 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
