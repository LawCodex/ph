# Republic Act No. 2136

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Malibayo, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Malibayo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2136**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Malibayo, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Malibayo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Malibayo, barrio of Simlong, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Malibayo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
