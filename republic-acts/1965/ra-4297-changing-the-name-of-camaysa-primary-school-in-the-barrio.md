# Republic Act No. 4297

**An Act Changing the Name of Camaysa Primary School in the Barrio of Dapdap, Municipality of Tayabas, Province of Quezon, to Dapdap Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4297**

An Act Changing the Name of Camaysa Primary School in the Barrio of Dapdap, Municipality of Tayabas, Province of Quezon, to Dapdap Primary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Camaysa Primary School in the Barrio of Dapdap, Municipality of Tayabas, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Dapdap Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
