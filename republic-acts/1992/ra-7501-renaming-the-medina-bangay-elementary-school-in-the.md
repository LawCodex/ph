# Republic Act No. 7501

**An Act Renaming the Medina-bangay Elementary School in the Municipality of Dingras, Province of Ilocos Norte, To Medina-parado Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7501, May 18, 1992 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE MEDINA-BANGAY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DINGRAS, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO MEDINA-PARADO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Medina-Bangay Elementary School located in the Municipality of Dingras, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby renamed as the Medina-Parado Elementary School.

## Section 2

All allocations provided for the operation and maintenance of the Medina-Bangay Elementary School shall be implemented to the Medina-Parado Elementary School.

## Section 3

The complete organizational staff of the Medina-Bangay Elementary School is deemed adopted by the Medina-Parado Elementary School.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
