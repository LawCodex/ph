# Republic Act No. 8452

**An Act Declaring the Twelfth Day of July of Each Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Laguindingan, Province of Misamis Oriental**

Tenth Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8452, January 11, 1998 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE TWELFTH DAY OF JULY OF EACH YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LAGUINDINGAN, PROVINCE OF MISAMIS ORIENTAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Laguindingan, Misamis Oriental, was officially created as a municipality on July 12, 1963 by virtue of Republic Act No. 3824.Ꮮαwρhi৷

## Section 2

The Twelfth day of July of each year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Laguindingan, Province of Misamis Oriental, in celebration of its charter day to be known as Araw ng Laguindingan.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, Lapsed into law on January 11, 1998, without the signature of the President, in accordance with Article VI, Section 27 (1) of the Constitution.
