# Republic Act No. 1512

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Bayugo, Palay-Palay, and Sipsipin, Municipality of Jalajala, Province of Rizal, into Barrios of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1512**

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Bayugo, Palay-Palay, and Sipsipin, Municipality of Jalajala, Province of Rizal, into Barrios of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bayugo, Palay-Palay, and Sipsipin, Municipality of Jalajala, Province of Rizal, are converted into barrios of said municipality to be known as the barrios of Bayugo, Palay-Palay, and Sipsipin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
