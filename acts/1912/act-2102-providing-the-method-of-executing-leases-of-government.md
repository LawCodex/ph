# Act No. 2102

**An Act Providing the Method of Executing Leases of Government Buildings or Cottages.**

[ Act No. 2102, January 25, 1912 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING THE METHOD OF EXECUTING LEASES OF GOVERNMENT BUILDINGS OR COTTAGES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

All contracts and leases for the use and occupancy of Government buildings or cottages in the territory under the exclusive control of the Philippine Commission shall hereafter be executed by the Director of Public Works, with the approval of the Secretary of Commerce and Police.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, January 25, 1912.
