# Republic Act No. 7254

**An Act Declaring January Six of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the City of Toledo, Province of Cebu**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7254, March 20, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY SIX OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF TOLEDO, PROVINCE OF CEBU

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

January Six of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the City of Toledo, Province of Cebu, to commemorate its charter day anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
