# Republic Act No. 6898

**An Act Establishing a Ten-bed Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Don Marcelino, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Be Known as the Don Marcelino Municipal Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6898, March 09, 1990 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A TEN-BED MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DON MARCELINO, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE DON MARCELINO MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Department of Health, a ten-bed municipal hospital in the Municipality of Don Marcelino, Province of Davao del Sur, to be known as the Don Marcelino Municipal Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be charged against the appropriations authorized for the purpose under the Fiscal Year 1989 General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of the herein hospital shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.
