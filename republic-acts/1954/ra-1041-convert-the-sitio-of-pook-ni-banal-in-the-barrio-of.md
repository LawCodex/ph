# Republic Act No. 1041

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Pook Ni Banal in the Barrio of Malaking Pook, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1041**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Pook Ni Banal in the Barrio of Malaking Pook, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio of Pook ni Banal in the Barrio of Malaking Pook, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Pook ni Banal.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
