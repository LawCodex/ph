# Republic Act No. 6786

**An Act Changing the Name of the National Orthopedic Hospital and Rehabilitation Medical Center in Quezon City to Philippine Orthopedic Center**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6786, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE NATIONAL ORTHOPEDIC HOSPITAL AND REHABILITATION MEDICAL CENTER IN QUEZON CITY TO PHILIPPINE ORTHOPEDIC CENTER

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the National Orthopedic Hospital and Rehabilitation Medical Center in Quezon City is hereby changed to Philippine Orthopedic Center.1a⍵⍴h!1
