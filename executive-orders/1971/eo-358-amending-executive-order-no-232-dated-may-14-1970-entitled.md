# Executive Order No. 358

**Amending Executive Order No. 232 Dated May 14, 1970, Entitled “Creating the Presidential Development Budget Committee”**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 358, December 21, 1971 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 232 DATED MAY 14, 1970, ENTITLED “CREATING THE PRESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT BUDGET COMMITTEE.”

Executive Order No. 232 dated May 14, 1970, is hereby amended to include the Executive Secretary or his representative as member of the Presidential Development Budget Committee.

Done in the City of Manila, this 21st day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-one.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **ALEJANDRO MELCHOR**
Executive Secretary
