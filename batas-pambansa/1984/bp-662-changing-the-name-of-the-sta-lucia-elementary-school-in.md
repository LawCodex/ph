# Batas Pambansa Blg. 662

**An Act Changing the Name of the sta. Lucia Elementary School in Barangay Kabayanan, Municipality of San Juan, Metropolitan Manila, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Kabayanan**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 662**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Lucia Elementary School in Barangay Kabayanan, Municipality of San Juan, Metropolitan Manila, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Kabayanan**

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Lucia Elementary School in Barangay Kabayanan, Municipality of San Juan, Metropolitan Manila, is hereby changed to Mababang Paaralan ng Kabayanan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
