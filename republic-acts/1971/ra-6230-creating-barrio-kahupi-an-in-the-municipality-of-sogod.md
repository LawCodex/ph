# Republic Act No. 6230

**An Act Creating Barrio Kahupi-An in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6230, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO KAHUPI-AN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SOGOD, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bood Taas, Tabunan, Hap-on, Kabugua-an, Tigbawan, Lubong Sapa, Kahupian and Pangalkagan in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte, are hereby constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Kahupi-an.

The proposed barrio of Kahupi-an shall be bounded as follows:

<table>
<tr>
<td>North--</td>
<td>Lubong Sapa Creek</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>East--</td>
<td>Bulak River</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>South--</td>
<td>Pan-Phil. Highway, Km. 13</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>WesWLayug River</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
