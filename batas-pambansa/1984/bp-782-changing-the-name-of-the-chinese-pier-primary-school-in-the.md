# Batas Pambansa Blg. 782

**An Act Changing the Name of the Chinese Pier Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Rasul**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 782**

An Act Changing the Name of the Chinese Pier Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Rasul

## Section 1

The name of the Chinese Pier Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, is changed to Mababang Paaralan ng Rasul.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
