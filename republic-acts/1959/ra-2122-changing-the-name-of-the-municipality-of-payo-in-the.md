# Republic Act No. 2122

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Payo in the Province of Catanduanes to Panganiban**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2122**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Payo in the Province of Catanduanes to Panganiban**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Payo in the Province of Catanduanes is changed to Panganiban.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
