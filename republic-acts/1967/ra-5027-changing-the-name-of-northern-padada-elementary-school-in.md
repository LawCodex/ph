# Republic Act No. 5027

**An Act Changing the Name of Northern Padada Elementary School in the Barrio of Guihing, Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, to Guihing Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5027**

An Act Changing the Name of Northern Padada Elementary School in the Barrio of Guihing, Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, to Guihing Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Northern Padada Elementary School in the barrio of Guihing. Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, is hereby changed to Guihing Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
