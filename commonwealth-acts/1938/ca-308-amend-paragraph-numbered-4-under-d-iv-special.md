# Commonwealth Act No. 308

**An Act to Amend Paragraph Numbered 4, Under D-IV-Special Appropriations, Section Two, Pastes Fifty-Nine and Sixty, English Version, of Commonwealth Act Numbered Two Hundred Forty-Five Pertaining to the Appropriation of the Department of the Interior So as to Reitemize Item D-IV-4-0) thereof and for Other Purposes.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 308, June 09, 1938 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND PARAGRAPH NUMBERED 4, UNDER D-IV-SPECIAL APPROPRIATIONS, SECTION TWO, PASTES FIFTY-NINE AND SIXTY, ENGLISH VERSION, OF COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED TWO HUNDRED FORTY-FIVE PERTAINING TO THE APPROPRIATION OF THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR SO AS TO REITEMIZE ITEM D-IV-4-0) THEREOF AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:
