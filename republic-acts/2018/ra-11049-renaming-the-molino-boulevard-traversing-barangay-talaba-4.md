# Republic Act No. 11049

**An Act Renaming the Molino Boulevard Traversing Barangay Talaba 4 and Barangay San Nicolas 3 in the City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite as Bacoor Boulevard**

Seventeenth Congress  
Second Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand seventeen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11049**

"An Act Renaming the Molino Boulevard Traversing Barangay Talaba 4 and Barangay San Nicolas 3 in the City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite as Bacoor Boulevard"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Molino Boulevard which traverses barangay Talaba 4, Niog 3, Ligas 2, Ligas 3, Bayanan, Mambog 4, Molino 2, Molino 3, San Nicolas 1 and San Nicolas 3 in the City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite is hereby renamed as Bacoor Boulevard.

## Section 2

The Department of Public Works and Highways shall place appropriate markers along Bacoor Boulevard and issue the necessary orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,

**AQUILINO "KOKO" PIMENTEL III**
President of the Senate

**PANTALEON D. ALVAREZ**
