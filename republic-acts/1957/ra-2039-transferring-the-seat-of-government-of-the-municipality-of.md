# Republic Act No. 2039

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Kayan, Mountain Province, from its Present Site to the Site of the Barrio of Tadian in the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2039**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Kayan, Mountain Province, from its Present Site to the Site of the Barrio of Tadian in the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Kayan, Mountain Province, is transferred to the site of the barrio of Tadian in the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 23, 1957.
