# Republic Act No. 1588

**An Act Condoning the Liquidated Damages in the Amount of Four Thousand Five Hundred Sixty Pesos Imposed on Engineer-Contractor Andres Bolinas, Jr., of DARaga, Albay, Due to His Failure to Finish Within the Contract Period the Construction of the Legaspi Slaughterhouse**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1588**

**An Act Condoning the Liquidated Damages in the Amount of Four Thousand Five Hundred Sixty Pesos Imposed on Engineer-Contractor Andres Bolinas, Jr., of Daraga, Albay, Due to His Failure to Finish Within the Contract Period the Construction of the Legaspi Slaughterhouse**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The liquidated damages in the amount of four thousand five hundred sixty pesos imposed on Engineer-Contractor Andres Bolinas, Jr., of Daraga, Albay, due to his failure to finish within the contract period the construction of the Legaspi Slaughterhouse, is condoned.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
