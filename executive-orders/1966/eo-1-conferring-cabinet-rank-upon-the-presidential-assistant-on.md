# Executive Order No. 1

**Conferring Cabinet Rank Upon the Presidential Assistant on Community Development**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 1, January 01, 1966 ]

CONFERRING THE CABINET RANK UPON THE PRESIDENTIAL ASSISTANT COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT.

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, **I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS** , President of the Philippines , do hereby elevate the Presidential Assistant Community Development to the rank of a Member of a Cabinet, with all the rights, honors and privileges pertaining to that position.

Done in the City of Manila , this 1st day of January, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and sixty-six.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **RAFAEL M. SALAS**
Executive Secretary
