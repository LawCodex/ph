# Republic Act No. 1528

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagongbayan in the Barrio of Ilian, Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1528**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagongbayan in the Barrio of Ilian, Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bagongbayan in the Barrio of Ilian, Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the Barrio of Bagongbayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
