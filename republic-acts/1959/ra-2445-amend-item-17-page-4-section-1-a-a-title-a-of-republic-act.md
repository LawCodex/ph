# Republic Act No. 2445

**An Act to Amend Item (17), Page 4, Section 1-A-a, Title (A) of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2445**

**An Act to Amend Item (17), Page 4, Section 1-A-a, Title (A) of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (17), page 4, section 1-A-a, Title (A) of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(17) Industrial Arts Annex, Iloilo Vocational High School-Lambunao ₱20,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
