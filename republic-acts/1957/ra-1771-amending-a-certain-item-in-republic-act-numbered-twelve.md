# Republic Act No. 1771

**An Act Amending a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1771**

**An Act Amending a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (36) under q. Cebu, title A, section two of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred is amended to read as follows:

| "(36) Construction of a permanent 2-room Academic Building, Plan No. 1-4, San Fernando | ₱12,800.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
