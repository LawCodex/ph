# Republic Act No. 6808

**An Act Naming the Plaridel-Bustos-Angat-Norzagaray-Santa Maria Highway in the Province of Bulacan as the General Alejo G. Santos Highway**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6808 December 13, 1989**

AN ACT NAMING THE PLARIDEL-BUSTOS-ANGAT- NORZAGARAY-SANTA MARIA HIGHWAY IN THE PROVINCE OF BULACAN AS THE GENERAL ALEJO G. SANTOS HIGHWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Plaridel-Bustos-Angat-Norzagaray-Santa Maria Highway in the Province of Bulacan is hereby named as the General Alejo G. Santos Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 13, 1989**
