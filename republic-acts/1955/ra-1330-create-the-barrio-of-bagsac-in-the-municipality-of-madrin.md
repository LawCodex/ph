# Republic Act No. 1330

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Bagsac in the Municipality of Madrin, Province of Surigao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1330**

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Bagsac in the Municipality of Madrin, Province of Surigao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bagsac in the Municipality of Madrid, Province of Surigao, is hereby constituted into a barrio in the same municipality, to be known as Bagsac.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
