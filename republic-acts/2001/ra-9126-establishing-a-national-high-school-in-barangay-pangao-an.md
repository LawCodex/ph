# Republic Act No. 9126

**An Act Establishing a National High School in Barangay Pangao-An, Municipality of Magpet, Province of Cotabato to Be Known as Pangao-An National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9126 April 24, 2001**

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY PANGAO-AN, MUNICIPALITY OF MAGPET, PROVINCE OF COTABATO TO BE KNOWN AS PANGAO-AN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby created a new national high school in Barangay Pangao-an, Municipality of Magpet, Province of Cotabato to be known as Pangao-an National High School.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) AQUILINO Q. PIMENTEL JR. President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on August 22, 2000 and February 5, 2001, respectively.
