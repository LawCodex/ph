# Republic Act No. 5781

**An Act Amending Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Fifty-One Hundred Eighty-Seven (Republic Works Projects in the Province of Catanduanes)**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5781, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT AMENDING CERTAIN ITEMS IN REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED FIFTY-ONE HUNDRED EIGHTY-SEVEN (REPUBLIC WORKS PROJECTS IN THE PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES).

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Item (e), subparagraph 11, for the Province of Catanduanes, page 424, paragraph "a", Title E, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Fifty-one hundred eighty-seven is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(e) San Andres Seawall 110,000"

## Section 2

Item 42, page 1211, paragraph "a", Title J, Section five of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

"42. Port of Virac, Catanduanes, including shore protection 500,000"

## Section 3

Item 107, page 1215, paragraph "b", _Title_ J, Section five of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

"107. Port of Calolbon, Catanduanes, including shore protection 200,000"

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 21, 1969.
