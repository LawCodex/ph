# Republic Act No. 12228

**An Act Declaring The Seventh Day of November of Every Year A Special National Working Holiday In Commemoration of The Establishment of The First Philippine Mosque and The Introduction of Islam In The Philippines**

Nineteenth Congress  
First Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fifth day of July, two thousand twenty two.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12228, July 09, 2025 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE SEVENTH DAY OF NOVEMBER OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NATIONAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN COMMEMORATION O THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE FIRST PHILIPPINE MOSQUE AND THE INTRODUCTION OF ISLAM IN THE PHILIPPINES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Short Title._ \- This Act shall be known as the "Sheikh Karum'ul Makhdum Day".

## Section 2

_Declaration of Policy._ \- It is the policy of the State to instill inclusivity to all regardless of religious beliefs, in line with the constitutional right to freely exercise and enjoy their religion. Moreover, it is the State's policy to provide due recognition to the indispensable contribution of Muslim Filipinos in the development of Philippine culture and civilization.

## Section 3

_National Holiday._ \- Every seventh (7th) day of November is hereby declared as "Sheikh Karim'ul Makhdum Day", a special national working holiday in commemoration of the establishment of the first Philippine Mosque, and the introduction of Islam in the Philippines.

## Section 4

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
