# Republic Act No. 1740

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Malirong, Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, to Libertad**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1740**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Malirong, Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, to Libertad**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Malirong, Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, is changed to Libertad.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
