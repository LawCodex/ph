# Batas Pambansa Blg. 736

**An Act Changing the Name of Andagaw Elementary School Located in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Juan M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 736**

An Act Changing the Name of Andagaw Elementary School Located in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Juan M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Andagaw Elementary School located in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, is hereby changed to Honorable Juan M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
