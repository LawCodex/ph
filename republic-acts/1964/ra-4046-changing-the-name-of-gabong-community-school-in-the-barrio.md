# Republic Act No. 4046

**An Act Changing the Name of Gabong, Community School in the Barrio of Pandanan, Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan, to Tuan Hadji Jiakaria General Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4046**

**An Act Changing the Name of Gabong, Community School in the Barrio of Pandanan, Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan, to Tuan Hadji Jiakaria General Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Gabong Community School in the barrio of Pandanan, Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan, is hereby changed to Tuan Hadji Jiakaria General Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
