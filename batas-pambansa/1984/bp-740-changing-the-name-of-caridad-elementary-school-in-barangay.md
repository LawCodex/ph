# Batas Pambansa Blg. 740

**An Act Changing the Name of Caridad Elementary School in Barangay Bagroy in the City of Bago to Perez-Poblete Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 740**

An Act Changing the Name of Caridad Elementary School in Barangay Bagroy in the City of Bago to Perez-Poblete Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Caridad Elementary School in Barangay Bagroy, in the City of Bago, is hereby changed to Perez-Poblete Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
