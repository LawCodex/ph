# Republic Act No. 2336

**An Act Establishing a Summer Resort in Sampaloc, Municipality of Tanay, Province of Rizal**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2336**

**An Act Establishing a Summer Resort in Sampaloc, Municipality of Tanay, Province of Rizal**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby established a summer resort in Sampaloc, Municipality of Tanay, Province of Rizal. The Commission on Parks and Wildlife shall issue such rules and regulations for the establishment of such resort.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1959.
