# Batas Pambansa Blg. 795

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tondo High School in the City of Manila to Aurelio V. Tolentino High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 795**

An Act Changing the Name of the Tondo High School in the City of Manila to Aurelio V. Tolentino High School

## Section 1

The name of the Tondo High School in the City of Manila is changed to Aurelio V. Tolentino High nynySchool.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
