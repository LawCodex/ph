# Republic Act No. 1036

**An Act to Change the Name of the Naipit Community School in the Barrio of Bulauan, Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, to Bulauan Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1036**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Naipit Community School in the Barrio of Bulauan, Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, to Bulauan Community School**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the Naipit Community School in the Barrio of Bulauan, Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, is hereby changed to Bulauan Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
