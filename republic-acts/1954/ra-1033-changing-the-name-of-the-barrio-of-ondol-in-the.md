# Republic Act No. 1033

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Ondol in the Municipality of Mabini, Province of Bohol, to San Jose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1033**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Ondol in the Municipality of Mabini, Province of Bohol, to San Jose**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the Barrio of Ondol in the municipality of Mabini, Province of Bohol, is hereby changed to San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
