# Batas Pambansa Blg. 682

**An Act Changing the Name of Agmalabo Elementary School in the Municipality of IVIsan, Province of Capiz, Into Pang-Alaalang Paaralan Panfilo Mendoza**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 682**

An Act Changing the Name of Agmalabo Elementary School in the Municipality of Ivisan, Province of Capiz, into Pang-Alaalang Paaralang Panfilo Mendoza

## Section 1

The name of Agmalabo Elementary School in the Municipality of Ivisan, Province of Capiz, is changed to Pang-alaalang Paaralang Panfilo Mendoza.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
