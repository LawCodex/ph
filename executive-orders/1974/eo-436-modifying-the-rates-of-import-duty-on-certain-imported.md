# Executive Order No. 436

**Modifying the Rates of Import Duty on Certain Imported Articles as Provided Under Republic Act No. 1937, Otherwise Known as the Tariff and Customs Code of the Philippines, as Amended**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 436 October 15, 1974**

MODIFYING THE RATES OF IMPORT DUTY ON CERTAIN IMPORTED ARTICLES AS PROVIDED UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NO. 1937, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE TARIFF AND CUSTOMS CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES, AS AMENDED

WHEREAS, it is the government policy to accelerate the sports program and the development of sports in the country;

WHEREFORE, pursuant to the powers vested in me by Section 401 of Republic Act No. 1937, as amended, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby direct and order:

## Sec. 1

The articles specifically listed hereunder are classified under Section 104 of Republic Act No. 1937 shall pay the following rates of import duty:

Val. 20% |

## Sec. 2

This Order shall take effect immediately.

## Sec. 3

Any unwarranted increase in the prices of local products benefited by these tariff changes may be sufficient ground for the revocation of such tariff changes.

DONE in the City of Manila, this 15th day of October, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-four.
