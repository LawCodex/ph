# Republic Act No. 3312

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twenty Hundred and Ninety-Three**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3312**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twenty Hundred and Ninety-Three**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (b), under 11-Cebu, page 162, paragraph (b), Title G, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twenty hundred and ninety-three, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(b) Talaytay Communal Irrigation Project, Argao ₱20,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
