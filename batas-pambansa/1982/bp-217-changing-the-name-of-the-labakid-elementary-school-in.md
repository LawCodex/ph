# Batas Pambansa Blg. 217

**An Act Changing the Name of the Labakid Elementary School in Barangay Labakid, Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, to Apolonio Molina Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 217**

An Act Changing the Name of the Labakid Elementary School in Barangay Labakid, Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, to Apolonio Molina Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Labakid Elementary School in Barangay Labakid, Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Apolonio Molina Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
