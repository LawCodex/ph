# Republic Act No. 1536

**An Act Creating the Municipal District of Dingalan in the Municipality of Baler, Subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1536**

**An Act Creating the Municipal District of Dingalan in the Municipality of Baler, Subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Dingalan in the Municipality of Baler, subprovince of Aurora, Province of Quezon, is hereby converted into a municipal district.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
