# Republic Act No. 6789

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dr. Efrain C. Montemayor Medical Center in Baguio City to Baguio General Hospital and Medical Center**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6789, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DR. EFRAIN C. MONTEMAYOR MEDICAL CENTER IN BAGUIO CITY TO BAGUIO GENERAL HOSPITAL AND MEDICAL CENTER

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Dr. Efrain C. Montemayor Medical Center in Baguio City is hereby changed to Baguio General Hospital and Medical Center.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
