# Republic Act No. 4183

**An Act to Changing the Name of Inudaran Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Guro Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4183**

An Act to Changing the Name of Inudaran Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Guro Central Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Inudaran Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, is changed to Sultan Guro Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 21, 1965.
