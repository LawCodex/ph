# Batas Pambansa Blg. 657

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Veterans VIllage in the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, Into Barangay Luis C. Ruiz, Sr.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 657**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Veterans Village in the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, into Barangay Luis C. Ruiz, Sr.**

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Veterans Village in the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is changed into Barangay Luis C. Ruiz, Sr.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
