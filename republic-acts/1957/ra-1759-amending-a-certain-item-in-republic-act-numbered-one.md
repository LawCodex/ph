# Republic Act No. 1759

**An Act Amending a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred, Entitled “An Act Appropriating Funds for Public Works and Amending Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred and Twenty”**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1759**

**An Act Amending a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred, Entitled "An Act Appropriating Funds for Public Works and Amending Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred and Twenty"**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item eleven, for the Province of Negros Oriental, of section one under title B, Highways, page two hundred seventy-two of Republic Act Numbered One thousand two hundred, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "11. Lipayo-Mag-aso Feeder Road, Dauin—Construction | ₱25,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
