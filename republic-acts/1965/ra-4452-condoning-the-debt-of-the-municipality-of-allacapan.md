# Republic Act No. 4452

**An Act Condoning the Debt of the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, to the National Government in the Amount of Twenty-Six Thousand Three Hundred Eighty-Three Pesos and Sixty-Nine Centavos**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4452**

An Act Condoning the Debt of the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, to the National Government in the Amount of Twenty-Six Thousand Three Hundred Eighty-Three Pesos and Sixty-Nine Centavos

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The debt of the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, to the National Government in the amount of twenty-six thousand three hundred eighty-three pesos and sixty-nine centavos, is condoned.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
