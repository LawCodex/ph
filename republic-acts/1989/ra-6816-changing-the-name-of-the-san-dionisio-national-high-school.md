# Republic Act No. 6816

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Dionisio National High School in the Municipality of San Dionisio, Province of Iloilo, to Nicomedes R. Tubar, Sr. National High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6816 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN DIONISIO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN DIONISIO, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO NICOMEDES R. TUBAR, SR. NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Dionisio National High School in the Municipality of San Dionisio, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Nicomedes R. Tubar, Sr. National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
