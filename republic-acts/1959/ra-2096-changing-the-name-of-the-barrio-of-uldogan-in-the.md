# Republic Act No. 2096

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Uldogan in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Isabela, to Laoag**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2096**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Uldogan in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Isabela, to Laoag**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Uldogan in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Isabela, is changed to Laoag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 22, 1959.
