# Republic Act No. 4182

**An Act to Change the Name of Eastern Pangasinan Emergency Hospital to Eastern Pangasinan General Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4182**

An Act to Change the Name of Eastern Pangasinan Emergency Hospital to Eastern Pangasinan General Hospital

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Eastern Pangasinan Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Eastern Pangasinan General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 21, 1965.
