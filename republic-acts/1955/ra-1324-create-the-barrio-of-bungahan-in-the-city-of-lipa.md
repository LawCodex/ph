# Republic Act No. 1324

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Bungahan in the City of Lipa**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1324**

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Bungahan in the City of Lipa**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Duhatan, Tagbakin, Initan, Malabong and Halang, all in the barrio of Pinagtungolan, City of Lipa, are separated from the said barrio, and constituted into a separate barrio in the said city to be known as the barrio of Bungahan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
