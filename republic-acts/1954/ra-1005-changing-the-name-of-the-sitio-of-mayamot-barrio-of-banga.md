# Republic Act No. 1005

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sitio of Mayamot, Barrio of Banga, Municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, to Bagong Silang**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1005**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sitio of Mayamot, Barrio of Banga, Municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, to Bagong Silang**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the Sitio of Mayamot, in the Barrio of Banga, Municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, is changed to Bagong Silang.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
