# Republic Act No. 1750

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Emilod, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1750**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Emilod, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Emilod, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Emilod.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
