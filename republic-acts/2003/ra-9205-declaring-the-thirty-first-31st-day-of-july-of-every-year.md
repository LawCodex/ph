# Republic Act No. 9205

**An Act Declaring the Thirty-First (31st) Day of July of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Sagay, Province of Camiguin, to Be Known as "Sagay Foundation Day"**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9205 April 20, 2003**

AN ACT DECLARING THE THIRTY-FIRST (31ST) DAY OF JULY OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAGAY, PROVINCE OF CAMIGUIN, TO BE KNOWN AS "SAGAY FOUNDATION DAY"

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

July thirty-one of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Sagay, Province of Camiguin, to commemorate its foundation day anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
