# Batas Pambansa Blg. 867

**An Act Further Extending the Effectivity of Batas Pambansa Bilang 25 to June 30, 1985**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 867**

An Act Further Extending the Effectivity of Batas Pambansa Bilang 25 to June 30, 1985

## Section 1

The effectivity of Batas Pambansa Bilang 25 as extended by Presidential Decree No. 1912 up to December 31, 1984 is hereby further extended to June 30, 1985.

## Section 2

Any existing law or decree contrary to this Act is hereby repealed, amended, or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 28, 1984
