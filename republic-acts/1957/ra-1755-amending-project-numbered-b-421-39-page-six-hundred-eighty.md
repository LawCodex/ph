# Republic Act No. 1755

**An Act Amending Project Numbered B-421.39, Page Six Hundred Eighty-A, of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1755**

**An Act Amending Project Numbered B-421.39, Page Six Hundred Eighty-A, of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Project numbered B-421.39, page six hundred eighty-A, of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "B-421.39, Remedios Suspension Bridge, Mauban | ₱3,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
