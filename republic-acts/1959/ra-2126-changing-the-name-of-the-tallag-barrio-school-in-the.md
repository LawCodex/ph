# Republic Act No. 2126

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tallag Barrio School in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, to Biuag Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2126**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tallag Barrio School in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, to Biuag Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tallag Barrio School in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to Biuag Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
