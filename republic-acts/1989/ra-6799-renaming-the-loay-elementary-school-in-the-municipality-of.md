# Republic Act No. 6799

**An Act Renaming the Loay Elementary School in the Municipality of Loay, Province of Bohol, to Senate President Pro Tempore Jose A. Clarin Memorial School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6799 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT RENAMING THE LOAY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LOAY, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, TO SENATE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE JOSE A. CLARIN MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Loay Elementary School located in the Municipality of Loay, Province of Bohol, is hereby renamed as the Senate President Pro Tempore Jose A. Clarin Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
