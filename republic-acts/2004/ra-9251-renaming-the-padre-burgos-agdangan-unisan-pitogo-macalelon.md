# Republic Act No. 9251

**An Act Renaming the Padre Burgos - Agdangan - Unisan - Pitogo - Macalelon - General Luna - Catanauan - Mulanay - San Francisco - San Andres - San Narciso - Buenavista National Road in Bondoc Peninsula in the Province of Quezon as the Miguel Suarez Highway**

Twelfth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eight day of July, two thousand and three.

**Republic Act No. 9251 February 19 2004**

AN ACT RENAMING THE PADRE BURGOS - AGDANGAN - UNISAN - PITOGO - MACALELON - GENERAL LUNA - CATANAUAN - MULANAY - SAN FRANCISCO - SAN ANDRES - SAN NARCISO - BUENAVISTA NATIONAL ROAD IN BONDOC PENINSULA IN THE PROVINCE OF QUEZON AS THE MIGUEL SUAREZ HIGHWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

The Padre Burgos - Agdangan - Unisan - Pitogo - Macalelon - General Luna - Catanauan - Mulanay - San Franciso - San Andres - San Narciso - Buenavista National Road in Bondoc Peninsula in the Province of Quezon shall hereafter be known as the Miguel Suarez Highway.

## SEC. 2

The Act shall take effect upon its approval.
