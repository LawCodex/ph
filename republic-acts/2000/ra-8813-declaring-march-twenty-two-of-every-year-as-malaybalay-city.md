# Republic Act No. 8813

**An Act Declaring March Twenty-Two of Every Year as Malaybalay City Charter Day and a Special Working Holiday in the City of Malaybalay in Order to Commemorate the Anniversary of the Said City**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8813, August 21, 2000 ]

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH TWENTY-TWO OF EVERY YEAR AS MALAYBALAY CITY CHARTER DAY AND A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF MALAYBALAY IN ORDER TO COMMEMORATE THE ANNIVERSARY OF THE SAID CITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

In order to commemorate the charter anniversary of the City of Malaybalay, March twenty-two of every year is hereby declared as Malaybalay City Charter Day and a special working holiday in the said city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
