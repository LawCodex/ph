# Republic Act No. 5289

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Cawayanon in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bukidnon, to Vintar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5289**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Cawayanon in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bukidnon, to Vintar

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Cawayanon in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bukidnon, is hereby changed to Vintar.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
