# Act No. 2033

**An Act to Authorize the Municipal Council of VIgan, Ilocos Sur, to Give Up the Possession of Calle Corta, Situated West of the VIgan High School Building, Between Calles Burgos and Lincoln, and to Cede the Same to the Province of Ilocos Sur as a Lot for the Provincial High School.**

[ Act No. 2033, February 03, 1911 ]

AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE THE MUNICIPAL COUNCIL OF VIGAN, ILOCOS SUR, TO GIVE UP THE POSSESSION OF CALLE CORTA, SITUATED WEST OF THE VIGAN HIGH SCHOOL BUILDING, BETWEEN CALLES BURGOS AND LINCOLN, AND TO CEDE THE SAME TO THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR AS A LOT FOR THE PROVINCIAL HIGH SCHOOL.

By authority of the United Slates, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The municipal council of Vigan, Ilocos Sur, is hereby authorized to close Calle Corta, situate between Calles Burgos and Lincoln, west of the building known as Vigan High School, to public use and cede the same to the Province of Ilocos Sur to be used as a lot for the aforesaid provincial high school.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the same shall take effect on its passage, in accordance with section one of Act Numbered Nineteen, hundred and forty-five of the Philippine Legislature.

Enacted, February 3, 1911.
