# Republic Act No. 4332

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Walang Balahibo in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, to Barrio San Isidro**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4332**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Walang Balahibo in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, to Barrio San Isidro

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Walang Balahibo in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Barrio San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
