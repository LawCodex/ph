# Republic Act No. 6215

**An Act Changing the Name of Governor Forbes Street in the Third District of Manila to Mayor Arsenio H. Lacson.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6215, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF GOVERNOR FORBES STREET IN THE THIRD DISTRICT OF MANILA TO MAYOR ARSENIO H. LACSON.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Governor Forbes Street in the Third District of Manila is hereby changed to Mayor Arsenio H. Lacson.
