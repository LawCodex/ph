# Republic Act No. 3366

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Laparan in the Municipality of Kauswagan, Province of Lanao del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3366**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Laparan in the Municipality of Kauswagan, Province of Lanao del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Larapan in the Municipality of Kauswagan, Province of Lanao del Norte, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Larapan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
