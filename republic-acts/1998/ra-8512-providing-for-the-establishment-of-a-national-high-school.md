# Republic Act No. 8512

**An Act Providing for the Establishment of a National High School in Poblacion 1-A, Municipality of Motiong, Province of Samar, to Be Known as the Motiong National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8512, February 13, 1998 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN POBLACION 1-A, MUNICIPALITY OF MOTIONG, PROVINCE OF SAMAR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE MOTIONG NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby established a national high school in Poblacion 1-A, Municipality of Motiong, Province of Samar, under the supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on February 13, 1998, without the President's signature in accordance with Article VI, Sec. 27 (1), of the Constitution.
