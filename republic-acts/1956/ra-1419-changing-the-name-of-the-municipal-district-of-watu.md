# Republic Act No. 1419

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Watu, Province of Lanao, to Municipal District of Balindong**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1419**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Watu, Province of Lanao, to Municipal District of Balindong**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipal District of Watu, Province of Lanao, is changed to Municipal District of Balindong.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1956.
