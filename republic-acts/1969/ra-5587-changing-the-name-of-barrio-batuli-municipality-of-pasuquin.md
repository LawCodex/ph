# Republic Act No. 5587

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Batuli, Municipality of Pasuquin, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio San Isidro**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5587, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO BATULI, MUNICIPALITY OF PASUQUIN, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO BARRIO SAN ISIDRO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Batuli in the Municipality of Pasuquin, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
