# Republic Act No. 6838

**An Act Changing the Name of the Suop Elementary School in Barangay Igangon, Municipality of Asuncion, Province of Davao, to Igangon Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6838

An Act Changing the Name of the Suop Elementary School in Barangay Igangon, Municipality of Asuncion, Province of Davao, to Igangon Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Suop Elementary School in Barangay Igangon, Municipality of Asuncion, Province of Davao, is hereby changed to Igangon Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
