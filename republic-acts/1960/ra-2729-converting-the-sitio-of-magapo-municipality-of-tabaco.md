# Republic Act No. 2729

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Magapo, Municipality of Tabaco, Province of Albay, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2729**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Magapo, Municipality of Tabaco, Province of Albay, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Magapo, Municipality of Tabaco, Province of Albay, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Magapo.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
