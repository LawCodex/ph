# Republic Act No. 1647

**An Act Changing the Name of Putol Elementary School in the Barrio of Putol, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, to Putol-Sta. Isabel Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1647**

**An Act Changing the Name of Putol Elementary School in the Barrio of Putol, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, to Putol-Sta. Isabel Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Putol Elementary School located at the boundary of the barrios of Putol and Sta. Isabel, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, is changed to Putol-Sta. Isabel Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
