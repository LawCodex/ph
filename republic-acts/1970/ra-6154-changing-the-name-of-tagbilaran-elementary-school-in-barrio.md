# Republic Act No. 6154

**An Act Changing the Name of Tagbilaran Elementary School in Barrio Doña Regina, Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Davao del Sur, to Doña Regina Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6154**

**An Act Changing the Name of Tagbilaran Elementary School in Barrio Doña Regina, Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Davao del Sur, to Doña Regina Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tagbilaran Elementary School in Barrio Doña Regina, Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Davao del Sur, is hereby changed to Doña Regina Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 9, 1970.
