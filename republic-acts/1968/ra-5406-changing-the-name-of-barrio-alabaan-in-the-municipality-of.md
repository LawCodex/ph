# Republic Act No. 5406

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Alabaan in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Daquioag**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5406**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Alabaan in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Daquioag

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Alabaan in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Daquioag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
