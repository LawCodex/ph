# Executive Order No. 45

**Amending Executive No. 36, Series of 1963, Creating the Loans and Investment Council**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 45, August 19, 1963 ]

**AMENDING EXECUTIVE NO. 36, SERIES OF 1963, CREATING THE LOANS AND INVESTMENT COUNCIL**

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, **DIOSDADO MACAPAGAL,** President of the Philippines, do hereby amend Executive Order No. 36, series of 1963, creating the Loans and Investment Council as follows:
