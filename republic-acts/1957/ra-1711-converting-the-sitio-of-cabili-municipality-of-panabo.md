# Republic Act No. 1711

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Cabili, Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1711**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Cabili, Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cabili, Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Quezon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
