# Republic Act No. 6639

**An Act Renaming the Manila International Airport as the Ninoy Aquino International Airport**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6639 December 10, 1987**

AN ACT RENAMING THE MANILA INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT AS THE NINOY AQUINO INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Manila International Airport is hereby changed to Ninoy Aquino International Airport.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

***_Enacted without executive approval_ , November 27, 1987.**
