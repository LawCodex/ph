# Act No. 4259

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Fifty Thousand Pesos to Be Spent for Necessary Alterations, Repairs and Improvement to Public Buildings, Grounds and Equipment to Be Occupied And/Or Used By the Government of the Commonwealth of the Philippines.**

[ Acts No. 4259, November 06, 1935 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS TO BE SPENT FOR NECESSARY ALTERATIONS, REPAIRS AND IMPROVEMENT TO PUBLIC BUILDINGS, GROUNDS AND EQUIPMENT TO BE OCCUPIED AND/OR USED BY THE GOVERNMENT OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF THE PHILIPPINES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of fifty thousand pesos, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of the general funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to cover necessary expenses for alterations, repairs and improvement to public buildings, grounds and buildings, etc. equipment to be occupied and/or used by the Government of the Commonwealth of the Philippines.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 6, 1935.
