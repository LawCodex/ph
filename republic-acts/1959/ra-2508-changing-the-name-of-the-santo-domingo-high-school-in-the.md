# Republic Act No. 2508

**An Act Changing the Name of the Santo Domingo High School in the Municipality of Santo Domingo, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Julia Ortiz Luis High School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2508**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Santo Domingo High School in the Municipality of Santo Domingo, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Julia Ortiz Luis High School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Santo Domingo High School in the Municipality of Santo Domingo, Province of Nueva Ecija, is changed to Julia Ortiz Luis High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
