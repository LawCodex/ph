# Republic Act No. 3380

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Burnay in Mountain Province to Lagawe**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3380**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Burnay in Mountain Province to Lagawe**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipal District of Burnay in Mountain Province is changed to Lagawe.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
