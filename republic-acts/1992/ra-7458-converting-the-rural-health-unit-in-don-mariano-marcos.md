# Republic Act No. 7458

**An Act Converting the Rural Health Unit in Don Mariano Marcos Avenue, Congressional Hills, Quezon City, Into A Five-bed Capacity Health Infirmary, To Be Known As the Congressional Hills Health Infirmary, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7458, April 27, 1992 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE RURAL HEALTH UNIT IN DON MARIANO MARCOS AVENUE, CONGRESSIONAL HILLS, QUEZON CITY, INTO A FIVE-BED CAPACITY HEALTH INFIRMARY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE CONGRESSIONAL HILLS HEALTH INFIRMARY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled;

## Section 1

The rural health unit in Don Mariano Marcos Avenue, Congressional Hills, Quezon City, is hereby converted into a five-bed capacity health infirmary, to be known as the Congressional Hills Health Infirmary.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.
