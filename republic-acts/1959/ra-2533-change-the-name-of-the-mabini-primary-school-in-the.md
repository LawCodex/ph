# Republic Act No. 2533

**An Act to Change the Name of the Mabini Primary School in the Municipality of Valladolid, Province of Negros Occidental, to Emilio Infante Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2533**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Mabini Primary School in the Municipality of Valladolid, Province of Negros Occidental, to Emilio Infante Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mabini Primary School in the Municipality of Valladoid, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Emilio Infante Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
