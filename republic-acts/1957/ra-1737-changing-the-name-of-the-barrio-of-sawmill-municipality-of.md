# Republic Act No. 1737

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Sawmill, Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, to Concepcion**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1737**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Sawmill, Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, to Concepcion**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Sawmill, Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, is changed to Concepcion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
