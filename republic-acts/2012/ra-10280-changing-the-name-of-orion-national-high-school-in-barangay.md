# Republic Act No. 10280

**An Act Changing the Name of Orion National High School in Barangay Balagtas, Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan to Justice Emilio Angeles Gancayco Memorial High School**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-three day of July, two thousand twelve.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10280**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ORION NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BALAGTAS, MUNICIPALITY OF ORION, PROVINCE OF BATAAN TO JUSTICE EMILIO ANGELES GANCAYCO MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of a National High School._ – The name of Orion National High School in Barangay Balagtas, Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan is hereby changed to Justice Emilio Angeles Gancayco Memorial High School.

## Section 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ – The main campus of the Justice Emilio Angeles Gancayco Memorial High School shall remain in its present location in Barangay Balagtas, Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan.

## Section 3

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on October 4, 2010 and July 24, 2012, respectively.
