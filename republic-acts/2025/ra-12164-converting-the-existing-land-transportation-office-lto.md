# Republic Act No. 12164

**An Act Converting the Existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur Into a Regular District Office and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12164, April 11, 2025 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE EXISTING LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) EXTENSION OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PILI, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR INTO A REGULAR DISTRICT OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby converted into a regular district office.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the regular LTO Extension Office in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
