# Republic Act No. 7533

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Don Amadeo J. Perez, Sr., Memorial General Hospital in the Municipality of Urdaneta, Province of Pangasinan, From Fifty (50) To Seventy-five (75) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7533, May 22, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE DON AMADEO J. PEREZ, SR., MEMORIAL GENERAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF URDANETA, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Don Amadeo J. Perez, Sr., Memorial General Hospital in the Municipality of Urdaneta, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby increased from fifty (50) to seventy-five (75) beds.1âшphi1

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
