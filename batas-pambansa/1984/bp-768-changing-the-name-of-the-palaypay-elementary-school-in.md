# Batas Pambansa Blg. 768

**An Act Changing the Name of the Palaypay Elementary School in Barangay Palaypay, in the Municipality of Anilao, Province of Iloilo, to Pascual Buyco Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 768**

An Act Changing the Name of the Palaypay Elementary School in Barangay Palaypay, in the Municipality of Anilao, Province of Iloilo, to Pascual Buyco Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Palaypay Elementary School in Barangay Palaypay, in the Municipality of Anilao, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Pascual Buyco Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

The name of the Palaypay Elementary School.

Approved: April 13, 1984
