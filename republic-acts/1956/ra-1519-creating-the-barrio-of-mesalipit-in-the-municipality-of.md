# Republic Act No. 1519

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Mesalipit in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1519**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Mesalipit in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Mesalipit in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga, is hereby constituted into a barrio in the said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Mesalipit.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
