# Republic Act No. 7464

**An Act Changing the Name of the Alugan School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries in Barangay Alugan, Municipality of San Policarpo, Province of Eastern Samar, To Alugan National School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7464, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ALUGAN SCHOOL OF CRAFTSMANSHIP AND HOME INDUSTRIES IN BARANGAY ALUGAN, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN POLICARPO, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO ALUGAN NATIONAL SCHOOL OF CRAFTSMANSHIP AND HOME INDUSTRIES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:1âшphi1

## Section 1

The name of the Alugan School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries in Barangay Alugan, Municipality of San Policarpo, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Alugan National School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
