# Republic Act No. 7313

**An Act Converting the Medicare Community Hospital in the Municipality of Manukan, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, Into a Twenty-five-bed Capacity Hospital to Be Known as the Manukan District Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7313, March 30, 1992 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE MEDICARE COMMUNITY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MANUKAN, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, INTO A TWENTY-FIVE-BED CAPACITY HOSPITAL TO BE KNOWN AS THE MANUKAN DISTRICT HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Medicare Community Hospital in the Municipality of Manukan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby converted into a twenty-five-bed capacity hospital to be known as the Manukan District Hospital which shall be under the supervision of the Department of Health.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be charged against the lump-sum appropriations authorized for the purpose under the current General Appropriations Act. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of the hospital shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
