# Batas Pambansa Blg. 680

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Rio Hondo in Zamboanga City to Kauman Muslimin**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 680**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Rio Hondo in Zamboanga City to Kauman Muslimin

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Rio Hondo in Zamboanga City is hereby changed to Kauman Muslimin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
