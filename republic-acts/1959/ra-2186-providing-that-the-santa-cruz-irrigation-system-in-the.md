# Republic Act No. 2186

**An Act Providing that the Santa Cruz Irrigation System in the Province of Laguna be Hereafter Known as the Magsaysay Irrigation System**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2186**

**An Act Providing that the Santa Cruz Irrigation System in the Province of Laguna be Hereafter Known as the Magsaysay Irrigation System**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Santa Cruz Irrigation System in the Province of Laguna shall hereafter be known as the Magsaysay Irrigation System.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
