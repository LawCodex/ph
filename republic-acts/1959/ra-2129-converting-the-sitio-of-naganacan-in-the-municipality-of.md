# Republic Act No. 2129

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Naganacan in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Isabela, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Naganacan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2129**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Naganacan in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Isabela, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Naganacan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Naganacan in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Isabela, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as barrio Naganacan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
