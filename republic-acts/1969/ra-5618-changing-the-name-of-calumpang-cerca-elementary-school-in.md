# Republic Act No. 5618

**An Act Changing the Name of Calumpang Cerca Elementary School in Barrio Calumpang Cerca, Municipality of Indang, Province of Cavite, to Dr. Alfredo Pio De Roda Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5618, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF CALUMPANG CERCA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARRIO CALUMPANG CERCA, MUNICIPALITY OF INDANG, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO DR. ALFREDO PIO DE RODA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Calumpang Cerca Elementary School in Barrio Calumpang Cerca, Municipality of Indang, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to Dr. Alfredo Pio de Roda Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
