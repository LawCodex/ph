# Republic Act No. 1248

**An Act to Create the Municipality of Upi in the Province of Cotabato**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1248**

**An Act to Create the Municipality of Upi in the Province of Cotabato**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Upi in the Municipality of Dinaig, Province of Cotabato, is separated from the said municipality and constituted into a municipality in the said province, to be known as the Municipality of Upi.

## Section 2

The first municipal mayor, vice-mayor and councilors of the newly created municipality shall be appointed by the President of the Philippines to hold office until their successors are duly elected and have qualified.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
