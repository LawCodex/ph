# Act No. 3720

**An Act Making It the Duty of the National Museum to Cooperate in the Preservation of Permanent Public Works and Monuments of Value to Philippine History and Culture, and Authorizing the Director of the National Museum to Make Rules and Regulations for Carrying Out the Purposes of this Act.**

[ Act No. 3720, November 21, 1930 ]

AN ACT MAKING IT THE DUTY OF THE NATIONAL MUSEUM TO COOPERATE IN THE PRESERVATION OF PERMANENT PUBLIC WORKS AND MONUMENTS OF VALUE TO PHILIPPINE HISTORY AND CULTURE, AND AUTHORIZING THE DIRECTOR OF THE NATIONAL MUSEUM TO MAKE RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR CARRYING OUT THE PURPOSES OF THIS ACT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The Director of the National Museum shall cooperate in the work of preserving all permanent public works and monuments of artistic or historical value, which are owned by the Philippine Government, or any of its political subdivisions, and make a fair and accurate record thereof.

## Section 2

The Director of the National Museum, subject to the approval of the Secretary of Agriculture and Natural Resources, may make rules and regulations for carrying out the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

All acts and parts of acts inconsistent with the Repealing clause, provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 21, 1930.
