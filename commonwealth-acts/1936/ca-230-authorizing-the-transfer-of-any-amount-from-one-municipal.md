# Commonwealth Act No. 230

**An Act Authorizing the Transfer of Any Amount From One Municipal Fund to Another, Whenever the Public Interests So Require.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 230, November 29, 1936 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF ANY AMOUNT FROM ONE MUNICIPAL FUND TO ANOTHER, WHENEVER THE PUBLIC INTERESTS SO REQUIRE.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

Whenever the public interests so require, the municipal council may, with the approval of the Department Head, transfer any amount from the existing balances of the different municipal funds to another fund in order to cover deficiencies in the matter.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted, without Executive approval, November 30, 1936.
