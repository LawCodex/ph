# Republic Act No. 6806

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gatbuca Elementary School in the Municipality of Calumpit, Province of Bulacan, to Arsenio Santos Memorial Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6806 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE GATBUCA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CALUMPIT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO ARSENIO SANTOS MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Gatbuca Elementary School in the Municipality of Calumpit, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Arsenio Santos Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
