# Commonwealth Act No. 532

**An Act to Fix the Salary of the Auditor General At Fifteen thousand Pesos Per Annum.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 532, May 26, 1940 ]

AN ACT TO FIX THE SALARY OF THE AUDITOR GENERAL AT FIFTEEN THOUSAND PESOS PER ANNUM.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The Auditor General shall receive an annual salary of fifteen thousand pesos.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out-the provision of this Act-for the rest of the fiscal year ending June thirty, nineteen hundred forty, and for the fiscal year nineteen hundred forty-one shall be taken from the savings of the appropriation for the General Auditing Office during the said fiscal years.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, May 26, 1940.
