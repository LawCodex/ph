# Batas Pambansa Blg. 692

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Talipao in the Province of Sulu to Municipality of Arolas Tulawie**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 692**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Talipao in the Province of Sulu to Municipality of Arolas Tulawie

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Talipao in the Province of Sulu is changed to Municipality of Arolas Tulawie.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
