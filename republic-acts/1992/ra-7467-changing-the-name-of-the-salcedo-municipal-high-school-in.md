# Republic Act No. 7467

**An Act Changing the Name of the Salcedo Municipal High School in the Municipality of Salcedo, Province of Eastern Samar, to Salcedo High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7467, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SALCEDO MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SALCEDO, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO SALCEDO HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Salcedo Municipal High School in the Municipality of Salcedo, Province of Samar, is hereby changed to Salcedo High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, April 28, 1992.
