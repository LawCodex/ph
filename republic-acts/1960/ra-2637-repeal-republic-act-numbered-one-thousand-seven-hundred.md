# Republic Act No. 2637

**An Act to Repeal Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Seven Hundred Ninety-Eight**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2637**

**An Act to Repeal Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Seven Hundred Ninety-Eight**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Act Numbered One thousand seven hundred ninety-eight is repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1960.
