# Republic Act No. 334

**An Act Granting Monetary Reward to Any Person Who Shall Locate or Find the Remains of the Late Governor Hilario Abellana of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 334**

An Act Granting Monetary Reward to Any Person Who Shall Locate or Find the Remains of the Late Governor Hilario Abellana of Cebu

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Any person who shall locate or find the remains of the late Governor Hilario Abellana of Cebu shall be granted a reward of five thousand pesos.1aшphi1

## Section 2

The sum of five thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to carry out the purposes of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: July 20, 1948.
