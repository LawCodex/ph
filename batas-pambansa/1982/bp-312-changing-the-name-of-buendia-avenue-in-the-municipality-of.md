# Batas Pambansa Blg. 312

**An Act Changing the Name of Buendia Avenue in the Municipality of Makati and Pasay City, Both in Metro Manila, to Senator Gil J. Puyat Avenue.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 312**

An Act Changing the Name of Buendia Avenue in the Municipality of Makati and Pasay City, Both in Metro Manila, to Senator Gil J. Puyat Avenue

## Section 1

The name of Buendia Avenue in the Municipality of Makati and Pasay City, both in Metro Manila, is changed to Senator Gil J. Puyat Avenue.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
