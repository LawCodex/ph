# Republic Act No. 1817

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of One Hundred Thousand Pesos as a Grant to Mrs. Luz B. Magsaysay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1817**

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of One Hundred Thousand Pesos as a Grant to Mrs. Luz B. Magsaysay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sum of one hundred thousand pesos is appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, as a grant to Mrs. Luz B. Magsaysay, widow of the late President Ramon Magsaysay. This amount shall be tax exempt.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
