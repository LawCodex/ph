# Batas Pambansa Blg. 318

**An Act Changing the Name of the Celestino Gallares Hospital in the Municipality of Carmen, Province of Bohol, to Congressman Simeon G. Toribio Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 318**

An Act Changing the Name of the Celestino Gallares Hospital in the Municipality of Carmen, Province of Bohol, to Congressman Simeon G. Toribio Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Celestino Gallares Hospital in the Municipality of Carmen, Province of Bohol, is changed to Congressman Simeon G. Toribio Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
