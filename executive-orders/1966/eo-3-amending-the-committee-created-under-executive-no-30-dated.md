# Executive Order No. 3

**Amending the Committee Created Under Executive No. 30 Dated January 14, 1963, as Amended By Executive Order No. 69 Dated February 7, 1964**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 3, January 05, 1966 ]

AMENDING THE COMMITTEE CREATED UNDER EXECUTIVE NO. 30 DATED JANUARY 14, 1963, AS AMENDED BY EXECITUVE ORDER NO. 69 DATED FEBRUARY 7, 1964

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, FERDINAND E. MRCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby amend Executive Order No. 30 dated January 14, 1963, as amended, by designating the following to compose the National Parks Development Committee created thereunder:

| Mrs. Imelda R. Marcos | Chairman |
|---|---|
| Atty. Teodoro F. Valencia | Vice-Chairman |
| The President of the Lions Club | Member |
| The President of the Junior Chamber of Commerce | Member |
| The President of the Rotary Club | Member |
| Hon. Rafael M. Salas Executive Secretary | Treasurer |

Done in the City of Manila, this 5th day of January, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen and sixty-six.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **RAFAEL M. SALAS**
Executive Secretary
