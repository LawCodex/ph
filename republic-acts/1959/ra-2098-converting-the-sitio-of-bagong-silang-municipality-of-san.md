# Republic Act No. 2098

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagong Silang, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Bagong Silang**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2098**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagong Silang, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Bagong Silang**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bagong Silang, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Bagong Silang.1aшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 28, 1959.
