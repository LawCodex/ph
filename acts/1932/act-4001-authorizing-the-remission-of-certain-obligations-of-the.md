# Act No. 4001

**An Act Authorizing the Remission of Certain Obligations of the Mountain Province With the Philippine Health Service.**

[ Acts No. 4001, December 05, 1932 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE REMISSION OF CERTAIN OBLIGATIONS OF THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE WITH THE PHILIPPINE HEALTH SERVICE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The remission of the indebtedness in the amount of twenty thousand and three hundred eighty-one pesos and seventy-two centavos of the Mountain Province with the Philippine Health Service on account of salaries paid up to December thirty-first, nineteen hundred and thirty-one, by that Service to its officers and employees assigned to that province is hereby authorized.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 5, 1932.
