# Republic Act No. 8252

**An Act Declaring July Six of Every Year A Special Nonworking Public Holiday in the Municipality of Mambajao, Province of Camiguin, To Be Known As "Mambajao Charter Day"**

Tenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8252, February 11, 1997 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JULY SIX OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAMBAJAO, PROVINCE OF CAMIGUIN, TO BE KNOWN AS "MAMBAJAO CHARTER DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

July six is the charter day anniversary of the Municipality of Mambajao, Province of Camiguin, and its celebration is highlighted with colorful parade, stage drama, and beauty pageant.

## Section 2

July six of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking public holiday in the Municipality of Mambajao.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, February 11, 1997.
