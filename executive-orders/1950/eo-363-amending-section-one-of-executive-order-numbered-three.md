# Executive Order No. 363

**Amending Section One of Executive Order Numbered Three Hundred Thirty-One, as Amended By Executive Order Numbered Three Hundred and Fifty-Nine**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 363 ]

AMENDING SECTION ONE OF EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED THIRTY-ONE, AS AMENDED BY EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-NINE
