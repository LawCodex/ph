# Republic Act No. 3716

**An Act Changing the Name of the Biid Elementary School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Dammang Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3716**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Biid Elementary School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Dammang Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Biid Elementary School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Panglima Dammang Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
