# Republic Act No. 2217

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Isidro Community School in the Municipality of Malitbog, Province of Leyte, to Oppus Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2217**

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Isidro Community School in the Municipality of Malitbog, Province of Leyte, to Oppus Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Isidro Community School in the Municipality of Malitbog, Province of Leyte, is changed to Oppus Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
