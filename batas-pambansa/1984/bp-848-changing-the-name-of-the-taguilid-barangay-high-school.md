# Batas Pambansa Blg. 848

**An Act Changing the Name of the Taguilid Barangay High School Located in the Municipality of Pamplona, Province of Camarines Sur, Into Don Mariano Veneracion Community High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 848**

An Act Changing the Name of the Taguilid Barangay High School Located in the Municipality of Pamplona, Province of Camarines Sur, into Don Mariano Veneracion Community High School

## Section 1

The barangay high school located at Barangay Veneracion in the Municipality of Pamplona Province of Camarines Sur, now known as the Taguilid Barangay High School hereafter be known as Don Mariano Veneracion Community High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
