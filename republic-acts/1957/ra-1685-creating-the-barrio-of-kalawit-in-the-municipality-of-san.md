# Republic Act No. 1685

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Kalawit in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1685**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Kalawit in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Kalawit is separated from the barrio of Alas-as, Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas, and constituted into a barrio of the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Kalawit.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
