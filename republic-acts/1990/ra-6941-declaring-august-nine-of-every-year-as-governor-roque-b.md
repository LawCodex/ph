# Republic Act No. 6941

**An Act Declaring August Nine of Every Year as Governor Roque B. Ablan, Sr. Day, a Special Holiday in the Province of Ilocos Norte**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6941 April 4, 1990**

AN ACT DECLARING AUGUST NINE OF EVERY YEAR AS GOVERNOR ROQUE B. ABLAN, SR. DAY, A SPECIAL HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

August Nine of every year is hereby declared as Governor Roque B. Ablan, Sr. Day, a special holiday in the Province of Ilocos Norte to celebrate the birthday and commemorate the memory of the late governor and labor leader.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **April 4, 1990**
