# Republic Act No. 4977

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Aurora, Province of Quezon, to San Francisco**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4977**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Aurora, Province of Quezon, to San Francisco

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Aurora, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to San Francisco.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
