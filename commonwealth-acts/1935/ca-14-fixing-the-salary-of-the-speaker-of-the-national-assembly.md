# Commonwealth Act No. 14

**An Act Fixing the Salary of the Speaker of the National Assembly At Twelve thousand Pesos Per Annum.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 14, December 31, 1935 ]

AN ACT FIXING THE SALARY OF THE SPEAKER OF THE NATIONAL ASSEMBLY AT TWELVE THOUSAND PESOS PER ANNUM.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The Speaker of the National Assembly shall receive an annual salary of twelve thousand pesos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 31, 1935.
