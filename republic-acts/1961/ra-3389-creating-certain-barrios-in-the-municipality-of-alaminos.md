# Republic Act No. 3389

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Alaminos, Province of Pangasinan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3389**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Alaminos, Province of Pangasinan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

(a) The sitio of Quibuar East and West in the barrio of Alos, Municipality of Alaminos, Province of Pangasinan, is separated from said barrio and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Quibuar; and

(b) The sitio of Dimayat in the barrio of Alos, Municipality of Alaminos, Province of Pangasinan, is separated from said barrio and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Dimayat.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
