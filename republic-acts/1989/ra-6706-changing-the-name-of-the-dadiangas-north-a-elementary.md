# Republic Act No. 6706

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dadiangas North "A" Elementary School to Jose P. Laurel Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6706, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DADIANGAS NORTH "A" ELEMENTARY SCHOOL TO JOSE P. LAUREL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Dadiangas North "A" Elementary School in Cahilsot District, General Santos City, South Cotabato, shall hereafter be known and referred to as the Jose P. Laurel Elementary School.

## Section 2
