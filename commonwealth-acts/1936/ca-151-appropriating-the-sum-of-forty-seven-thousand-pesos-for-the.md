# Commonwealth Act No. 151

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Forty Seven thousand Pesos for the Construction and Repair of Certain Dams Inside the Friar Land Estates.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 151, November 08, 1936 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FORTY SEVEN THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION AND REPAIR OF CERTAIN DAMS INSIDE THE FRIAR LAND ESTATES.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The sum of forty seven thousand pesos or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Philippine Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be spent by the Director of Lands, with the approval of the Secretary of Agriculture and Commerce, as follows:

| For the repair of the Diezmo Dam, in Santa Rosa, Laguna | P7,000.00 |
|---|---|
| For the repair of the Anabu Dam in Imus, Cavite | 10,000.00 |
| For the construction of a dam, to be known as the Katipunan Dam in the barrio of Palangue, Naic, Cavite | 10,000.00 |
| For the repair of the Tubigan Dam in Binan, Laguna | 20,000.00 |
| | ------------- |
| Total. | P47,000.00 |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 8, 1936, except the last two items.
