# Batas Pambansa Blg. 259

**An Act Establishing a School of Fisheries At the Municipality of Cajidiocan, Sibuyan Island, Province of Romblon, to Be Known as the Sibuyan School of Fisheries.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 259**

An Act Establishing a School of Fisheries at the Municipality of Cajidiocan, Sibuyan Island, Province of Romblon, to be Known as the Sibuyan School of Fisheries

## Section 1

There shall be established a school of fisheries at the Municipality of Cajidiocan, Sibuyan Island, Province of Romblon, to be known as the Sibuyan School of Fisheries.

The Director of Secondary Education shall promulgate rules and regulations to implement this Act.

## Section 2

The amount of one hundred fifty thousand pesos for the operation of the school shall be funded out of the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 131 for the Ministry of Education and Culture intended for new national schools. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be provided for in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 13, 1982
