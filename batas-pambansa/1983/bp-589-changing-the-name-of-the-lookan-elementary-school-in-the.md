# Batas Pambansa Blg. 589

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lookan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Ladja E. Idanan Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 589**

An Act Changing the Name of the Lookan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Ladja E. Idanan Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Lookan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Ladja E. Idanan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
