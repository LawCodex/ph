# Republic Act No. 1746

**An Act Amending Item Forty-Three, Page One Hundred Eighteen of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1746**

**An Act Amending Item Forty-Three, Page One Hundred Eighteen of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item forty-three, page one hundred eighteen of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred, is amended to read as follows:

| "(43) Construction of Central School, Mauban | ₱5,000.00." |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
