# Republic Act No. 6787

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cuambogan Elementary School in Barangay Cuambogan, Municipality of Tagum, Province of Davao, to Catalan Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6787, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE CUAMBOGAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY CUAMBOGAN, MUNICIPALITY OF TAGUM, PROVINCE OF DAVAO, TO CATALAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cuambogan Elementary School in Barangay Cuambogan, Municipality of Tagum, Province of Davao, is hereby changed to Catalan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
