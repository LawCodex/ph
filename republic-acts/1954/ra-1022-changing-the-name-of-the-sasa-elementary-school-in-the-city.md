# Republic Act No. 1022

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sasa Elementary School in the City of Davao to Francisco Bangoy Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1022**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sasa Elementary School in the City of Davao to Francisco Bangoy Elementary School**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The Sasa Elementary School in the City of Davao shall hereafter be known as the Francisco Bangoy Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
