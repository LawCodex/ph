# Republic Act No. 9228

**An act establishing the Second District Engineering Office in the Province of Capiz to be located in the Municipality of Dumalag and appropriating funds therefor**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Metro Manila

Twelfth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eigth day of July, two thousand three.

**Republic Act No. 9228 November 28, 2003**

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE SECOND DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE IN THE PROVINCE OF CAPIZ TO BE LOCATED IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DUMALAG AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Public Works and Highways, a second engineering district in the Province of Capiz, covering the municipalities of Dumalag, Sigma, Jamindan, Mambusao, Sapian, Tapaz, Cuartero, Dao, Dumarao and Ivisan, to be located in the Municipality of Dumalag and to be known as the Capiz Second Engineering District.

## SECTION 2

The Secertary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall include in its program the funding requirements for the operationalization of the Capiz Second Engineering District in the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## SECTION 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
