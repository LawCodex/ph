# Batas Pambansa Blg. 757

**An Act Changing the Name of Southern District Hospital in the Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Gregorio Matas District Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 757**

An Act Changing the Name of Southern District Hospital in the Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Gregorio Matas District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Southern District Hospital in the Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao del Sur, is hereby changed to Gregorio Matas District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
