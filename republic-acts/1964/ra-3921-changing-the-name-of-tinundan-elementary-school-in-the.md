# Republic Act No. 3921

**An Act Changing the Name of Tinundan Elementary School in the Municipality of President Roxas, Province of Capiz, to Elizalde Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3921**

**An Act Changing the Name of Tinundan Elementary School in the Municipality of President Roxas, Province of Capiz, to Elizalde Memorial Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tinundan Elementary School in the Municipality of President Roxas, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to Elizalde Memorial Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
