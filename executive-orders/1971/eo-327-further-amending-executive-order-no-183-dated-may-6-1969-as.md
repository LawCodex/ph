# Executive Order No. 327

**Further Amending Executive Order No. 183 Dated May 6, 1969, as Amended By Executive Order No. 285 Dated January 21, 1971, Creating the National Food and Agriculture Council**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 327, July 13, 1971 ]

FURTHER AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 183 DATED MAY 6, 1969, AS AMENDED BY EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 285 DATED JANUARY 21, 1971, CREATING THE NATIONAL FOOD AND AGRICULTURE COUNCIL

The composition of the National Food and Agriculture Council is hereby amended to read as follows:

| 1 | The Secretary, Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources | Chairman |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | The Undersecretary for Agriculture | Vice-Chairman & Action Officer |
| 3 | The Chairman-General Manager, Rice and Corn Administration | Member |
| 4 | The Director, Bureau of Plant Industry | Member |
| 5 | The Commissioner, Agricultural Productivity Commission | Member |
| 6 | The Director, Bureau of Soils | Member |
| 7 | The Dean, College of Agriculture , University of the Philippines | Member |
| 8 | The Director, Bureau of Animal Industry | Member |
| 9 | The Commissioner, Budget Commission | Member |
| 10. | The Commissioner, Philippine Fisheries Commission | Member |
| 11. | The Chairman, National Land Reform Council | Member |
| 12. | The Administrator, Agricultural Credit Administration | Member |
| 13. | The Secretary, Presidential Arm on Community Development | Member |
| 14. | The Director, Department of Rural Banks, Central Bank of the Philippines | Member |
| 15. | The Vice-President (Loan Operation), Philippine National Bank | Member |
| 16. | The Manager, Agricultural Department, Development Bank of the Philippines | Member |
| 17. | The Administrator, National Irrigation Administration | Member |
| 18. | The Administrator, Irrigation Service Unit, DPWC | Member |
| 19. | The Director, Bureau of Agricultural Economics | Member |
| 20. | The Director, Office of Statistical Coordination & Standards, National Economic Council | Member |
| 21. | The Executive Director, Rice and Corn Board | Member |
| 22. | The Research Director, Food and Nutrition Research Center , National Coordinating Council on Food and Nutrition | Member |

The Chairman or, in his absence, the Action Officer is hereby authorized to organize the staff necessary to enable the council to perform its functions effectively and efficiently.

The National Food and Agriculture Council shall submit to the President periodic progress reports of its activities.

Done in the City of Manila, this 13th day of July, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-one.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **ALEJANDRO MELCHOR**
Executive Secretary
