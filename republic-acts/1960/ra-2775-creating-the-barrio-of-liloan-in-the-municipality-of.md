# Republic Act No. 2775

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Liloan in the Municipality of Catarman, Subprovince of Camiguin, Province of Misamis Oriental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2775**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Liloan in the Municipality of Catarman, Subprovince of Camiguin, Province of Misamis Oriental**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Manduaw, Pasanan, Bae, Kiab, Banao, Dumilag, Napo, Cogon, Tubos and Tag-ibo in the Municipality of Catarman, Sub-Province of Camiguin, Province of Misamis Oriental, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Liloan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
