# Republic Act No. 4113

**An Act Repealing Proclamation Numbered Four Hundred Eleven, Dated September Eighteen, Nineteen Hundred and Fifty-Three, of the President of the Philippines**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4113**

**An Act Repealing Proclamation Numbered Four Hundred Eleven, Dated September Eighteen, Nineteen Hundred and Fifty-Three, of the President of the Philippines**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Proclamation Numbered Four hundred eleven, dated September eighteen, nineteen hundred and fifty-three, of the President of the Philippines is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1964.
