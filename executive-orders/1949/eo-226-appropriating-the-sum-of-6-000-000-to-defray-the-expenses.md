# Executive Order No. 226

**Appropriating the Sum of ?6,000,000 to Defray the Expenses, in Connection With, and Incidental To, the Holding of the National Elections to Be Held on the Second Tuesday of November, 1949**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 226, June 15, 1949 ]

**APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF P6,000,000 TO DEFRAY THE EXPENSES, IN CONNECTION WITH, AND INCIDENTAL TO, THE HOLDING OF THE NATIONAL ELECTIONS TO BE HELD ON THE SECOND TUESDAY OF NOVEMBER, 1949**

**WHEREAS,** the First Congress of the Philippines in its fourth session failed to pass an Act appropriating funds for the national elections to be held on the second Tuesday of November, 1949; and

**WHEREAS,** it is imperative that funds be provided for the holding of said elections;

**NOW, THEREFORE, I, ELPIDIO QUIRINO** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution and existing laws, particularly Commonwealth Act No. 671, do hereby order:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Philippine Treasury not otherwise appropriated the sum of P6,000,000 or so much thereof as may be necessary for the payment, subject to the approval of the Commission on Elections, of the expenses of the national elections to be held on the second Tuesday of November, 1949.

## Section 2

This Order shall take effect immediately.

Done in the City of Manila, this 15th day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and forty-nine, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the third.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
