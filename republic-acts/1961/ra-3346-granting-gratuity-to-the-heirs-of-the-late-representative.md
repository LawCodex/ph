# Republic Act No. 3346

**An Act Granting Gratuity to the Heirs of the Late Representative Atanacio Mapping and Appropriating Funds for the Purpose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3346**

**An Act Granting Gratuity to the Heirs of the Late Representative Atanacio Mapping and Appropriating Funds for the Purpose**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The legal heirs of the late Representative Atanacio MAPPING, who was among those killed in the sinking of S.S. Corregidor, are hereby given a gratuity of fifteen thousand pesos.

## Section 2

There is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifteen thousand pesos for the purpose indicated in the preceding section.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
