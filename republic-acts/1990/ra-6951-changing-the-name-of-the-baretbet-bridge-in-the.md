# Republic Act No. 6951

**An Act Changing the Name of the Baretbet Bridge in the Municipality of Bagabag, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, to San Lorenzo Ruiz Memorial Bridge**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6951 May 8, 1990**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BARETBET BRIDGE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BAGABAG, PROVINCE OF NUEVA VIZCAYA, TO SAN LORENZO RUIZ MEMORIAL BRIDGE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Baretbet Bridge in the Municipality of Bagabag, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, is hereby changed to San Lorenzo Ruiz Memorial Bridge.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **May 8, 1990**
