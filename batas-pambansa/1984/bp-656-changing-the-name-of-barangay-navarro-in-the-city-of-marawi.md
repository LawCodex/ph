# Batas Pambansa Blg. 656

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Navarro in the City of Marawi to Barangay Datu Saber**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 656**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Navarro in the City of Marawi to Barangay Datu Saber**

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Navarro in the City of Marawi is changed to Barangay Datu Saber.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
