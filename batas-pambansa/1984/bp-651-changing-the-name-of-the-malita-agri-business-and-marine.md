# Batas Pambansa Blg. 651

**An Act Changing the Name of the Malita Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology to Southern Philippines Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 651**

An Act Changing the Name of the Malita Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology to Southern Philippines Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology

## Section 1

The name of the Malita Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology in the Municipality of Manila, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Southern Philippines Agri-Business and Marine and Aquatic School of Technology.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
