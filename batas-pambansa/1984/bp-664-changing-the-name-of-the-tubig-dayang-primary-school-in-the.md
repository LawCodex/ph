# Batas Pambansa Blg. 664

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tubig Dayang Primary School in the Municipality of South Ubian, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Hadji Abdulcadil Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 664**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tubig Dayang Primary School in the Municipality of South Ubian, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Hadji Abdulcadil Primary School**

## Section 1

The name of the Tubig Dayang Primary School in the Municipality of South Ubian, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Hadji Abdulcadil Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
