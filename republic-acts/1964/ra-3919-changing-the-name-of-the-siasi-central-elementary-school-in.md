# Republic Act No. 3919

**An Act Changing the Name of the Siasi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Siasi, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Julkanain Taup Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3919**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Siasi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Siasi, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Julkanain Taup Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Siasi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Siasi, Province of Sulu, is hereby to Panglima Julkanain Taup Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
