# Republic Act No. 9332

**An Act Naming the Philippine National Police (PNP) Provincial Office in the City of Maasin, Province of Southern Leyte as Camp Governor Alfredo Kangleon Bantug**

Twelfth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eight day of July, two thousand and three.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9332**

AN ACT NAMING THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE (PNP) PROVINCIAL OFFICE IN THE CITY OF MAASIN, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE AS CAMP GOVERNOR ALFREDO KANGLEON BANTUG

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Philippine National Police (PNP) Provincial Office in the City of Maasin, Province of Southern Leyte is hereby named Camp Governor Alfredo Kangleon Bantug.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
