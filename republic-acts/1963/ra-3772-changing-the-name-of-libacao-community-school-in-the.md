# Republic Act No. 3772

**An Act Changing the Name of Libacao Community School in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Remigio Limsiaco Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3772**

**An Act Changing the Name of Libacao Community School in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Remigio Limsiaco Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the invaluable services rendered to the people of Barrio Libacao, Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, by the late Don Remigio Limsiaco, a philanthropist and civic leader of the said barrio, the name of Libacao Community School is changed to Don Remigio Limsiaco Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
