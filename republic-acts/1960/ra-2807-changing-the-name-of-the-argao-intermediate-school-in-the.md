# Republic Act No. 2807

**An Act Changing the Name of the Argao Intermediate School in the Municipality of Argao, Province of Cebu, to Antonio Miñoza Intermediate School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2807**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Argao Intermediate School in the Municipality of Argao, Province of Cebu, to Antonio Miñoza Intermediate School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Argao Intermediate School in the Municipality of Argao, Province of Cebu, is hereby changed to Antonio Miñoza Intermediate School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
