# Republic Act No. 3736

**An Act Changing the Name of Lacaron Elementary School in Barrio Lacaron, Municipality of Valladolid, Negros Occidental, to Maria Palacios Presbitero Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3736**

**An Act Changing the Name of Lacaron Elementary School in Barrio Lacaron, Municipality of Valladolid, Negros Occidental, to Maria Palacios Presbitero Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lacaron Elementary School in barrio Lacaron, Municipality of Valladolid, Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Maria Palacios Presbitero Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
