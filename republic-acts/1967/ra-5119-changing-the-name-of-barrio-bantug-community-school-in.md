# Republic Act No. 5119

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Bantug Community School in Barrio Bantug in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Doña S. Belisario-Adamos Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5119**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Bantug Community School in Barrio Bantug in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Doña S. Belisario-Adamos Community School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Bantug Community School in Barrio Bantug in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Doña S. Belisario-Adamos Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon it approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
