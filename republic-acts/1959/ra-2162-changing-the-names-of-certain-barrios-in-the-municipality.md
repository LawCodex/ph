# Republic Act No. 2162

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2162**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the following barrios in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte, are changed as follows:

1\. Barrio of Santo Rosario to Barrio Catmon;

2\. Barrio La Purisima Concepcion to Barrio Agpangi;

3\. Barrio San Vicente to Barrio Lico;

4\. Barrio Santa Fe to Barrio Talostusan;

5\. Barrio San Isidro to Barrio Locso-on; and

6\. Barrio Sulphur Valley to Barrio Villa Caneja.1aшphi1

## Section 2

Republic Act Numbered One thousand three hundred thirty-three is repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
