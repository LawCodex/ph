# Republic Act No. 9747

**An Act Renaming the Occidental Mindoro National College in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Occidental Mindoro as the Occidental Mindoro State College**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9747**

AN ACT RENAMING THE OCCIDENTAL MINDORO NATIONAL COLLEGE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN JOSE, PROVINCE OF OCCIDENTAL MINDORO AS THE OCCIDENTAL MINDORO STATE COLLEGE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Occidental Mindoro National College in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Occidental Mindoro is hereby renamed as the **_"Occidental Mindoro State College"._**

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,
