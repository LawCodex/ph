# Republic Act No. 7630

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dinagat National High School in the Municipality of Dinagat, Province of Surigao Del Norte, To Don Ruben E. Ecleo, Sr. Memorial National High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7630, July 17, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DINAGAT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DINAGAT, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL NORTE, TO DON RUBEN E. ECLEO, SR. MEMORIAL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Dinagat National High School in the Municipality of Dinagat, Province of Surigao del Norte, is hereby changed to Don Ruben E. Ecleo, Sr. Memorial National High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports is hereby directed to take cognizance and implement the provision of Section 1 hereof.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1992.
