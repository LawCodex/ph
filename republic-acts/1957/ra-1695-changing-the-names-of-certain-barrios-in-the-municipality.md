# Republic Act No. 1695

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1695**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the following barrios in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, are changed as follows:

Barrio Patay to barrio Balagtas;

Barrio Sambat Ilaya to barrio Kumintang Ilaya; and

Barrio Sambat Ibaba to barrio Kumintang Ibaba.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
