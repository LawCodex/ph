# Executive Order No. 78

**Further Amending Executive Order No. 24 Dated November 12 1946, Entitled “Creating the National Advisory Health Council”**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 78, October 25, 1954 ]

FURTHER AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 24 DATED NOVEMBER 12, 1946, ENTITLED “CREATING THE NATIONAL ADVISORY HEALTH COUNCIL.”

The second paragraph of Executive Order No. 24 dated November 12, 1946, as amended by Executive Order No. 86 dated September 3, 1947, Executive Order No. 424 dated March 10, 1951, Executive Order No. 429 dated April 7, 1951, and Executive Order No. 454 dated June 22, 1951, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

“**NOW, THEREFORE, I, RAMON MAGSAYSAY** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by law, do hereby create a body to be known as the National Advisory Health Council to study problems of public health and sanitation and to make such recommendations as it may deem necessary for the improvement of public health and sanitation and the promotion of medical research. The Council shall be composed of the following:
