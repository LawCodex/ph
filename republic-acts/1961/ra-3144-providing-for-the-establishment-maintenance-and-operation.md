# Republic Act No. 3144

**An Act Providing for the Establishment, Maintenance and Operation of a General Hospital in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3144**

**An Act Providing for the Establishment, Maintenance and Operation of a General Hospital in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the Bureau of Medical Services, a twenty-five-bed general hospital in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, to be known as the Occidental Negros South General Hospital.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Health shall promulgate such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry into effect the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

The sum of seventy-five thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the maintenance and operation of said hospital: _Provided,_ That no amount herein authorized to be appropriated will be released unless the Secretary of Health certifies that a suitable hospital building is available.1avvphi1 In subsequent years, the operation and maintenance of said hospital shall be financed in accordance with Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and thirty-nine.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1961.
