# Republic Act No. 4749

**An Act Changing the Name of Sampaloc Avenue in Quezon City to Don Tomas Morato Avenue**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4749**

An Act Changing the Name of Sampaloc Avenue in Quezon City to Don Tomas Morato Avenue

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Sampaloc Avenue in Quezon City is hereby changed to Don Tomas Morato Avenue, the provision of any law, ordinance, resolution or executive order to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
