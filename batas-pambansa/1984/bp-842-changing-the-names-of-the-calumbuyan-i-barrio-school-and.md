# Batas Pambansa Blg. 842

**An Act Changing the Names of the Calumbuyan I Barrio School and Calumbuyan II Barrio School Both in Barangay Santa Ana, Municipality of Calatagan, Province of Batangas, to Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School I and Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School II, Respectively**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 842**

An Act Changing the Names of the Calumbuyan I Barrio School and Calumbuyan Ii Barrio School Both in Barangay Santa Ana, Municipality of Calatagan, Province of Batangas, to Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School I and Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School Ii, Respectively

## Section 1

The names of the Calumbuyan I Barrio School and Calumbuyan II Barrio School both in Barangay Santa Ana, Municipality of Calatagan, Province of Batangas are changed to Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School I and Pedro Palacio, Sr. Memorial Barrio School II, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
