# Batas Pambansa Blg. 334

**An Act Changing the Name of the Atok National High School in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Jose Mencio National High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 334**

An Act Changing the Name of the Atok National High School in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Jose Mencio National High School

## Section 1

The name of the Atok National High School in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, is changed to Jose Mencio National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
