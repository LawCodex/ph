**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6160**

**An Act Changing the Name of Pansol Elementary School in Barrio Pansol, Municipality of Calamba, Province of Laguna, to Mababang Paaralang Don Eduardo E. Barreto**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Pansol Elementary School in the barrio of Pansol, Municipality of Calamba, Province of Laguna, is hereby changed to Mababang Paaralang Don Eduardo E. Barreto.1âшphi1
