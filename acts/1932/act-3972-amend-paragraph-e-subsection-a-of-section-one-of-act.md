# Act No. 3972

**An Act to Amend Paragraph (E), Subsection a of Section One of Act Numbered thirty-Eight Hundred and thirteen, Entitled "An Act Making Appropriations for Public Works."**

[ Act No. 3972, December 02, 1932 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND PARAGRAPH (E), SUBSECTION A OF SECTION ONE OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-EIGHT HUNDRED AND THIRTEEN, ENTITLED "AN ACT MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR PUBLIC WORKS."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Paragraph (e), subsection A of section one of Act Numbered Thirty-eight hundred and thirteen is hereby amended so that item four on page twenty-one under the caption "Occidental Misamis" shall read as follows:

"4. For the Oroquieta Home Economics Building ....................... P3,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 2, 1932.
