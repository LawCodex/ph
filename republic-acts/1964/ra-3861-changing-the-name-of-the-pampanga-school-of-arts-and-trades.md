# Republic Act No. 3861

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pampanga School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga, to Don Honorio Ventura Memorial School of Arts and Trades**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3861**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pampanga School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga, to Don Honorio Ventura Memorial School of Arts and Trades**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pampanga School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Bacolor, Province of Pampanga, is changed to Don Honorio Ventura Memorial School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1964.
