# Republic Act No. 4328

**An Act Increasing the Number of Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Oriental Mindoro, Amending for the Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4328**

AN ACT INCREASING THE NUMBER OF ASSISTANT PROVINCIAL FISCALS FOR THE PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION SIXTEEN HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, AS AMENDED.

## Section 1

The provisions of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Oriental Mindoro, is further amended to read as follows:

"Oriental Mindoro, five;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965
