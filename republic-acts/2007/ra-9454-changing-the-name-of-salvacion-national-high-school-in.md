# Republic Act No. 9454

**An Act Changing the Name of Salvacion National High School in Barangay Talojongon, Municipality of Tigaon, Province of Camarines Sur to the Dr. Rodolfo V. Pamor, Jr. Memorial National High School**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9454 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SALVACION NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TALOJONGON, MUNICIPALITY OF TIGAON, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR TO THE DR. RODOLFO V. PAMOR, JR. MEMORIAL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

_Renaming of National High School._ \- The Salvacion National High School in Barangay Talojongon, Municipality of Tigaon, Province of Camarines Sur is hereby changed to Dr. Rodolfo V. Pamor, Jr. Memorial National High School.

## SEC. 2

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._
