# Republic Act No. 6559

**An Act Establishing An Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Las Piñas, Province of Rizal, to Be Known as Las Piñas Emergency Hospital, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6559, September 30, 1972 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LAS PIÑAS, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, TO BE KNOWN AS LAS PIÑAS EMERGENCY HOSPITAL, AND AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS THEREFOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established under the Bureau of Medical Services, an emergency hospital in the Municipality of Las Pinas, Province of Rizal, to be known as Las Pinas Emergency Hospital.

## Section 2

The sum of two hundred thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the establishment, operation and maintenance of the said hospital for the fiscal year nineteen hundred seventy-two: _Provided,_ That no portion of the appropriation shall be transferred. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, September 30, 1972.
