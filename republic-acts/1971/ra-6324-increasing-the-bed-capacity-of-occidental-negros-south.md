# Republic Act No. 6324

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of Occidental Negros South General Hospital in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6324, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF OCCIDENTAL NEGROS SOUTH GENERAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HIMAMAYLAN, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, AND AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS THEREFOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of Occidental Negros South General Hospital is hereby increased from twenty-five beds to fifty.

## Section 2

The financing of this hospital shall be in accordance with the provisions of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred thirty-nine, otherwise known as the Hospital Financing Act. The necessary sums for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 19, 1971.
