# Batas Pambansa Blg. 687

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Matalahib of Quezon City to Barangay sto. Domingo**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 687**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Matalahib of Quezon City to Barangay Sto. Domingo

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Matalahib in Quezon City is hereby changed to Barangay Sto. Domingo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
