# Batas Pambansa Blg. 89

**An Act Changing the Name of the Antique General Hospital in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Angel Salazar Memorial General Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 89**

An Act Changing the Name of the Antique General Hospital in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Angel Salazar Memorial General Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Antique General Hospital in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Angel Salazar Memorial General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: October 14, 1980
