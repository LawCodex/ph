# Republic Act No. 1757

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Sinonogan in the Municipality of Pambujan, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1757**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Sinonogan in the Municipality of Pambujan, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Canagman, Pamantangan, Cabalyanan, Nagbong, Nabaye, Ele, Amotayong, and Cagkilikili in the Municipality of Pambujan, Province of Samar, are hereby constituted into a barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Sinonogan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
