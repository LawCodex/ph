# Republic Act No. 6794

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tolosa Barangay High School in Tolosa, Leyte, to Tolosa National High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6794, December 12, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE TOLOSA BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN TOLOSA, LEYTE, TO TOLOSA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tolosa Barangay High School in Tolosa, Leyte, is hereby changed to Tolosa National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
