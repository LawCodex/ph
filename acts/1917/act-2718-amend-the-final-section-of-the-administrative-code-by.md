# Act No. 2718

**An Act to Amend the Final Section of the Administrative Code By striking From the List of Acts Repealed thereby, Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Ninety-Seven.**

[ Act No. 2718, March 17, 1917 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND THE FINAL SECTION OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE BY STRIKING FROM THE LIST OF ACTS REPEALED THEREBY, ACT NUMBERED SIXTEEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-SEVEN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled, and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The Administrative Code is hereby amended by striking from the list of Acts thereby repealed in their entirety, Act Numbered Sixteen hundred and ninety-seven.

## Section 2

Sections three and four of Act Numbered Sixteen hundred and ninety-seven are hereby declared again in force.

## Section 3

Whoever in a solemn affirmation made in any of the cases covered by section three of Act Numbered Sixteen hundred and ninety-seven makes a false statement shall incur the penalties prescribed by said section three.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 17, 1917.
