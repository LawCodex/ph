# Republic Act No. 6433

**An Act Changing the Name of Hagonoy Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Bulacan, to Mayor Emilio G. Perez Memorial Hospital.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6433, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF HAGONOY EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HAGONOY, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO MAYOR EMILIO G. PEREZ MEMORIAL HOSPITAL.

## Section 1

The name of Hagonoy Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Bulacan is hereby changed to Mayor Emilio G. Perez Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1972.
