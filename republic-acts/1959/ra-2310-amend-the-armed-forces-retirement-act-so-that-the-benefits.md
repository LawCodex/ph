# Republic Act No. 2310

**An Act to Amend the Armed Forces Retirement Act so that the Benefits Therein Granted Shall not be Subject to Attachment, Levy, Execution, or Any Tax Whatsoever**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2310**

**An Act to Amend the Armed Forces Retirement Act so that the Benefits Therein Granted Shall not be Subject to Attachment, Levy, Execution, or Any Tax Whatsoever**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Act Numbered Three hundred forty, otherwise known as the Armed Forces Retirement Act, is amended by inserting between sections ten and eleven thereof the following new section:

## Sec. 10-A

The benefits herein granted shall not be subject to attachment, levy, execution or any tax whatsoever."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1959.
