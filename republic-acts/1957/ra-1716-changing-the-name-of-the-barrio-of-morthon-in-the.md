# Republic Act No. 1716

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Morthon in the Municipality of Balete, Province of Capiz, to Barrio Fulgencio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1716**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Morthon in the Municipality of Balete, Province of Capiz, to Barrio Fulgencio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Morthon in the Municipality of Balete, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to barrio Fulgencio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
