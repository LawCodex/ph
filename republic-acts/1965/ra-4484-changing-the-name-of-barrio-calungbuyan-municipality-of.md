# Republic Act No. 4484

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Calungbuyan, Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to be Known as Barrio Aglipay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4484**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Calungbuyan, Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to be Known as Barrio Aglipay

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Calungbuyan, Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to be known as Barrio Aglipay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
