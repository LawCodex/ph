# Republic Act No. 8016

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol National High School in Tagbilaran City, Province of Bohol, to Dr. Cecilio Putong National High School**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8016, May 25, 1995 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BOHOL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN TAGBILARAN CITY, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, TO DR. CECILIO PUTONG NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bohol National High School in Tagbilaran City, Province of Bohol, is hereby changed to Dr. Cecilio Putong National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on May 25, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
