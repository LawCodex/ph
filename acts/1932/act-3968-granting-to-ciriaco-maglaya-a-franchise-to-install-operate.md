# Act No. 3968

**An Act Granting to Ciriaco Maglaya a Franchise to Install, Operate and Maintain An Electric Light, Heat and Power System in the Municipality of Mariveles, Province of Bataan, Philippine Islands.**

[ Act No. 3968, December 02, 1932 ]

AN ACT GRANTING TO CIRIACO MAGLAYA A FRANCHISE TO INSTALL, OPERATE AND MAINTAIN AN ELECTRIC LIGHT, HEAT AND POWER SYSTEM IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARIVELES, PROVINCE OF BATAAN, PHILIPPINE ISLANDS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Subject to the rules and conditions established in Act Numbered Thirty-six hundred and thirty-six, there is hereby granted to Ciriaco Maglaya, for a period of fifty years from the approval of this Act, the right, privilege and authority to construct, maintain and operate an electric light, heat and power system for the purpose of generating and distributing electric light, heat and power, for sale within limits of the municipality of Mariveles, Province of Bataan, Philippine Islands.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 2, 1932.
