# Republic Act No. 641

**An Act Creating the Municipality of Limbuhan in the Province of Masbate**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 641, June 11, 1951 ]

AN ACT CREATING THE MUNICIPALITY OF LIMBUHAN IN THE PROVINCE OF MASBATE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The barrios of Limbuhan, Guindawahan, Palho, Casabangan, Salvacion, Alegria, Tanke, Bundukan, Bugtong, and Cabangrayan are separated from the municipality of Cataingan, Province of Masbate, and constituted into a new municipality to be known as the Municipality of Limbuhan in said province, with the seat of government at the present site of the barrio of Limbuhan.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

The first mayor, vice-mayor, and councilors of the said new municipality shall be appointed by the President and shall continue to hold office until their successors shall have been elected and shall have qualified.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 11, 1951.
