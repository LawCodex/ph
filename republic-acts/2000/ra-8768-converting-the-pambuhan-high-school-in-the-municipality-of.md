# Republic Act No. 8768

**An Act Converting the Pambuhan High School in the Municipality of Garchitorena, Province of Camarines Sur, into a National High School to be known as the Pambuhan National High School and appropriating funds therefor**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8768 March 28, 2000**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE PAMBUHAN HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GARCHITORENA, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE PAMBUHAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

## SECTION 1

The Pambuhan High School in the Municipality of Garchitorena, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as the Pambuhan National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports (DECS) is hereby directed to implement the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **March 28, 2000**
