# Republic Act No. 1256

**An Act Changing the Name of Manuguit Street, a National Highway in the City of Manila, to Jose Abad Santos Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1256**

**An Act Changing the Name of Manuguit Street, a National Highway in the City of Manila, to Jose Abad Santos Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Manuguit Street, a national highway in the City of Manila, shall hereafter be known as Jose Abad Santos Street.

## Section 2

This Act take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
