# Republic Act No. 3914

**An Act Changing the Name of Taglibi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Patikul, Province of Sulu, to Datu Uddin Bahjin Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3914**

**An Act Changing the Name of Taglibi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Patikul, Province of Sulu, to Datu Uddin Bahjin Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Taglibi Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Patikul, Province of Sulu, is changed to Datu Uddin Bahjin Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
