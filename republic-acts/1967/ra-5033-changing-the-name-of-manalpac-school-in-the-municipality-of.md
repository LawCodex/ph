# Republic Act No. 5033

**An Act Changing the Name of Manalpac School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Manalpac-Mariquet Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5033**

An Act Changing the Name of Manalpac School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Manalpac-Mariquet Primary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Manalpac School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Manalpac-Mariquet Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
