# Republic Act No. 6796

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mabiton Barangay High School in the Municipality of Claveria, Province of Masbate, to Felixberto Del Rosario, Sr. Memorial High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6796, December 12, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MABITON BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CLAVERIA, PROVINCE OF MASBATE, TO FELIXBERTO DEL ROSARIO, SR. MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mabiton Barangay High School in Barangay Mabiton, Municipality of Claveria, Province of Masbate, is hereby changed to Felixberto del Rosario, Sr. Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
