# Act No. 4156

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Twenty-Five thousand Pesos for the Printing of the "Diario De Sesiones" of the Philippine Legislature, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Acts No. 4156, December 01, 1934 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TWENTY-FIVE THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE PRINTING OF THE "DIARIO DE SESIONES" OF THE PHILIPPINE LEGISLATURE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated the sum of twenty-five thousand pesos out of the funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated to defray the expenses for the printing of the "Diario de Sesiones" of the Philippine Legislature.

## Section 2

The printing of the "Diario de Sesiones" and the disbursement of the funds herein appropriated as well as the distribution of said "Diario de Sesiones" shall be made under the direction and supervision of the Presidents of both Houses or of the officer or person they may designate.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 1, 1934.
