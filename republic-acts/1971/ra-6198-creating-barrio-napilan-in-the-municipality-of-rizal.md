# Republic Act No. 6198

**An Act Creating Barrio Napilan in the Municipality of Rizal, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6198, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO NAPILAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF RIZAL, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Napilan in the poblacion of the Municipality of Rizal, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is separated therefrom, and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio in said municipality, to be known as Barrio Napilan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
