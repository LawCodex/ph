# Republic Act No. 10663

**An Act Naming as the 'President Corazon C. Aquino Avenue' the Circumferential Road Which Commences at the Junction of the Ilo-ilo-Dumangas Coastal Road in Balabago Jaro District, Iloilo City, Then Follows a General Westward Direction Along the Floodways South Bank Towards Buhang, Jaro; Tacas, Jaro and Ungka II, Pavia, Then Traverses Southwards to the Mandurriao District, Iloilo City-Pavia-San Miguel Tri-Border, and Ends at the Ilo-ilo Antique Road, Near the Center of Arevalo District, Iloilo City**

Sixteenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eighth day of July, two thousand fourteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10663**

AN ACT NAMING AS THE 'PRESIDENT CORAZON C. AQUINO AVENUE' THE CIRCUMFERENTIAL ROAD WHICH COMMENCES AT THE JUNCTION OF THE ILO-ILO-DUMANGAS COASTAL ROAD IN BALABAGO JARO DISTRICT, ILOILO CITY, THEN FOLLOWS A GENERAL WESTWARD DIRECTION ALONG THE FLOODWAYS SOUTH BANK TOWARDS BUHANG, JARO; TACAS, JARO AND UNGKA II, PAVIA, THEN TRAVERSES SOUTHWARDS TO THE MANDURRIAO DISTRICT, ILOILO CITY-PAVIA-SAN MIGUEL TRI-BORDER, AND ENDS AT THE ILO-ILO ANTIQUE ROAD, NEAR THE CENTER OF AREVALO DISTRICT, ILOILO CITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

A four (4)-lane circumferential road that spans 14.3217 kilometers and commences at the junction of the Ilo-ilo Dumangas Coastal Road in Balabago, Jaro District, Iloilo City, then follows a general westward direction along the floodway's south bank towards Buhang, Jaro; Tacas, Jaro and Ungka II, Pavia, then traverses southwards to the Mandurriao District Iloilo City-Pavia-San Miguel tri-border, and ends at the Iloilo-Antique Road, near the center of Arevalo District, Iloilo City, shall be named as the **_"President Corazon C. Aquino Avenue"._**

## Section 2

The Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
