# Republic Act No. 1735

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Poscolon in the Barrio of Calaigang, Municipality of Barotac Viejo, Province of Iloilo, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Poscolon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1735**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Poscolon in the Barrio of Calaigang, Municipality of Barotac Viejo, Province of Iloilo, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Poscolon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Poscolon in the barrio of Calaigang, Municipality of Barotac Viejo, Province of Iloilo, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Poscolon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
