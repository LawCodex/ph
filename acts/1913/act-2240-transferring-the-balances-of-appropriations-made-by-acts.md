# Act No. 2240

**An Act Transferring the Balances of Appropriations Made By Acts Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Sixty-Six. Eighteen Hundred and Nine, and Nineteen Hundred and Ninety-One to the Appropriation Made By Act Numbered Twenty-One Hundred and Ninety-Nine, and Reducing the Appropriation of the Last Mentioned Act in An Equal Amount.**

[ Act No. 2240, February 11, 1913 ]

AN ACT TRANSFERRING THE BALANCES OF APPROPRIATIONS MADE BY ACTS NUMBERED SIXTEEN HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX. EIGHTEEN HUNDRED AND NINE, AND NINETEEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-ONE TO THE APPROPRIATION MADE BY ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-ONE HUNDRED AND NINETY-NINE, AND REDUCING THE APPROPRIATION OF THE LAST MENTIONED ACT IN AN EQUAL AMOUNT.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The unexpended balances in the Insular Treasury of appropriations made by Acts Numbered Sixteen hundred and sixty-six, Eighteen hundred and nine, and Nineteen hundred and ninety-one, and all accretions thereto by reason of interest payments, or other causes, are hereby directed to be transferred to the appropriation made by Act Numbered Twenty-one hundred and ninety-nine and made available for all the purposes enumerated in that

## Section 2

The appropriation of five hundred thousand pesos made by Act Numbered Twenty-one hundred and ninety-nine is hereby reduced in an amount equal to the accretions which it will receive by operation of the preceding section.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its passage.

Enacted, February 11, 1913.
