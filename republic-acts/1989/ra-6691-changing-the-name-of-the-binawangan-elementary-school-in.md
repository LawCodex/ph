# Republic Act No. 6691

**An Act Changing the Name of the Binawangan Elementary School in Barangay Binawangan, Municipality of Capalonga, Province of Camarines Norte, to Marca Murillo Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6691, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BINAWANGAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BINAWANGAN, MUNICIPALITY OF CAPALONGA, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO MARCA MURILLO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Binawangan Elementary School in Barangay Binawangan, Municipality of Capalonga, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Marca Murillo Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
