# Republic Act No. 6700

**An Act Changing the Name of the Kalamansig Central Pilot School in Kalamansig, Sultan Kudarat, to Datu Guiabar Memorial School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6700, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE KALAMANSIG CENTRAL PILOT SCHOOL IN KALAMANSIG, SULTAN KUDARAT, TO DATU GUIABAR MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Kalamansig Central Pilot School in Kalamansig, Sultan Kudarat, is hereby changed to Datu Guiabar Memorial School.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
