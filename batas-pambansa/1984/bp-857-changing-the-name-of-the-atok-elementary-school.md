# Batas Pambansa Blg. 857

**An Act Changing the Name of the Atok Elementary School, Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Filomena P. Cating Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 857**

An Act Changing the Name of the Atok Elementary School, Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Filomena P. Cating Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Atok Elementary School, in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, is changed to Filomena P. Cating Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
