# Republic Act No. 3577

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gutad Elementary School in the Municipality of Floridablanca, Province of Pampanga, to Atanacio Pangan Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3577**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gutad Elementary School in the Municipality of Floridablanca, Province of Pampanga, to Atanacio Pangan Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Gutad Elementary School in the Municipality of Floridablanca, Province of Pampanga, is hereby changed to Atanacio Pangan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
