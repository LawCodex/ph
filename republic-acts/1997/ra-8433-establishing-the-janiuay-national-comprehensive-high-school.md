# Republic Act No. 8433

**An Act Establishing the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School in the Municipality of Janiuay, Province of Iloilo, to Be Known as the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8433, December 15, 1997 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE JANIUAY NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF JANIUAY, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE JANIUAY NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby established the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School to be located in the Municipality of Janiuay, Iloilo.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports is hereby directed to take such measures as are necessary to establish the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.1aшphi1

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, Lapsed into law on December 15, 1997, without the signature of the President, in accordance with Article VI, Section 27 (1) of the Constitution.
