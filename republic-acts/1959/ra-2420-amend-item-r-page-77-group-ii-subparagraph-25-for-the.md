# Republic Act No. 2420

**An Act to Amend Item (r), Page 77, Group II, Subparagraph (25), for the Province of Laguna, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2420**

**An Act to Amend Item (r), Page 77, Group II, Subparagraph (25), for the Province of Laguna, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (r), page 77, group II, subparagraph (25), for the Province of Laguna, paragraph f, section two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is amended to read as follows:

"(r) Majayjay Slaughterhouse Repair and improvement ₱3,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
