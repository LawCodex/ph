# Republic Act No. 10849

**An Act Changing the Name of Alcantara National Trade School to Romblon National Institute of Technology**

Sixteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10849**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ALCANTARA NATIONAL TRADE SCHOOL TO ROMBLON NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## SECTION 1

_Renaming of a National Trade School._ -The name of Alcantara National Trade School in the Municipality of Alcantara, Province of Romblon is hereby changed to Romblon National Institute of Technology.

## SECTION 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ – The main campus of the Romblon National Institute of Technology shall continue to remain in its present location in the Municipality of Alcantara, Province of Romblon.

## SECTION 3

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.1âwphi1
