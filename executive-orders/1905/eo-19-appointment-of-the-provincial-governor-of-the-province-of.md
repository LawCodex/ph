# Executive Order No. 19

**Appointment of the Provincial Governor of the Province of Bulacan**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 19**

The provincial Governor of the Province of Bulacan is hereby appointed member of the committee created in and bye section four of Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and eighty-one to make the improvements in the town of Sibul, municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, in said section and Act provided, in lieu of Donato Teodoro, resigned.

**W. CAMERON FORBES**
_Governor-General_

By **THOMAS CARY WELCH**
_Acting Executive Secretary_
