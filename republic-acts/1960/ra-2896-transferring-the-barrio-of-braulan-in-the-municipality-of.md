# Republic Act No. 2896

**An Act Transferring the Barrio of Braulan in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, to the Municipality of San Enrique**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2896**

**An Act Transferring the Barrio of Braulan in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, to the Municipality of San Enrique**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Braulan in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, is separated from said municipality, and made a part of the Municipality of San Enrique.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
