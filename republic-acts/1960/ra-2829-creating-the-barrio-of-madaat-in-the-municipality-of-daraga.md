# Republic Act No. 2829

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Madaat in the Municipality of DARaga, Province of Albay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2829**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Madaat in the Municipality of Daraga, Province of Albay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Madaat in the barrio of San Vicente Grande, Municipality of Daraga, Province of Albay is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Madaat.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
