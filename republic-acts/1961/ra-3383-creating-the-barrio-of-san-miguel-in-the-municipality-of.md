# Republic Act No. 3383

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Miguel in the Municipality of Lobo, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3383**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Miguel in the Municipality of Lobo, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Viga in the barrio of Tayuman, Municipality of Lobo, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of San Miguel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
