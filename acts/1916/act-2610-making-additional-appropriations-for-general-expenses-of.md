# Act No. 2610

**An Act Making Additional Appropriations for General Expenses of the Judiciary for the Fiscal Year Ended December thirty-First, Nineteen Hundred and Fifteen.**

[ Act No. 2610, February 04, 1916 ]

AN ACT MAKING ADDITIONAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR GENERAL EXPENSES OF THE JUDICIARY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDED DECEMBER THIRTY-FIRST, NINETEEN HUNDRED AND FIFTEEN.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The sum of forty-nine thousand one hundred p and forty-five pesos and thirty-eight centavos, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the general expenses of the judiciary, to cover urgent deficiencies in the appropriations for the fiscal year ended December thirty-first, nineteen hundred and fifteen:

Courts of First Instance: For salaries of judges of Courts of First Instance, in addition to the sum appropriated by Act No. 2431 . . . . . . . . . . . . P13,227.78

Justices of the peace: For salaries of justices of the peace, in addition to the sum appropriated by Act No. 2431 . . . . . . . . . .20,000.00

Contingent expenses, judiciary: For office supplies . . . . . .9,182.06

For incidental expenses . . . . . . . . . . . . 6,735.54

Total of appropriations by this Act . . . . . . . . . . . . . . 49,145.38

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, February 4, 1916.
