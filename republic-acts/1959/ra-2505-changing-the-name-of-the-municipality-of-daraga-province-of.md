# Republic Act No. 2505

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of DARaga, Province of Albay, to Municipality of Locsin**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2505**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Daraga, Province of Albay, to Municipality of Locsin**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Daraga, Province of Albay, is changed to Municipality of Locsin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
