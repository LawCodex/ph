# Republic Act No. 9754

**An Act Changing the Name of San Narciso Vocational High School in San Narciso, Province of Quezon to Godofredo M. Tan Memorial School of Arts and Trades**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9754**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SAN NARCISO VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN SAN NARCISO, PROVINCE OF QUEZON TO GODOFREDO M. TAN MEMORIAL SCHOOL OF ARTS AND TRADES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of High School._ \- The name of San Narciso Vocational High School in the Municipality of San Narciso, Province of Quezon is hereby changed to **_Godofredo M. Tan Memorial School of Arts and Trades._**

## Section 2

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
