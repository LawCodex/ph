# Republic Act No. 1628

**An Act Changing the Name of the Naujan Farm School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Mariano P. Leuterio Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1628**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Naujan Farm School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Mariano P. Leuterio Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Naujan Farm School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is changed to Mariano P. Leuterio Memorial School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
