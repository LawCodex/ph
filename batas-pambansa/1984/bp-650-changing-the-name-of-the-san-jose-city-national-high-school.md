# Batas Pambansa Blg. 650

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Jose City National High School in the Province of Nueva Ecija to Constancio Padilla National High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 650**

An Act Changing the Name of the San Jose City National High School in the Province of Nueva Ecija to Constancio Padilla National High School

## Section 1

The name of the San Jose City National High School in the Province of Nueva Ecija is changed to Constancio Padilla National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
