# Republic Act No. 2100

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Putol in the Province of Nagcarlan, Province of Laguna, to Malaya**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2100**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Putol in the Province of Nagcarlan, Province of Laguna, to Malaya**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Putol in the Municipality of Nagcarlan, Province of Laguna, is changed to Malaya.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 28, 1959.
