# Republic Act No. 2605

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Obay in the Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Obay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2605**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIO OF OBAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF POLANCO, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, INTO A BARRIO TO BE KNOWN AS BARRIO OBAY.

## Section 1

The sitio of Obay in the Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Obay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without executive approval, June 21, 1959
