# Republic Act No. 10104

**An Act Converting the Interior Circumferential Road of Lanao Del Norte, Province of Lanao Del Norte Into a National Road**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10104**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE INTERIOR CIRCUMFERENTIAL ROAD OF LANAO DEL NORTE, PROVINCE OF LANAO DEL NORTE INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The interior circumferential road which starts from the Iligan - Aurora National Highway at Iligan and traverses Tagoloan, Baloi, Pantao - Ragat, Munai, Tangkal, Magsaysay, Tubod, Salvador, Sapad, Nunungan and ends at Malabang - Tukuran National Highway at Sultan Naga Dimaporo, Province of Lanao del Norte is hereby converted into a national road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
