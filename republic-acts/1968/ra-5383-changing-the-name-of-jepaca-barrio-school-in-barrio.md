# Republic Act No. 5383

**An Act Changing the Name of Jepaca Barrio School in Barrio Jelicuon Lusaya, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, to Pablo Muyuela Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5383**

An Act Changing the Name of Jepaca Barrio School in Barrio Jelicuon Lusaya, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, to Pablo Muyuela Barrio School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Jepaca Barrio School in Barrio Jelicuon Lusaya, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Pablo Muyuela Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
