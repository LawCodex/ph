# Republic Act No. 3507

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sibutu Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Sulu, to Datu Jaafar Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3507**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sibutu Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Sulu, to Datu Jaafar Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sibutu Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Sulu, is changed to Datu Jaafar Central Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
