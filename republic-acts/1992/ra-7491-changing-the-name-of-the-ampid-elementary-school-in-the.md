# Republic Act No. 7491

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ampid Elementary School in the Municipality of San Mateo, Province of Rizal, To Vicente Santiago Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7491, May 13, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE AMPID ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN MATEO, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, TO VICENTE SANTIAGO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Ampid Elementary School located in the Municipality of San Mateo, Province of Rizal, is hereby changed to Vicente Santiago Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
