# Republic Act No. 4758

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ebus in the Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to Barrio San Vicente**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4758**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ebus in the Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to Barrio San Vicente

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Ebus in the Municipality of Guagua Province of Pampanga, is changed to Barrio San Vicente.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
