# Republic Act No. 3532

**An Act Appropriating One Hundred Fifty Thousand Pesos for the Construction of a Monument at Fort Santiago, to Honor the Memory of the Unknown Soldiers**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3532**

**An Act Appropriating One Hundred Fifty Thousand Pesos for the Construction of a Monument at Fort Santiago, to Honor the Memory of the Unknown Soldiers**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sum of one hundred fifty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the construction of a monument at Fort Santiago, to honor the memory of the unknown soldiers.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1963.
