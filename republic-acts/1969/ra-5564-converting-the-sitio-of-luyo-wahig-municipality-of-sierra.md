# Republic Act No. 5564

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Luyo Wahig, Municipality of Sierra Bullones, Province of Bohol, Into a Barrio**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5564, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIO OF LUYO WAHIG, MUNICIPALITY OF SIERRA BULLONES, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, INTO A BARRIO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Luyo Wahig in the Municipality of Sierra Bullones, Province of Bohol, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Santa Cruz of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
