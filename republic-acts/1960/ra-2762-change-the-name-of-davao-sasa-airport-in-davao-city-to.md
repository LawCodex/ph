# Republic Act No. 2762

**An Act to Change the Name of Davao (Sasa) Airport in Davao City to Francisco Bangoy Airport in Honor of the Late Don Francisco Bangoy**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2762**

**An Act to Change the Name of Davao (Sasa) Airport in Davao City to Francisco Bangoy Airport in Honor of the Late Don Francisco Bangoy**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the philanthropic acts of the late Don Francisco Bangoy, the name of Davao (Sasa) Airport in Davao City is hereby changed to Francisco Bangoy Airport.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
