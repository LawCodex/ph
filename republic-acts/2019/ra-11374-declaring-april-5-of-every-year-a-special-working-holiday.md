# Republic Act No. 11374

**An Act Declaring April 5 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Province of Pangasinan, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to be Known as the “Pangasinan Day”**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11374**

**An Act Declaring April 5 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Province of Pangasinan, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to be Known as The "Pangasinan Day"**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress assembled:

## Section 1

April 5 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the Province of Pangasinan, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as the "Pangasinan Day". Upon proclamation of the President, April 5 of every year shall be a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Pangasinan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

(Sgd) **VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

(Sgd) **GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
Speaker of the House of Representatives

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives on May 22, 2017, amended by the Senate of the Philippines on June 3, 2019, and which amendments were concurred in by the House of Representatives on June 4, 2019.
