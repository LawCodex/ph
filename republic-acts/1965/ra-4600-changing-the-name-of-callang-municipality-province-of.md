# Republic Act No. 4600

**An Act Changing the Name of Callang Municipality, Province of Isabela, to San Manuel Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4600**

An Act Changing the Name of Callang Municipality, Province of Isabela, to San Manuel Municipality

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Callang Municipality in the Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to San Manuel Municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
