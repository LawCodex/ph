# Batas Pambansa Blg. 777

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ozamis City National Emergency Hospital in the City of Ozamis to Mayor Hilarion A. Ramiro, Sr. General Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 777**

An Act Changing the Name of the Ozamis City National Emergency Hospital in the City of Ozamis to Mayor Hilarion A. Ramiro, Sr. General Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Ozamis City National Emergency Hospital in the City of Ozamis is changed to Mayor Hilarion A. Ramiro, Sr. General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
