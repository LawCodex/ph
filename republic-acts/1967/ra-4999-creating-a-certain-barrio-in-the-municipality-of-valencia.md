# Republic Act No. 4999

**An Act Creating a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bohol**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4999**

An Act Creating a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bohol

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Kisaw and Can-apo are hereby constituted into a distinct and independent barrio, to be known as Barrio Mahayag, in the Municipality of Valencia, Province of Bohol.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
