# Republic Act No. 7093

**An Act Changing the Name of the Alangalang Agro-industrial School in Alangalang, Leyte, to Leyte State School of Agriculture**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7093, August 02, 1991 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ALANGALANG AGRO-INDUSTRIAL SCHOOL IN ALANGALANG, LEYTE, TO LEYTE STATE SCHOOL OF AGRICULTURE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Alangalang Agro-Industrial School in Alangalang, Leyte, is hereby changed to Leyte State School of Agriculture.

## Section 2

The College shall offer secondary course in agriculture, short-term technical or vocational courses, and higher technological and professional instruction and training in agricultural and industrial fields as may be authorized by the Department of Education, Culture and Sports.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
