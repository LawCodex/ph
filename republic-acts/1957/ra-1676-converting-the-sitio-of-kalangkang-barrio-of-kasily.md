# Republic Act No. 1676

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kalangkang, Barrio of Kasily, Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Kalangkang of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1676**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kalangkang, Barrio of Kasily, Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Kalangkang of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Kalangkang, barrio of Kasily, Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Kalangkang.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
