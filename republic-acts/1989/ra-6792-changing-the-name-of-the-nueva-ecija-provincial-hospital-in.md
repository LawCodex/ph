# Republic Act No. 6792

**An Act Changing the Name of the Nueva Ecija Provincial Hospital in San Jose City, Province of Nueva Ecija, to San Jose City General Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6792, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE NUEVA ECIJA PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL IN SAN JOSE CITY, PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECIJA, TO SAN JOSE CITY GENERAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Nueva Ecija Provincial Hospital in San Jose City, Province of Nueva Ecija, is hereby changed to San Jose City General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
