# Republic Act No. 1752

**An Act Changing the Name of Antipolo Elementary School, Municipality of Antipolo, Province of Rizal, to Juan Sumulong Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1752**

**An Act Changing the Name of Antipolo Elementary School, Municipality of Antipolo, Province of Rizal, to Juan Sumulong Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1
