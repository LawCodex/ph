# Act No. 3269

**An Act Setting Aside a Sum of Money From the Funds Assigned to Miscellaneous Expenses in the Appropriations for Both Bodies of the Legislature During the Year Nineteen Hundred and Twenty-Six for the Payment of Salaries of Such Temporary and Supplementary Force as May Be Necessary**

[ Act No. 3269, July 30, 1926 ]

AN ACT SETTING ASIDE A SUM OF MONEY FROM THE FUNDS ASSIGNED TO MISCELLANEOUS EXPENSES IN THE APPROPRIATIONS FOR BOTH BODIES OF THE LEGISLATURE DURING THE YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX FOR THE PAYMENT OF SALARIES OF SUCH TEMPORARY AND SUPPLEMENTARY FORCE AS MAY BE NECESSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated, for paying the salaries of the temporary employees and the supplementary force of both Bodies of the Legislature, during nineteen hundred and twenty-six, the sum of twenty-six thousand five hundred pesos for the Senate, and forty-six thousand pesos for the House of Representatives, to be taken from their respective appropriations for miscellaneous expenses, made in the General Appropriation Act and the Additional Appropriation Act, Numbered Thirty-two hundred and twenty-seven and Thirty-two hundred and sixty.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, July 30, 1926.
