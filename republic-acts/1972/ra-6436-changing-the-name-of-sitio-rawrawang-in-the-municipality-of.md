# Republic Act No. 6436

**An Act Changing the Name of Sitio Rawrawang in the Municipality of Espiritu, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Don Mariano.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6436, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SITIO RAWRAWANG IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ESPIRITU, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO BARRIO DON MARIANO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:1âшphi1

## Section 1

The name of Sitio Rawrawang in the Municipality of Espiritu, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Don Mariano.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1972.
