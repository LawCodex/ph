# Batas Pambansa Blg. 332

**An Act Changing the Name of the City of Roxas High School in the City of Roxas to Congressman Ramon A. Arnaldo High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 332**

An Act Changing the Name of the City of Roxas High School in the City of Roxas to Congressman Ramon A. Arnaldo High School

## Section 1

The name of the City of Roxas High School in the City of Roxas is changed to Congressman Ramon A. Arnaldo High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
