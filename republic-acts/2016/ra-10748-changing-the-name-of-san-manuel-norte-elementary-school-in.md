# Republic Act No. 10748

**An Act Changing the Name of San Manuel Norte Elementary School in the Municipality of Agoo, Province of La Union to Dr. Manuel T. Cases Sr. Elementary School**

Sixteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10748**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SAN MANUEL NORTE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF AGOO, PROVINCE OF LA UNION TO DR. MANUEL T. CASES SR. ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## SECTION 1

_Renaming of an Elementary School._ – The name of San Manuel Norte Elementary School in the Municipality of Agoo, Province of La Union is hereby changed to Dr. Manuel T. Cases Sr. Elementary School.

## SECTION 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ – The main campus of the Dr. Manuel T. Cases Sr. Elementary School shall continue to remain in its present location in the Municipality of Agoo, Province of La Union.

## SECTION 3

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
