# Republic Act No. 2161

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Turod, Paco, Calamagui and Kim-Malabasa in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, into Barrios**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2161**

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Turod, Paco, Calamagui and Kim-Malabasa in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, into Barrios**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Turod, Paco, Calamagui and Kim-malabasa in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, are hereby converted into barrios.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
