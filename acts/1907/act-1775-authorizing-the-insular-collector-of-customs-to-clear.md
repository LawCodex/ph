# Act No. 1775

**An Act Authorizing the Insular Collector of Customs to Clear Foreign Vessels for the Ports of Legaspi and Tabaco.**

[ Act No. 1775, October 11, 1907 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE INSULAR COLLECTOR OF CUSTOMS TO CLEAR FOREIGN VESSELS FOR THE PORTS OF LEGASPI AND TABACO.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The Insular Collector of Customs is hereby authorized to clear foreign vessels for the ports of Legaspi and Tabaco, customs. Province of Albay, under such conditions and regulations as he may, with the approval of the Secretary of Finance and Justice, impose: _Provided,_ That all expense incident to the entrance of a Port expenses. foreign vessel in I o the ports of Legaspi and Tabaco shall be a charge against such vessel and shall be collected before the vessel is granted a foreign clearance.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, October 11, 1907.
