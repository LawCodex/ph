# Republic Act No. 6721

**An Act Declaring November Thirteen of Each Year a Special Public Holiday in the Province of Pangasinan**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6721 March 20, 1989**

AN ACT DECLARING NOVEMBER THIRTEEN OF EACH YEAR A SPECIAL PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

In order to commemorate the birth anniversary of the late Speaker Eugenio Perez, November Thirteen of each year is hereby declared a special non-working holiday in the Province of Pangasinan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **March 20, 1989.**
