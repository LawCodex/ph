# Batas Pambansa Blg. 291

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Magsaysay, in the Province of Isabela, to the Municipality of Delfin Albano.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 291**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Magsaysay, in the Province of Isabela, to the Municipality of Delfin Albano

## Section 1

The Municipality of Magsaysay, in the Province of Isabela, shall hereafter be known as the Municipality of Delfin Albano.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
