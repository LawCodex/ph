# Republic Act No. 1766

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Behia in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1766**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Behia in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Behia in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, is separated from the barrio of Callejon, and is constituted into a new barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Behia.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
