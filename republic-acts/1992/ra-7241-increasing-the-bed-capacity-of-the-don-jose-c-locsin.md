# Republic Act No. 7241

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Don Jose C. Locsin Memorial Hospital in the City of Silay, Province of Negros Occidental, From Seventy-five (75) to One Hundred Fifty (150) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7241, March 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE DON JOSE C. LOCSIN MEMORIAL HOSPITAL IN THE CITY OF SILAY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, FROM SEVENTY-FIVE (75) TO ONE HUNDRED FIFTY (150) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Don Jose C. Locsin Memorial Hospital in the City of Silay, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby increased from seventy-five (75) to one hundred fifty (150) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
