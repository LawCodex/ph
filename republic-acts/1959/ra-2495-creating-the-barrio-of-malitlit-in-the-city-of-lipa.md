# Republic Act No. 2495

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Malitlit in the City of Lipa**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2495**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Malitlit in the City of Lipa**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Malitlit in the City of Lipa is converted into a barrio of said city to be known as the barrio of Malitlit.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
