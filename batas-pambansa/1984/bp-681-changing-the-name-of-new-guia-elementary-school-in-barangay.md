# Batas Pambansa Blg. 681

**An Act Changing the Name of New Guia Elementary School in Barangay New Guia, Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, to Don Paco Dinglasan Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 681**

An Act Changing the Name of New Guia Elementary School in Barangay New Guia, Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, to Don Paco Dinglasan Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of New Guia Elementary School in Barangay New Guia, Municipality of Maayon, Province of Capiz, is changed to Don Paco Dinglasan Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
