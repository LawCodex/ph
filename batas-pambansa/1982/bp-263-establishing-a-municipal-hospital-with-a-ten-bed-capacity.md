# Batas Pambansa Blg. 263

**An Act Establishing a Municipal Hospital With a Ten-Bed Capacity and Secondary Level Capability in the Municipality of Madalag, Province of Aklan, to Be Known as the Don Leovigildo N. Diapo Sr. Municipal Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 263**

An Act Establishing a Municipal Hospital with a Ten-Bed Capacity and Secondary Level Capability in the Municipality of Madalag, Province of Aklan, to be Known as the Don Leovigildo N. Diapo, Sr. Municipal Hospital

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Office of the Minister (Ministry of Health), a municipal hospital with a ten-bed capacity and secondary level capability in the Municipality of Madalag, Province of Aklan, to be known as the Don Leovigildo N. Diapo, Sr. Municipal Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount needed for the operation of the hospital shall be determined in accordance with Section 40 of Presidential Decree No. 1177 and charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 131 for the Ministry of Health intended for new hospitals. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The Minister of Health shall determine the manner by which said hospital shall be made operational.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 13, 1982
