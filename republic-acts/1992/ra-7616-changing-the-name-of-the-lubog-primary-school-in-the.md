# Republic Act No. 7616

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lubog Primary School in the Municipality of Sapian, Province of Capiz, To Telesforo Aperocho Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7616, June 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LUBOG PRIMARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAPIAN, PROVINCE OF CAPIZ, TO TELESFORO APEROCHO MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lubog Primary School in the Municipality of Sapian, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to Telesforo Aperocho Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
