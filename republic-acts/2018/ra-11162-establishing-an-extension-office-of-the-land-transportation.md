# Republic Act No. 11162

**An Act Establishing An Extension Office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the Municipality of Brooke’S Point, Province of Palawan and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11162**

AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EXTENSION OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BROOKE’S POINT, PROVINCE OF PALAWAN AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) Palawan District Office, an Extension Office of the LTO in the Municipality of Brooke’s Point, Province of Palawan.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department’s programs the operationalization of the LTO Extension Office in the Municipality of Brooke’s Point, Province of Palawan, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

The Chairperson of the LTO shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
