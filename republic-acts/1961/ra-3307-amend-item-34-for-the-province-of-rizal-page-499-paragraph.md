# Republic Act No. 3307

**An Act to Amend Item 34, for the Province of Rizal, Page 499, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3307**

**An Act to Amend Item 34, for the Province of Rizal, Page 499, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 34, for the Province of Rizal, page 499, paragraph g, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"34. Municipal Building, Morong ₱15,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
