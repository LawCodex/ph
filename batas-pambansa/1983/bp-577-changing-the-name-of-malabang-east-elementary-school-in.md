# Batas Pambansa Blg. 577

**An Act Changing the Name of Malabang East Elementary School in Barangay San Guillermo, Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao Del Sur, to San Guillermo Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 577**

An Act Changing the Name of Malabang East Elementary School in Barangay San Guillermo, Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao Del Sur, to San Guillermo Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Malabang East Elementary School in Barangay San Guillermo, Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao del Sur, shall hereafter be known and referred to as the San Guillermo Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
