# Batas Pambansa Blg. 196

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Tominobo Proper in Iligan City to Barangay Tomas L. Cabili.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 196**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Tominobo Proper in Iligan City to Barangay Tomas L. Cabili

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Tominobo Proper in Iligan City is hereby changed to Barangay Tomas L. Cabili.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 16, 1982
