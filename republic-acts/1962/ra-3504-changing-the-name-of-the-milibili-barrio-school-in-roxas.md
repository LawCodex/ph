# Republic Act No. 3504

**An Act Changing the Name of the Milibili Barrio School in Roxas City to Don Manuel Arnaldo Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3504**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Milibili Barrio School in Roxas City to Don Manuel Arnaldo Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Milibili Barrio School in Roxas City is hereby changed to Don Manuel Arnaldo Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
