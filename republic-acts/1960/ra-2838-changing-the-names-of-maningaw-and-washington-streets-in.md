# Republic Act No. 2838

**An Act Changing the Names of Maningaw and Washington Streets in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, to Marcelo H. Del Pilar Street and Magsaysay Street, Respectively**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2838**

**An Act Changing the Names of Maningaw and Washington Streets in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, to Marcelo H. Del Pilar Street and Magsaysay Street, Respectively**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of Maningaw Street and Washington Street in the Municipality of Bilar, Province of Bohol, are hereby changed to Marcelo H. del Pilar Street and Magsaysay Street, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
