# Republic Act No. 7615

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pantok-palangoy Elementary School in the Municipality of Binangonan, Province of Rizal, To Susana Madrigal Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7615, June 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PANTOK-PALANGOY ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BINANGONAN, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, TO SUSANA MADRIGAL MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pantok-Palangoy Elementary School in the Municipality of Binangonan, Province of Rizal, is hereby changed to Susana Madrigal Memorial Elementary School.
