# Republic Act No. 618

**An Act to Change the Name of Bulacan High School to Marcelo H. Del Pilar High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 618, May 15, 1951 ]

AN ACT TO CHANGE THE NAME OF BULACAN HIGH SCHOOL TO MARCELO H. DEL PILAR HIGH SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the distinguished services rendered by Marcelo H. del Pilar, Filipino hero and patriot and a distinguished son of the Province of Bulacan, the name of Bulacan High School is changed to Marcelo H. del Pilar High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, May 15, 1951.
