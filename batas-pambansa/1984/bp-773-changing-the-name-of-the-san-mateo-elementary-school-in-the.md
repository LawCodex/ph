# Batas Pambansa Blg. 773

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Mateo Elementary School in the Municipality of Aleosan, Province of North Cotabato, to Aleosan Central Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 773**

An Act Changing the Name of the San Mateo Elementary School in the Municipality of Aleosan, Province of North Cotabato, to Aleosan Central Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the San Mateo Elementary School in the Municipality of Aleosan, Province of North Cotabato, is changed to Aleosan Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
