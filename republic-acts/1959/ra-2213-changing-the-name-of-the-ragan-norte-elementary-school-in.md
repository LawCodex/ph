# Republic Act No. 2213

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ragan Norte Elementary School in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, to San Ramon Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2213**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ragan Norte Elementary School in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, to San Ramon Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Ragan Norte Elementary School in the Municipality of Magsaysay, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to San Ramon Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
