# Republic Act No. 1308

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Ilaya Labac, Matalisay, Pajo and Cacawan in Isla Verde, in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Antonio of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1308**

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Ilaya Labac, Matalisay, Pajo and Cacawan in Isla Verde, in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Antonio of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Ilaya, Labac, Matalisay, Pajo and Cacawan in the barrio of San Agapito in Isla Verde, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, are hereby converted to a barrio to be known as Barrio San Antonio of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
