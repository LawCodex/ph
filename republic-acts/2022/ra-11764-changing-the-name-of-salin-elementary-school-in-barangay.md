# Republic Act No. 11764

**An Act Changing the Name of Salin Elementary School in Barangay Sadsadan, Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province to Talban P. Bitayan Elementary School**

Eighteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-sixth day of July, two thousand twenty one.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11764, April 29, 2022 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SALIN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SADSADAN, MUNICIPALITY OF BAUKO, MOUNTAIN PROVINCE TO TALBAN P. BITAYAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Salin Elementary School located in Barangay Sadsadan, Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province is hereby changed to Talban P. Bitayan Elementary School.ℒαwρhi৷

## Section 2

This Act shall become effective fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

| (SGD.) LORD ALLAN JAY Q. VELASCO Speaker of the House of Representatives | (SGD.) VICENTE C. SOTTO III President of the Senate |
