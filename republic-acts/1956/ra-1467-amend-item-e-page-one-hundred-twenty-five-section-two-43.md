# Republic Act No. 1467

**An Act to Amend Item (e) Page One Hundred Twenty-Five, Section Two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1467**

**An Act to Amend Item (e) Page One Hundred Twenty-Five, Section Two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (e), page one hundred twenty-five, section two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "e-1. Candelaria Public Market premises, Asphalting and canalization | ₱5,000.00 |
|---|---|
| "e-2. Masalakot Barrio Road, Candelaria, Repair | 3,000.00 |
| "e-3. Sta. Catalina Barrio Road, Candelaria, Repair | 1,000.00." |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
