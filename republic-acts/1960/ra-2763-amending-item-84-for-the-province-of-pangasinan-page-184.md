# Republic Act No. 2763

**An Act Amending Item 84, for the Province of Pangasinan, Page 184, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2763**

**An Act Amending Item 84, for the Province of Pangasinan, Page 184, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 84, for the Province of Pangasinan, page 184, paragraph g, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is hereby amended to read as follows:

84\. Pogon-Sili Barrio School Building, Aguilar ₱5,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
