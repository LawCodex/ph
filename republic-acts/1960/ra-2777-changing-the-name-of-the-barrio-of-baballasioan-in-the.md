# Republic Act No. 2777

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baballasioan in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Barrio Pinsal**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2777**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baballasioan in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Barrio Pinsal**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Baballasioan in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Barrio Pinsal.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
