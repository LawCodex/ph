# Batas Pambansa Blg. 342

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol School of Arts and Trades in Tagbilaran City to Dr. Nemesio Agunod School of Arts and Trades.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 342**

An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol School of Arts and Trades in Tagbilaran City to Dr. Nemesio Agunod School of Arts and Trades

## Section 1

The name of the Bohol School of Arts and Trades in Tagbilaran City is changed to Dr. Nemesio Agunod School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 25, 1983
