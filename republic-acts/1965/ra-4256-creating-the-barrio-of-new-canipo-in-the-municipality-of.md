# Republic Act No. 4256

**An Act Creating the Barrio of New Canipo in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4256**

An Act Creating the Barrio of New Canipo in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Capalpalan in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan, is hereby converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as Barrio New Canipo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
