# Act No. 4191

**An Act Remitting the Bond of Twenty-Five Hundred Pesos Furnished By Jose Nazareno in the Case of "the People of the Philippine Islands Versus Jose Castro Y Obregon Et Al."**

[ Acts No. 4191, December 08, 1934 ]

AN ACT REMITTING THE BOND OF TWENTY-FIVE HUNDRED PESOS FURNISHED BY JOSE NAZARENO IN THE CASE OF "THE PEOPLE OF THE PHILIPPINE ISLANDS VERSUS JOSE CASTRO Y OBREGON ET AL."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The bond of twenty-five hundred pesos furnished by Jose Nazareno in favor of the Philippine Government for the provisional release of Jose Castro y Obregon in criminal case No. 33547 of the Court of First Instance of Manila, entitled "The People of the Philippine Islands versus Jose Castro y Obregon, et al.," which has been declared forfeited by said court, is hereby remitted after the expenses actually incurred by the Government in the execution of the said bail bond have been deducted.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 8, 1934.
