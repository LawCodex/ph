# Republic Act No. 11172

**An Act Changing the Name of Inoyonan High School in Barangay Inoyonan, Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur to Felipe P. Panton High School**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11172**

**An Act Changing the Name of Inoyonan High School in Barangay Inoyonan, Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur to Felipe P. Panton High School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

\- The name of Inoyonan High School in Barangay Inoyonan, Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur is hereby changed to Felipe P. Panton High School.

## Section 2

\- The main campus of the Felipe P. Panton High School shall continue to remain in its location in Barangay Inoyonan, Municipality of Bula, Province of Camarines Sur.

## Section 3

\- This act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in any newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
