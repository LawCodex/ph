# Batas Pambansa Blg. 695

**An Act Changing the Name of the Old Provincial Capitol Building of the Province of Antique to Justice Calixto O. Zaldivar Memorial Hall of Justice**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 695**

An Act Changing the Name of the Old Provincial Capitol Building of the Province of Antique to Justice Calixto O. Zaldivar Memorial Hall of Justice

## Section 1

The old provincial capitol building of the Province of Antique located in the Municipality of San Jose shall hereafter be known and referred to as the Justice Calixto O. Zaldivar Memorial Hall of Justice.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
