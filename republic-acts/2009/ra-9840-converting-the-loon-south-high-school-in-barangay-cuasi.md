# Republic Act No. 9840

**An Act Converting the Loon South High School in Barangay Cuasi, Municipality of Loon, Province of Bohol into a National High School to Be Known as Loon South National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9840**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE LOON SOUTH HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY CUASI, MUNICIPALITY OF LOON, PROVINCE OF BOHOL INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS LOON SOUTH NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Conversion into a National High School._ \- The Loon South High School in Barangay Cuasi, Municipality of Loon, Province of Bohol is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as **_Loon South National High School._**

## Section 2

_Transfer of Assets and Liabilities._ \- All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Loon South High School are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Loon South National High School.

## Section 3

_Appropriations._ \- The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of Loon South National High School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.lawphil

## Section 4

_Implementation._ \- The Secretary of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 5

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.
