# Republic Act No. 6208

**An Act Changing the Name of Bulikullol Elementary School in the Municipality of Siasi, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Ikbala Elementary School.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6208, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BULIKULLOL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SIASI, PROVINCE OF SULU, TO PANGLIMA IKBALA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate -and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Bulikullol Elementary School in the Municipality of Siasi, Province of Sulu, is changed to Panglima Ikbala Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
