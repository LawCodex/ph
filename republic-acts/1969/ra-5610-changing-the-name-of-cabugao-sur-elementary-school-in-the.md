# Republic Act No. 5610

**An Act Changing the Name of Cabugao Sur Elementary School in the Barrio of Cabugao Norte, Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Iloilo, to Cabugao Norte Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5610, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF CABUGAO SUR ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE BARRIO OF CABUGAO NORTE, MUNICIPALITY OF STA. BARBARA, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO CABUGAO NORTE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Cabugao Sur Elementary School in the. Barrio of Cabugao Norte, Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Cabugao Norte Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
