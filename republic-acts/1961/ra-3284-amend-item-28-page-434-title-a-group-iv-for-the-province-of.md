# Republic Act No. 3284

**An Act to Amend Item (28), Page 434, Title (A), Group IV for the Province of Iloilo, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3284**

**An Act to Amend Item (28), Page 434, Title (A), Group IV for the Province of Iloilo, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 28, page 434, title (A), Group IV for the Province of Iloilo, Section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is amended to read as follows:

"(20) So-ol barrio school building, Barotac Nuevo Construction, repair and improvement ₱3,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
