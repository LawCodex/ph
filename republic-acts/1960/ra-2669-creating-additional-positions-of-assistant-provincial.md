# Republic Act No. 2669

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscals in the Provinces of Cebu and Pangasinan, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2669**

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscals in the Provinces of Cebu and Pangasinan, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the provinces of Cebu and Pangasinan, is further amended to read as follows:

"Cebu, thirteen assistant provincial fiscals;

"Pangasinan, eighteen assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1960.
