# Republic Act No. 6704

**An Act Renaming the Banaran Elementary School in Barangay Banaran, Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, as the Datu Ismael Yusop Memorial Central Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6704, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE BANARAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BANARAN, MUNICIPALITY OF SAPA-SAPA, PROVINCE OF TAWI-TAWI, AS THE DATU ISMAEL YUSOP MEMORIAL CENTRAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Banaran Elementary School in Barangay Banaran, Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby renamed as the Datu Ismael Yusop Memorial Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
