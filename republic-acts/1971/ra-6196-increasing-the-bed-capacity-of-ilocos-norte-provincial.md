# Republic Act No. 6196

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of Ilocos Norte Provincial Hospital and Appropriating Funds Therefor.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6196, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF ILOCOS NORTE PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of llocos Norte Provincial Hospital is hereby increased from fifty to one hundred fifty beds.

## Section 2

Such sums as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance and other facilities shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
