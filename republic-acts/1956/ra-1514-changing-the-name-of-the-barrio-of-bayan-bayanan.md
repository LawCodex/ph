# Republic Act No. 1514

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bayan-Bayanan, Municipality of Marikina, Province of Rizal, to Concepcion**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1514**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bayan-Bayanan, Municipality of Marikina, Province of Rizal, to Concepcion**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Bayan-Bayanan, Municipality of Marikina, Province of Rizal, is changed to Concepcion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
