# Batas Pambansa Blg. 199

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu National Regional Agricultural School in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Tawi-Tawi National Regional Agricultural School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 199**

An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu National Regional Agricultural School in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Tawi-Tawi National Regional Agricultural School

## Section 1

The name of the Sulu National Regional Agricultural School in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Tawi-Tawi National Regional Agricultural School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 16, 1982
