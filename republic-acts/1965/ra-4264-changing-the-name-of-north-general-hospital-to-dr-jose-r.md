# Republic Act No. 4264

**An Act Changing the Name of North General Hospital to Dr. Jose R. Reyes Memorial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4264**

An Act Changing the Name of North General Hospital to Dr. Jose R. Reyes Memorial Hospital

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The North General Hospital shall hereafter be known as Dr. Jose R. Reyes Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
