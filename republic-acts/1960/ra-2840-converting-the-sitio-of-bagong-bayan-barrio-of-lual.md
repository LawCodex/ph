# Republic Act No. 2840

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagong Bayan, Barrio of Lual, Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, into a Distinct and Independent Barrio to be Known as the District of Bagong Bayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2840**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bagong Bayan, Barrio of Lual, Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, into a Distinct and Independent Barrio to be Known as the District of Bagong Bayan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bagong Bayan is hereby separated from the barrio of Lual in the Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, and is converted into a distinct and independent barrio to be known as the District of Bagong Bayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
