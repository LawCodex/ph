**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3954**

**An Act Changing the Name of Mambalite Primary School in Barrio Mambalite, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, to Guito Pimentel Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Mambalite Primary School in the barrio of Mambalite, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Guito Pimentel Community School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
