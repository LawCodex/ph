# Republic Act No. 1423

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sabang Elementary School in the Barrio of Sabang, City of Lipa, to G.B. Lontok Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1423**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sabang Elementary School in the Barrio of Sabang, City of Lipa, to G.B. Lontok Memorial Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sabang Elementary School in the barrio of Sabang, City of Lipa, is changed to G.B. Lontok Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1956.
