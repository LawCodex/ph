# Republic Act No. 4893

**An Act Changing the Name of Itawes Agricultural College in the Municipality of Piat, Province of Cagayan, to the Northern Luzon State College of Agriculture**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4893**

An Act Changing the Name of Itawes Agricultural College in the Municipality of Piat, Province of Cagayan, to the Northern Luzon State College of Agriculture

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Itawes Agricultural College in the Municipality of Piat, Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to the Northern Luzon State College of Agriculture.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
