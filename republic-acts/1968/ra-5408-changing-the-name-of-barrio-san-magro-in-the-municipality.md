# Republic Act No. 5408

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Magro in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Santiago**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5408**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Magro in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Santiago

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio San Magro in the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Santiago.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
