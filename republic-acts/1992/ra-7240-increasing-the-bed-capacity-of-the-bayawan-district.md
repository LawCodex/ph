# Republic Act No. 7240

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Bayawan District Hospital in the Municipality of Bayawan, Province of Negros Oriental, From Fifty (50) to Seventy-five (75) Beds and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7240, March 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE BAYAWAN DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BAYAWAN, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Bayawan District Hospital in the Municipality of Bayawan, Province of Negros Oriental, is hereby increased from fifty (50) to seventy-five (75) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.
