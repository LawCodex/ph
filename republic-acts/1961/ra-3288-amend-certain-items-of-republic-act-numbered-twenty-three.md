# Republic Act No. 3288

**An Act to Amend Certain Items of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3288**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (cu) on page 255, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred one is amended to read as follows:

"(cu) Pook-Biasong Road, Talisay, Cebu ₱5,000.00."

## Section 2

Item (cv) on page 255, Section one of the same Act is amended to read as follows:

"(cv) Lawaan-Maghaway Road, Talisay, Cebu ₱5,000.00"

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
