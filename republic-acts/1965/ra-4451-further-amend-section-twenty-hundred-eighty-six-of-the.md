# Republic Act No. 4451

**An Act to Further Amend Section Twenty Hundred Eighty-Six of the Administrative Code by Providing Quarters for the Provincial Vice-Governor, or Allowing the Value Therefor in Addition to His Salary**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4451**

An Act to Further Amend Section Twenty Hundred Eighty-Six of the Administrative Code by Providing Quarters for the Provincial Vice-Governor, or Allowing the Value Therefor in Addition to His Salary

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section twenty hundred eighty-six of the Administrative Code, as amended by Republic Act Numbered Three thousand two hundred sixty-six, is further amended to read as follows:

"Sec. 2086. Quarters of Provincial Governors and Vice-Governors.— The provincial board may, in its discretion provide quarters for the provincial governor and vice-governor, or allow the value thereof, in addition to their salary."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
