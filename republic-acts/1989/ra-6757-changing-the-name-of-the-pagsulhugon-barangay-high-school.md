# Republic Act No. 6757

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pagsulhugon Barangay High School in Babatngon, Leyte, to Pagsulhugon National High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6757 August 11, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PAGSULHUGON BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN BABATNGON, LEYTE, TO PAGSULHUGON NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pagsulhugon Barangay High School in Babatngon, Leyte, is hereby changed to Pagsulhugon National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **August 11, 1989.**
