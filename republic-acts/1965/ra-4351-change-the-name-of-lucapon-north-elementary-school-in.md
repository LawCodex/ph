# Republic Act No. 4351

**An Act to Change the Name of Lucapon North Elementary School in Barrio Lucapon, Municipality of Santa Cruz, Province of Zambales, to Don Brigido Miraflor Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4351**

An Act to Change the Name of Lucapon North Elementary School in Barrio Lucapon, Municipality of Santa Cruz, Province of Zambales, to Don Brigido Miraflor Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lucapon North Elementary School in Barrio Lucapon, Municipality of Santa Cruz, Province of Zambales, is hereby changed to Don Brigido Miraflor Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
