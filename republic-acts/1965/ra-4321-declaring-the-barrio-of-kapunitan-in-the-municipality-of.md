# Republic Act No. 4321

**An Act Declaring the Barrio of Kapunitan in the Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan, a Duly Constituted Barrio Thereof**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4321**

An Act Declaring the Barrio of Kapunitan in the Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan, a Duly Constituted Barrio Thereof

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Kapunitan in the Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan, is hereby declared a duly constituted barrio of said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
