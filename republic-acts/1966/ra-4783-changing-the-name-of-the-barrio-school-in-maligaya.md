# Republic Act No. 4783

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio School in Maligaya, Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac, from Buenavista-Tete-Ungot Primary School to Maligaya Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4783**

An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio School in Maligaya, Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac, from Buenavista-Tete-Ungot Primary School to Maligaya Primary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio school in Maligaya, Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac, is hereby changed from Buenavista-Tete-Ungot Primary School to Maligaya Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
