# Act No. 2797

**An Act to Amend Section Two thousand One Hundred and Six of the Administrative Code, By Adding Subsection (F) At the End thereof.**

[ Act No. 2797, February 24, 1919 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION TWO THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED AND SIX OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, BY ADDING SUBSECTION (F) AT THE END THEREOF.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Section two thousand one hundred and six of Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and eleven, entitled the Administrative Code, is hereby amended by adding, at the end thereof, subsection (f), to read as follows:

"(f) To permit, upon favorable recommendation by the Secretary of Commerce and Communications, and subject to such conditions as may properly protect the public interests, the construction and maintenance, for private use, of railways, conduits, and telephone lines across public thoroughfares, streets, roads, and other public property in the province: _Provided,_ That such construction and private use shall not prevent or obstruct the public use of such thoroughfares, streets, roads, or other public property, and that the permit granted shall at all times be subject to revocation by the Secretary of the Interior, if in the judgment of that official, the public interest requires it."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, February 24, 1919.
