# Republic Act No. 6181

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Butong in the Municipality of Cabuyao, Province of Laguna.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6181, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING THE BARRIO OF BUTONG IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CABUYAO, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Butong in the Municipality of Cabuyao, Province of Laguna, is hereby converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the Barrio of Butong.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
