# Republic Act No. 6254

**An Act Amending Item Nine, Paragraph A, Title H, Section One, of Republic Act Numbered Forty-Eight Hundred Fifty-Four, By Transferring the Appropriation of the Butuan City (Magallanes-Lower Bansa) Airport to the Bancasi Airport, Both in Butuan City.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6254, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ITEM NINE, PARAGRAPH A, TITLE H, SECTION ONE, OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED FORTY-EIGHT HUNDRED FIFTY-FOUR, BY TRANSFERRING THE APPROPRIATION OF THE BUTUAN CITY (MAGALLANES-LOWER BANSA) AIRPORT TO THE BANCASI AIRPORT, BOTH IN BUTUAN CITY.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Item nine, under Secondary Airports, paragraph a, Title H, of Section one, Republic Act Numbered Forty-eight hundred fifty-four, is amended to read as follows:

"9. Bancasi Airport ---------------------P1,000,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
