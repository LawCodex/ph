# Republic Act No. 6667

**An Acting Renaming the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School, in the Municipality of Janiuay, Province of Iloilo, as the Don Tibursio A. Lutero National Comprehensive High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6667, June 22, 1988 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE JANIUAY NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL, IN MUNICIPALITY OF JANIUAY, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, AS THE DON TIBURSIO A. LUTERO NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Janiuay National Comprehensive High School in Municipality of Janiuay, Province of Iloilo is hereby changed to Don Tibursio A. Lutero National Comprehensive High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
