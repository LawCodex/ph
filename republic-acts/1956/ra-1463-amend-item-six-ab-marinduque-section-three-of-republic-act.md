# Republic Act No. 1463

**An Act to Amend Item Six, ab. Marinduque, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1463**

**An Act to Amend Item Six, ab. Marinduque, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item six, ab. Marinduque, section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "6. Lamesa Barrio School, Mogpog—Construction | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|
| "6-a. Mogpog Central School—Repair and Improvement | ₱5,000.00 |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956
