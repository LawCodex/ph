# Republic Act No. 2557

**An Act Changing the Name of the Patao Primary School in the Barrio of Bawod, Municipality of Bantayan, Province of Cebu, to Bawod Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2557**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Patao Primary School in the Barrio of Bawod, Municipality of Bantayan, Province of Cebu, to Bawod Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Patao Primary School in the barrio of Bawod, Municipality of Bantayan, Province of Cebu, is changed to Bawod Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
