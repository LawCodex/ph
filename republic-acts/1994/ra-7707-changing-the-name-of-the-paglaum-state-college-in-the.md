# Republic Act No. 7707

**An Act Changing the Name of the Paglaum State College in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Negros Occidental, to Carlos C. Hilado Memorial State College**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Phillipines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7707, May 05, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PAGLAUM STATE COLLEGE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALISAY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, TO CARLOS C. HILADO MEMORIAL STATE COLLEGE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Paglaum State College in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Carlos C. Hilado Memorial State College.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

Lapsed into law on May 5, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
