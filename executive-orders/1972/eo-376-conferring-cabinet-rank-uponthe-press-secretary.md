# Executive Order No. 376

**Conferring Cabinet Rank Uponthe Press Secretary**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 376 February 18, 1972**

CONFERRING CABINET RANK UPON THE PRESS SECRETARY

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby elevate the Press Secretary to the rank of a member of the Cabinet, with rights, honors and privileges pertaining to that position.

Executive Order No. 208, series of 1970, is hereby modified accordingly.

Done in the City of Manila, this 18th day of February, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-two.
