# Republic Act No. 8844

**An Act Converting the Dr. Arcadio Santos High School Into Dr. Arcadio Santos National High School in the City of Paraññaque, Metro Manila and Providing Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8844, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE DR. ARCADIO SANTOS HIGH SCHOOL INTO DR. ARCADIO SANTOS NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF PARAñÑAQUE, METRO MANILA AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Dr. Arcadio Santos High School in the City of Parañaque, Metro Manila is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as the Dr. Arcadio Santos National High School.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
