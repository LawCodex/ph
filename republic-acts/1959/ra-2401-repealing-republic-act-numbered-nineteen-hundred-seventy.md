# Republic Act No. 2401

**An Act Repealing Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred Seventy-Five**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2401**

**An Act Repealing Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred Seventy-Five**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred seventy-five, entitle "An Act to grant to Amador Dayola and R. Mulet (partners) a franchise for an electric light, heat and power system in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao," is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
