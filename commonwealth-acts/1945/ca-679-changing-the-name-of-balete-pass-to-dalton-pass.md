# Commonwealth Act No. 679

**An Act Changing the Name of Balete Pass to Dalton Pass**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 679, September 12, 1945 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BALETE PASS TO DALTON PASS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In memory of Brigadier General James L. Dalton who was killed in the battle for the capture of Balete Pass, Nueva Vizcaya, the name "Balete Pass" is changed to "Dalton Pass."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, September 12, 1945.
