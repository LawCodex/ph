# Republic Act No. 12098

**An Act Declaring April 4 of Every Year A Special Nonworking Holiday in the City of Antipolo, Province of Rizal, in Commemoration of Its Cityhood Anniversary ra2024/pdra_12098_2024.pdf**

Nineteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12098, December 13, 2024 ]

AN ACT DECLARING APRIL 4 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF ANTIPOLO, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS CITYHOOD ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

April 4 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the City of Antipolo, Province of Rizal, in commemoration of its cityhood anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.ℒαwρhi৷
