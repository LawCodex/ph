# Republic Act No. 7599

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Cuyo District Hospital in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, From Fifty (50) To Seventy-five (75) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefore**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7599, June 03, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE CUYO DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CUYO, PROVINCE OF PALAWAN, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFORE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Cuto District Hospital in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, is hereby increased from fifty 50) to seventy-five (75) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following is enactment into law thereafter.
