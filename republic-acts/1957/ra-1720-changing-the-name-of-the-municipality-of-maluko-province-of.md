# Republic Act No. 1720

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Maluko, Province of Bukidnon, to Municipality of Manolo Fortich**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1720**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Maluko, Province of Bukidnon, to Municipality of Manolo Fortich**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Maluko, Province of Bukidnon, is changed to Municipality of Manolo Fortich.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
