# Republic Act No. 9779

**An Act Changing the Name of Lamo National High School in Barangay Lamo, Municipality of Dupax Del Norte, Province of Nueva Vizcaya to Dupax Del Norte National High School.**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9779**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF LAMO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY LAMO, MUNICIPALITY OF DUPAXDELNORTE, PROVINCE OF NUEVA VIZCAYA TO DUPAX DEL NORTE NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of a National High School._ \- The name of Lama National High School in Barangay Lama, Municipality of Dupax del Norte, Province of Nueva Vizcaya is hereby changed to **_Dupax del Norte National High School._**

## Section 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ \- The main campus of the Dupax del Norte National High School shall continue to remain in its present location in Barangay Lama, Municipality of Dupax del Norte, Province of Nueva Vizcaya.

## Section 3

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
