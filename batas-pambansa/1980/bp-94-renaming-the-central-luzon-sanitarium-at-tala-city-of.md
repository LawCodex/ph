# Batas Pambansa Blg. 94

**An Act Renaming the Central Luzon Sanitarium At Tala, City of Caloocan, as Dr. Jose N. Rodriguez Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 94**

An Act Renaming the Central Luzon Sanitarium at Tala, City of Caloocan, as Dr. Jose N. Rodriguez Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

In due recognition of the outstanding services of the late Dr. Jose N. Rodriguez in the improvement of the lot of leprosy patients and as a fitting memorial to all the good things which he accomplished during his lifetime for the welfare and benefit of the said patients, the Central Luzon Sanitarium at Tala, City of Caloocan, of which he was the first Director, is hereby renamed Dr. Jose N. Rodriguez Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 24, 1980
