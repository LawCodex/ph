# Republic Act No. 4896

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Saravia in the Province of Negros Occidental to Enrique B. Magalona in Honor of the Late Senator Enrique B. Magalona**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4896**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Saravia in the Province of Negros Occidental to Enrique B. Magalona in Honor of the Late Senator Enrique B. Magalona

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Saravia in the Province of Negros Occidental is changed to Enrique B. Magalona in honor of the late Senator Enrique B. Magalona.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
