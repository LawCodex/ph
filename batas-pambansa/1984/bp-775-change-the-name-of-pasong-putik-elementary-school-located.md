# Batas Pambansa Blg. 775

**An Act to Change the Name of Pasong Putik Elementary School Located At st. Dominic Subdivision, Barangay Kaligayahan, Quezon City, Metropolitan Manila, to Delfin Geraldez Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 775**

An Act Changing the Name of Pasong Putik Elementary School Located at St. Dominic Subdivision, Barangay Kaligayahan, Quezon City, Metropolitan Manila, to Delfin Geraldez Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

Pasong Putik Elementary School located at St. Dominic Subdivision, Barangay Kaligayahan, Quezon City, Metropolitan Manila, is hereby named Delfin Geraldez Memorial Elementary School in honor of the late Delfin Geraldez, a philanthropist who donated the lot where the school is now located.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
