# Republic Act No. 9488

**An Act Renaming the Sagrada – Lidong Road Extending From the Province of Camarines Sur to the Province of Albay as Diosdado Macapagal Highway**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9488 June 24, 2007**

AN ACT RENAMING THE SAGRADA-LIDONG ROAD EXTENDING FROM THE PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR TO THE PROVINCE OF ALBAY AS DIOSDADO MACAPAGAL HIGHWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Sagrada - Lidong Road extending from Sagrada, Buhi in the Province of Camarines Sur to Lidong, Polangui in the Province of Albay is hereby renamed as Diosdado Macapagal Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
