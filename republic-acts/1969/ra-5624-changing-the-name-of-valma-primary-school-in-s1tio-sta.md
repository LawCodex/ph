# Republic Act No. 5624

**An Act Changing the Name of Valma Primary School in S1tio Sta. Maria, Barrio Ngan, Municipality of Compostela, Province of Davao Del Norte, to Tranquilino H. Valderrama Primary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5624, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF VALMA PRIMARY SCHOOL IN S1TIO STA. MARIA, BARRIO NGAN, MUNICIPALITY OF COMPOSTELA, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL NORTE, TO TRANQUILINO H. VALDERRAMA PRIMARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Valma Primary School in Sitio Sta. Maria, Barrio Ngan, Municipality of Compostela, Province of Davao del Norte, is hereby changed to Tranquilino H. Valderrama Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
