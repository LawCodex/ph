# Batas Pambansa Blg. 715

**An Act Changing the Name of the Kayapa Field Hospital in the Municipality of Kayapa, Province of Nueva VIzcaya, to Lt. Tidang Memorial Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 715**

An Act Changing the Name of the Kayapa Field Hospital in the Municipality of Kayapa, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, to Lt. Tidang Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Kayapa Field Hospital in the Municipality of Kayapa, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, is hereby change to Lt. Tidang Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
