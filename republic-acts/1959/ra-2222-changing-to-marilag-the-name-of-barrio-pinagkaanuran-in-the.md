# Republic Act No. 2222

**An Act Changing to Marilag the Name of Barrio Pinagkaanuran in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2222**

**An Act Changing to Marilag the Name of Barrio Pinagkaanuran in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Pinagkaanuran in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Marilag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
