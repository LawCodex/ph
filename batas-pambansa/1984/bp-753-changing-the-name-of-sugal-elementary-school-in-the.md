# Batas Pambansa Blg. 753

**An Act Changing the Name of Sugal Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Manuel Medel, Sr. Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 753**

An Act Changing the Name of Sugal Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Manuel Medel, Sr. Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Sugal Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Manuel Medel, Sr. Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
