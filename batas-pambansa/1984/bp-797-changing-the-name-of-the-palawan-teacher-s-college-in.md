# Batas Pambansa Blg. 797

**An Act Changing the Name of the Palawan Teacher'S College in Puerto Princesa City to the Palawan state College and Authorizing the Same to Continue Offering Its Present Collegiate Courses**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 797**

An Act Changing the Name of the Palawan Teacher’s College in Puerto Princesa City to the Palawan State College and Authorizing the Same to Continue Offering its Present Collegiate Courses

## Section 1

The name of the Palawan Teacher’s College in Puerto Princesa City to the Palawan, is changed to Palawan State College.

## Section 2

The Palawan State College is authorized to continue offering its present collegiate courses and other courses in arts, sciences and technology.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
