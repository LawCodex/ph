# Republic Act No. 3334

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, from the Barrio of Mucas to the Barrio of Salug**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3334**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, from the Barrio of Mucas to the Barrio of Salug**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is transferred from the barrio of Mucas to the barrio of Salug.1avvphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
