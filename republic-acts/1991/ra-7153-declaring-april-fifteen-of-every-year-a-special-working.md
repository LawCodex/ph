# Republic Act No. 7153

**An Act Declaring April Fifteen of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Municipality of Talisay, Negros Occidental, in Honor of the Late General Anecito Lacson**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 7153 August 30, 1991**

AN ACT DECLARING APRIL FIFTEEN OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALISAY, NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, IN HONOR OF THE LATE GENERAL ANECITO LACSON

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

April Fifteen of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Negros Occidental, in honor of the late General Anecito Lacson.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **August 30, 1991**.
