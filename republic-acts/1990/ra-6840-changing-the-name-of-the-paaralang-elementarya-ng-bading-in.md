# Republic Act No. 6840

**An Act Changing the Name of the Paaralang Elementarya ng Bading in Butuan City to Paaralang Elementarya ng Obrero**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6840

An Act Changing the Name of the Paaralang Elementarya ng Bading in Butuan City to Paaralang Elementarya ng Obrero

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Paaralang Elementarya ng Bading in Butuan City is hereby changed to Paaralang Elementarya ng Obrero.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
