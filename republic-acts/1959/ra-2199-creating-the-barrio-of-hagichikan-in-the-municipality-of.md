# Republic Act No. 2199

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Hagichikan in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2199**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Hagichikan in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Hagichikan, Bagakay, Hubasan, Cambonot and Gisok in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Hagichikan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
