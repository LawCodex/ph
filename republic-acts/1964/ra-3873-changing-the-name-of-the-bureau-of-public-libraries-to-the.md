# Republic Act No. 3873

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bureau of Public Libraries to the National Library**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3873**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bureau of Public Libraries to the National Library**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bureau of Public Libraries is hereby changed to that of The National Library.

## Section 2

All references to the Bureau of Public Libraries in laws, executive orders, rules and regulations shall henceforth be understood to refer to The National Library.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
