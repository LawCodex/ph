# Republic Act No. 5595

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Poro, Province of Cebu**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5595, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAMES OF CERTAIN BARRIOS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PORO, PROVINCE OF CEBU.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Hereafter, Barrio Tiguis shall Toe known as Barrio Martinez; Barrio Cagcagan as Barrio Garciano; and Barrio Cansabusab as Barrio Sosmeña.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
