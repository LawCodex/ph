# Republic Act No. 8354

**An Act Establishing A National High School in Barangay Lipit, Municipality of Cabugao, Province of Ilocos Sur, To Be Known As Lipit National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8354, October 09, 1997 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY LIPIT, MUNICIPALITY OF CABUGAO, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS LIPIT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national high school in Barangay Lipit, Municipality of Cabugao, Province of Ilocos Sur, to be known as Lipit National High School.1aшphi1

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue the rules and regulations to implement this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, Lapsed into law on October 9, 1997 without the signature of the President, in accordance with Article VI, Section 27 (1) of the Constitution.
