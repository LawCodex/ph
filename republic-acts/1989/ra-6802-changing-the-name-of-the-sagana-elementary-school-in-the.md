# Republic Act No. 6802

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sagana Elementary School in the Municipality of Bongabong, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Tomas S. Villanueva Memorial School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6802 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAGANA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BONGABONG, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO TOMAS S. VILLANUEVA MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sagana Elementary School in the Municipality of Bongabong, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Tomas S. Villanueva Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
