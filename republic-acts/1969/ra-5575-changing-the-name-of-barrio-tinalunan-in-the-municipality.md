# Republic Act No. 5575

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tinalunan in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Barrio Gaudencio Antonino**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5575, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO TINALUNAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GLORIA, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO BARRIO GAUDENCIO ANTONINO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Tinalunan in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Barrio Gaudencio Antonino.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
