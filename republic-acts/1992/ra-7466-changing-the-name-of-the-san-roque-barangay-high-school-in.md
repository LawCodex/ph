# Republic Act No. 7466

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Roque Barangay High School in Tanauan, Leyte, To Tanauan High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7466, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN ROQUE BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN TANAUAN, LEYTE, TO TANAUAN HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Roque Barangay High School in Tanauan, Leyte, is hereby changed to Tanauan High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
