# Republic Act No. 4287

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Tagumpay in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4287**

An Act Creating the Barrio of Tagumpay in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Buayan in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, is hereby converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Tagumpay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
