# Republic Act No. 10052

**An Act Renaming Real Street in Dumaguete City as Mayor Ramon T. Pastor Sr. Street**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10052**

AN ACT RENAMING REAL STREET IN DUMAGUETE CITY AS MAYOR RAMON T. PASTOR SR. STREET

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Real Street in Dumaguete City shall hereinafter be known as Mayor Ramon T. Pastor Sr. Street.

## Section 2

The Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on January 20, 2010 and May 25,2009, respectively.
