# Batas Pambansa Blg. 770

**An Act Changing the Name of the Laoac Central School in the Municipality of Laoac, Province of Pangasinan, to Don Rufino Tabayoyong Central School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 770**

An Act Changing the Name of the Laoac Central School in the Municipality of Laoac, Province of Pangasinan, to Don Rufino Tabayoyong Central School

## Section 1

The name of the Laoac Central School in the Municipality of Laoac, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Don Rufino Tabayoyong Central School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
