# Batas Pambansa Blg. 796

**An Act Changing the Name of the Margarita Roxas Elementary School in sta. Ana, Manila, to Margarita Roxas De Ayala Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 796**

An Act Changing the Name of the Margarita Roxas Elementary School in Sta. Ana, Manila, to Margarita Roxas De Ayala Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Margarita Roxas Elementary School in Sta. Ana, Manila, is hereby changed to the Margarita Roxas de Ayala Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
