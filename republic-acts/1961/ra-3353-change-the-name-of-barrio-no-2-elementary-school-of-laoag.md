# Republic Act No. 3353

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio No. 2 Elementary School of Laoag, Ilocos Norte, to Agripino P. Santos Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3353**

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio No. 2 Elementary School of Laoag, Ilocos Norte, to Agripino P. Santos Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name "Barrio No. 2. Elementary School" in the Municipality of Laoag, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Agripino P. Santos Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
