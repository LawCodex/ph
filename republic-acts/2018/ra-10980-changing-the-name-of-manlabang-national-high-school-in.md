# Republic Act No. 10980

**An Act Changing the Name of Manlabang National High School in Barangay Manlabang, Municipality of Caibiran, Province of Biliran to Caibiran National High School**

Seventeenth Congress  
Second Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand seventeen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10980**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF MANLABANG NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MANLABANG, MUNICIPALITY OF CAIBIRAN, PROVINCE OF BILIRAN, TO CAIBIRAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the_ _Philippines in Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of Manlabang National High School in Barangay Manlabang, Municipality of Caibiran, Province of Biliran, is hereby changed to Caibiran National High School.1âwphi1

## Section 2

The main campus of the Caibiran National High School shall continue to remain in its present location in Barangay Manlabang, Municipality of Caibiran, Province of Biliran.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.
