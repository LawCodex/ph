# Republic Act No. 4923

**An Act Changing the Name of a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, to Barrio San Isidro**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4923**

An Act Changing the Name of a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, to Barrio San Isidro

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Tete in the Municipality of Aroroy, Province of Masbate, is charged to Barrio San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
