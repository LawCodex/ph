# Republic Act No. 6219

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bibihan School in Barrio Puting-Tubig, Municipality of Calapan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Pedro C. Tolentino Memorial School.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6219, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BIBIHAN SCHOOL IN BARRIO PUTING-TUBIG, MUNICIPALITY OF CALAPAN, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO PEDRO C. TOLENTINO MEMORIAL SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bibihan School, in Barrio Puting-Tubig, Municipality of Calapan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Pedro C. Tolentino Memorial School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
