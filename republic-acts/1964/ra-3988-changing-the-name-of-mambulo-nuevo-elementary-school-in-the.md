# Republic Act No. 3988

**An Act Changing the Name of Mambulo Nuevo Elementary School in the Municipality of Libmanan, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Antonio Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3988**

**An Act Changing the Name of Mambulo Nuevo Elementary School in the Municipality of Libmanan, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Antonio Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Mambulo Nuevo Elementary School in the Municipality of Libmanan, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby changed to San Antonio Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
