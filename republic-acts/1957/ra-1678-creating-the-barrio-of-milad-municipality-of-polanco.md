# Republic Act No. 1678

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Milad, Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1678**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Milad, Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Dilawa, Desin, Lambog, Gumatob, Boboringan, Tubongon, Debolok, Tiaman, Guintom, Lower Disoy, and New Tipan, Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are constituted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Milad of said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
