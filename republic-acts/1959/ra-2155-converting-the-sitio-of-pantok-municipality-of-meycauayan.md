# Republic Act No. 2155

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pantok, Municipality of Meycauayan, Province of Bulacan, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Pantok**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2155**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIO OF PANTOK, MUNICIPALITY OF MEYCAUAYAN, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, INTO A BARRIO TO BE KNOWN AS THE BARRIO OF PANTOK.

## Section 1

The sitio of Pantok, Municipality of Meycauayan, Province of Bulacan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Pantok.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without executive approval, April 24, 1959.
