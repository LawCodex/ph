# Batas Pambansa Blg. 679

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Diongan in the Municipality of Siayan, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, to Barangay Datu Tangkilan Andus**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 679**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Diongan in the Municipality of Siayan, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, to Barangay Datu Tangkilan Andus

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Diongan in the Municipality of Siayan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is changed to Barangay Datu Tangkilan Andus.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
