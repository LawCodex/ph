# Republic Act No. 2876

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Looc Norte in the Municipality of Asturias, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2876**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Looc Norte in the Municipality of Asturias, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Looc Norte in the Municipality of Asturias, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of the said municipality, to be known as barrio of Looc Norte.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
