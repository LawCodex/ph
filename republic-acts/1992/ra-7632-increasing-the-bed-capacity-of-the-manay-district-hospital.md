# Republic Act No. 7632

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Manay District Hospital in the Municipality of Manay, Province of Davao Oriental, From Ten (10) To Twenty-five (25) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7632, July 17, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE MANAY DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MANAY, PROVINCE OF DAVAO ORIENTAL, FROM TEN (10) TO TWENTY-FIVE (25) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Manay District Hospital in the Municipality of Manay, Province of Davao Oriental, is hereby increased from ten (10) to twenty-five (25) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.
