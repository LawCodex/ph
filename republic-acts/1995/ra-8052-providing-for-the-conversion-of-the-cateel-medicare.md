# Republic Act No. 8052

**An Act Providing for the Conversion of the Cateel Medicare Hospital in the Municipality of Cateel, Province of Davao Oriental, Into a District Hospital to Be Known as the Cateel District Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8052, June 15, 1995 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE CONVERSION OF THE CATEEL MEDICARE HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CATEEL, PROVINCE OF DAVAO ORIENTAL, INTO A DISTRICT HOSPITAL TO BE KNOWN AS THE CATEEL DISTRICT HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Cateel Medicare Hospital in the Municipality of Cateel, Province of Davao Oriental, is hereby converted into a district hospital to be known as the Cateel District Hospital.

## Section 2

The Department of Health, in consultation with the local government officials concerned, shall promulgate the rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act.1aшphi1

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law; thereafter, funds necessary for the maintenance and operation of the hospital shall be provided in accordance with the provisions of the Local Government Code or the applicable law at the time.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on June 15, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
