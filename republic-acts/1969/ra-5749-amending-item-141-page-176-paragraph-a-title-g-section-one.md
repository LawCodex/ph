# Republic Act No. 5749

**An Act Amending Item 141, Page 176, Paragraph "A," Title G, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Forty-Eight Hundred Fifty-Four (Re Public Works Act)**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5749, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ITEM 141, PAGE 176, PARAGRAPH "A," TITLE G, SECTION ONE OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED FORTY-EIGHT HUNDRED FIFTY-FOUR (RE PUBLIC WORKS ACT).

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Item 141, page 176, paragraph "a," _Title_ G, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Forty-eight hundred fifty-four, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"141. Barucbuc Communal Irrigation Project, Mallig, Isabela - ₱25,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 21, 1969.
