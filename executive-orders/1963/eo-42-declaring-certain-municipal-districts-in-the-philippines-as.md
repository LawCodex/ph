# Executive Order No. 42

**Declaring Certain Municipal Districts in the Philippines as Municipalities**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 42, June 25, 1963 ]

DECLARING CERTAIN MUNICIPAL DISTRICTS IN THE PHILIPPINES AS MUNICIPALITIES

By operation of the provisions of section two of Republic Act Numbered fifteen hundred and fifteen, the municipal districts hereunder listed having realized each an average annual revenues of more than four thousand pesos for the last four consecutive fiscal years ending June 30, 1962, as certified by the General Auditing Office, are hereby declared and considered fifth class (equivalent to seventh class pursuant to Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and sixty eight) municipalities beginning July 1, 1962:
