# Republic Act No. 2146

**An Act to Change the Name of the Kiañgan Hospital to Ifugao Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2146**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Kiañgan Hospital to Ifugao Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Kiañgan Hospital in the Municipality of Ifugao, Mountain Province, is hereby changed to Ifugao Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
