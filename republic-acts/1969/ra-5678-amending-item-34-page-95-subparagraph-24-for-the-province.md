# Republic Act No. 5678

**An Act Amending Item 34, Page 95, Subparagraph 24, for the Province of Isabela, Paragraph "A", Title B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Thirty-One Hundred One (Re Public Works Projects for the Province of Isabela)**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5678, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ITEM 34, PAGE 95, SUBPARAGRAPH 24, FOR THE PROVINCE OF ISABELA, PARAGRAPH "A", TITLE B, SECTION ONE OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-ONE HUNDRED ONE (RE PUBLIC WORKS PROJECTS FOR THE PROVINCE OF ISABELA).

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Item 34, page 95, subparagraph 24, for the Province of Isabela, paragraph "a", _Title_ B, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred one is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "34. Masigun Bridge, (Masigun Creek), Burgos cons­truction | 40,000.00" | |
|---|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
