# Batas Pambansa Blg. 676

**An Act Changing the Name of Lapulapu street in Danao City to Paulo G. Almendras street**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 676**

An Act Changing the Name of Lapulapu Street in Danao City to Paulo G. Almendras Street

## Section 1

The street in Danao City, in the island of Cebu, Philippines, now known as Lapulapu Street, is hereby renamed, and shall be known as Paulo G. Almendras Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
