# Batas Pambansa Blg. 761

**An Act Changing the Name of Kulukipus Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Rolando Pardeñas Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 761**

An Act Changing the Name of Kulukipus Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Rolando Pardenas Primary School

## Section 1

The name of Kulukipus Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Rolando Pardenas Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
