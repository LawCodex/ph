# Act No. 2522

**An Act Making Applicable to the Department of Mindanao and Sulu Act Numbered Twenty-Four Hundred and Seventy-Two.**

[ Act No. 2522, July 19, 1915 ]

AN ACT MAKING APPLICABLE TO THE DEPARTMENT OF MINDANAO AND SULU ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-FOUR HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

Act Numbered Twenty-four hundred and seventy-two of the Philippine Legislature, entitled "An Act repealing Acts Numbered Eight hundred and seventeen, Eight hundred and thirty-four, and Twenty-one hundred and twenty-one by changing the method of appointment of locust boards, and.by denning the relation of the Bureau of Agriculture to locust boards, to be known as the 'Locust Act,' " is hereby made applicable to the Department of Mindanao and Sulu.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, July 19, 1915.
