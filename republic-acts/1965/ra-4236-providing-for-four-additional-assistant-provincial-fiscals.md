# Republic Act No. 4236

**An Act Providing for Four Additional Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Camarines Sur, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administration Code, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4236**

An Act Providing for Four Additional Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Camarines Sur, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administration Code, as Amended

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Camarines Sur, is further amended to read as follows:

"Camarines Sur, eight assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
