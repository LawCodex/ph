# Batas Pambansa Blg. 707

**An Act to Change the Name of Concepcion Elementary School Unit I in the Municipality of Marikina, Metro Manila, to H. Bautista Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 707**

An Act to Change the Name of Concepcion Elementary School Unit I in the Municipality of Marikina, Metro Manila, to H. Bautista Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Concepcion Elementary School Unit I located at J.P. Rizal Street, Barangay Concepcion, Municipality of Marikina, Metro Manila, is hereby changed to H. Bautista Elementary School in honor of one of the heroes of the Philippine Revolution, General Hermogenes "Menes" Bautista.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
