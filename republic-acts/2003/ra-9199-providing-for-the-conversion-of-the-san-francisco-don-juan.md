# Republic Act No. 9199

**An Act Providing for the Conversion of the San Francisco-Don Juan Verceles-Maniogan-Tala-San Andres-San Narciso Provincial Road, Covering Said Municipalities in the Southern Part of Bondoc Peninsula, Quezon Province, Into a National Road, and Appropriating Funds for the Maintenance and Improvement Thereon**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9199 March 13, 2003**

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE CONVERSION OF THE SAN FRANCISCO-DON JUAN VERCELES-MANIOGAN-TALA-SAN ANDRES-SAN NARCISO PROVINCIAL ROAD, COVERING SAID MUNICIPALITIES IN THE SOUTHERN PART OF BONDOC PENINSULA, QUEZON PROVINCE, INTO A NATIONAL ROAD, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR THE MAINTENANCE AND IMPROVEMENT THEREON

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The provincial road connecting the San Francisco-Don Juan Verceles-Maniogan- Tala- San Andres-San Narciso Provincial Road, all in the Province of Quezon is hereby converted into a national road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall include in its program the improvement, maintenance and repair of the said road, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
