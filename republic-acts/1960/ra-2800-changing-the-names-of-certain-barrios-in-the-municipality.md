# Republic Act No. 2800

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2800**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the following barrios in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, are changed as follows:

1\. Barrio Bincungan to Barrio Guadalupe; and

2\. Barrio Tagurot to Barrio San Nicolas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
