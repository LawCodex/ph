# Republic Act No. 3706

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gabaldon Elementary School in the Municipality of Vintar, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Florentino Camaquin Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3706**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gabaldon Elementary School in the Municipality of Vintar, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Florentino Camaquin Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Gabaldon Elementary School in the Municipality of Vintar, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Florentino Camaquin Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
