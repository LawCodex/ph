# Batas Pambansa Blg. 341

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol National High School in the City of Tagbilaran to Dr. Cecilio Putong National High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 341**

An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol National High School in the City of Tagbilaran to Dr. Cecilio Putong National High School

## Section 1

The name of the Bohol National High School in the City of Tagbilaran is changed to Dr. Cecilio Putong National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 25, 1983
