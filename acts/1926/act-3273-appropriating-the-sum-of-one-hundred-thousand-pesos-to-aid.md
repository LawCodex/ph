# Act No. 3273

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of One Hundred thousand Pesos to Aid Indigent Sufferers in the Provinces of Batangas, Tayabas, Laguna, and Cavite From the Recent Typhoon**

[ Act No. 3273, November 12, 1926 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS TO AID INDIGENT SUFFERERS IN THE PROVINCES OF BATANGAS, TAYABAS, LAGUNA, AND CAVITE FROM THE RECENT TYPHOON

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise set aside, the sum of one hundred thousand pesos for the relief of indigent sufferers in the Provinces of Batangas, Tayabas, Laguna, and Cavite from the typhoon which occurred on November sixth, nineteen, hundred and twenty-six.

## Section 2

The sum above appropriated shall be disbursed by the Secretary of the Interior, with the approval of the Governor-General, in the manner deemed most convenient.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 12, 1926.
