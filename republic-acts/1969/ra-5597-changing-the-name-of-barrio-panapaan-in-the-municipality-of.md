# Republic Act No. 5597

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Panapaan in the Municipality of Bacoor, Province of Cavite, to Barrio Pedro Espiritu**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5597, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO PANAPAAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BACOOR, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO BARRIO PEDRO ESPIRITU.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Panapaan in the Municipality of Bacoor, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to Barrio Pedro Espiritu.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
