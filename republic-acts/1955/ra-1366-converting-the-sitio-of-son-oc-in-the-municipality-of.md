# Republic Act No. 1366

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Son-oc, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Bohol, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Fatima**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1366**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Son-oc, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Bohol, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Fatima**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Son-oc, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Bohol, is hereby converted to a barrio to be known as barrio Fatima.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1955.
