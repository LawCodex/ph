# Republic Act No. 7493

**An Act Renaming the Filipinas National High School in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Ilocos Norte, As the San Nicolas National High School, and For Other Purposes**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7493, May 13, 1992 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE FILIPINAS NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN NICOLAS, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, AS THE SAN NICOLAS NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Filipinas National High School located in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby renamed as the San Nicolas National High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

All allocations provided for the operation and maintenance of the Filipinas National High School shall be carried over to the San Nicolas National High School.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
