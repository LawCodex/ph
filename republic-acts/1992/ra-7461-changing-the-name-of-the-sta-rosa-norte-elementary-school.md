# Republic Act No. 7461

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Rosa Norte Elementary School in Barangay Sta. Rosa Norte, Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Camarines Norte, To Segundo Aguirre Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7461, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE STA. ROSA NORTE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY STA. ROSA NORTE, MUNICIPALITY OF JOSE PANGANIBAN, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO SEGUNDO AGUIRRE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Rosa Norte Elementary School in Barangay Sta. Rosa Norte, Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Segundo Aguirre Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
