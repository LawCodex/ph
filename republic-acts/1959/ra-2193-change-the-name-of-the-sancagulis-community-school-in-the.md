# Republic Act No. 2193

**An Act to Change the Name of the Sancagulis Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Cason Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2193**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Sancagulis Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Cason Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sancagulis Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Cason Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
