# Act No. 2677

**An Act Conferring Upon the Supreme Court Jurisdiction to Hear in the Last Instance Criminal Cases Filed Originally in the Justice of the Peace and Municipal Courts.**

[ Act No. 2677, March 09, 1917 ]

AN ACT CONFERRING UPON THE SUPREME COURT JURISDICTION TO HEAR IN THE LAST INSTANCE CRIMINAL CASES FILED ORIGINALLY IN THE JUSTICE OF THE PEACE AND MUNICIPAL COURTS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Sentences pronounced by Courts of First Instance in criminal cases orginating in justice of the peace and municipal courts shall not be final if the defendant appeals within the period provided by law. The defendant may appeal from such sentences to the Supreme Court in all cases.

The procedure in said cases in the Courts of First Instance shall be the same as in the courts of origin.

## Section 2

All Acts or parts of Acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 9, 1917.
