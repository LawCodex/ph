# Republic Act No. 8840

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tuba National High School At Twin Peaks, Municipality of Tuba, Province of Benguet to the Twin Peaks National High School**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8840, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE TUBA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AT TWIN PEAKS, MUNICIPALITY OF TUBA, PROVINCE OF BENGUET TO THE TWIN PEAKS NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tuba National High School at Twin Peaks in the Municipality of Tuba, Province of Benguet, is hereby changed to Twin Peaks National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) FRANKLIN M. DRILON President of Senate | (Sgd.) MANUEL B. VILLAR JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
