# Republic Act No. 3795

**An Act Providing for Four Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Lanao del Sur, by Further Amending Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3795**

**An Act Providing for Four Assistant Provincial Fiscals for the Province of Lanao del Sur, by Further Amending Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to assistant provincial fiscals, is further amended so that the Province of Lanao Del Sur shall have four assistant provincial fiscals.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
