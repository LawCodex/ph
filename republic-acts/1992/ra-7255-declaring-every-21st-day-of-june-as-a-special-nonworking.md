# Republic Act No. 7255

**An Act Declaring Every 21st Day of June as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Cabarroguis, Province of Quirino, to Be Known as "Araw Ng Cabarroguis"**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7255, March 20, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING EVERY 21ST DAY OF JUNE AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CABARROGUIS, PROVINCE OF QUIRINO, TO BE KNOWN AS "ARAW NG CABARROGUIS"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

June 21st of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Cabarroguis, Province of Quirino, to be known as "Araw ng Cabarroguis."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
