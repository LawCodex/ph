# Batas Pambansa Blg. 675

**An Act Changing the Name of Pooc Elementary School in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Jose M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 675**

An Act Changing the Name of Pooc Elementary School in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Jose M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Pooc Elementary School in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, is changed to Honorable Jose M. Reyes Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
