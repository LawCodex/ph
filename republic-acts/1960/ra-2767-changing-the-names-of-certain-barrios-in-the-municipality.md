# Republic Act No. 2767

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2767**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Sominot in the Municipality of Liargao, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is changed to Oliva.

## Section 2

The name of the barrio of Lower Malating in the same municipality is changed to Magsaysay.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
