# Republic Act No. 2783

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tucalan-Miñañga in the Municipality of Lasam, Province of Cagayan, to Nicolas Agatep**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2783**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tucalan-Miñañga in the Municipality of Lasam, Province of Cagayan, to Nicolas Agatep**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Tucalan-Miñañga in the Municipality of Lasam, Province of Cagayan, is changed to Nicolas Agatep.1âшphi1

## Section 2
