# Republic Act No. 2596

**An Act Dividing the Barrio of Casibarag in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, into Two Barrios to be Known as Casibarag Norte and Casibarag Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2596**

**An Act Dividing the Barrio of Casibarag in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, into Two Barrios to be Known as Casibarag Norte and Casibarag Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Casibarag in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, is hereby divided into two barrios to be known as Casibarag Norte and Casibarag Sur.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
