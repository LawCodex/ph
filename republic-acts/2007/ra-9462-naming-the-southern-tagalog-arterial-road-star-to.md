# Republic Act No. 9462

**An Act Naming the Southern Tagalog Arterial Road (STAR) to Apolinario Mabini Superhighway (AMS)**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9462 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT NAMING THE SOUTHERN TAGALOG ARTERIAL ROAD (STAR) TO APOLINARIO MABINI SUPERHIGHWAY (AMS)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

This Act shall be officially known as the **"_Apolinario Mabini Superhighway Act_ "**.

## Sec. 2

This Act officially names the Southern Tagalog Arterial Road (STAR) to Apolinario Mabini Superhighway (AMS) .

## Sec. 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
