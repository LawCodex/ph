# Republic Act No. 3997

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Banna in the Province of Ilocos Norte, to Espiritu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3997**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Banna in the Province of Ilocos Norte, to Espiritu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Banna in the Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Espiritu.(awÞhi(

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
