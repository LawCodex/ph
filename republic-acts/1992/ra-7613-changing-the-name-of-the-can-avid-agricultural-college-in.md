# Republic Act No. 7613

**An Act Changing the Name of the Can-avid Agricultural College in the Municipality of Can-avid, Province of Eastern Samar, To Can-avid National Agricultural College**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7613, June 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE CAN-AVID AGRICULTURAL COLLEGE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAN-AVID, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO CAN-AVID NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL COLLEGE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Can-Avid Agricultural College in the Municipality of Can-Avid, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Can-Avid National Agricultural College.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
