# Republic Act No. 2864

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sitio of Dammang in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan, to San Juan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2864**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sitio of Dammang in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan, to San Juan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the sitio of Dammang in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan, is changed to San Juan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
