# Republic Act No. 2097

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Basilio in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Isabela, to San Jose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2097**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Basilio in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Isabela, to San Jose**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Basilio in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 22, 1959.
