# Act No. 3165

**An Act to Appropriate Additional Funds for the Expenses of the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines for the Year Nineteen Hundred and Twenty-Four**

[ Act No. 3165, September 16, 1924 ]

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE ADDITIONAL FUNDS FOR THE EXPENSES OF THE SENATE AND HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE PHILIPPINES FOR THE YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND TWENTY-FOUR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of two hundred and sixty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise set aside, in addition to the funds appropriated for the expenses of the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and twenty-three, of which sum one hundred thousand pesos shall be for the Philippine Senate and one hundred and sixty thousand pesos for the House of Representatives: _Provided,_ That this additional appropriation shall be exempt from the operation of Joint Resolution Numbered One of the Legislature, approved March sixteenth, nineteen hundred and twenty-three.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, September 16, 1924.
