# Republic Act No. 7402

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Maria General Hospital in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Bulacan, To Rogaciano M. Mercado Memorial General Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7402, April 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE STA. MARIA GENERAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. MARIA, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO ROGACIANO M. MERCADO MEMORIAL GENERAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Maria General Hospital in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Rogaciano M. Mercado Memorial General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
