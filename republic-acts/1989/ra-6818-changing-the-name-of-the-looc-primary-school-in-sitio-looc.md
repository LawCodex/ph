# Republic Act No. 6818

**An Act Changing the Name of the Looc Primary School in Sitio Looc, Barangay Taloto-An, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, to Donato Valderama Primary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6818 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LOOC PRIMARY SCHOOL IN SITIO LOOC, BARANGAY TALOTO-AN, MUNICIPALITY OF CONCEPCION, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO DONATO VALDERAMA PRIMARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Looc Primary School in Sitio Looc, Barangay Taloto-an, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Donato Valderama Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
