# Republic Act No. 4997

**An Act Converting Sitio Hebulo in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Santa Fe**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4997**

An Act Converting Sitio Hebulo in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Santa Fe

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Hebulo in the Municipality of Catubig, Province of Samar, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Santa Fe.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
