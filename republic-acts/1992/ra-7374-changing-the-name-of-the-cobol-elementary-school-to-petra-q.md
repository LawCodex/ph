# Republic Act No. 7374

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cobol Elementary School to Petra Q. Pimentel Elementary School, and for Other Purposes**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7374, April 10, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE COBOL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL TO PETRA Q. PIMENTEL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cobol Elementary School situated in Barangay Petra Q. Pimentel, Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Petra Q. Pimentel Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
