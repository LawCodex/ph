# Batas Pambansa Blg. 658

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Lower Ipil Heights in the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Barangay Tirso Babiera**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 658**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Lower Ipil Heights in the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Barangay Tirso Babiera**

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Lower Ipil Heights the Municipality of Ipil, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Barangay Tirso Babiera.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
