# Republic Act No. 8795

**An Act Naming the 2nd Mandaue-Mactan Bridge in the Province of Cebu as the Marcelo B. Fernan Bridge**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8795 June 27, 2000**

AN ACT NAMING THE 2ND MANDAUE-MACTAN BRIDGE IN THE PROVINCE OF CEBU AS THE MARCELO B. FERNAN BRIDGE

## SECTION 1

The 2nd Mandaue-Mactan Bridge situated in the Province of Cebu is hereby named as the Marcelo B. Fernan Bridge.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **June 27, 2000**
