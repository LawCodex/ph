# Batas Pambansa Blg. 308

**An Act Changing the Name of sto. Tomas Elementary School in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, Into Fidelidad J. Santibañez Memorial Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 308**

An Act Changing the Name of Sto. Tomas Elementary School in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, into Fidelidad J. Santibañez Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Sto. Tomas Elementary School in the Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Fidelidad J. Santibanez Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
