# Republic Act No. 4005

**An Act to Change the Name of Caridad Elementary School in the City of Cavite to Ladislao Diwa Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4005**

**An Act to Change the Name of Caridad Elementary School in the City of Cavite to Ladislao Diwa Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Caridad Elementary School in the City of Cavite is hereby changed to Ladislao Diwa Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
