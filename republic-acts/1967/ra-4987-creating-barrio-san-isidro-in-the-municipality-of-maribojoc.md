# Republic Act No. 4987

**An Act Creating Barrio San Isidro in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4987**

An Act Creating Barrio San Isidro in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sitios Taytay, Datag, Saon and Kabulihan, in Barrio Tinibgan, Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol, are separated from said barrio, and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as Barrio San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
