# Republic Act No. 2841

**An Act Changing the Name of Paragos Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Paragos-San Gabriel II Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2841**

**An Act Changing the Name of Paragos Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Paragos-San Gabriel II Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Paragos Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Paragos-San Gabriel II Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
