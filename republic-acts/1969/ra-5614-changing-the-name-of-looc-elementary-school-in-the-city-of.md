# Republic Act No. 5614

**An Act Changing the Name of Looc Elementary School in the City of Danao to Bibiana Mercado Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5614, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF LOOC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF DANAO TO BIBIANA MERCADO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Looc Elementary School in the City of Danao is hereby changed to Bibiana Mercado Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
