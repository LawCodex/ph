# Republic Act No. 2455

**An Act to Amend Item (g) Group II, Page 29, Subparagraph (10), for the Province of Bulacan, Paragraph “f”, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2455**

**An Act to Amend Item (g) Group II, Page 29, Subparagraph (10), for the Province of Bulacan, Paragraph "f", Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (g), group II, page 29, subparagraph (10), for the Province of Bulacan, paragraph "f," section two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"(g) Sta. Maria Public Market Acquisition of site and construction ₱15,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
