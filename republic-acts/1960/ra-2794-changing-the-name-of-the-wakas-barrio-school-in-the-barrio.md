# Republic Act No. 2794

**An Act Changing the Name of the Wakas Barrio School in the Barrio of Wakas, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, to General Tomas Mascardo Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2794**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Wakas Barrio School in the Barrio of Wakas, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, to General Tomas Mascardo Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Wakas Barrio School in the barrio of Wakas, Municipality of Kawit, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to General Tomas Mascardo Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
