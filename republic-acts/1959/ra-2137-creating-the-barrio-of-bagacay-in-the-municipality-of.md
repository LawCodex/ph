# Republic Act No. 2137

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bagacay in the Municipality of Lapinig, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2137**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bagacay in the Municipality of Lapinig, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Kajaguekjekan and Lobok in the Municipality of Lapinig, Province of Samar, are hereby constituted into a barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Bagacay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
