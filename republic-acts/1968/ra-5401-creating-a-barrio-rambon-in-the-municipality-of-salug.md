# Republic Act No. 5401

**An Act Creating a Barrio Rambon in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5401**

An Act Creating a Barrio Rambon in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Rambon in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as Barrio Rambon. Said barrio shall be bounded as follows:

1\. North-Salug River;

2\. East-Matingon Creek;

3\. South-Starting from corner of Lot 3391, 3392, 3393, 3398 and 3447, thence draw a straight line due East until it reaches Matingon Creek;

4\. West-Guisapong Creek.

All areas within bounds of delineation comprise the proposed barrio of Rambon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
