# Republic Act No. 1237

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Atelan, in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to San Miguel**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1237**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Atelan, in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to San Miguel**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Atelan, in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to barrio San Miguel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 7, 1955.
