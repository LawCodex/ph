# Republic Act No. 7705

**An Act Changing the Name of the Buhangin Barangay High School in Barangay Buhangin, Davao City, to Bernardo Carpio Barangay High School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7705, May 05, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BUHANGIN BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BUHANGIN, DAVAO CITY, TO BERNARDO CARPIO BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Buhangin Barangay High School in Barangay Buhangin, Davao City, is hereby changed to Bernardo Carpio Barangay High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

Lapsed into law on May 5, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
