# Batas Pambansa Blg. 653

**An Act Changing the Name of the Latuan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Imam Gayong Ligaddung Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 653**

An Act Changing the Name of the Latuan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Imam Gayong Ligaddung Elementary School

## Section 1

The Latuan Elementary School in the Municipality of Sapa-Sapa, Province of Tawi-Tawi, shall hereafter be known as the Imam Gayong Ligaddung Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
