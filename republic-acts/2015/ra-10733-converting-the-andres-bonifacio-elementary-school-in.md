# Republic Act No. 10733

**An Act Converting the Andres Bonifacio Elementary School in Barangay Jalandoni-Wilson, City Proper District, Iloilo City Into An Integrated School to Be Known as Andres Bonifacio Integrated School**

Sixteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10733**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE ANDRES BONIFACIO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY JALANDONI-WILSON, CITY PROPER DISTRICT, ILOILO CITY INTO AN INTEGRATED SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS ANDRES BONIFACIO INTEGRATED SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Confess assembled:

## SECTION 1

_Conversion into an Integrated School._ – The Andres Bonifacio Elementary School in Barangay Jalandoni-Wilson, City Proper District, Iloilo City is hereby converted into an integrated school to be known as Andres Bonifacio Integrated School.

## SECTION 2

_Transfer of Assets and Liabilities._ – All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Andres Bonifacio Elementary School are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Andres Bonifacio Integrated School.

## SECTION 3

_Appropriations._ – The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department’s program the operationalization of the Andres Bonifacio Integrated School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## SECTION 4

_Implementation._ – The Secretary of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## SECTION 5

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
