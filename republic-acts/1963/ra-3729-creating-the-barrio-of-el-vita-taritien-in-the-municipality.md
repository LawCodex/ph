# Republic Act No. 3729

**An Act Creating the Barrio of El-Vita Taritien in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3729**

AN ACT CREATING THE BARRIO OF EL-VITA TARITIEN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ABORLAN, PROVINCE OF PALAWAN.

## Section 1

The sitio of El-Vita Taritien in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of El-Vita Taritien.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963
