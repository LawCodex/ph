# Republic Act No. 6435

**An Act Changing the Name of San Marcelino Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of San Marcelino, Province of Zambales, to Mayor Leopoldo F. Rabanes Memorial Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6435, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SAN MARCELINO EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN MARCELINO, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES, TO MAYOR LEOPOLDO F. RABANES MEMORIAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of San Marcelino Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of San Marcelino, Province of Zambales, is hereby changed to Mayor Leopoldo F. Rabanes Memorial Hospital.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1972.
