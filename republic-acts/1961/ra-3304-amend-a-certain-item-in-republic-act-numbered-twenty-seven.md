# Republic Act No. 3304

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twenty Seven Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3304**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Twenty Seven Hundred and One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (t), under Group III, for the Province of Cebu, page 340, paragraph (c), Section two of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and one, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(t) Carcar Central School, Carcar Construction Buildings, "Noel type" ₱30,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
