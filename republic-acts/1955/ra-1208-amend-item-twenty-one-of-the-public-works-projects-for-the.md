# Republic Act No. 1208

**An Act to Amend Item Twenty-One of the Public Works Projects for the Province of Quezon, Sub-Section (a), Title A, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Six Hundred Seventy**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1208**

**An Act to Amend Item Twenty-One of the Public Works Projects for the Province of Quezon, Sub-Section (a), Title A, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Six Hundred Seventy**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item twenty-one of the public works projects for the Province of Quezon, subsection (a), title A, section one of Republic Act Numbered Six hundred seventy, is amended to read as follows:

| "21. For the construction of the Sto. Cristo School Building, Sariaya | ₱5,000.00." |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 20, 1955.
