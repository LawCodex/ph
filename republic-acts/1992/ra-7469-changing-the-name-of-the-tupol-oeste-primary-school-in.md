# Republic Act No. 7469

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tupol Oeste Primary School in Barangay Tupol Oeste, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, To Julian B. Pueyo Primary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7469, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE TUPOL OESTE PRIMARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TUPOL OESTE, MUNICIPALITY OF CABATUAN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO JULIAN B. PUEYO PRIMARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tupol Oeste Primary SchooI in Barangay Tupol 0este, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Julian B. Pueyo Primary School.
