# Republic Act No. 10267

**An Act Changing the Name of Gusa National High School in Barangay Gusa, City of Cagayan De Oro, Province of Misamis Oriental to Gusa Regional Science High School - X**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-three day of July, two thousand twelve.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10267**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF GUSA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY GUSA, CITY OF CAGAYAN DE ORO, PROVINCE OF MISAMIS ORIENTAL TO GUSA REGIONAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL – X

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of a National High School._ – The name of Gusa National High School in Barangay Gusa, City of Cagayan de Oro, Province of Misamis Oriental is hereby changed to Gusa Regional Science High School – X.

## Section 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ – The main campus of Gusa Regional Science High School – X shall continue to remain in its present location in Barangay Gusa, City of Cagayan de Oro, Province of Misamis Oriental.

## Section 3

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on October 4, 2010 and July 24, 2012, respectively.
