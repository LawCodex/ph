# Republic Act No. 5087

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Patpat in the Municipality of Malaybalay, Province of Bukidnon, to Lapu-Lapu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5087**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Patpat in the Municipality of Malaybalay, Province of Bukidnon, to Lapu-Lapu

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Patpat in the Municipality of Malaybalay, Province of Bukidnon, is hereby changed to Lapu-Lapu.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
