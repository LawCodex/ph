# Republic Act No. 6722

**An Act Declaring April Twenty-Five a Special Non-Working Holiday in the Province of Aklan**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6722 March 20, 1989**

AN ACT DECLARING APRIL TWENTY-FIVE A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF AKLAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

April Twenty-five of every year is hereby declared a special non-working holiday in the Province of Aklan to commemorate the creation of said province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **March 20, 1989.**
