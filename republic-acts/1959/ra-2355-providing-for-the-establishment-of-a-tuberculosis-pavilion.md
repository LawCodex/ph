# Republic Act No. 2355

**An Act Providing for the Establishment of a Tuberculosis Pavilion in the Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2355**

**An Act Providing for the Establishment of a Tuberculosis Pavilion in the Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Director of Health Services, a tuberculosis pavilion in the Municipality of Guiuan, Province of Samar, to be known as the Guiuan Tuberculosis Pavilion.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Health shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1959.
