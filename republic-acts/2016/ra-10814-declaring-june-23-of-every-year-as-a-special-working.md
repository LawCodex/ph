# Republic Act No. 10814

**An Act Declaring June 23 of Every Year as a Special Working Holiday and No Class Day in Schools in the City of Bacoor to Commemorate Its Anniversary as a City**

Sixteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10814**

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 23 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY AND NO CLASS DAY IN SCHOOLS IN THE CITY OF BACOOR TO COMMEMORATE ITS ANNIVERSARY AS A CITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## SECTION 1

June 23 of every year is hereby declared as a special working holiday and no class day in schools in the City of Bacoor to commemorate its anniversary as a City.

## SECTION 2

For work performed on a special working holiday, an employee is entitled to his/her regular wage.1âwphi1

## SECTION 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,
