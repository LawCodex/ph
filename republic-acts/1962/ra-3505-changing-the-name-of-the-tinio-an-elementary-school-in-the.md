# Republic Act No. 3505

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tinio-an Elementary School in the Municipality of Cabanatuan, Province of Iloilo, to Doroteo Lujan Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3505**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tinio-an Elementary School in the Municipality of Cabanatuan, Province of Iloilo, to Doroteo Lujan Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tinio-an Elementary School in the Municipality of Cabanatuan, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Doroteo Lujan Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
