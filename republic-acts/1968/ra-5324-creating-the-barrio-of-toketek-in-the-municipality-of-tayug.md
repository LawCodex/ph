# Republic Act No. 5324

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Toketek in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5324**

An Act Creating the Barrio of Toketek in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Toketek in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as Barrio Toketek.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
