# Republic Act No. 5620

**An Act Changing the Name of Ising Elementary School in the Municipality of Carmen, Province of Davao Del Norte, to Carmen Central Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5620, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ISING ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARMEN, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL NORTE, TO CARMEN CENTRAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Ising Elementary School in the Municipality of Carmen, Province of Davao del Norte, is hereby changed to Carmen Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
