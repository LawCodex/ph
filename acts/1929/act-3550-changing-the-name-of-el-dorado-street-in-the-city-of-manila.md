# Act No. 3550

**An Act Changing the Name of El Dorado street, in the City of Manila, to Martin Ocampo street.**

[ Act No. 3550, November 23, 1929 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF EL DORADO STREET, IN THE CITY OF MANILA, TO MARTIN OCAMPO STREET.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

In commemoration of the illustrious Filipino patriot Martin Ocampo, El Dorado Street, in the City of of Manila, shall hereafter be known as Martin Ocampo Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 23, 1929.
