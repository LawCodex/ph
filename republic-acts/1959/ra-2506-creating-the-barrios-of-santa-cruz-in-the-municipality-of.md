# Republic Act No. 2506

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Santa Cruz in the Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2506**

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Santa Cruz in the Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Kalatubog and Kilometer 32 of Puerto Princesa North Road in the Municipality of Puerto Princesa, Province of Palawan, are constituted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Santa Cruz.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
