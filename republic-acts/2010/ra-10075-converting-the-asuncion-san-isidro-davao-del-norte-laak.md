# Republic Act No. 10075

**An Act Converting the Asuncion - San Isidro, Davao Del Norte - Laak, Compostela Valley - Veruela, Agusan Del Sur Road Section Into a National Secondary Road**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10075**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE ASUNCION - SAN ISIDRO, DAVAO DEL NORTE - LAAK, COMPOSTELA VALLEY - VERUELA, AGUSAN DEL SUR ROAD SECTION INTO A NATIONAL SECONDARY ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Asuncion - San Isidro, Davao del Norte - Laak, Compostela Valley - Veruela, Agusan del Sur Road Section is hereby converted into a national secondary road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
