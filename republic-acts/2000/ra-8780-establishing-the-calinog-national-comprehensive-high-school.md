# Republic Act No. 8780

**An Act Establishing the Calinog National Comprehensive High School in the Municipality of Calinog, Province of Iloilo, to be known as the Calinog National Comprehensive High School and appropriating funds therefor**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8780 April 27, 2000**

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE CALINOG NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CALINOG, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE CALINOG NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

## SECTION 1

There is hereby established the Calinog National Comprehensive High School to be located in the Municipality of Calinog, Iloilo.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports is hereby directed to take such measures as are necessary to establish the Calinog National Comprehensive High School.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **April 27, 2000**
