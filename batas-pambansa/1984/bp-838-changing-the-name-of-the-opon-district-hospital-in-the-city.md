# Batas Pambansa Blg. 838

**An Act Changing the Name of the Opon District Hospital in the City of Lapu-Lapu to Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 838**

An Act Changing the Name of the Opon District Hospital in the City of Lapu-Lapu to Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Opon District Hospital in the City of Lapu-Lapu is hereby changed to Lapu-Lapu City District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
