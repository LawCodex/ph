# Act No. 2378

|---|---|

| (g) For the construction of a wharf in the municipality of Silay, of another wharf in the municipality of Bacolod, and of another wharf in the municipality of Pulupandan, Occidental Negros | 30,000.00 |
| (h) For port works at lloilo, Iloilo | 50,000.00 |
| (i) For the extension and permanent improvement of the wharf established in the port of Catbalogan, Samar | 10,000.00 |

## Section 3

All balances remaining unexpended when any public work or permanent improvement appropriated for by this Act is completed, shall be reverted to the general fund of the Insular Treasury and shall not thereafter be available for withdrawal or disbursement except upon appropriation by the Legislature.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, February 28, 1914.
