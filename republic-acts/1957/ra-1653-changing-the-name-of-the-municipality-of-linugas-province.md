# Republic Act No. 1653

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Linugas, Province of Misamis Oriental, to Magsaysay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1653**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Linugas, Province of Misamis Oriental, to Magsaysay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Linugas, Province of Misamis Oriental, is hereby change to "Magsaysay".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
