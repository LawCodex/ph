# Executive Order No. 59

**Extending the Period of Registration and Deposit of Emergency Currency Under the Provisions of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Two**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 59, June 09, 1947 ]

EXTENDING THE PERIOD OF REGISTRATION AND DEPOSIT OF EMERGENCY CURRENCY UNDER THE PROVISIONS OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-TWO.

By virtue of the powers vested in me by section six of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-two, and the public interest so requiring, **I, MANUEL ROXAS** , President of the Philippines, do hereby extend the period of registration and deposit of emergency currency for two months, or from June first to July thirty-first, both dates inclusive, nineteen hundred and forty-seven,

Done at the City of Manila, this 9th day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and forty-seven, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the first.

(Sgd.) **MANUEL ROXAS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(Sgd.) **EMILIO ABELLO**
Chief of the Executive Office
