# Republic Act No. 7449

**An Act Declaring August Thirty A Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Bulacan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7449, April 27, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING AUGUST THIRTY A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF BULACAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

August Thirty of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Bulacan to commemorate the birth anniversary of Marcelo H. del Pilar.

## Section 2
