# Batas Pambansa Blg. 739

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sebaste Central School in the Municipality of Sebaste, Province of Antique, to Juan O. Dela Cruz Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 739**

An Act Changing the Name of the Sebaste Central School in the Municipality of Sebaste, Province of Antique, to Juan O. Dela Cruz Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Sebaste Central School in the Municipality of Sebaste, Province of Antique, is changed to Juan O. dela Cruz Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
