# Batas Pambansa Blg. 836

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mountain Province General Hospital in the Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province, to Luis Hora Memorial Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 836**

An Act Changing the Name of the Mountain Province General Hospital in the Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province, to Luis Hora, Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The Mountain Province General Hospital in the Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province, is hereby called Luis Hora Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
