# Republic Act No. 1642

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Loboc, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Oy Primary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Bartolome Doria Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1642**

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Loboc, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Oy Primary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Bartolome Doria Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The school in the Municipality of Loboc, Province of Bohol, at present known as Oy Primary School, shall hereafter be known as Bartolome Doria Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
