# Republic Act No. 3771

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dikalungan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Ampatuan, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Sangki Ampatuan Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3771**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dikalungan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Ampatuan, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Sangki Ampatuan Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Dikalungan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Ampatuan, Province of Cotabato, is hereby changed to Datu Sangki Ampatuan Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
