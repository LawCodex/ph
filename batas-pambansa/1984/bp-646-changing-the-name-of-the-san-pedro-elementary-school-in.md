# Batas Pambansa Blg. 646

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Pedro Elementary School in Barangay San Pedro, Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Assemblyman Segundo Moscoso Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 646**

An Act Changing the Name of the San Pedro Elementary School in Barangay San Pedro, Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Assemblyman Segundo Moscoso Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the San Pedro Elementary School in Barangay San Pedro, Municipality of San Jose, province of Antique, is changed to Assemblyman Segundo Moscoso Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
