# Republic Act No. 7174

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Quirino Memorial Medical Center in Quezon City, Metro Manila, From Two Hundred (200) to Two Hundred Fifty (250) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7174, January 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE QUIRINO MEMORIAL MEDICAL CENTER IN QUEZON CITY, METRO MANILA, FROM TWO HUNDRED (200) TO TWO HUNDRED FIFTY (250) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Quirino Memorial Medical Center in Quezon City, Metro Manila, is hereby increased from two hundred (200) to two hundred fifty (250) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
