# Act No. 2964

**An Act to Make the Province of Mindoro a Regularly Organized Province.**

[ Act No. 2964, February 20, 1921 ]

AN ACT TO MAKE THE PROVINCE OF MINDORO A REGULARLY ORGANIZED PROVINCE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

All the provisions of laws now applicable or which may hereafter be applicable to the regularly organized provinces are hereby extended and applied to the Province of Mindoro.

## Section 2

The provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-eight hundred and twenty-nine regulating the salaries of the provincial officers are likewise made applicable to the Province of Mindoro: _Provided,_ That the salary of the provincial fiscal of Mindoro shall not be less than three thousand pesos per annum, any provision of law to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Section 3

All acts or parts of acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on July first, nineteen hundred and twenty-one.

Approved, February 20, 1921.
