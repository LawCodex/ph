# Republic Act No. 3624

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Tatarikan in the Province of Lanao del Sur, to Pagayawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3624**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Tatarikan in the Province of Lanao del Sur, to Pagayawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipal District of Tatarikan in the Province of Lanao del Sur, is hereby changed to Pagayawan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
