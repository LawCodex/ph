# Act No. 2889

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Malabon, Province of Cavite, to General Trias, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Act No. 2889, February 24, 1920 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF MALABON, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO GENERAL TRIAS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of Malabon, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to General Trias, and after the approval of this Act, all territory comprised in the jurisdiction of said municipality of Malabon shall be called General Trias, as herein provided.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, February 24, 1920.
