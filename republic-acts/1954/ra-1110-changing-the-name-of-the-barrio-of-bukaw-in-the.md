# Republic Act No. 1110

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bukaw in the Municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo, to that of Alameda**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1110**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bukaw in the Municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo, to that of Alameda**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Bukaw in the municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to that of Alameda.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1954.
