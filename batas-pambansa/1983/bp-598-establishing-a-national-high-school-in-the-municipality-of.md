# Batas Pambansa Blg. 598

**An Act Establishing a National High School in the Municipality of San Isidro, Province of Isabela, to Be Known as the San Isidro National High School, Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 598**

An Act Establishing a National High School in the Municipality of San Isidro, Province of Isabela, to be Known as the San Isidro National High School, Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

There is established a national high school in the Municipality of San Isidro, Province of Isabela, to be known as the San Isidro National High School.

## Section 2

Such sum as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of said school shall be provided in subsequent General Appropriations Acts until which time the operation of the institution as a national high school shall be deferred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
