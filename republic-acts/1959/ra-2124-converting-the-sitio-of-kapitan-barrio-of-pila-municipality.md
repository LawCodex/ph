# Republic Act No. 2124

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kapitan, Barrio of Pila, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Kapitan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2124**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kapitan, Barrio of Pila, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Kapitan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Kapitan, barrio of Pila, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Kapitan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
