# Republic Act No. 8641

**An Act Separating the Guindaruhan National High School Extension in the Municipality of Minglanilla, Province of Cebu, From the Guindaruhan National High School and Converting It Into An Independent National High School, to Be Known as the Tungkop National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8641, May 17, 1998 ]

AN ACT SEPARATING THE GUINDARUHAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL EXTENSION IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MINGLANILLA, PROVINCE OF CEBU, FROM THE GUINDARUHAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE TUNGKOP NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Guindaruhan National High School Extension in Barangay Tungkop, Municipality of Minglanilla, Province of Cebu, is hereby separated from the Guindaruhan National High School in the Municipality of Minglanilla and converted into an independent national high school, to be known as the Tungkop National High School.Ꮮαwρhi৷

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Guindaruhan National High School Extension are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Tungkop National High School.

## Section 4

The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the Guindaruhan National High School-Barangay Tungkop Extension. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of the Tungkop National High School shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
