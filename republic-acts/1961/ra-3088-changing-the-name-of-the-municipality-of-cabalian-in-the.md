# Republic Act No. 3088

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Cabalian in the Province of Southern Leyte to San Juan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3088**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Cabalian in the Province of Southern Leyte to San Juan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Cabalian, Province of Southern Leyte, is hereby changed to San Juan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1961.
