# Act No. 2295

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Fifty thousand Pesos, Not Otherwise Appropriated, for the Extermination of Locusts.**

[ Act No. 2295, November 10, 1913 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS, NOT OTHERWISE APPROPRIATED, FOR THE EXTERMINATION OF LOCUSTS.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The sum of fifty thousand pesos Philippine currency is hereby appropriated out of any Insular funds not otherwise appropriated, to be expended in the destruction of locusts, in accordance with the provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-one hundred and twenty-one.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the same shall iake effect on its passage, in accordance with section one of Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and forty-five of the Philippine Legislature.

Enacted, November 10, 1913.
