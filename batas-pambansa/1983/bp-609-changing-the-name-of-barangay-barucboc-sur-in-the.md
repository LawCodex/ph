# Batas Pambansa Blg. 609

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Barucboc Sur in the Municipality of Mallig, Province of Isabela, to Barangay San Pedro.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 609**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Barucboc Sur in the Municipality of Mallig, Province of Isabela, to Barangay San Pedro

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Barucboc Sur in the Municipality of Mallig, Province of Isabela, is changed to Barangay San Pedro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
