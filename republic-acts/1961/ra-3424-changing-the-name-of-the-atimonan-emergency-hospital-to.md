# Republic Act No. 3424

**An Act Changing the Name of the Atimonan Emergency Hospital to Doña Marta Memorial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3424**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Atimonan Emergency Hospital to Doña Marta Memorial Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the invaluable services rendered to the people of the municipality of Atimonan, Province of Quezon, by the late Doña Marta Montes Guinto, a former civic and spiritual leader of the said municipality, the name of the Atimonan Emergency Hospital is changed to Doña Marta Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
