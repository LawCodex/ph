# Republic Act No. 8315

**An Act Declaring December Eight of Every Year A Special Nonworking Holiday in Angeles City in Commemoration of Its Foundation Day**

Tenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8315, June 30, 1997 ]

AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER EIGHT OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN ANGELES CITY IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDATION DAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

December eight of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in Angeles City in commemoration of its foundation day.1aшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on June 30, 1997 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27 (1), Article VI of the Constitution.
