# Batas Pambansa Blg. 606

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Ventinilla West in the Municipality of sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, to Barangay Ventinilla.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 606**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Ventinilla West in the Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, to Barangay Ventinilla

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Ventinilla West in the Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Barangay Ventinilla.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
