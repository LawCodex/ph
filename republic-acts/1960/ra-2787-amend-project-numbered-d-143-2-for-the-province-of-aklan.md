# Republic Act No. 2787

**An Act to Amend Project Numbered D-143.2, for the Province of Aklan, Page 928-A, Title D, Appendix a of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2787**

**An Act to Amend Project Numbered D-143.2, for the Province of Aklan, Page 928-A, Title D, Appendix A of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Project numbered D-143.2, for the Province of Aklan, page 928-A, Title D, Appendix A of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"D-143.2 Aklan Waterworks, for the towns GOB 500 (500) (300) (200) 4 New project of Makato, Kalibo, Banga, New Washington, Lezo, Malinao, Numancia and Tangalan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
