# Republic Act No. 7709

**An Act Changing the Name of the Malabon Municipal High School Situated in Barangay Hulong-duhat, Municipality of Malabon, Metro Manila, to Malabon National High School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7709, May 05, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MALABON MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL SITUATED IN BARANGAY HULONG-DUHAT, MUNICIPALITY OF MALABON, METRO MANILA, TO MALABON NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Malabon Municipal High School situated in Barangay Hulong-Duhat, Municipality of Malabon, Metro Manila, is hereby changed to Malabon National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on May 5, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
