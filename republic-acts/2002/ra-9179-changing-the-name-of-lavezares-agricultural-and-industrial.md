# Republic Act No. 9179

**An act changing the name of Lavezares Agricultural and Industrial School, Municipality of Lavezares, Province of Northern Samar to Basilio B. Chan Memorial Agricultural and Industrial School**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two.

**Republic Act No. 9179 November 13, 2002**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF LAVEZARES AGRICULTURAL AND INDUSTRIAL SCHOOL, MUNICIPALITY OF LAVEZARES, PROVINCE OF NORTHERN SAMAR TO BASILIO B. CHAN MEMORIAL AGRICULTURAL AND INDUSTRIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lavezares Agricultural and Industrial School in the Municipality of Lavezares, Province of Northern Samar is hereby changed to Basilio B. Chan Memorial Agricultural and Industrial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
