# Republic Act No. 4289

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Carabao Island in the Province of Romblon to Municipal District of San Jose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4289**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Carabao Island in the Province of Romblon to Municipal District of San Jose

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Municipal District of Carabao Island in the Province of Romblon shall hereafter be known for all intents and purposes as the Municipal Districts of San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
