Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

The following items of appropriation under section two, subsection A, paragraph (a), of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred and sixty-nine are amended so as to read as follows:

The following items under section 2, subsection A, paragraph (a) of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred and sixty-nine are supressed:

Items )8, 39, 40, 41, and 42 under Cagayan ;

Item 44 under Laguna ;

Item 17 under Nueva Ecija ;

Items 105, 106, and 107 under Pangasinan;

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
