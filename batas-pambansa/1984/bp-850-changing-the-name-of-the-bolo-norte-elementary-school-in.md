# Batas Pambansa Blg. 850

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bolo Norte Elementary School in Barangay Bolo Norte in the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur to Soledad R. VIllafuerte Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 850**

An Act Changing the Name of the Bolo Norte Elementary School in Barangay Bolo Norte in the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur, to Soledad R. Villafuerte Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Bolo Norte Elementary School in Barangay Bolo Norte in the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby changed to Soledad R. Villafuerte Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
