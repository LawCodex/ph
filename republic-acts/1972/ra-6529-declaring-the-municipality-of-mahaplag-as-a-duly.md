# Republic Act No. 6529

**An Act Declaring the Municipality of Mahaplag as a Duly Constituted Municipality in the Province of Leyte.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6529, July 22, 1972 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAHAPLAG AS A DULY CONSTITUTED MUNICIPALITY IN THE PROVINCE OF LEYTE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The present Municipality of Mahaplag, Province of Leyte, is hereby declared as a duly constituted municipality to be known as the Municipality of Mahaplag as of July 21, 1958.

## Section 2

The seat of government of the said Municipality of Mahaplag shall be at Barrios Malinao and San Isidro.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without the Executive approval, July 22, 1972.
