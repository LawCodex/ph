# Republic Act No. 12126

**An Act Establishing a Land Transportation Office (LTO) District Office in the Municipality of Sulop, Province of Davao Del Sur, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12126, March 14, 2025 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) DISTRICT OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SULOP, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL SUR, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby established a district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the Municipality of Sulop, Province of Davao del Sur.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the LTO District Office in the Municipality of Sulop, Province of Davao del Sur, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
