# Republic Act No. 7381

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Cagayan District Hospital in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan, From Fifty (50) to Seventy-five (75) Beds, and Upgrading It Into a Provincial Hospital to Be Known as the Aparri Provincial Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7381, April 10, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE CAGAYAN DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF APARRI, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS, AND UPGRADING IT INTO A PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL TO BE KNOWN AS THE APARRI PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Cagayan District Hospital in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan, is hereby expanded and upgraded from fifty (50) to seventy-five (75) beds and upgrading it into a provincial hospital to be known as the Aparri Provincial Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
