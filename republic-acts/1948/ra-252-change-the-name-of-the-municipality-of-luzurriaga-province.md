# Republic Act No. 252

**An Act to Change the Name of the Municipality of Luzurriaga, Province of Negros Oriental, to that of "Valencia"**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 252**

An Act to Change the Name of the Municipality of Luzurriaga, Province of Negros Oriental, to that of "Valencia"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of Luzurriaga, Province of Negros Oriental, is changed to that of "Valencia."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1948.
