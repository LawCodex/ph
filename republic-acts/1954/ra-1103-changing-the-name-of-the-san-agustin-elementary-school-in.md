# Republic Act No. 1103

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Agustin Elementary School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Santiago Garong Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1103**

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Agustin Elementary School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Santiago Garong Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Agustin Elementary School in the municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is changed to Santiago Garong Memorial School.
