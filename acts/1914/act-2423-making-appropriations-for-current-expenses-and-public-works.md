# Act No. 2423

**An Act Making Appropriations for Current Expenses and Public Works and Permanent Improvements in the Department of Mindanao and Sulu for the Fiscal Year Ending December thirty-First, Nineteen Hundred Fifteen.**

[ Act No. 2423, December 03, 1914 ]

AN ACT MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR CURRENT EXPENSES AND PUBLIC WORKS AND PERMANENT IMPROVEMENTS IN THE DEPARTMENT OF MINDANAO AND SULU FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING DECEMBER THIRTY-FIRST, NINETEEN HUNDRED FIFTEEN.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 2

Authority is hereby granted Lieutenant A. K. Buck, Philippine Constabulary, acting as assistant to the governor of the Province of Agusan for the district of Gibung-Simulao, for the period January first to August thirty-first, to receive compensation in addition to his pay and allowances as an officer of the Philippine Constabulary notwithstanding the provisions of Act Numbered One hundred and forty-eight, Philippine Commission.

The provisions of the last paragraph of section seven of Act Numbered Twenty-four hundred and eight are hereby made applicable to the Provinces of Agusan and Bukidnon as of date January first, nineteen hundred fourteen.

## Section 3

The unexpended and unappropriated balances of unexpended and funds pertaining to the treasury of the Department of Mindanao and Sulu on December thirty-first, nineteen hundred fourteen, shall be available for reappropriation by the administrative council subject to the supervision of the Philippine Commission as provided by section six (a) of Act Numbered Twenty-four hundred and eight.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, December 3, 1914.
