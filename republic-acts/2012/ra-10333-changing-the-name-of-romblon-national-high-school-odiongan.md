# Republic Act No. 10333

**An Act Changing the Name of Romblon National High School-Odiongan in the Municipality of Odiongan, Province of Romblon to Odiongan National High School**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-three day of July, two thousand twelve.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10333, November 15, 2012 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ROMBLON NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL-ODIONGAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ODIONGAN, PROVINCE OF ROMBLON TO ODIONGAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of a National High School._ – The name of Romblon National High School - Odiongan in the Municipality of Odiongan, Province of Romblon is hereby changed to Odiongan National High School.

## Section 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ \- The main campus of the Odiongan National High School shall continue to remain in its present location in the Municipality of Odiongan, Province of Romblon.ℒαwρhi৷

## Section 3

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on March 21, 2012 and August 28, 2012, respectively.
