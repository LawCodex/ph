# Batas Pambansa Blg. 177

**An Act Changing the Name of Talibon District Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Garcia Memorial Provincial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 177**

An Act Changing the Name of Talibon District Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Garcia Memorial Province Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Talibon District Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, is changed to Garcia Memorial Provincial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 12, 1982
