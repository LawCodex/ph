# Republic Act No. 2778

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2778**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of Sagnay, Province of Camarines Sur, are constituted as distinct and independent barrios of said municipality, to wit:

1\. The sitios of Aniog and Bolo, to be known as the barrio of Aniog; and

2\. The sitios of Bognalon, Suboc, Oyagno, Amislag and Cagbalogo, to be known as the barrio of Bognalon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
