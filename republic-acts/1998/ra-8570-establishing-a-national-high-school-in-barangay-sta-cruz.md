# Republic Act No. 8570

**An Act Establishing a National High School in Barangay Sta. Cruz, Municipality of Dumalag, Province of Capiz, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8570, February 27, 1998 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY STA. CRUZ, MUNICIPALITY OF DUMALAG, PROVINCE OF CAPIZ, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, a national high school in Barangay Sta. Cruz, Municipality of Dumalag, Province of Capiz.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports shall take the necessary steps for the establishment of the school so as to make it operational during the first school term after the effectivity of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
