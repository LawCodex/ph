# Republic Act No. 9234

**An act renaming the Camarines Sur Portion of the Quirino Highway as Rolando R. Andaya Highway**

Twelfth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eight day of July, two thousand and three.

**Republic Act No. 9234 January 16, 2004**

AN ACT RENAMING THE CAMARINES SUR PORTION OF THE QUIRINO HIGHWAY AS ROLANDO R. ANDAYA HIGHWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The existing Quirino Highway starting from the Camarines Sur-Quezon boundary up to the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur is hereby renamed Rolando R. Andaya Highway.

## SEC. 2

The Department of Public Works and Highways is hereby directed to implement the provisions of this Act.

## SEC. 3

All laws, decrees, orders and issuances or any part thereof inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

## SEC. 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
