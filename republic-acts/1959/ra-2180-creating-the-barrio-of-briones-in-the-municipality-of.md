# Republic Act No. 2180

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Briones in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2180**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Briones in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Eu-ok in the barrio of Mabilo, Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, is separated from said barrio, and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Briones.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 2, 1959.
