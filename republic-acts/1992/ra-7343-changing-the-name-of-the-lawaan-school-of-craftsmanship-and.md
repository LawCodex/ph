# Republic Act No. 7343

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lawaan School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries in Lawaan, Eastern Samar, to Lawaan National School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7343, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LAWAAN SCHOOL OF CRAFTSMANSHIP AND HOME INDUSTRIES IN LAWAAN, EASTERN SAMAR, TO LAWAAN NATIONAL SCHOOL OF CRAFTSMANSHIP AND HOME INDUSTRIES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lawaan School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries in Lawaan, Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Lawaan National School of Craftsmanship and Home Industries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
