# Republic Act No. 1715

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pinana-An in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar, into a Regular Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1715**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pinana-An in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar, into a Regular Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pinana-an in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known henceforth as the barrio of Pinalangga.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
