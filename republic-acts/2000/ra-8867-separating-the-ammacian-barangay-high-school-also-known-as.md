# Republic Act No. 8867

**An Act Separating the Ammacian Barangay High School, Also Known as Pinukpuk Vocational School-Ammacian Annex in the Municipality of Pinukpuk, Province of Kalinga, From the Pinukpuk Vocational School in the Municipality of Pinukpuk, and Converting It Into a National Agricultural and Vocational High School to Be Known as the Cal-Owan Agricultural and Vocational National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8867, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT SEPARATING THE AMMACIAN BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL, ALSO KNOWN AS PINUKPUK VOCATIONAL SCHOOL-AMMACIAN ANNEX IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PINUKPUK, PROVINCE OF KALINGA, FROM THE PINUKPUK VOCATIONAL SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PINUKPUK, AND CONVERTING IT INTO A NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL AND VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE CAL-OWAN AGRICULTURAL AND VOCATIONAL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Ammacian Barangay High School, also known as Pinukpuk Vocational School - Ammacian Annex in the Municipality of Pinukpuk, Province of Kalinga, is hereby separated from the Pinukpuk Vocational School in the Municipality of Pinukpuk and converted into a national agricultural and vocational high school to be known as the Cal-Owan Agricultural and Vocational National High School.ℒαwρhi৷

## Section 2

The school shall, in addition to its present secondary curriculum, offer technical and other vocational subjects.

## Sec. 3

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 4

All personnel, records, assets and liabilities of the Ammacian Barangay High School are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Cal-Owan Agricultural and Vocational National High School.

## Section 5

The amount necessary for the implementation of this Act shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the Pinukpuk Vocational School - Ammacian Annex. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of the Cal-Owan Agricultural and Vocational National High School shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 6

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, August 22, 2000.
