**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4011**

**An Act Changing the Name of Pangalalan Elementary School in Barrio Pangalalan, Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Juan Bugarin Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Pangalalan Elementary School in Barrio Pangalalan, Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Juan Bugarin Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
