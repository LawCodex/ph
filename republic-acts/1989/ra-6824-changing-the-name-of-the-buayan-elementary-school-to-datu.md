# Republic Act No. 6824

**An Act Changing the Name of the Buayan Elementary School to Datu Acad Dalid Central Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6824 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BUAYAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL TO DATU ACAD DALID CENTRAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Buayan Elementary School in General Santos City, Province of South Cotabato, shall hereafter be known and referred to as the Datu Acad Dalid Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
