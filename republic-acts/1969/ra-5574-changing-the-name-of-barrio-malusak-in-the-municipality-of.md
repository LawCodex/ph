# Republic Act No. 5574

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Malusak in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental M1nd0r0, to Barrio Narra**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5574, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO MALUSAK IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GLORIA, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL M1ND0R0, TO BARRIO NARRA.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Malusak in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Barrio Narra.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
