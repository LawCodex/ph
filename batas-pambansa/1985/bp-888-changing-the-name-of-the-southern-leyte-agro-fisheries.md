# Batas Pambansa Blg. 888

**An Act Changing the Name of the Southern Leyte Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Southern Leyte, to Ruperto K. Kangleon Memorial Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 888**

An Act Changing the Name of the Southern Leyte Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Southern Leyte, to Ruperto K. Kangleon Memorial Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute

## Section 1

The name of the Southern Leyte Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Southern Leyte, is changed to Ruperto K. Kangleon Memorial Agro-Fisheries Technical Institute.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 3, 1985
