# Republic Act No. 10811

**An Act Changing the Name of Juan Lumosad Memorial National High School - Monte Alegre Annex in Barangay Monthe Alegre, Municipality of Aurora, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur to Monte Alegre National High School**

Sixteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10811, May 11, 2016 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF JUAN LUMOSAD MEMORIAL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL - MONTE ALEGRE ANNEX IN BARANGAY MONTHE ALEGRE, MUNICIPALITY OF AURORA, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR TO MONTE ALEGRE NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Juan Lumosad Memorial National High School - Monte Alegre Annex in Barangay Monte Alegre, Municipality of Aurora, Province of Zamboanga del Sure is hereby changed to Monte Alegre National High School.

## Section 2

The main campus of the Monte Alegre National High School shall continue to remain in tis present location in Barangay Monte Alegre, Municipality of Aurora, Province of Zamboanga del Sur.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) FRANKLIN M. DRILON President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on August 10, 2015 and February 2, 2016, respectively.
