# Republic Act No. 3715

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sapa-Sapa Barrio School in the Municipality of Tandu Bas, Province of Sulu, to Tuan Ligaddung Lipae Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3715**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sapa-Sapa Barrio School in the Municipality of Tandu Bas, Province of Sulu, to Tuan Ligaddung Lipae Barrio School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sapa-Sapa Barrio School in the Municipality of Tandu Bas, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Tuan Ligaddung Lipae Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
