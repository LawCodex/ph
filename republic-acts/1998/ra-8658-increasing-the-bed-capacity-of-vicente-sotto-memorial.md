# Republic Act No. 8658

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center, Located in Cebu City, From Four Hundred (400) to Eight Hundred (800) Bed Capacity and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8658, June 22, 1998 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF VICENTE SOTTO MEMORIAL MEDICAL CENTER, LOCATED IN CEBU CITY, FROM FOUR HUNDRED (400) TO EIGHT HUNDRED (800) BED CAPACITY AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center is hereby increased from four hundred (400) to eight hundred (800) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

The Department of Health is directed to implement this Act.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in two (2) national newspapers of general circulation.
