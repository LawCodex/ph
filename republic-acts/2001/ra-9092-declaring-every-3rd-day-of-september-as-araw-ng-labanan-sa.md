# Republic Act No. 9092

**An Act Declaring Every 3rd Day of September as Araw Ng Labanan Sa Imus, a Special Working Holiday in the Municipality of Imus, Cavite**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9092 April 08, 2001**

AN ACT DECLARING EVERY 3RD DAY OF SEPTEMBER AS ARAW NG LABANAN SA IMUS, A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF IMUS, CAVITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

September 3 of every year is hereby declared as Araw ng Labanan sa Imus, a special working holiday in the Municipality of Imus, Cavite, in commemoration of the historic "Labanan sa Imus."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately after its publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) AQUILINO Q. PIMENTEL, JR. President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE, JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on February 6, 2001 and June 8, 2000, respectively.
