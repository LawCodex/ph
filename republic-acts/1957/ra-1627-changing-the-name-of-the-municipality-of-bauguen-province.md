# Republic Act No. 1627

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bauguen, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Salcedo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1627**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bauguen, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Salcedo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Bauguen, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Salcedo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
