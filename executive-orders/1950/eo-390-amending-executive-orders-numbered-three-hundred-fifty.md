# Executive Order No. 390

**Amending Executive Orders Numbered Three Hundred Fifty-Three, Three Hundred Forty-Two, Three Hundred Fifty-Nine, and Three Hundred Sixty-Three, and for Other Purposes.**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 390, December 27, 1950 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDERS NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED FIFTY-THREE, THREE HUNDRED FORTY-TWO, THREE HUNDRED FIFTY-NINE, AND THREE HUNDRED SIXTY-THREE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

## Section 2

The ceiling prices hereinabove fixed shall include the 7 per cent sales tax.

## Section 3

Section 1 of Executive Order No, 342 dated August 14, 1950, fixing the ceiling prices of commodities for the different provinces of the Philippines is hereby amended by excluding drugs, medicines, and household remedies from the operation thereof. In fixing the provincial ceiling prices of drugs, medicines, and household remedies, the following increases to their basic ceiling prices in Manila shall be allowed:

Zone I, 2 per cent; Zone II, 3 per cent; Zone III, 4 per cent; Zone IV, 4.5 per cent; and Zone V, 5 per cent: _Provided,_ however, That in cases of Kotex, Kleenex, Modess, and Nursing Bottles, in the Visayan provinces, 3 per cent shall he added and in the Mindanao provinces, 5 per cent shall be added.

## Section 4

Section 1 of Executive Order No. 359 dated November 3, 1950, is hereby amended by eliminating the producers’ ceiling prices.

## Section 5

Section 1 of Executive Order No. 363 dated November 9, 1950, is hereby amended by eliminating the word “refined” in the last sentence so as to read as follows:

| “Sugar packed in cellophane or cotton bags | ........................... | P0.05 additional per kilo.” |
|---|---|---|

## Section 6

This Order shall take effect five days from the date hereof.

Done in the City of Manila, this 27th day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fifth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
