# Executive Order No. 748

**Amending Executive Order No. 708 Dated July 27, 1981**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 748 November 19, 1981**

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 708 DATED JULY 27, 1981

By virtue of the powers vested in me by the law, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby amend Section 8 of Executive Order No. 708 dated July 27, 1981, to read as follows:

"Sec. 8. The following entities shall continue to be directly under the President:

1\. National Intelligence and Security Authority

2\. Office of the Presidential Assistant on National Minorities

3\. President's Center for Special Studies

4\. Presidential Commission on Reorganization

5\. Development Coordinating Council for Leyte and Samar (DCCLS)

Done in the City of Manila, this 19th day of November, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and eighty-one.
