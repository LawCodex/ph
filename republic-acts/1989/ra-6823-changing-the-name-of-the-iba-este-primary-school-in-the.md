# Republic Act No. 6823

**An Act Changing the Name of the Iba Este Primary School in the Municipality of Calumpit, Province of Bulacan, to Dr. Vicente Reyes Memorial School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6823 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE IBA ESTE PRIMARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CALUMPIT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO DR. VICENTE REYES MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Iba Este Primary School in the Municipality of Calumpit, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Dr. Vicente Reyes Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
