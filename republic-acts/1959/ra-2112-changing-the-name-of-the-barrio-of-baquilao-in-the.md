# Republic Act No. 2112

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baquilao in the Municipality of Alcala, Province of Cagayan, to San Esteban**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2112**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baquilao in the Municipality of Alcala, Province of Cagayan, to San Esteban**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Baquilao in the Municipality of Alcala, Province of Cagayan, is changed to San Esteban.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
