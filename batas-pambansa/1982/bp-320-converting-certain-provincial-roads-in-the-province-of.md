# Batas Pambansa Blg. 320

**An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Antique Into National Roads.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 320**

An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Antique into National Roads

## Section 1

The following provincial roads in the Province of Antique are converted into and declared as national roads:

(a) Anini-y-Tobias Fornier Hamtic Road

(b) Bugasong Walderama Road

(c) Bagumbayan-Pandan-Libertad to Aklan-Antique Boundary

(d) Sibalom Egrana-Bia-an Hamtic Road.

## Section 2

Any provision of law, decree, order, rule or regulation inconsistent with this Act is repealed or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
