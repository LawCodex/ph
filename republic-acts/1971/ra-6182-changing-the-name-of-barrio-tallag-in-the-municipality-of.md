# Republic Act No. 6182

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tallag in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, to Barrio Biuag.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6182, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO TALLAG IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CABAGAN, PROVINCE OF ISABELA, TO BARRIO BIUAG.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Tallag in the Municipality of Cabagan, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to Barrio Biuag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
