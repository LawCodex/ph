# Republic Act No. 7602

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bayambang National High School, Carungay Annex, in Barangay Carungay, Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, To Moises B. Rebamontan Memorial High School**

Fifth Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fifth day of July, two thousand twenty two.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7602, June 03, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BAYAMBANG NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CARUNGAY ANNEX, IN BARANGAY CARUNGAY, MUNICIPALITY OF BAYAMBANG, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN, TO MOISES B. REBAMONTAN MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bayambang National High School, Carungay Annex, in Barangay Carungay, Municipality of Bayambang Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Moises B. Rebamontan Memorial High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval
