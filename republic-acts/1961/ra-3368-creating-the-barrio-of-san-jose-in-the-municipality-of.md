# Republic Act No. 3368

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Jose in the Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3368**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Jose in the Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Sangaynapo, Caloyan, Napuyan, Bariocan and Maliwanag in the Municipality of Polanco, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
