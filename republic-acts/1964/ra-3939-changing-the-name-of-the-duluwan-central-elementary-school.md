# Republic Act No. 3939

**An Act Changing the Name of the Duluwan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Datu Piang, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Gumbay Piang Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3939**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Duluwan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Datu Piang, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Gumbay Piang Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Duluwan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Datu Piang, Province of Cotabato, is hereby changed to Datu Gumbay Piang Central Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
