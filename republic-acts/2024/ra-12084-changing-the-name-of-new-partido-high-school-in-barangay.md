# Republic Act No. 12084

**An Act Changing the Name of New Partido High School in Barangay Catagbacan, Municipality of Goa, Province of Camarines Sur to New Partido National High School**

Nineteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12084, December 13, 2024 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF NEW PARTIDO HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY CATAGBACAN, MUNICIPALITY OF GOA, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR TO NEW PARTIDO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of New Partido High School in Barangay Catagbacan, Municipality of Goa, Province of Camarines Sur is hereby changed to New Partido National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.ℒαwρhi৷
