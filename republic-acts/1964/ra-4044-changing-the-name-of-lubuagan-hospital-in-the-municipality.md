# Republic Act No. 4044

**An Act Changing the Name of Lubuagan Hospital in the Municipality of Lubuagan, Mountain Province, to Kalinga Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4044**

**An Act Changing the Name of Lubuagan Hospital in the Municipality of Lubuagan, Mountain Province, to Kalinga Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lubuagan Hospital in the Municipality of Lubuagan, Mountain Province, is hereby changed to Kalinga Hospital.(awÞhi(

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
