# Republic Act No. 2845

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Simbahan in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2845**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Simbahan in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Mabudo, Simbahan, Talagas and Abulig are hereby separated from the barrio of Dinalungan in the Municipality of Casiguran, Province of Quezon, and are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio to be known as the barrio of Simbahan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
