# Republic Act No. 11138

**An Act Declaring January 11 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Bataan in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary to Be Known as Bataan Foundation Day**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11138**

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY 11 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF BATAAN IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY TO BE KNOWN AS "BATAAN FOUNDATION DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

January 11 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Bataan in commemoration of its founding anniversary to be known as "Bataan Foundation Day".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

**GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
Speaker of the House of Representatives

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on August 29, 2017 and September 17, 2018, respectively.
