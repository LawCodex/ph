# Republic Act No. 4471

**An Act Granting the Name of Calumpang Community School in the Municipality of Naval, Sub-Province of Biliran, Province of Leyte, to Father Sergio Eamiguel Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4471**

An Act Granting the Name of Calumpang Community School in the Municipality of Naval, Sub-Province of Biliran, Province of Leyte, to Father Sergio Eamiguel Community School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Calumpang Community School in the Municipality of Naval, Sub-Province of Biliran, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Father Sergio Eamiguel Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
