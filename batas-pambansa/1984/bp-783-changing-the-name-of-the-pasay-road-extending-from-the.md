# Batas Pambansa Blg. 783

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pasay Road Extending From the Municipality of Makati to Pasay City Both in Metropolitan Manila, to Antonio S. Arnaiz Avenue**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 783**

An Act Changing the Name of the Pasay Road Extending from the Municipality of Makati to Pasay City Both in Metropolitan Manila, to Antonio S. Arnaiz Avenue

## Section 1

The name of Pasay Road extending from the Fort-Bonifacio Road (station 12.820) in the Municipality of Makati, to Roxas Boulevard-Libertad intersection (station 4.330) in Pasay City both in Metropolitan Manila, is changed to Antonio S. Arnaiz Avenue.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
