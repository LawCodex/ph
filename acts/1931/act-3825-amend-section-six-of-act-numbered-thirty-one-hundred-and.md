# Act No. 3825

**An Act to Amend Section Six of Act Numbered thirty-One Hundred and Sixty-Six, Entitled "An Act Providing for the Use of Rinderpest Vaccine in the Control of Rinderpest and Other Contagious and Infectious Cattle Diseases, Appropriates the Sum of One Hundred thousand Pesos, and for Other Purposes."**

[ Act No. 3825, October 08, 1931 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION SIX OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX, ENTITLED "AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE USE OF RINDERPEST VACCINE IN THE CONTROL OF RINDERPEST AND OTHER CONTAGIOUS AND INFECTIOUS CATTLE DISEASES, APPROPRIATES THE SUM OF ONE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Section six of Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and sixty-six, entitled "An Act providing for the use of rinderpest vaccine in the control of rinderpest and other contagious and infectious cattle diseases, appropriates the sum of one hundred thousand pesos, and for other purposes," is hereby amended to read as follows:

"**Section 6.** The funds herein appropriated shall be expended for the purchase of animals, equipments, supplies, and medicines required for the manufacture and distribution of the rinderpest vaccine, serum, and virulent blood; for salaries and wages of additional technical and other personnel connected with the manufacture and application of these products; and for rents and freights: _Provided,_ That any balance of the funds herein appropriated at the end of each fiscal year shall not revert to the Insular Treasury but shall be used for the purposes for which it is appropriated until exhausted."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, October 8, 1931.
