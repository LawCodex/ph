# Republic Act No. 6666

**An Act Changing the Name of the Siling Bata Elementary School Situated in the Municipality of Pandi, Province of Bulacan, to Matias Salvador Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6666, June 22, 1988 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SILING BATA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL SITUATED IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PANDI, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO MATIAS SALVADOR MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Siling Bata Elementary School situated in the Municipality of Pandi, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Matias Salvador Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
