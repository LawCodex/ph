# Republic Act No. 10227

**An Act Renaming the Aparri School of Arts and Trade (ASAT) in Aparri, Cagayan Into the Aparri Polytechnic Institute (API)**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-three day of July, two thousand twelve.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10227**

AN ACT RENAMING THE APARRI SCHOOL OF ARTS AND TRADE (ASAT) IN APARRI, CAGAYAN INTO THE APARRI POLYTECHNIC INSTITUTE (API)

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of School._ – The name of the Aparri School of Arts and Trade (ASAT) in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan is hereby changed to the Aparri Polytechnic Institute (API).

## Section 2

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on March 9, 2011 and July 24, 2012, respectively.
