# Republic Act No. 7358

**An Act Changing the Name of the La Paz Elementary School, Laoag City, to Eulalio F. Siazon Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7358, April 08, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LA PAZ ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, LAOAG CITY, TO EULALIO F. SIAZON MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the La Paz Elementary School situated in Barangay La Paz, Laoag City, is hereby changed to Eulalio F. Siazon Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports is hereby directed to promulgate and issue rules for the implementation of this Act.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
