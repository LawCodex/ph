# Republic Act No. 1656

**An Act Changing the Name of the Marber Interior Elementary School in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, to New Clarin Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1656**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Marber Interior Elementary School in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, to New Clarin Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Marber Interior Elementary School in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, is changed to New Clarin Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
