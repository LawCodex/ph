# Republic Act No. 3387

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Curva in the Municipality of Medellin, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3387**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Curva in the Municipality of Medellin, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Curva in the Municipality of Medellin, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Curva.1avvphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
