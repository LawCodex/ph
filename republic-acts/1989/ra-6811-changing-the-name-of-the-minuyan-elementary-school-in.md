# Republic Act No. 6811

**An Act Changing the Name of the Minuyan Elementary School in Barangay Minuyan, Municipality of Norzagaray, Province of Bulacan, to Timoteo Policarpio Memorial Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6811 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MINUYAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MINUYAN, MUNICIPALITY OF NORZAGARAY, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO TIMOTEO POLICARPIO MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Minuyan Elementary School located in Barangay Minuyan, Municipality of Norzagaray, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Timoteo Policarpio Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
