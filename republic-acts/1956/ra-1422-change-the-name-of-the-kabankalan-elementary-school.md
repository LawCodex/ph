# Republic Act No. 1422

**An Act to Change the Name of the Kabankalan Elementary School, Situated at Kabankalan, Negros Occidental, to Esteban R. Abada Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1422**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Kabankalan Elementary School, Situated at Kabankalan, Negros Occidental, to Esteban R. Abada Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Kabankalan Elementary School situated at Kabankalan, Negros Occidental, is hereby named and to be known henceforth as the Esteban R. Abada Memorial School.

## Section 2

The Director of Public Schools shall effect the change immediately upon the approval in this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1956.
