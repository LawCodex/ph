# Republic Act No. 6803

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mayo Elementary School in Barangay Mayo, Municipality of Mati, Province of Davao Oriental, to Vicente Almario, Sr. Memorial School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6803 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MAYO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MAYO, MUNICIPALITY OF MATI, PROVINCE OF DAVAO ORIENTAL, TO VICENTE ALMARIO, SR. MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mayo Elementary School in Purok Malipayon, Barangay Mayo, Municipality of Mati, Province of Davao Oriental, is hereby changed to Vicente Almario, Sr. Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
