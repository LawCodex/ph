# Act No. 3991

**An Act Granting to Jose S. Valenciano the Right to Sell, Convey, Assign or Transfer His Franchise and All Property and Rights Acquired thereunder to Install, Operate and Maintain An Electric Current, Heat and Power System in the Municipality of Camalig, Province of Albay in Favor of Saturnino Benito.**

[ Act No. 3991, December 03, 1932 ]

AN ACT GRANTING TO JOSE S. VALENCIANO THE RIGHT TO SELL, CONVEY, ASSIGN OR TRANSFER HIS FRANCHISE AND ALL PROPERTY AND RIGHTS ACQUIRED THEREUNDER TO INSTALL, OPERATE AND MAINTAIN AN ELECTRIC CURRENT, HEAT AND POWER SYSTEM IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CAMALIG, PROVINCE OF ALBAY IN FAVOR OF SATURNINO BENITO.

Be it enacted by the Senate end House of Representatives of the Philippines in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Subject to the terms and conditions prescribed in section fifteen of Act Numbered Thirty-six hundred and sixty-four, Jose S. Valenciano is hereby authorized to sell, lease, grant, convey, assign, give in usufruct, or transfer his franchise and all property and rights acquired thereunder to install, operate and maintain an electric current, heat and power system in the municipality of Camalig, Province of Albay, in favor of Saturnino Benito.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately on its approval.

Approved, December 3, 1932.
