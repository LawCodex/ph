# Republic Act No. 6668

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Francisco Elementary School in Barangay San Francisco, San Pablo City, Province of Laguna, to Don Enrique Bautista Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6668, June 22, 1988 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN FRANCISCO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO CITY, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA, TO DON ENRIQUE BAUTISTA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Francisco Elementary School is hereby changed to Don Enrique Bautista Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
