# Republic Act No. 5581

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Bucana in the Municipality of Siocon, Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, to Barrio Suhaile Arabi**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5581, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO BUCANA IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SIOCON, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, TO BARRIO SUHAILE ARABI.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Bucana, Municipality of Siocon, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Suhaile Arabi.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
