# Republic Act No. 6693

**An Act Renaming the Barangay Bulwagan Elementary School in the Municipality of Hinoba-An, Province of Negros Occidental, as the Paz Vda De Escanilla Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6693, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE BARANGAY BULWAGAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HINOBA-AN, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, AS THE PAZ VDA DE ESCANILLA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Barangay Bulwagan Elementary School locates in Barangay Bulwagan, Municipality of Hinoba-an, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby renamed as the Paz Vda de Escanilla Elementary School.1a⍵⍴h!1
