# Batas Pambansa Blg. 655

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ilaya-IVIsan Elementary School in the Municipality of IVIsan, Province of Capiz, to Don Juan S. Jarencio Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 655**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ilaya-Ivisan Elementary School in the Municipality of Ivisan, Province of Capiz, to Don Juan S. Jarencio Memorial School**

## Section 1

The Ilaya-Ivisan Elementary School in the Municipality of Ivisan, Province of Capiz, shall be known as the Don Juan S. Jarencio Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
