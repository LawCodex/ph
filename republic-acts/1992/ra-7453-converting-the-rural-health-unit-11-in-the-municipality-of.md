# Republic Act No. 7453

**An Act Converting the Rural Health Unit 11 in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Cebu, Into A Five-bed Capacity Health Infirmary, To Be Known As the Talisay Health Infirmary, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7453, April 27, 1992 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE RURAL HEALTH UNIT 11 IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALISAY, PROVINCE OF CEBU, INTO A FIVE-BED CAPACITY HEALTH INFIRMARY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE TALISAY HEALTH INFIRMARY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The rural health unit II in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Cebu, is hereby converted into a five-bed capacity health infirmary, to be known as the Talisay Health Infirmary.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.
