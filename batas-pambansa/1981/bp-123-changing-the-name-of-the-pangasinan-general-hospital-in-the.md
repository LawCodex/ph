# Batas Pambansa Blg. 123

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pangasinan General Hospital in the City of Dagupan to Governor Teofilo Sison Provincial Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 123**

An Act Changing the Name of the Pangasinan General Hospital in the City of Dagupan to Governor Teofilo Sison Provincial Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Pangasinan General Hospital in the City of Dagupan is hereby changed to Governor Teofilo Sison Provincial Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 10, 1981
