# Republic Act No. 4218

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of San Policarpio, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4218**

An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of San Policarpio, Province of Samar

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Sta. Cruz, Tan-awan, Bangon, Guin-osokan, Barras, Lipata and Libas in the Municipality of San Policarpio, Province of Samar, are converted into barrios of said municipality, to be known as the Barrios of Sta. Cruz, Tan-awan, Bangon, Guin-osakan, Barras, Lipata and Libas, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
