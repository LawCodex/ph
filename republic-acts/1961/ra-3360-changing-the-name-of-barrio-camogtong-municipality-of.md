# Republic Act No. 3360

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Camogtong, Municipality of Despujols, Province of Romblon, to Mabini**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3360**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Camogtong, Municipality of Despujols, Province of Romblon, to Mabini**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Camogtong in the Municipality of Despujols, Province of Romblon, shall hereafter be known as Barrio Mabini.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
