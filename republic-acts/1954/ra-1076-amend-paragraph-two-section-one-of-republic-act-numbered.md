# Republic Act No. 1076

**An Act to Amend Paragraph Two, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred and Forty-Eight**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1076**

**An Act to Amend Paragraph Two, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred and Forty-Eight**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

Paragraph two, section one of Republic Act Numbered Nine hundred and forty-eight is amended to read as follows:

"2. For the construction of the North General Hospital Building, including the purchase of the site, if necessary, and of hospital equipment; one million five hundred thousand pesos: _Provided,_ That not more than two hundred thousand pesos may be used for the purchase, reclamation or development of the site."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1954.
