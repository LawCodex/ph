# Republic Act No. 2215

**An Act Converting the Pototan Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pototan, Province of Iloilo, into a General Hospital to be Known as the Pototan General Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2215**

**An Act Converting the Pototan Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pototan, Province of Iloilo, into a General Hospital to be Known as the Pototan General Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Pototan Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pototan, Province of Iloilo, is converted into a general hospital to be known as the Pototan General Hospital.

## Section 2

The expenses for the operation and maintenance shall be governed by the provisions of Republic Act Numbered One thousand nine hundred thirty-nine, otherwise known as the Hospital Financing Act: _Provided,_ That the present appropriation for its operation and maintenance shall continue to be borne by the National Government and shall be increased as conditions warrant.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
