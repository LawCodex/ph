# Act No. 2074

**An Act Amending Act Numbered Two thousand and Seventy- Two Entitled "An Act Conferring Jurisdiction Upon Justices of the Peace in the Moro Province to Try Persons Charged With Infraction of Municipal Ordinances" By Postponing the Date of the Taking Effect thereof.**

[ Act No. 2074, September 26, 1911 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ACT NUMBERED TWO THOUSAND AND SEVENTY- TWO ENTITLED "AN ACT CONFERRING JURISDICTION UPON JUSTICES OF THE PEACE IN THE MORO PROVINCE TO TRY PERSONS CHARGED WITH INFRACTION OF MUNICIPAL ORDINANCES" BY POSTPONING THE DATE OF THE TAKING EFFECT THEREOF.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

Section two of Act Numbered Two thousand and seventy-two, entitled "An Act conferring jurisdiction upon justices of the peace in the Moro Province to try persons charged with infraction of municipal ordinances" is hereby amended to read as follows:

"**Section 2.** This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and twelve."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, September 26, 1911.
