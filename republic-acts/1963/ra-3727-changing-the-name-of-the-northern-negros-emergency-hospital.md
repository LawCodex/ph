# Republic Act No. 3727

**An Act Changing the Name of the Northern Negros Emergency Hospital in Balintawak, Escalante, Negros Occidental, to Congressman Vicente F. Gustilo Memorial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3727**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Northern Negros Emergency Hospital in Balintawak, Escalante, Negros Occidental, to Congressman Vicente F. Gustilo Memorial Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Northern Negros Emergency Hospital in Balintawak, Escalante, Negros Occidental, is changed to Congressman Vicente F. Gustilo Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
