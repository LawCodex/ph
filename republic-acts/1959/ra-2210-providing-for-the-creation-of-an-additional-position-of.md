# Republic Act No. 2210

**An Act Providing for the Creation of an Additional Position of Municipal Judge for the City of Cavite, Amending for the Purpose Section Seventy-Four of Commonwealth Act Numbered Five Hundred Forty-Seven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2210**

**An Act Providing for the Creation of an Additional Position of Municipal Judge for the City of Cavite, Amending for the Purpose Section Seventy-Four of Commonwealth Act Numbered Five Hundred Forty-Seven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The first paragraph of section seventy-four of Commonwealth Act Numbered Five hundred forty-seven, otherwise known as the Charter of the City of Cavite, is amended to read as follows:

"**Sec. 74.** _Regular, auxiliary and acting judges of municipal court.—_ There shall be a municipal court for the City of Cavite, for which two municipal judges and an auxiliary municipal judge shall be appointed."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 15, 1959.
