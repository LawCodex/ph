# Republic Act No. 2512

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of Taluksangay District, City of Zamboanga, to Pasilmanta, Sakol Island**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2512**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of Taluksangay District, City of Zamboanga, to Pasilmanta, Sakol Island**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of Taluksangay District, City of Zamboanga, is hereby transferred from Taluksangay proper to Pasilmanta, Sakol Island.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
