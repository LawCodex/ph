# Republic Act No. 7177

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Siaton, Province of Negros Oriental, From Twenty-five (25) to Fifty (50) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7177, January 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE SIATON, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL, FROM TWENTY-FIVE (25) TO FIFTY (50) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Siaton District Hospital in the Municipality of Siaton, Province of Negros Oriental, is hereby increased from twenty-five (25) to fifty (50) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
