# Republic Act No. 2901

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Binanwaan in the Municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2901**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Binanwaan in the Municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Binanwa-an in the Municipality of Igbaras, Province of Iloilo, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Binanwa-an.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
