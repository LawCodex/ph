# Act No. 3158

**An Act to Repeal Act Numbered Twenty-One Hundred and Seventy-Six**

[ Act No. 3158, March 08, 1924 ]

AN ACT TO REPEAL ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-ONE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-SIX

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Act Numbered Twenty-one hundred and seventy-six is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 8, 1924.
