# Batas Pambansa Blg. 381

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Ponot in Zamboanga Del Norte to Jose Dalman.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 381**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Ponot in Zamboanga Del Norte to Jose Dalman

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Ponot in the Province of Zamboanga del Norte is hereby changed to Jose Dalman.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 8, 1983
