# Republic Act No. 6815

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lafu Elementary School in Barangay San Antonio, Municipality of Lallo, Province of Cagayan, to San Antonio Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6815 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LAFU ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SAN ANTONIO, MUNICIPALITY OF LALLO, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, TO SAN ANTONIO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lafu Elementary School in Barangay San Antonio, Municipality of Lallo, Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to San Antonio Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
