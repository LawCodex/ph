# Batas Pambansa Blg. 817

**An Act Changing the Name of San Pablo Elementary School in the Municipality of Manapla, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Emilio Miraflores, Sr. Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 817**

An Act Changing the Name of San Pablo Elementary School in the Municipality of Manapla, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Emilio Miraflores, Sr. Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of San Pablo Elementary School in the Municipality of Manapla, Province of Negros Occidental, is changed to Don Emilio Miraflores, Sr. Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
