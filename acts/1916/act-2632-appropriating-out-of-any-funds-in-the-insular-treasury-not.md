# Act No. 2632

**An Act Appropriating Out of Any Funds in the Insular Treasury Not Otherwise Appropriated the Sum of Seven thousand Five Hundred Pesos for Assistance of Private Schools Estavlished in the Mountain Province.**

[ Act No. 2632, February 23, 1916 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING OUT OF ANY FUNDS IN THE INSULAR TREASURY NOT OTHERWISE APPROPRIATED THE SUM OF SEVEN THOUSAND FIVE HUNDRED PESOS FOR ASSISTANCE OF PRIVATE SCHOOLS ESTAVLISHED IN THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The sum of seven thousand five hundred pesos is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be allotted by the Secretary of the Interior, for the assistance of private schools established in the MOuntain Province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its passage.

Enacted, February 23, 1916.
