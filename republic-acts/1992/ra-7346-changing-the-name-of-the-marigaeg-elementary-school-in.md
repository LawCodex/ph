# Republic Act No. 7346

**An Act Changing the Name of the Marigaeg Elementary School in Salaman, Lebak, Sultan Kudarat, to Ampad-guiabar Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7346, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MARIGAEG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN SALAMAN, LEBAK, SULTAN KUDARAT, TO AMPAD-GUIABAR MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Marigaeg Elementary School in Salaman, Lebak, Sultan Kudarat, is hereby changed to Ampad-Guiabar Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take upon its approval.
