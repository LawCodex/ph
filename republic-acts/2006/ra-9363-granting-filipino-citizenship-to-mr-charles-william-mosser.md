# Republic Act No. 9363

**An Act Granting Filipino Citizenship to Mr. Charles William Mosser, with all the Rights, Privileges and Prerogatives Appurtenant Thereto**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9363 November 30, 2006**

AN ACT GRANTING FILIPINO CITIZENSHIP TO MR. CHARLES WILLIAM MOSSER, WITH ALL THE RIGHTS, PRIVILEGES AND PREROGATIVES APPURTENANT THERETO

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

There is hereby granted a special recognition to Mr. Charles William Mosser for his long and outstanding service to the Filipino people through his involvement in developmental and socio-civic projects in the country, proving himself more than worthy to be a Filipino.

## SEC. 2

The Republic of the Philippines will be considered the permanent place of abode of Mr. Charles William Mosser to which he shall forever owe and pledge allegiance and loyalty.

## SEC. 3

Philippine citizenship is hereby granted to Mr. Charles William Mosser, with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## SEC. 4

This Act shall take effect upon approval.

Approved,
