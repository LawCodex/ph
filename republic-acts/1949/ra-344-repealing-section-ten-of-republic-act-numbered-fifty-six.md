# Republic Act No. 344

**An Act Repealing Section Ten of Republic Act Numbered Fifty-Six**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 344**

AN ACT REPEALING SECTION TEN OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED FIFTY-SIX

## Section 1

Section ten of Republic Act Numbered Fifty-six is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 14, 1949
