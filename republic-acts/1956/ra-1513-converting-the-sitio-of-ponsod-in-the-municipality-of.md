# Republic Act No. 1513

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Ponsod, in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Ponsod**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1513**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Ponsod, in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Ponsod**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Ponsod, in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol, is hereby converted to a barrio to be known as Barrio Ponsod.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
