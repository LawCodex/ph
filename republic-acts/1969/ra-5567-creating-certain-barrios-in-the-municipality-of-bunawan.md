# Republic Act No. 5567

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Bunawan, Province of Agusan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5567, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING CERTAIN BARRIOS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BUNAWAN, PROVINCE OF AGUSAN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in. Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of San Andrea and Ko-o in the Municipality of Bunawan, Province of Agusan, are hereby converted into barrios of said municipality, to be known as barrios San Andres and San Rafael, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
