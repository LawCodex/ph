# Republic Act No. 1311

**An Act Making the Barrio of New Sabonga in the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, as a Part of the Municipality of Compostela of the Same Province**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1311**

**An Act Making the Barrio of New Sabonga in the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, as a Part of the Municipality of Compostela of the Same Province**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of New Sabonga in the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, is hereby declared as a part of the Municipality of Compostela of the same province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
