# Republic Act No. 5407

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Lacub in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Fabian Marcos**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5407**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Lacub in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Fabian Marcos

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Lacub in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Fabian Marcos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
