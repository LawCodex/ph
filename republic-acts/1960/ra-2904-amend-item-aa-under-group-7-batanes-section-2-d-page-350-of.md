# Republic Act No. 2904

**An Act to Amend Item (aa), Under Group 7, Batanes, Section 2-d, Page 350 of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Three Hundred One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2904**

**An Act to Amend Item (aa), Under Group 7, Batanes, Section 2-d, Page 350 of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Three Hundred One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (aa), under Group 7, Batanes, Section 2-d, page 350 of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred one is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(aa) For drilling and construction of artesian wells in the Municipality of Basco ₱10,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
