# Batas Pambansa Blg. 209

**An Act Changing the Name of Mababang Paaralan Ng Orani North Annex in Barangay Paraiso, Municipality of Orani, Province of Bataan, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Paraiso.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 209**

An Act Changing the Name of Mababang Paaralan Ng Orani North Annex in Barangay Paraiso, Municipality of Orani, Province of Bataan, to Mababang Paaralan Ng Paraiso

## Section 1

The name of Mababang Paaralan ng Orani North Annex in Barangay Paraiso, Municipality of Orani, Province of Bataan, is hereby changed to Mababang Paaralan ng Paraiso.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
