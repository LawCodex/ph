# Batas Pambansa Blg. 666

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bunawan Central Elementary School in the City of Davao to Daniel M. Perez Central Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 666**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bunawan Central Elementary School in the City of Davao to Daniel M. Perez Central Elementary School**

## Section 1

The name of the Bunawan Central Elementary School in the City of Davao is changed to Daniel M. Perez Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
