# Republic Act No. 1529

**An Act Amending Item 1, Under “Ad. Mindoro Occidental,” Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred and Eleven, Entitled “An Act Appropriating Funds for Public Works”**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1529**

**An Act Amending Item 1, Under "ad. Mindoro Occidental," Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred and Eleven, Entitled "An Act Appropriating Funds for Public Works"**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 1, under "ad. Mindoro Occidental," section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred and eleven, is amended to read as follows:

| "1. Wawa Barrio School Building, Abra de Ilog, Construction | ₱4,500.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
