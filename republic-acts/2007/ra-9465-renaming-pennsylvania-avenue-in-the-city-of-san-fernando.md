# Republic Act No. 9465

**An Act Renaming Pennsylvania Avenue in the City of San Fernando, Province of La Union as Governor Joaquin L. Ortega Avenue**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9465 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT RENAMING PENNSYLVANIA AVENUE IN THE CITY OF SAN FERNANDO, PROVINCE OF LA UNION AS GOVERNOR JOAQUIN L. ORTEGA AVENUE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Pennsylvania Avenue, a secondary national road situated in the City of San Fernando, Province of La Union is hereby renamed as Governor Joaquin L. Ortega Avenue.

## Sec. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
