# Republic Act No. 5604

**An Act Changing the Name of Baybay Primary School in the City of Roxas to Judge Conrado Barrios Memorial School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5604, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BAYBAY PRIMARY SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF ROXAS TO JUDGE CONRADO BARRIOS MEMORIAL SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and; House of Representatives, of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Baybay Primary School in the City of Roxas, is hereby changed to Judge Conrado Barrios Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
