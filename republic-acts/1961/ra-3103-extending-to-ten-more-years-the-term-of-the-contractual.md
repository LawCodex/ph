# Republic Act No. 3103

**An Act Extending to Ten More Years the Term of the Contractual Grant Under Commonwealth Act Numbered Four Hundred and Eighty-Five**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3103**

**An Act Extending to Ten More Years the Term of the Contractual Grant Under Commonwealth Act Numbered Four Hundred and Eighty-Five**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The term of the contractual grant under Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred eighty-five is hereby extended to ten more years from the date the original term of twenty-five years shall have expired, subject to the same terms and conditions.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1961.
