# Republic Act No. 2158

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon, to Municipality of Banton**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2158**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon, to Municipality of Banton**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Jones, Province of Romblon, is changed to Municipality of Banton.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
