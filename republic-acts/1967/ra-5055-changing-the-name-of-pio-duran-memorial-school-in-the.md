# Republic Act No. 5055

**An Act Changing the Name of Pio Duran Memorial School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, to Marcial O. Rañola Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5055**

An Act Changing the Name of Pio Duran Memorial School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, to Marcial O. Rañola Memorial School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Pio Duran Memorial School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, is hereby changed to Marcial O. Rañola Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
