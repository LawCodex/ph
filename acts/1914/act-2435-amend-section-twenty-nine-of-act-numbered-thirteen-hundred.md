# Act No. 2435

**An Act to Amend Section Twenty-Nine of Act Numbered thirteen Hundred and Ninety-Six, Known as the Special Provincial Government Act, By Authorizing the Delegate of the Secretary of the Interior for the Non-Christian People to Make the Official VIsit and Inspection of the Special Government Provinces.**

[ Act No. 2435, December 23, 1914 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION TWENTY-NINE OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTEEN HUNDRED AND NINETY-SIX, KNOWN AS THE SPECIAL PROVINCIAL GOVERNMENT ACT, BY AUTHORIZING THE DELEGATE OF THE SECRETARY OF THE INTERIOR FOR THE NON-CHRISTIAN PEOPLE TO MAKE THE OFFICIAL VISIT AND INSPECTION OF THE SPECIAL GOVERNMENT PROVINCES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

Section twenty-nine of Act Numbered Thirteen hundred and' ninety-six is hereby amended by adding at the end thereof the following proviso:

"_Provided, however,_ That in the discretion of the Secretary of the Interior the delegate of the Secretary of the Interior for the non-Christian people may make the official visit and inspection of the special government provinces."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, December 23, 1914.
