# Republic Act No. 1025

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Casa Real in Municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Casa Real**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1025**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Casa Real in Municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Casa Real**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio of Casa Real in Municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, is hereby converted to a barrio to be known as barrio Casa Real.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
