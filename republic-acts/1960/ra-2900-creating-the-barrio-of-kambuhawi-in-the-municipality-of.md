# Republic Act No. 2900

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Kambuhawi in the Municipality of Balamban, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2900**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Kambuhawi in the Municipality of Balamban, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Kambuhawi in the Municipality of Balamban, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Kambuhawi.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
