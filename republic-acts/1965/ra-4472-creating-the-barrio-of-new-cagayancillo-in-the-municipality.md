# Republic Act No. 4472

**An Act Creating the Barrio of New Cagayancillo in the Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4472**

An Act Creating the Barrio of New Cagayancillo in the Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Tarumbasi, Malanta, Baring-Baringin, Bonbon and Casayapan in the Municipality of Balabac, Province of Palawan, are hereby constituted into a district and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the Barrio of New Cagayancillo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
