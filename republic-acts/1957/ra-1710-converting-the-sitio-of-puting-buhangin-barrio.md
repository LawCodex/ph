# Republic Act No. 1710

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Puting Buhangin, Barrio Magalanggalang, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Orense**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1710**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Puting Buhangin, Barrio Magalanggalang, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Orense**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Puting Buhangin, barrio Magalanggalang, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Orense.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
