# Republic Act No. 2170

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Paypay in the Municipality of Cabadbaran, Province of Agusan, to Santiago**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2170**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Paypay in the Municipality of Cabadbaran, Province of Agusan, to Santiago**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Paypay in the Municipality of Cabadbaran, Province of Agusan, is changed to Santiago.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 26, 1959.
