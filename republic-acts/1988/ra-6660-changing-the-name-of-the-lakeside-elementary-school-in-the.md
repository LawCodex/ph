# Republic Act No. 6660

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lakeside Elementary School in the City of San Pablo, Province of Laguna, to Corazon M. Azcarate Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6660 June 22, 1988**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LAKESIDE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF SAN PABLO, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA, TO CORAZON M. AZCARATE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lakeside Elementary School in the City of San Pablo, Province of Laguna, is hereby changed to Corazon M. Azcarte Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **June 22, 1988.**
