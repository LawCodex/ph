# Republic Act No. 11116

**An Act Declaring September 17 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Los Banos, Province of Laguna, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary and the Annual Bañamos Festival**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11116**

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER 17 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LOS BAÑOS, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY AND THE ANNUAL BAÑAMOS FESTIVAL

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

September 17 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Los Baños, Province of Laguna, in commemoration of its founding anniversary and the annual Bañamos Festival.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
