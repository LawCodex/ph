# Republic Act No. 1709

**An Act to Amend Item (d), Page One Hundred Twenty-Five, Section Two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1709**

**An Act to Amend Item (d), Page One Hundred Twenty-Five, Section Two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (d), page one hundred twenty-five, section two-43 (Quezon) of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "(d) Masalocot Barrio Road, Candelaria—Construction | ₱3,000.00 |
|---|---|
| "(d-1) San Isidro Barrio School Building, Candelaria—Construction | 2,000.00" |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
