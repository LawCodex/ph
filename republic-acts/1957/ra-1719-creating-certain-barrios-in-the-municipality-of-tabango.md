# Republic Act No. 1719

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Tabango, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1719**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Tabango, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Gibacungan, Catmon and Manlawa-an, Municipality of Tabango, Province of Leyte, are converted into barrios of said municipality to be known as the barrios of Gibacungan, Catmon and Manlawa-an, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
