# Republic Act No. 6777

**An Act Changing the Name of the Guisican Elementary School in Barangay Guisican, Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, to Florence Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6777, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE GUISICAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY GUISICAN, MUNICIPALITY OF LABO, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO FLORENCE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Guisican Elementary School in Barangay Guisican, Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Florence Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
