# Republic Act No. 5609

**An Act Changing the Name of the Manhayang Elementary School in the Barrio of Manhayang, Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Iloilo, to Lamagusa Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5609, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MANHAYANG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE BARRIO OF MANHAYANG, MUNICIPALITY OF STA. BARBARA, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO LAMAGUSA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Manhayang Elementary School in the barrio of Manhayang, Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Lamagusa Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
