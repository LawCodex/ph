# Republic Act No. 2478

**An Act to Change the Name of Campaclan Primary School, Sibulan, Negros Oriental, to Magsaysay Memorial School, Sibulan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2478**

**An Act to Change the Name of Campaclan Primary School, Sibulan, Negros Oriental, to Magsaysay Memorial School, Sibulan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Campaclan Primary School at Sibulan, Negros Oriental, is changed to Magsaysay Memorial School, Sibulan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
