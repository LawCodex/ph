# Republic Act No. 6819

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bagacay National High School in Barangay Bagacay, Municipality of San Dionisio, Province of Iloilo, to Carlos Lopez National High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6819 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BAGACAY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BAGACAY, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN DIONISIO, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO CARLOS LOPEZ NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bagacay National High School in Barangay Bagacay, Municipality of San Dionisio, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Carlos Lopez National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
