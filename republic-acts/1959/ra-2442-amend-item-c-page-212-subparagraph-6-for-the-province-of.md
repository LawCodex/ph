# Republic Act No. 2442

**An Act to Amend Item (c), Page 212, Subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2442**

**An Act to Amend Item (c), Page 212, Subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

**Section 1 I** tem (c), page 212, subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, paragraph f, section two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen, is amended to read as follows:

"(c) District Engineer Building, Balanga—Construction ₱7,000.00."

**Section 2 T** his Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
