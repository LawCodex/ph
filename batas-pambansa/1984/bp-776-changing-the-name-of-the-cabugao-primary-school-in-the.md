# Batas Pambansa Blg. 776

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cabugao Primary School in the Municipality of Matanog, Province of Maguindanao, to Datu Cawi A. Lidasan Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 776**

An Act Changing the Name of the Cabugao Primary School in the Municipality of Matanog, Province of Maguindanao, to Datu Cawi A. Lidasan Primary School

## Section 1

The name of the Cabugao Primary School in the Municipality of Matanog, Province of Maguindanao, is changed to Datu Cawi A. Lidasan Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
