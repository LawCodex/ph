# Republic Act No. 2569

**An Act Changing the Name of Agpangi Elementary School in the Municipality of Calatrava, Province of Negros Occidental, to Ramon Magsaysay Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2569**

**An Act Changing the Name of Agpangi Elementary School in the Municipality of Calatrava, Province of Negros Occidental, to Ramon Magsaysay Memorial Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Agpangi Elementary School in the Municipality of Calatrava, Province of Negros Occidental, is changed to Ramon Magsaysay Memorial Elementary School.
