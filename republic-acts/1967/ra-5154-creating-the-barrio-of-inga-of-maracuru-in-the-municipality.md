# Republic Act No. 5154

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Inga of Maracuru in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5154**

An Act Creating the Barrio of Inga of Maracuru in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Inga of Maracuru in the Municipality of Enrile, Province of Cagayan, is hereby converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the Barrio of Inga of Maracuru.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
