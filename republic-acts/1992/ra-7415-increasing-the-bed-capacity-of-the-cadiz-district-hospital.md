# Republic Act No. 7415

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Cadiz District Hospital From A Twenty-five-bed Capacity To A One Hundred-bed Capacity, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7415, April 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE CADIZ DISTRICT HOSPITAL FROM A TWENTY-FIVE-BED CAPACITY TO A ONE HUNDRED-BED CAPACITY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Cadiz District Hospital is hereby expanded and improved by increasing the bed capacity from a twenty-five-bed to a one hundred-bed capacity district hospital under the supervision of the Department of Health.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
