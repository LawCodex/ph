# Republic Act No. 6628

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Father Pedro S. De Achutegui.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6628, October 23, 1972 ]

AN ACT GRANTING PHILIPPINE CITIZENSHIP TO FATHER PEDRO S. DE ACHUTEGUI.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Father Pedro S. de Achutegui is hereby granted Philippine Citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
