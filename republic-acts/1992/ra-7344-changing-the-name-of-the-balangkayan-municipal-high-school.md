# Republic Act No. 7344

**An Act Changing the Name of the Balangkayan Municipal High School in the Municipality of Balangkayan, Province of Eastern Samar, to Balangkayan High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7344, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BALANGKAYAN MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BALANGKAYAN, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO BALANGKAYAN HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Balangkayan Municipal High School in the Municipality of Balangkayan, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Balangkayan High School.
