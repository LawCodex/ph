# Republic Act No. 6699

**An Act Renaming the Tuy-An Elementary School in Barangay Tuy-An, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, as the Colomer Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6699, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE TUY-AN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TUY-AN, MUNICIPALITY OF CABATUAN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, AS THE COLOMER ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tuy-an Elementary School in Barangay Tuy-an, Municipality of Cabatuan, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Colomer Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
