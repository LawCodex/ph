# Batas Pambansa Blg. 100

**An Act Establishing An Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Ragay, Province of Camarines Sur, to Be Known as Ragay Emergency Hospital, and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 100**

An Act Establishing an Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Ragay, Province of Camarines Sur, to be Known as Ragay Emergency Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

There shall be established under the direct supervision of the Director of Medical Services, Ministry of Health, an emergency hospital in the Municipality of Ragay, Province of Camarines Sur, to be known as the Ragay Emergency Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount needed for the operation of the hospital shall be determined in accordance with Section 40 of the Presidential Decree No. 1177 and charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 40 and 80 for the Ministry of Health intended for new hospitals. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The Minister of Health shall determine the manner by which said hospital shall be operationalized.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 27, 1980
