# Executive Order No. 544

**Terminating the Collection of Tolls At the Barcelona and Caluscos Toll Bridges, Sorsogon**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 544, November 07, 1952 ]

TERMINATING THE COLLECTION OF TOLLS AT THE BARCELONA AND CALUSCOS TOLL BRIDGES, SORSOGON.

The total cost of the Barcelona and Caluscos toll bridges in the Province of Sorsogon, plus interest thereon at the rate of four per cent per annum, having been fully recovered, as certified, in accordance with the provisions of Act No. 3500, as amended, it is hereby ordered that the collection of tolls at said bridges be terminated.

This Order shall take effect upon receipt hereof by the Provincial Treasurer of Sorsogon.

Done in the City of Manila, this 7th day of November, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-two, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the seventh.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **MARCIANO ROQUE**
Acting Executive Secretary
