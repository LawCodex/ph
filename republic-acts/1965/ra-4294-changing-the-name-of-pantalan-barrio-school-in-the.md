# Republic Act No. 4294

**An Act Changing the Name of Pantalan Barrio School in the Municipality of Carles, Province of Iloilo, to Tiburcio Betita Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4294**

An Act Changing the Name of Pantalan Barrio School in the Municipality of Carles, Province of Iloilo, to Tiburcio Betita Memorial School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pantalan Barrio School in the Municipality of Carles, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Tiburcio Betita Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
