# Republic Act No. 3575

**An Act Changing the Name of the Albay High School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, to Pio Duran Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3575**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Albay High School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, to Pio Duran Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Albay High School in the Municipality of Guinobatan, Province of Albay, is hereby changed to Pio Duran Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
