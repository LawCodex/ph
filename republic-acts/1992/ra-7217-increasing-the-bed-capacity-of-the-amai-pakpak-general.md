# Republic Act No. 7217

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Amai-pakpak General Hospital in Marawi City, Lanao Del Sur, From Seventy-five (75) to One Hundred (100) Beds, Upgrading It Into a Provincial Hospital to Be Known as the Amai-pakpak Provincial Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7217, March 06, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE AMAI-PAKPAK GENERAL HOSPITAL IN MARAWI CITY, LANAO DEL SUR, FROM SEVENTY-FIVE (75) TO ONE HUNDRED (100) BEDS, UPGRADING IT INTO A PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL TO BE KNOWN AS THE AMAI-PAKPAK PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Amai-Pakpak General Hospital in Marawi City, Lanao del Sur, is hereby increased from seventy-five (75) to one hundred (100) beds and upgrading it into a provincial hospital to be known as the Amai-Pakpak Provincial Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.
