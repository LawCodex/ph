# Republic Act No. 3388

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Daligdigan in the Municipality of Tuburan, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3388**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Daligdigan in the Municipality of Tuburan, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Daligdigan in the Municipality of Tuburan, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Daligdigan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
