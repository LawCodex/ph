# Act No. 30

**An Act Authorizing An Increase of the Salary of the Interpreter and Translator in the Office of the Superintendent of Police for the City of Manila.**

[ Act No. 30, October 24, 1900 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING AN INCREASE OF THE SALARY OF THE INTERPRETER AND TRANSLATOR IN THE OFFICE OF THE SUPERINTENDENT OF POLICE FOR THE CITY OF MANILA.

By authority of the President of the United States, be it enacted by the United States Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The salary of the chief interpreter and translator in the office of the Superintendent of Police in the city of Manila is hereby increased to one hundred dollars per month, in money of the United States.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on the first day of November, nineteen hundred.

Enacted, October 24, 1900.
