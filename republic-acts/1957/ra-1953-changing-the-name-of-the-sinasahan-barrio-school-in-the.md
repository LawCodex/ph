# Republic Act No. 1953

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sinasahan Barrio School in the Barrio of Sinasahan, Municipality of Peñaranda, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Kapitan Pedro Villanueva Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1953**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sinasahan Barrio School in the Barrio of Sinasahan, Municipality of Peñaranda, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Kapitan Pedro Villanueva Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sinasahan Barrio School in the barrio of Sinasahan, Municipality of Peñaranda, Province of Nueva Ecija, is changed to Kapitan Pedro Villanueva Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
