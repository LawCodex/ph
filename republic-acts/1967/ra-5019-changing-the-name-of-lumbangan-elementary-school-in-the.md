# Republic Act No. 5019

**An Act Changing the Name of Lumbangan Elementary School in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Province of Batangas, to Nasugbu East Central School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5019**

An Act Changing the Name of Lumbangan Elementary School in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Province of Batangas, to Nasugbu East Central School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lumbangan Elementary School in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Nasugbu East Central School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
