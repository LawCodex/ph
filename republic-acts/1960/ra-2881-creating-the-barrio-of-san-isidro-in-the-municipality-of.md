# Republic Act No. 2881

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2881**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Matico, Panabagon, West Luya and Nabalan in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
