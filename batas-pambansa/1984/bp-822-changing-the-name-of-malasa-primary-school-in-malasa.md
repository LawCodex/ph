# Batas Pambansa Blg. 822

**An Act Changing the Name of Malasa Primary School in Malasa, Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Panglima Sarawi Sadalani Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 822**

An Act Changing the Name of Malasa Primary School in Malasa, Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Panglima Sarawi Sadalani Primary School

## Section 1

The name of Malasa Primary School in Malasa, Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Panglima Sarawi Sadalani Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
