# Republic Act No. 5201

**An Act Changing the Name of Binacag Elementary School in the Municipality of Espiritu, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Quiaoit Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5201**

An Act Changing the Name of Binacag Elementary School in the Municipality of Espiritu, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Quiaoit Memorial Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Binacag Elementary School in the Municipality of Espiritu, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Quiaoit Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1968.
