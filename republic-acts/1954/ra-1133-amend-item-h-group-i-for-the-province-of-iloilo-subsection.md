# Republic Act No. 1133

**An Act to Amend Item (h), Group I, for the Province of Iloilo, Subsection (a), Title C, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1133**

**An Act to Amend Item (h), Group I, for the Province of Iloilo, Subsection (a), Title C, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (h), Group I, for the Province of Iloilo, subsection (a), Title C, section one of Republic Act Numbered Nine hundred twenty is amended to read as follows;

| "(h) For the roadside development of the Dingle-San Enrique road, including the improvement of the Moroboro Spring and the establishment of a traveler’s rest-house | ₱25,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1954.
