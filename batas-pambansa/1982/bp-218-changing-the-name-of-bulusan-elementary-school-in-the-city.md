# Batas Pambansa Blg. 218

**An Act Changing the Name of Bulusan Elementary School in the City of Dagupan, to Don Jose De Venecia, Sr., Memorial School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 218**

An Act Changing the Name of Bulusan Elementary School in the City of Dagupan, to Don Jose De Venecia, Sr., Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Bulusan Elementary School in the City of Dagupan is changed to Don Jose de Venecia, Sr., Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
