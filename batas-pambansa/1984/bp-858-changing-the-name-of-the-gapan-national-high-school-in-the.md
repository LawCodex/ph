# Batas Pambansa Blg. 858

**An Act Changing the Name of the Gapan National High School in the Municipality of Gapan, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Juan R. Liwag Memorial High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 858**

An Act Changing the Name of the Gapan National High School in the Municipality of Gapan, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Juan R. Liwag Memorial High School

## Section 1

The name of the Gapan National High School in the Municipality of Gapan, Province of Nueva Ecija, is changed to Juan R. Liwag Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
