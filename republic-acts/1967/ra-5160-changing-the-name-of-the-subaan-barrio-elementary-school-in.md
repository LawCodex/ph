# Republic Act No. 5160

**An Act Changing the Name of the Subaan Barrio Elementary School in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Ginhawa Barrio Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5160**

An Act Changing the Name of the Subaan Barrio Elementary School in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Ginhawa Barrio Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Subaan Barrio Elementary School in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Ginhawa Barrio Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
