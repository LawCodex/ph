# Republic Act No. 11115

**An Act Declaring June 16 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Villanueva, Province of Misamis Oriental, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to Be Known as "Araw ng Villanueva"**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11115**

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 16 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF VILLANUEVA, PROVINCE OF MISAMIS ORIENTAL, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS "ARAW NG VILLANUEVA"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

June 16 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Villanueva, Province of Misamis Oriental, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as "Araw ng Villanueva".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

**GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
Speaker of the House of Representatives

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on May 15, 2017 and September 10, 2018, respectively.
