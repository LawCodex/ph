# Republic Act No. 6711

**An Act Changing the Name of the Medicion Elementary School, in Medicion 2nd, Municipality of Imus, Province of Cavite, to Gov. Dominador Camerino Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6711 February 10, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MEDICION ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, IN MEDICION 2ND, MUNICIPALITY OF IMUS, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO GOV. DOMINADOR CAMERINO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Medicion Elementary School in the Municipality of Imus, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to Gov. Dominador Camerino Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

**_Enacted without executive approval, February 10, 1989._**
