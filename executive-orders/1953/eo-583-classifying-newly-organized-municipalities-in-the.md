# Executive Order No. 583

**Classifying Newly Organized Municipalities in the Philippines**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 583, April 04, 1953 ]

CLASSIFYING NEWLY ORGANIZED MUNICIPALITIES IN THE PHILIPPINES

Upon the recommendation of the Auditor General and the Secretary of Finance and by virtue of the authority vested in me by

law, the following newly organized municipalities are hereby given their respective initial tentative classifications as hereunder

indicated, effective as of the dates said municipalities respectively began to exist, the same to continue until said municipalities

shall have been reclassified pursuant to the provisions of section 5 of Republic Act No. 554:
