# Republic Act No. 11156

**An Act Establishing a Land Transportation Office (LTO) District Office in the City of Sipalay, Province of Negros Occidental and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11156**

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) DISTRICT OFFICE IN THE CITY OF SIPALAY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the City of Sipalay, Province of Negros Occidental.1âwphi1

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Transportation (DOTr) shall immediately include in the Department’s programs the operationalization of the LTO District Office in the City of Sipalay, Province of Negros Occidental, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

The Secretary of the DOTr shall issue rules and regulations necessary for the effective implementation of this Act within sixty (60) days from the effectivity of this Act.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
