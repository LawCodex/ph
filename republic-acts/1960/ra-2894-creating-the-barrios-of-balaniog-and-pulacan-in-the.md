# Republic Act No. 2894

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Balaniog and Pulacan in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2894**

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Balaniog and Pulacan in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bulanit, East Tawagan Norte and Dalapang in the Municipality of Labangan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Balaniog.

## Section 2

The sitios of Lower Pulacan, Limpang Proper, West Limpang, Waterfalls and Lantawan in the Municipality of Labangan, same province, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Pulacan.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
