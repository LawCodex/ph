# Republic Act No. 4920

**An Act Changing the Name of Economia Street in the District of Sampaloc, City of Manila, to Vicente G. Cruz**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4920**

An Act Changing the Name of Economia Street in the District of Sampaloc, City of Manila, to Vicente G. Cruz

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Economia street in the District of Sampaloc, City of Manila, is hereby changed to Vicente G. Cruz.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
