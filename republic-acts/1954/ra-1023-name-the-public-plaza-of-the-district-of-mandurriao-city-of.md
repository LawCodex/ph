# Republic Act No. 1023

**An Act to Name the Public Plaza of the District of Mandurriao, City of Iloilo, Serapion C. Torre Park**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1023**

**An Act to Name the Public Plaza of the District of Mandurriao, City of Iloilo, Serapion C. Torre Park**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The public plaza of the district of Mandurriao, City of Iloilo, shall hereafter be known as Serapion C. Torre Park.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
