# Republic Act No. 2921

**An Act Amending Item (i), Page 151, Paragraph 4, Title A, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2921**

**An Act Amending Item (i), Page 151, Paragraph 4, Title A, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (i), page 151, paragraph 4, Title A, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred, as amended by Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred and sixty-four, is further amended to read as follows:

"i. Nueva Vizcaya

"(2) Bayombong Home Economics Building, Bayombong ₱5,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
