# Republic Act No. 2220

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, from Barrio Timan to Upper Liloy Beach**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2220**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, from Barrio Timan to Upper Liloy Beach**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby transferred from Barrio Timan to upper Liloy Beach.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
