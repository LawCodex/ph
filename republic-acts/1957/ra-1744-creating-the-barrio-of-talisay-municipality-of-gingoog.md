# Republic Act No. 1744

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Talisay, Municipality of Giñgoog, Province of Misamis Oriental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1744**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Talisay, Municipality of Giñgoog, Province of Misamis Oriental**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Binuangan, Municipality of Giñgoog, Province of Misamis Oriental, is converted into a barrio thereof, to be hereafter known as the barrio of Talisay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
