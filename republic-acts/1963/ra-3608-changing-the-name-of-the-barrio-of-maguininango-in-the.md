# Republic Act No. 3608

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Maguininango in the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, to San Juan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3608**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Maguininango in the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, to San Juan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Maguininango in the Municipality of Allacapan, Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to San Juan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
