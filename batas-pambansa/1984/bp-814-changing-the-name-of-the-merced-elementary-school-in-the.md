# Batas Pambansa Blg. 814

**An Act Changing the Name of the Merced Elementary School in the Municipality of Banate, Province of Iloilo, to Rufino Cabañgal Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 814**

An Act Changing the Name of the Merced Elementary School in the Municipality of Banate, Province of Iloilo, to Rufino Cabangal Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Merced Elementary School in the Municipality of Banate, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Rufino Cabangal Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
