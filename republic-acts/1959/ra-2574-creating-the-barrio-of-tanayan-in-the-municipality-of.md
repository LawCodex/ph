# Republic Act No. 2574

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Tanayan in the Municipality of Katipunan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACTNo. 2574**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Tanayan in the Municipality of Katipunan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Pucay, Lapan, Upper Irasan, Montay and Upper Malupis in the Municipality of Katipunan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Tanayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
