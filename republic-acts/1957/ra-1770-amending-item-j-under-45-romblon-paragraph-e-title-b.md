# Republic Act No. 1770

**An Act Amending Item (j) Under 45, Romblon, Paragraph (e), Title B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1770**

**An Act Amending Item (j) Under 45, Romblon, Paragraph (e), Title B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Nine Hundred Twenty**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (j) under 45, Romblon, paragraph (e), title B, section one of Republic Act Numbered Nine hundred twenty is amended to read as follows:

| "(j) Central School, Despujols | ₱1,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
