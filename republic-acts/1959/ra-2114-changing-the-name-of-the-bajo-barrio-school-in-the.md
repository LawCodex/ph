# Republic Act No. 2114

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bajo Barrio School in the Municipality of Del Gallego, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Juan Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2114**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bajo Barrio School in the Municipality of Del Gallego, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Juan Barrio School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bajo Barrio School in the Municipality of Del Gallego, Province of Camarines Sur, is changed to San Juan Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
