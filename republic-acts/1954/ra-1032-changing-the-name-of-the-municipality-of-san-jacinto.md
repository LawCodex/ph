# Republic Act No. 1032

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of San Jacinto, Province of Bohol, to Catigbi-An**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1032**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of San Jacinto, Province of Bohol, to Catigbi-An**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of San Jacinto, Province of Bohol, is changed to Catigbi-an.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
