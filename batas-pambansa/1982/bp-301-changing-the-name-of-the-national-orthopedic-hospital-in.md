# Batas Pambansa Blg. 301

**An Act Changing the Name of the National Orthopedic Hospital in Quezon City to National Orthopedic Hospital and Rehabilitation Medicine Center.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 301**

An Act Changing the Name of the National Orthopedic Hospital in Quezon City to National Orthopedic Hospital and Rehabilitation Medicine Center

## Section 1

The name of the National Orthopedic Hospital in Quezon City is hereby changed to National Orthopedic Hospital and Rehabilitation Medicine Center.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
