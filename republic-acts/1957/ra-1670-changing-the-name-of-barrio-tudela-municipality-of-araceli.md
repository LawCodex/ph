# Republic Act No. 1670

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tudela, Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan, to Calandagan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1670**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tudela, Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan, to Calandagan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Tudela, Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan, is changed to barrio Calandagan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
