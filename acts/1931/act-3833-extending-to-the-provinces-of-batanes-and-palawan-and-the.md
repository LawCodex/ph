# Act No. 3833

**An Act Extending to the Provinces of Batanes and Palawan and the Special Provinces Under the Jurisdiction of the Bureau of Non-Christian Tribes the Provisions of Act Numbered thirty-Four Hundred and Twenty-Two, Which Confers Upon Municipal Councils General Authority to Levy Taxes Subject to Certain Limitations.**

[ Act No. 3833, November 05, 1931 ]

AN ACT EXTENDING TO THE PROVINCES OF BATANES AND PALAWAN AND THE SPECIAL PROVINCES UNDER THE JURISDICTION OF THE BUREAU OF NON-CHRISTIAN TRIBES THE PROVISIONS OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY-TWO, WHICH CONFERS UPON MUNICIPAL COUNCILS GENERAL AUTHORITY TO LEVY TAXES SUBJECT TO CERTAIN LIMITATIONS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The provisions of Act Numbered Thirty-four hundred and twenty-two, entitled "An Act conferring upon municipal councils general authority to levy taxes subject to certain limitations," as amended by Act Numbered Thirty-seven hundred and ninety, are hereby made extensive and applicable to the Provinces of Batanes and Palawan and to the special provinces of Agusan, Bukidnon, Cotabato, Davao, Lanao, Sulu, Zamboanga, Mountain Province, and Nueva Vizcaya.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and thirty-two.

Approved, November 5, 1931.
