# Act No. 3086

**An Act Providing for the Change of the Name of the Municipality of Lezo, in the Province of Capiz, to Numancia, and for Other Purposes**

[ Act No. 3086, March 16, 1923 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE CHANGE OF THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF LEZO, IN THE PROVINCE OF CAPIZ, TO NUMANCIA, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of Lezo, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to Numancia, by which name the pueblos of Numancia and Lezo, Province of Capiz, at present consolidated, shall hereafter be known.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 16, 1923.
