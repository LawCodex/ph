# Republic Act No. 2755

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cervantes Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Cervantes, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Bessang Pass Memorial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2755**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cervantes Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Cervantes, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Bessang Pass Memorial Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cervantes Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Cervantes, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Bessang Pass Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
