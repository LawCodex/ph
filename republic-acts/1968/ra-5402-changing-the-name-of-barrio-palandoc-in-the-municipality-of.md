# Republic Act No. 5402

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Palandoc in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Barrio Ramon Magsaysay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5402**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Palandoc in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Barrio Ramon Magsaysay

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Palandoc in the Municipality of Salug, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Ramon Magsaysay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
