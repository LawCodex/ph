# Republic Act No. 1107

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Buayan, in the Province of Cotabato, to General Santos**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1107**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Buayan, in the Province of Cotabato, to General Santos**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Buayan, in the Province of Cotabato, is changed to General Santos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1954.
