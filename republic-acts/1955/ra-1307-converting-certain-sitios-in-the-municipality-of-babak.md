# Republic Act No. 1307

**An Act Converting Certain Sitios in the Municipality of Babak, Province of Davao, into Barrios**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1307**

**An Act Converting Certain Sitios in the Municipality of Babak, Province of Davao, into Barrios**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Mambago, San Isidro, Sto. Niño, San Antonio, San Agustin, Dangcaan, Balet, Tambo, Camudmud, and Cogo, in the Municipality of Babak, Province of Davao, are converted into barrios.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
