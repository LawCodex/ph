# Republic Act No. 5646

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of San Pedro in the Province of Oriental Mindoro**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5646, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN PEDRO IN THE PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of San Pedro in the Province of Oriental Mindoro is hereby changed to Bulalakao.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
