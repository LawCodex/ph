# Republic Act No. 6753

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Joaquin Barangay High School in Palo, Leyte, to San Joaquin National High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6753, August 11, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN JOAQUIN BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN PALO, LEYTE, TO SAN JOAQUIN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Joaquin Barangay High School in Palo, Leyte, is hereby changed to San Joaquin National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
