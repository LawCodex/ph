# Republic Act No. 2844

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Pulongcawayan in the Municipality of La Paz, Province of Tarlac, to La Purisima**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2844**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Pulongcawayan in the Municipality of La Paz, Province of Tarlac, to La Purisima**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Pulongcawayan in the Municipality of La Paz, Province of Tarlac, is changed to La Purisima.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
