# Republic Act No. 5592

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Barangobong in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5592, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING THE BARRIO OF BARANGOBONG IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TAYUG, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of San Patricio, Abulao and Barangobong are separated from the barrio of Magallanes in the Municipality of Tayug, Province of Pangasinan, and constituted into a new separate and independent barrio to be known as the barrio of Barangobong in the said municipality and province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
