# Republic Act No. 7321

**An Act Changing the Name of the Hilongos Hospital in the Municipality of Hilongos, Province of Leyte, to Emiliano Melgazo Memorial Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7321, March 30, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE HILONGOS HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HILONGOS, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, TO EMILIANO MELGAZO MEMORIAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Hilongos Hospital in the Municipality of Hilongos, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Emiliano Melgazo Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
