# Republic Act No. 3579

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscals in the Province of Ilocos Norte, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred and Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3579**

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscals in the Province of Ilocos Norte, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred and Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section sixteen hundred and seventy-four of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Ilocos Norte, is further amended to read as follows:

"Ilocos Norte, six assistant provincial fiscals."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
