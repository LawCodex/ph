# Republic Act No. 1457

**An Act to Amend Section Three of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Four Hundred Eleven to Provide for the Construction of a Shop Building in the Municipality of Quezon, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1457**

**An Act to Amend Section Three of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Four Hundred Eleven to Provide for the Construction of a Shop Building in the Municipality of Quezon, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item fifty-one, roman numbered two, subsection aq. Quezon, section three of Republic Act Numbered One thousand four hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| 51. Quezon Central School Shop Building—Construction | ₱3,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
