# Batas Pambansa Blg. 816

**An Act Converting the Prieto Diaz Provincial Road in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, Into a National Road**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 816**

An Act Converting the Prieto Diaz Provincial Road in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, into a National Road

## Section 1

The Prieto Diaz Provincial Road in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, is converted into a national road to be known as the Prieto Diaz National Road.

## Section 2

The amount necessary for the improvement and maintenance of said road shall continue to be funded out of the lump-sum appropriation authorized under Batas Pambansa Blg. 640 for the repair and maintenance of local roads until such time that the maintenance funds for said road shall have been provided for in the succeeding appropriations acts.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
