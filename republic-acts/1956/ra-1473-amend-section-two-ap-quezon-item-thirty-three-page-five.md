# Republic Act No. 1473

**An Act to Amend Section Two, ap. Quezon, Item Thirty-Three, Page Five Hundred Seventy-Eight, of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1473**

**An Act to Amend Section Two, ap. Quezon, Item Thirty-Three, Page Five Hundred Seventy-Eight, of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section two, ap. Quezon, Item thirty-three, page five hundred seventy-eight, of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred is amended to read as follows:

| "33. Construction of additional building, Bignay Barrio School, Sariaya | ₱6,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
