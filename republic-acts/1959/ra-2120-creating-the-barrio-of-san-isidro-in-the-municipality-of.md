# Republic Act No. 2120

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2120**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Marinduque, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
