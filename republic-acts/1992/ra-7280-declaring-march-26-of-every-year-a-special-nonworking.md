# Republic Act No. 7280

**An Act Declaring March 26 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Cebu**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7280, March 24, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH 26 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALISAY, PROVINCE OF CEBU

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

March 26 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Talisay, Province of Cebu.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
