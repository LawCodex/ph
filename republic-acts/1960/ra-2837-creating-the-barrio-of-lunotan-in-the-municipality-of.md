# Republic Act No. 2837

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Lunotan in the Municipality of Gingoog, Province of Misamis Oriental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2837**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Lunotan in the Municipality of Gingoog, Province of Misamis Oriental**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Lunotan Hinandigan, Kiboco and Mabuhay in the Municipality of Gingoog, Province of Misamis Oriental, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Lunotan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
