# Republic Act No. 7271

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Barili District Hospital in the Province of Cebu, From Ten (10) to Twenty-five (25) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Fifth Regular Session

BBegun and held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, nineteen hundred and ninety-one

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7271, March 20, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE BARILI DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE PROVINCE OF CEBU, FROM TEN (10) TO TWENTY-FIVE (25) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Barili District Hospital in the Municipality of Barili, Province of Cebu, is hereby increased from ten (10) to twenty five (25) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
