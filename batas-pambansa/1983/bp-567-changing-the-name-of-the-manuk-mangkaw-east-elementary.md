# Batas Pambansa Blg. 567

**An Act Changing the Name of the Manuk Mangkaw East Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi. to Hadji Imam Banjal Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 567**

An Act Changing the Name of the Manuk Mangkaw East Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Hadji Imam Banjal Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Manuk Mangkaw East Elementary School in the Municipality of Simunul, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Hadji Imam Banjal Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
