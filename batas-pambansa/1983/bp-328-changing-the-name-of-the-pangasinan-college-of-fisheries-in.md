# Batas Pambansa Blg. 328

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pangasinan College of Fisheries in the Municipality of Binmaley, Province of Pangasinan, to Binmaley School of Fisheries.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 328**

An Act Changing the Name of the Pangasinan College of Fisheries in the Municipality of Binmaley, Province of Pangasinan, to Binmaley School of Fisheries

## Section 1

The name of the Pangasinan College of Fisheries, located in the Municipality of Binmaley, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Binmaley School of Fisheries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
