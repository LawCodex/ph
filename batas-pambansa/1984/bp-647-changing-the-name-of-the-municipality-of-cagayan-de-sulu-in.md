# Batas Pambansa Blg. 647

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Cagayan De Sulu in the Province of Tawi-Tawi to Cagayan De Tawi-Tawi**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 647**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Cagayan De Sulu in the Province of Tawi-Tawi to Cagayan De Tawi-Tawi

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Cagayan de Sulu in the Province of Tawi-Tawi is changed to Cagayan de Tawi-Tawi.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
