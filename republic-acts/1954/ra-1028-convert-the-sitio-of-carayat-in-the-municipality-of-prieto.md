# Republic Act No. 1028

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Carayat, in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1028**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Carayat, in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, into a Barrio**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio of Carayat, in the Municipality of Prieto Diaz, Province of Sorsogon, is hereby converted into a barrio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
