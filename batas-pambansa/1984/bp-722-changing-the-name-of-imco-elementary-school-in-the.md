# Batas Pambansa Blg. 722

**An Act Changing the Name of Imco Elementary School in the Municipality of Itogon, Province of Benguet, to Gaudencio E. Antonino, Sr. Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 722**

An Act Changing the Name of Imco Elementary School in the Municipality of Itogon, Province of Benguet, to Gaudencio E. Antonino, Sr. Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the IMCO Elementary School in the Municipality of Itogon, Province of Benguet, is changed to Gaudencio E. Antonino Sr. Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
