# Republic Act No. 7726

**An Act Renaming the Sapangbato High School in Angeles City, Province of Pampanga, as the Sapangbato National High School and Appropriating Funds Thereof**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7726, May 19, 1994 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE SAPANGBATO HIGH SCHOOL IN ANGELES CITY, PROVINCE OF PAMPANGA, AS THE SAPANGBATO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREOF

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Sapangbato High School is hereby renamed as the Sapangbato National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, May 19, 1994.
