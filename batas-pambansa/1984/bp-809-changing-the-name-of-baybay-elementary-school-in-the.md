# Batas Pambansa Blg. 809

**An Act Changing the Name of Baybay Elementary School in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, to Col. Jose Tando Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 809**

An Act Changing the Name of Baybay Elementary School in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, to Col. Jose Tando Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Baybay Elementary School located in Barangay Baybay in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, is hereby changed to Col. Jose Tando Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
