# Act No. 2093

**An Act Providing that the Provincial Secretary-Treasurer Shall Be Accountable for All Public Property in the Provinces of Agusan and Nueva VIzcaya.**

[ Act No. 2093, January 06, 1912 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING THAT THE PROVINCIAL SECRETARY-TREASURER SHALL BE ACCOUNTABLE FOR ALL PUBLIC PROPERTY IN THE PROVINCES OF AGUSAN AND NUEVA VIZCAYA.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

In the provinces of Agusan and Nueva Vizcaya the duties with respect to the purchase and custody of provincial property shall be performed by the provincial secretary-treasurer, who shall procure all stationery and office supplies and shall be accountable for the care, custody, preservation and proper use of all public property pertaining to the province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, January 6, 1912.
