# Batas Pambansa Blg. 861

**An Act Changing the Name of the Namuac Elementary School in Barangay San Andres, Municipality of Sanchez Mira, Province of Cagayan, to Namuac-San Andres Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 861**

An Act Changing the Name of the Namuac Elementary School in Barangay San Andres, Municipality of Sanchez Mira, Province of Cagayan, to Namuac-San Andres Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Namuac Elementary School in Barangay San Andres, Municipality of Sanchez Mira, Province of Cagayan, is changed to Namuac-San Andres Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
