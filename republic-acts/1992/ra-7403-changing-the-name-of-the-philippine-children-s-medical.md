# Republic Act No. 7403

**An Act Changing the Name of the Philippine Children's Medical Center in Quezon Boulevard, Quezon City, To Philippine Pediatric Hospital and Development Center**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7403, April 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PHILIPPINE CHILDREN'S MEDICAL CENTER IN QUEZON BOULEVARD, QUEZON CITY, TO PHILIPPINE PEDIATRIC HOSPITAL AND DEVELOPMENT CENTER

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Philippine Children's Medical Center in Quezon Boulevard, Quezon City, is hereby changed to Philippine Pediatric Hospital and Development Center.
