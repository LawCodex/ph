# Republic Act No. 5690

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of Lipa City Hospital and Converting the Same Into a National Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5690, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF LIPA CITY HOSPITAL AND CONVERTING THE SAME INTO A NATIONAL HOSPITAL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Lipa City Hospital in Lipa City is hereby converted into a national hospital with an authorized bed capacity of fifty beds.

## Section 2

The additional sum of two hundred thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated in the next General Appropriations Act for its maintenance and operation.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
