# Executive Order No. 135

**Further Amending Executive Order No. 36, Series of 1963, Creating the Loans and Investment Council**

MALACAÑANG  
RESIDENCE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 135, December 24, 1964 ]

**FURTHER AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 36, SERIES OF 1963, CREATING THE LOANS AND INVESTMENT COUNCIL**
