# Republic Act No. 3923

**An Act Changing the Name of Mapoti Primary School in the Municipality of Nuling, Province of Cotabato, to Nibuaya Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3923**

**An Act Changing the Name of Mapoti Primary School in the Municipality of Nuling, Province of Cotabato, to Nibuaya Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Mapoti Primary School in the Municipality of Nuling, Province of Cotabato, is hereby changed to Nibuaya Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
