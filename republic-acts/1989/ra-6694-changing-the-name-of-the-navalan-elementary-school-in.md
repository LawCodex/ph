# Republic Act No. 6694

**An Act Changing the Name of the Navalan Elementary School in Barangay Navalan, Municipality of Tukuran, Province of Zamboanga, Del Sur, to Margarito V. Lagare Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6694, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE NAVALAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY NAVALAN, MUNICIPALITY OF TUKURAN, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA, DEL SUR, TO MARGARITO V. LAGARE MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Navalan Elementary School in Barangay Navalan, Municipality of Tukuran, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Margarito V. Lagare Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
