# Batas Pambansa Blg. 660

**An Act Changing the Name of the Province of North Cotabato to Cotabato**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 660**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Province of North Cotabato to Cotabato**

## Section 1

The Province of North Cotabato shall hereafter be known as the Province of Cotabato.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
