# Republic Act No. 9909

**An Act Separating the Olutanga National High School - Mabuhay Annex in the Municipality of Mabuhay, Province of Zamboanga Sibugay from the Olutanga National High School, Converting it into an Independent National High School to Be Known as Mabuhay National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9909**

AN ACT SEPARATING THE OLUTANGA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL - MABUHAY ANNEX IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MABUHAY, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA SIBUGAY FROM THE OLUTANGA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS MABUHAY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

_Separation and Conversion into a National High School._ \- The Olutanga National High School - Mabuhay Annex in the Municipality of Mabuhay, Province of Zamboanga Sibugay is hereby separated from the Olutanga National High School and converted into an independent national high school to be known as **_Mabuhay National High School._**

## Section 2

_Transfer of Assets and Liabilities._ \- All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Olutanga National High School - Mabuhay Annex are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Mabuhay National High School.

## Section 3

_Appropriations._ \- The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the Mabuhay National High School, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the Olutanga National High School - Mabuhay Annex. Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation of the school shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 4

_Implementation._ \- The Secretary of Education shall issue rules and regulations that may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 5

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
