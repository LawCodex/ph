# Republic Act No. 9191

**An Act Declaring the Day of March of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the City of Muntinlupa to Be Known as the Muntinlupa City Charter Day**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9191 February 21, 2003**

AN ACT DECLARING THE DAY OF MARCH OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF MUNTINLUPA TO BE KNOWN AS THE MUNTINLUPA CITY CHARTER DAY

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

March 1 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the City of Muntinlupa to be known as "Muntinlupa City of Charter Day" in order to commemorate the Charter Anniversary of the said city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.
