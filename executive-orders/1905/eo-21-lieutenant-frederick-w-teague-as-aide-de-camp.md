# Executive Order No. 21

**Lieutenant Frederick W. Teague, as Aide-de-Camp**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 21**

Lieutenant Frederick W. Teague, First Field Artillery, U.S. Army, is hereby designated as an additional aide-de-camp to the Governor-General of the Philippine Islands. This order is effective on January thirty-first nineteen hundred and thirteen.

**W. CAMERON FORBES**
_Governor-General_
