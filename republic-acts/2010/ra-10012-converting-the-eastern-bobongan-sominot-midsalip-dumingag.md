# Republic Act No. 10012

**An Act Converting the Eastern Bobongan - Sominot - Midsalip - Dumingag Provincial Road Traversing the Municipalities of Ramon Magsaysay, Sominot, Midsalip and Dumingag, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur Into a National Road**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10012**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE EASTERN BOBONGAN - SOMINOT - MIDSALIP - DUMINGAG PROVINCIAL ROAD TRAVERSING THE MUNICIPALITIES OF RAMON MAGSAYSAY, SOMINOT, MIDSALIP AND DUMINGAG, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Eastern Bobongan - Sominot - Midsalip - Dumingag Provincial Road traversing the municipalities of Ramon Magsaysay, Sominot, Midsalip and Dumingag, Province of Zamboanga del Sur is hereby converted into a national road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
