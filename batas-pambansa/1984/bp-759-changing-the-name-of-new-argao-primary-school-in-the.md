# Batas Pambansa Blg. 759

**An Act Changing the Name of New Argao Primary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Isidro Geverola Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 759**

An Act Changing the Name of New Argao Primary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Isidro Geverola Primary School

## Section 1

The name of the New Argao Primary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Isidro Geverola Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
