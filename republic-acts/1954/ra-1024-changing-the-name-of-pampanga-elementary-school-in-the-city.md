# Republic Act No. 1024

**An Act Changing the Name of Pampanga Elementary School in the City of Davao to Vicente Hizon Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1024**

**An Act Changing the Name of Pampanga Elementary School in the City of Davao to Vicente Hizon Memorial School**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of Pampanga Elementary School in the City of Davao shall hereafter be known as the Vicente Hizon Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
