# Republic Act No. 2154

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Asia, Province of Occidental Negros, to Municipality of Hinoba-An**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2154**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Asia, Province of Occidental Negros, to Municipality of Hinoba-An**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Asia, Province of Occidental Negros, is changed to Municipality of Hinoba-an, and its seat of government shall be, as it is now, the site of the present barrio of Hinoba-an.

## Section 2

Executive Order Numbered One hundred eighty-six, series of 1948, is amended accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
