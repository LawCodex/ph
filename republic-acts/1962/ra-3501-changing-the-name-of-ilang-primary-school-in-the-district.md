# Republic Act No. 3501

**An Act Changing the Name of Ilang Primary School in the District of Tibungco, Davao City, to Sixto Babao Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3501**

**An Act Changing the Name of Ilang Primary School in the District of Tibungco, Davao City, to Sixto Babao Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Ilang Primary School in the District of Tibungco, Davao City, is changed to Sixto Babao Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
