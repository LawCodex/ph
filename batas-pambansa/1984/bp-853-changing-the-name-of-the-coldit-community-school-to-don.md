# Batas Pambansa Blg. 853

**An Act Changing the Name of the Coldit Community School to Don Hermenegildo Velasco Community School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 853**

An Act Changing the Name of the Coldit Community School to Don Hermenegildo Velasco Community School

## Section 1

The name of the Coldit Community School located at Barangay Coldit, Asingan, Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Don Hermenegildo Velasco Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
