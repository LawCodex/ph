# Republic Act No. 4981

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Albor, Province of Surigao del Norte, to Libjo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4981**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Albor, Province of Surigao del Norte, to Libjo

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Albor in the Province of Surigao del Norte is hereby changed to Libjo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
