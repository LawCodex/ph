# Republic Act No. 10042

**An Act Converting the Dapa - Union - Gen. Luna Road, Province of Surigao Del Norte Into a National Secondary Road**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10042**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE DAPA - UNION - GEN. LUNA ROAD, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL NORTE INTO A NATIONAL SECONDARY ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Dapa - Union - Gen. Luna Road, Province of Surigao del Norte is hereby converted into a national secondary road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
