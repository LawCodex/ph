# Republic Act No. 6708

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Imelda, Province of Camarines Norte, to San Lorenzo Ruiz**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6708, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF IMELDA, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO SAN LORENZO RUIZ

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name Municipality of Imelda, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Municipality of San Lorenzo Ruiz.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
