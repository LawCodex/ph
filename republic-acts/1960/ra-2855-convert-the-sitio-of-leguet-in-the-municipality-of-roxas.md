# Republic Act No. 2855

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Leguet in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Santa Teresa**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2855**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Leguet in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality to be Known as the Barrio of Santa Teresa**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Leguet in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, is hereby converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Santa Teresa.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
