# Republic Act No. 5606

**An Act Changing the Name of the Capicho Elementary School in Roxas City to Governor Rafael Acuna Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5606, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE CAPICHO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN ROXAS CITY TO GOVERNOR RAFAEL ACUNA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Capicho Elementary School in Roxas City is hereby changed to Governor Rafael Acuna Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
