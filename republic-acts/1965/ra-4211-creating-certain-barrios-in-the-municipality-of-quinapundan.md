# Republic Act No. 4211

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Quinapundan, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4211**

An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Quinapundan, Province of Samar

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Palactad, Pana-ugan, Cambilla, Anislag, Continio and Bagto in the Municipality of Quinapundan, Province of Samar, are converted into barrios of said municipality, to be known as the barrios of Palactad, Pana-ugan, Cambilla, Anislag, Continio and Bagto, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
