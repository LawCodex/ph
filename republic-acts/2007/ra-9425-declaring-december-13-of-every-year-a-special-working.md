# Republic Act No. 9425

**An Act Declaring December 13 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in General Trias in the Province of Cavite**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9425 April 10, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER 13 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN GENERAL TRIAS IN THE PROVINCE OF CAVITE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

December 13 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the Municipality of General Trias in the Province of Cavite to commemorate its founding day.

## SEC. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
