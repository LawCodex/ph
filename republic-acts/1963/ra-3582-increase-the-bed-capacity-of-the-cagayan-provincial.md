# Republic Act No. 3582

**An Act to Increase the Bed Capacity of the Cagayan Provincial Hospital in the Province of Cagayan from Fifty to Seventy-Five Beds and to Authorize the Appropriation of Funds for the Purpose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3582**

**An Act to Increase the Bed Capacity of the Cagayan Provincial Hospital in the Province of Cagayan from Fifty to Seventy-Five Beds and to Authorize the Appropriation of Funds for the Purpose**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Cagayan Provincial Hospital in the Province of Cagayan is hereby increased from fifty to seventy-five beds.

## Section 2

In addition to the appropriations for the said hospital under existing law, the sum of two hundred ninety-six thousand seven hundred twenty-one pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the operation and maintenance thereof.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
