# Act No. 2192

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Twenty thousand Pesos to Be Added to the Reimbursable Appropriation Made By Act Numbered Two thousand and Sixty-One for the Operation of the Sales Agency.**

[ Act No. 2192, October 17, 1912 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TWENTY THOUSAND PESOS TO BE ADDED TO THE REIMBURSABLE APPROPRIATION MADE BY ACT NUMBERED TWO THOUSAND AND SIXTY-ONE FOR THE OPERATION OF THE SALES AGENCY.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by Philippine the Commission, that:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated, out of any Appropriation, funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand pesos or so much thereof as the Sales Agency Board shall deem necessary, which shall ¦ be added to the reimbursable appropriation made by section five of Act Numbered Two thousand and sixty-one for the operation of the Sales Agency created by said Act.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, October 17, 1912.
