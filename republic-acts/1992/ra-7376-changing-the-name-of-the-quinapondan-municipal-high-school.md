# Republic Act No. 7376

**An Act Changing the Name of the Quinapondan Municipal High School in Quinapondan, Eastern Samar, to Quinapondan High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7376, April 10, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE QUINAPONDAN MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN QUINAPONDAN, EASTERN SAMAR, TO QUINAPONDAN HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:1âшphi1

## Section 1

The name of the Quinapondan Municipal High School in Quinapondan, Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Quinapondan High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
