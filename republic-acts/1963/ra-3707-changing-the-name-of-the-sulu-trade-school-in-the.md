# Republic Act No. 3707

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu Trade School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Hadji Butu School of Arts and Trades**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3707**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu Trade School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Hadji Butu School of Arts and Trades**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sulu Trade School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Hadji Butu School of Arts and Trades.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
