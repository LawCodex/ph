# Republic Act No. 4570

**An Act to Declare the Shores and Territorial Waters of La Union from Agoo to Damortis, La Union, a National Park**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4570**

An Act to Declare the Shores and Territorial Waters of La Union from Agoo to Damortis, La Union, a National Park

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Notwithstanding any law or laws to the contrary, the shores and the territorial waters from Agoo, La Union, southward to Damortis, Rosario, La Union, inclusive, are hereby declared a national park.

## Section 2

Any mining operations present or future in this area is hereby prohibited.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
