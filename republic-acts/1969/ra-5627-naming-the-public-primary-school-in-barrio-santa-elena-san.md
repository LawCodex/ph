# Republic Act No. 5627

**An Act Naming the Public Primary School in Barrio Santa Elena, San Pablo City, Placido L. Escudero Memorial School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5627, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT NAMING THE PUBLIC PRIMARY SCHOOL IN BARRIO SANTA ELENA, SAN PABLO CITY, PLACIDO L. ESCUDERO MEMORIAL SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Hereafter, the public primary school in Barrio Santa Elena, San Pablo City, shall be named Placido L. Escudero Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
