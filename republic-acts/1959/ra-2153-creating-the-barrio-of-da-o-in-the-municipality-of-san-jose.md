# Republic Act No. 2153

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Da-o in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2153**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Da-o in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Little Doñgon, Big Doñgon, Patag, Ongos-ongos and Dao-Fishpond in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Samar, are hereby constituted into a barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Da-o.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
