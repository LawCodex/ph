# Republic Act No. 4404

**An Act Changing the Name of Ticulon Elementary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao, to Toribio Limcaoco Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4404**

An Act Changing the Name of Ticulon Elementary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao, to Toribio Limcaoco Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Ticulon Elementary School in the Municipality of Malita, Province of Davao, is hereby changed to Toribio Limcaoco Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
