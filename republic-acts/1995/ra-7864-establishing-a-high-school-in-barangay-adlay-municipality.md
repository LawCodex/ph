# Republic Act No. 7864

**An Act Establishing a High School in Barangay Adlay, Municipality of Carrascal, Province of Surigao Del Sur, to Be Known as the Adlay National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 7864, January 21, 1995 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING a HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY ADLAY, MUNICIPALITY OF CARRASCAL, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE ADLAY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a high school in Barangay Adlay, Municipality of Carrascal, Province of Surigao del Sur, to be known as the Adlay National High School.1aшphi1

## Section 2

the Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

the amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
