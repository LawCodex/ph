# Republic Act No. 2444

**An Act Amending Item 81, for the Province of Laguna, Page 162, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2444**

**An Act Amending Item 81, for the Province of Laguna, Page 162, Paragraph g, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 81, for the Province of Laguna, page 162, paragraph g, section two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"81. Santa Cruz Town Hall, Sta. Cruz ₱10,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
