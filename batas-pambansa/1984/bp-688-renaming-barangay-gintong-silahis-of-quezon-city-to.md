# Batas Pambansa Blg. 688

**An Act Renaming Barangay Gintong Silahis of Quezon City to Barangay N.S. Amoranto**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 688**

An Act Renaming Barangay Gintong Silahis of Quezon City to Barangay N. S. Amoranto

## Section 1

In due recognition to the outstanding contributions of the late Quezon City Mayor Norberto S. Amoranto in the improvement of barangays and the development of Quezon City into a modern metropolis, Barangay Gintong Silahis in the first district of Quezon City is hereby renamed Barangay N. S. Amoranto.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
