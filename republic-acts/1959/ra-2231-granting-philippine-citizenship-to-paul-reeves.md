# Republic Act No. 2231

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Paul Reeves**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2231**

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Paul Reeves**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Paul Reeves is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 28, 1959.
