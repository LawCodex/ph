# Act No. 3453

**An Act Amending Paragraph (G) of Act Numbered thirty-Four Hundred and Twenty-Seven, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Act No. 3453, December 01, 1928 ]

AN ACT AMENDING PARAGRAPH (G) OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-FOUR HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the t authority of the same:

## Section 1

Paragraph (g) of Act Numbered Thirty-four hundred and twenty-seven is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(g) For the construction at Dipolog, Zamboanga, of the building of the Rizal Memorial Hospital . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . P20,000.00"

## Section 2

Upon the completion of this building, the operation of the Rizal Memorial Hospital shall be conducted therein with the property, funds and personnel of the present hospital.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 1, 1928.
