# Republic Act No. 4348

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tejero in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, to Barrio Tejeros Convention**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4348**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tejero in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, to Barrio Tejeros Convention

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Tejero in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, is hereby changed to Barrio Tejeros Convention.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
