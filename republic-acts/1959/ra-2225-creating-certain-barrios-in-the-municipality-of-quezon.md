# Republic Act No. 2225

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Quezon, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2225**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Quezon, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of Quezon, Province of Palawan, are constituted into barrios, to wit:

1\. The sitios of Punta Baja and Malabunga to be known as the barrio of Punta Baja; and

2\. The sitios of Maasin and Iwahig to be known as the barrio of Maasin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

**Approved:** May 17, 1959.
