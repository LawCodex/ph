# Republic Act No. 1259

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Bugsayon, Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, to Barrio B. Titong**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1259**

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Bugsayon, Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, to Barrio B. Titong**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the eminent and patriotic services of the late Benigno Titong, the name of Barrio Bugsayon, Municipality of Masbate, Province of Masbate, is changed to barrio B. Titong.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
