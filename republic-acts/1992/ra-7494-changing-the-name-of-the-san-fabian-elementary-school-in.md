# Republic Act No. 7494

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Fabian Elementary School in Echague, Isabela, To Echague East Central School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7494, May 13, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN FABIAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN ECHAGUE, ISABELA, TO ECHAGUE EAST CENTRAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Fabian Elementary School in Echague, Isabela, is hereby changed to Echague East Central School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
