# Republic Act No. 6705

**An Act Changing the Name of the Parang I Elementary School in Barangay Parang, Municipality of Marikina, Metro Manila, to Fortune Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6705, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PARANG I ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY PARANG, MUNICIPALITY OF MARIKINA, METRO MANILA, TO FORTUNE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Parang I Elementary School in Barangay Parang, Municipality of Marikina, Metro Manila, is hereby changed to Fortune Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
