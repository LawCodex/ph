# Act No. 2284

**An Act Making An Appropriation to Enable the Mountain Province and the Provinces of Nueva VIzeaya and Agusan to Make Exhibits of their Products, Industries, and Resources, At the Philippine Exposition to Be Held in the Year Nineteen Hundred and Fourteen.**

[ Act No. 2284, September 08, 1913 ]

AN ACT MAKING AN APPROPRIATION TO ENABLE THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE AND THE PROVINCES OF NUEVA VIZEAYA AND AGUSAN TO MAKE EXHIBITS OF THEIR PRODUCTS, INDUSTRIES, AND RESOURCES, AT THE PHILIPPINE EXPOSITION TO BE HELD IN THE YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND FOURTEEN.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of fifteen thousand pesos, to be expended by the Philippine Exposition Board created by Act Numbered Twenty-two hundred and twenty-four, for the purpose of enabling the Mountain Province and the Provinces of Nueva Vizcaya and Agusan to make exhibits of their products, industries and resources, at the Philippine exposition to be held in Manila in the year nineteen hundred and fourteen.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, September 8, 1913.
