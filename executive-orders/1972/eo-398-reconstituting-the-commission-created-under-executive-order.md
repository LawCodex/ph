# Executive Order No. 398

**Reconstituting the Commission Created Under Executive Order No. 341 Dated October 4, 1971, Entitled "Creatingthe Presidential Investigation and Recovery Commission"**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 398 June 30, 1972**

RECONSTITUTING THE COMMISSION CREATED UNDER EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 341 DATED OCTOBER 4, 1971, ENTITLED "CREATING THE PRESIDENTIAL INVESTIGATION AND RECOVERY COMMISSION"

The Commission created under Executive Order No. 341 dated October 4, 1971, is hereby reconstituted as follows:

| "The Secretary of Justice | Chairman |
|---|---|
| The Chairman-Gen. Manager, RCA | Vice-Chairman |
| The Solicitor General | Member |
| A representative of the Auditor General | Member |
| A representative of the PNB | Member |

Done in the City of Manila, this 30th day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-two.
