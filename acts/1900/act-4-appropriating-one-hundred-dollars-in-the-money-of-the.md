# Act No. 4

**An Act Appropriating One Hundred Dollars, in the Money of the United States, for the Purpose of Paying the Salary of the Property Clerk in the Department of Education for the Period of One Month From September Eighth to October Eighth, Nineteen Hundred.**

[ Act No. 4, September 12, 1900 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING ONE HUNDRED DOLLARS, IN THE MONEY OF THE UNITED STATES, FOR THE PURPOSE OF PAYING THE SALARY OF THE PROPERTY CLERK IN THE DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION FOR THE PERIOD OF ONE MONTH FROM SEPTEMBER EIGHTH TO OCTOBER EIGHTH, NINETEEN HUNDRED.

By authority of the President of the United States, be it enacted by the United States Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The sum of one hundred dollars, in the money of the United States, is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, for the purpose of paying the salary of the property clerk in the Department of Education, for the period of one month from September eighth to October eighth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 2

The Military Governor is authorized to draw his warrant in favor of the duly appointed property clerk fro the amount designated in section one of this Act, and the Insular Treasurer is directed to pay the same.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, September 12, 1900.
