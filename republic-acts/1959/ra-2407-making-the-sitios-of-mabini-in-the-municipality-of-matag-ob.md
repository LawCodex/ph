# Republic Act No. 2407

**An Act Making the Sitios of Mabini in the Municipality of Matag-ob, Province of Leyte, a Part of the Municipality of Palompon, Same Province**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2407**

**An Act Making the Sitios of Mabini in the Municipality of Matag-ob, Province of Leyte, a Part of the Municipality of Palompon, Same Province**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Mabini in the Municipality of Matag-ob, Province of Leyte, is separated from said municipality and made a part of the barrio of Santiago, Municipality of Palompon, same province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
