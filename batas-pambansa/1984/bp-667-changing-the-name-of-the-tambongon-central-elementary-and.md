# Batas Pambansa Blg. 667

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tambongon Central Elementary and Barangay High School in the City of Davao to Alejandra L. Navarro Central Elementary and Barangay High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 667**

An Act Changing the Name of the Tambongon Central Elementary and Barangay High School in the City of Davao to Alejandra L. Navarro Central Elementary and Barangay High School

## Section 1

The name of the Tambongon Central Elementary and Barangay High School in the City of Davao is changed to Alejandra L. Navarro Central Elementary and Barangay High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
