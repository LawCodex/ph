# Republic Act No. 7339

**An Act Renaming the San Isidro Elementary School in Barangay San Isidro, Sorsogon, to Pio Jebulan Elementary School, and for Other Purposes**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7339, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE SAN ISIDRO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SAN ISIDRO, SORSOGON, TO PIO JEBULAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The San Isidro Elementary School in Barangay San Isidro, Sorsogon, is hereby renamed and called as the Pio Jebulan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
