# Presidential Decree No. 1283

**Amending Presidential Decree No. 175**

MALACAÑANG  
M a n i l a

**PRESIDENTIAL DECREE No. 1283 January 16, 1978**

AMENDING PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 175

WHEREAS, the goal of increasing the income and purchasing power of the low-income sector of the population can be achieved with cooperatives investing in rural banks;

WHEREAS, banking operations of cooperatives should be under the supervision of the Central Bank;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, Presidential of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution, do hereby decree and order the following:

## Section 1

Section 8 of Presidential Decree No. 175 is hereby amended by adding the following paragraph after sub-paragraph (j):

Any provisions of law to the contrary notwithstanding the Monetary Board of the Central Bank shall have exclusive responsibility and authority over the banking functions and operations of cooperative banks.

## Section 2

This Decree shall take effect immediately.

DONE in the City of Manila, this 16th day of January, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-eight.
