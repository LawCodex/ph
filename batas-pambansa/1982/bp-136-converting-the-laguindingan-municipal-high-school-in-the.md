# Batas Pambansa Blg. 136

**An Act Converting the Laguindingan Municipal High School in the Municipality of Laguindingan, Province of Misamis Oriental, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Laguindingan National High School, and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 136**

An Act Converting the Laguindingan Municipal High School in the Municipality of Laguindingan, Province of Misamis Oriental, into a National High School to be Known as the Laguindingan National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

The Laguindingan Municipal High School in the Municipality of Laguindingan, province of Misamis Oriental, is hereby converted into a national High School to be known as the Laguindingan National High School. Said school shall offer general secondary curriculum, vocational curriculum and other technological courses.

## Section 2

The amount of two hundred thousand pesos for the operation of the school shall be charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 80 for the Ministry of Education and Culture intended for new national schools. Thereafter the sum necessary for the same purpose shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 8, 1982
