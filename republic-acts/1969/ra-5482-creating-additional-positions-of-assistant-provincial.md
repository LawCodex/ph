# Republic Act No. 5482

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Camarines Norte, Amending for the Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5482, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING ADDITIONAL POSITIONS OF ASSISTANT PROVINCIAL FISCAL IN THE PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, AMENDING FOR THE PURPOSE SECTION SIXTEEN HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR OF THE REVISED ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, AS AMENDED.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Camarines Norte, is further amended to read as follows:

"Camarines Norte, four assistant provincial fiscals: _Provided,_ That the two assistant provincial fiscals shall be assigned to the second branch of the Court of First Instance of Camarines Norte with station at the Municipality of Labo."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
