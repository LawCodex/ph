# Batas Pambansa Blg. 669

**An Act Changing the Name of the North Central Elementary School in the Municipality of sta. Cruz, Province of Zambales, to Don Marcelo C. Marty Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 669**

An Act Changing the Name of the North Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Zambales, to Don Marcelo C. Marty Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the North Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Sta. Cruz, Province of Zambales, is hereby changed to Don Marcelo C. Marty Elementary School, any law ordinance or regulation to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
