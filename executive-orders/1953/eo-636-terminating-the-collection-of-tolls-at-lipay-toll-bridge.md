# Executive Order No. 636

**Terminating the Collection of Tolls At Lipay Toll Bridge, Province of Zambales**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 636, October 27, 1953 ]

TERMINATING THE COLLECTION OF TOLLS AT LIPAY TOLL BRIDGE, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES

The total cost of the Lipay Toll Bridge, in the Province of Zambales, plus interest at the rate of live per cent per annum, having been fully recovered, as certified in accordance with the provisions of Act No. 3500, as amended, it is hereby ordered that the collection of tolls at Lipay Toll Bridge be terminated.

This Order shall take effect upon receipt of copy hereof by the Provincial Treasurer of Zambales.

Done in the City of Manila, this 27th day of October, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-three, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the eighth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **MARCIANO ROQUE**
Acting Executive Secretary
