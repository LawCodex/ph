# Republic Act No. 12167

**An Act Establishing a District Office of the Land Transportation Office in the Municipality of Ilog, Province of Negros Occidental, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12167, April 11, 2025 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A DISTRICT OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ILOG, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the Municipality of Ilog in the Sixth District Office of the Province of Negros Occidental.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the LTO District Office in the Municipality of Ilog in the Sixth District of the Province of Negros Occidental, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
