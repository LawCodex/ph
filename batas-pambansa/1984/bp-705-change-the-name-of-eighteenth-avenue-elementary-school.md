# Batas Pambansa Blg. 705

**An Act to Change the Name of Eighteenth Avenue Elementary School Located At Barangay San Roque, Quezon City, to Doña Josefa Edralin Marcos Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 705**

An Act to Change the Name of Eighteenth Avenue Elementary School Located at Barangay San Roque, Quezon City, to Doña Josefa Edralin Marcos Elementary School

## Section 1

Eighteenth Avenue Elementary School located at Barangay San Roque, Quezon City, is hereby renamed Doña Josefa Edralin Marcos Elementary School in honor of Doña Josefa Edralin Marcos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
