# Commonwealth Act No. 183

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Twenty-Five thousand Pesos as Aid to the Philippine Amateur Athletic Federation for the Promotion of Athletics And/Or Athletic Competition in the Schools and Other Civic Institutions of the Philippines.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 183, November 13, 1936 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TWENTY-FIVE THOUSAND PESOS AS AID TO THE PHILIPPINE AMATEUR ATHLETIC FEDERATION FOR THE PROMOTION OF ATHLETICS AND/OR ATHLETIC COMPETITION IN THE SCHOOLS AND OTHER CIVIC INSTITUTIONS OF THE PHILIPPINES.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The sum of twenty-five thousand pesos, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Philippine Treasury not otherwise appropriated, as aid to the Philippine Amateur Athletic Federation for the promotion of athletics and/or athletic competition in the schools and other civic institutions of the Philippines.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 13, 1936.
