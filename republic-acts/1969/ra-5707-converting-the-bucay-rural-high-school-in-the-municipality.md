# Republic Act No. 5707

**An Act Converting the Bucay Rural High School in the Municipality of Bucay, Province of Abra, to a National General High School, to Be Known as the Bucay National General High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5707, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE BUCAY RURAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BUCAY, PROVINCE OF ABRA, TO A NATIONAL GENERAL HIGH SCHOOL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE BUCAY NATIONAL GENERAL HIGH SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Bucay Rural High School in the Municipality of Bucay, Province of Abra, is hereby converted to a national general high school, to be known as the Bucay National General High School effective upon availability of funds.

## Section 2

All personnel, equipment, records, supplies appropriations and other assets, as well as liabilities, of the Bucay Rural High School are hereby transferred to the Bucay National General High School.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
