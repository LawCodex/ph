# Republic Act No. 8935

**An Act Establishing a National Agriculture High School in the Municipality of Palanas, Province of Masbate to Be Known as the Palanas National Agriculture High School and Providing Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8935, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL AGRICULTURE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PALANAS, PROVINCE OF MASBATE TO BE KNOWN AS THE PALANAS NATIONAL AGRICULTURE HIGH SCHOOL AND PROVIDING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national agriculture high school in the Municipality of Palanas, Province of Masbate to be known as the Palanas National Agriculture High School.

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
