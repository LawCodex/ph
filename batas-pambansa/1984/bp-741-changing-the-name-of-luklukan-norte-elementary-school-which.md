# Batas Pambansa Blg. 741

**An Act Changing the Name of Luklukan Norte Elementary School Which Is Located in Barangay Osmeña, Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Camarines Norte, to "Osmeña Elementary School"**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 741**

An Act Changing the Name of Luklukan Norte Elementary School Which is Located in Barangay Oemeña, Municipality of Jose Panganiban , Province of Camarines Norte to "Osmeña Elementary School"

## Section 1

The elementary school located in Barangay Osmeña, Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Camarines Norte, now known as Luklukan Norte Elementary School, shall hereafter be known as Osmeña Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
