# Republic Act No. 8141

**An Act Changing the Name of the Eastern Samar National Comprehensive High School in the Municipality of Borongan, Eastern Samar, to Eastern Samar National Polytechnic School**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8141, July 16, 1995 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE EASTERN SAMAR NATIONAL COMPREHENSIVE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BORONGAN, EASTERN SAMAR, TO EASTERN SAMAR NATIONAL POLYTECHNIC SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Eastern Samar National Comprehensive High School in the Municipality of Borongan, Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Eastern Samar National Polytechnic School.

## Section 2

The Eastern Samar National Polytechnic School shall be under the supervision of the Commission on Higher Education (CHED).

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) EDGARDO J. ANGARA President of the Senate | (Sgd.) JOSE DE VENECIA, JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representative was finally passed by the House of Representatives and Senate on February 16, 1995 and February 15, 1995, respectively.

| (Sgd.) EDGARDO E. TUMANGAN Secretary of the Senate | (Sgd.) CAMILO L. SABIO Secretary General House of Representatives |
|---|---|

Lapsed into law on July 16, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
