**An Act Making Additional Appropriations for the Bureau of Forestry and Lands.**

[ Act No. 2906, March 23, 1920 ]

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

For the purposes of the survey, classification and demarcation of the public lands and of the forest zones thereon, there are hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the following sums, which shall be added to the appropriations made in section one , subsections GG and HH, of Act Numbered Twenty-eight hundred and seventy-five:

## Section 2
