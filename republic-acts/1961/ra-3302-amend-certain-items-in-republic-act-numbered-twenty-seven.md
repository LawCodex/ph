# Republic Act No. 3302

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3302**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred and One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Items (e) and (f) under Group III, for the Province of Cebu, page 193, paragraph (b), Section two of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and one are hereby amended to read as follows:

"(e) Tungkop Elementary School Building, Minglanilla Repair and remodelling ₱8,000.00

"(f) Carcar Municipal Hall, Carcar Construction 10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
