# Republic Act No. 5133

**An Act Changing the Name of Siran Primary School in Barrio Abad Santos, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to Abad Santos Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5133**

An Act Changing the Name of Siran Primary School in Barrio Abad Santos, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to Abad Santos Primary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Siran Primary School in Barrio Abad Santos, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, is hereby changed to, and shall hereafter be known as, Abad Santos Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
