# Republic Act No. 5628

**An Act Changing the Name of South Pilot School in the Municipality of Paoay, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Evangelista Pilot School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5628, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SOUTH PILOT SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PAOAY, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO EVANGELISTA PILOT SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of South Pilot School in the Municipality of Paoay, Province of Ilocos Norte is hereby changed to Evangelista Pilot School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
