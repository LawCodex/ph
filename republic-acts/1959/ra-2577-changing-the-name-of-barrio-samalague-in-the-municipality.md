# Republic Act No. 2577

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Samalague in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Barrio Santa Maria**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2577**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Samalague in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Barrio Santa Maria**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Samalague in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, is changed to Barrio Santa Maria.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
