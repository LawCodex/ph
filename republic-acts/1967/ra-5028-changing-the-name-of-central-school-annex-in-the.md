# Republic Act No. 5028

**An Act Changing the Name of Central School Annex in the Municipality of Oroquieta, Province of Misamis Occidental, to Layawan Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5028**

An Act Changing the Name of Central School Annex in the Municipality of Oroquieta, Province of Misamis Occidental, to Layawan Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Central School Annex in the Municipality of Oroquieta, Province of Misamis Occidental, is hereby changed to and shall hereafter be known as Layawan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
