# Presidential Decree No. 710

**Naming the International Conference Center Building to Be Constructed By Central Bank Under P.D. No. 520 DatedJuly 23, 1974, as the Philippine International Convention Center**

MALACAÑANG  
M a n i l a

**PRESIDENTIAL DECREE No. 710 May 27, 1975**

NAMING THE INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE CENTER BUILDING TO BE CONSTRUCTED BY CENTRAL BANK UNDER PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NO. 520 DATED JULY 23, 1974, AS THE PHILIPPINE INTERNATIONAL CONVENTION CENTER

I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution, do hereby decree and order that the International Conference Center to be constructed by the Central Bank of the Philippines pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 520, dated July 23, 1974, shall be named the Philippine International Convention Center.

This Decree shall take effect immediately.

DONE in the City of Manila, this 27th day of May, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-five.
