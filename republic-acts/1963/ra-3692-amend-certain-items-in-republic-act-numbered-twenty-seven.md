# Republic Act No. 3692

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3692**

AN ACT TO AMEND CERTAIN ITEMS IN REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-SEVEN HUNDRED AND ONE.

## Section 1

Item (a) Group III, page 179, subparagraph 4, for the Province of Albay, paragraph (b), Section two of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and one is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "(a) | For the construction of School buildings with pre-fabricated steel framings in the Third District of Albay | P50,000.00" |
|---|---|---|

## Section 2

Item (a), Group III, page 255, subparagraph 4, for the Province of Albay, paragraph c, Section two of the same Act, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "(a) | For the construction of school buildings with pre-fabricated steel framings in the Third District of Albay | P250,000.00" |
|---|---|---|

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963
