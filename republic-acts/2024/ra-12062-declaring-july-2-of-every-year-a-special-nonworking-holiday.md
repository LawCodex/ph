# Republic Act No. 12062

**An Act Declaring July 2 of Every Year A Special Nonworking Holiday in Pasig City To Be Known As the “Araw Ng Pasig”**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12062, November 06, 2024 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JULY 2 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN PASIG CITY TO BE KNOWN AS THE "ARAW NG PASIG"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

July 2 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holidayt in Pasig City to be known as the **_Araw ng Pasig._**

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following its publication in the Official Gazette or in a national newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
