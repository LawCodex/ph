# Republic Act No. 5654

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Cebu, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5654, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING ADDITIONAL POSITIONS OF ASSISTANT PROVINCIAL FISCAL IN THE PROVINCE OF CEBU, AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE SECTION SIXTEEN HUNDRED SEVENTY-FOUR OF THE REVISED ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, AS AMENDED.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred 1 seventy-four of the Revised Administrative Code as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Cebu, is further amended to read as follows:

"Cebu, twenty-four assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
