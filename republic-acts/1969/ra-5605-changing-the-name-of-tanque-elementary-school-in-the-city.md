# Republic Act No. 5605

**An Act Changing the Name of Tanque Elementary School in the City of Roxas to President Manuel Roxas Memorial School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5605, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF TANQUE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF ROXAS TO PRESIDENT MANUEL ROXAS MEMORIAL SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tanque Elementary School in the City of Roxas is hereby changed to President Manuel Roxas Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
