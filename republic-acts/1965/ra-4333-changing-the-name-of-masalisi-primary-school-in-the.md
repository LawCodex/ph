# Republic Act No. 4333

**An Act Changing the Name of Masalisi Primary School in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, to Payapang Ibaba Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4333**

An Act Changing the Name of Masalisi Primary School in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, to Payapang Ibaba Primary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Masalisi Primary School in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Payapang Ibaba Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
