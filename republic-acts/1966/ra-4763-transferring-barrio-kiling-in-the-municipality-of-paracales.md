# Republic Act No. 4763

**An Act Transferring Barrio Kiling in the Municipality of Paracales, Mountain Province, to the Municipality of Potia, Same Province**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4763**

An Act Transferring Barrio Kiling in the Municipality of Paracales, Mountain Province, to the Municipality of Potia, Same Province

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Kiling in the Municipality of Paracales, Mountain Province, is hereby transferred to the Municipality of Potia, same province.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
