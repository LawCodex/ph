# Republic Act No. 2922

**An Act to Amend Priority Numbers 7 and 13, Page 58-B, Group III for the Province of Leyte, Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2922**

**An Act to Amend Priority Numbers 7 and 13, Page 58-B, Group III for the Province of Leyte, Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Priority numbers 7 and 13, page 58-B, Group III for the Province of Leyte, Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen are hereby amended to read as follows:

"7 Malitbog Maningning Community—Construction school building ₱4,000.00

"13 Malitbog Sta. Cruz Construction of shop building 4,000.00

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
