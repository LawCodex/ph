# Republic Act No. 6549

**An Act Approving the Transfer of All Rights of Susano B. Maxilom Under Republic Act Numbered Thirty-Two Hundred Thirty-Two, in Favor of Eastern Broadcasting Corporation.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6549, August 26, 1972 ]

AN ACT APPROVING THE TRANSFER OF ALL RIGHTS OF SUSANO B. MAXILOM UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-TWO HUNDRED THIRTY-TWO, IN FAVOR OF EASTERN BROADCASTING CORPORATION.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The transfer of all the rights of Susano B. Maxilom, under Republic Act Numbered Thirty-two hundred and thirty-two, entitled "An Act granting Susano B. Maxilom a franchise to construct, operate and maintain radio broadcasting stations and stations for television in the Philippines," in favor of Eastern Broadcasting Corporation, is approved.

## Section 2
