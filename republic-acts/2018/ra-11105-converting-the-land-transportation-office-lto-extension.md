# Republic Act No. 11105

**An Act Converting the Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office Located in Lapu-Lapu City, Province of Cebu, Into a Regular LTO District Office and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11105**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) EXTENSION OFFICE LOCATED IN LAPU-LAPU CITY, PROVINCE OF CEBU, INTO A REGULAR LTO DISTRICT OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office located in Lapu-Lapu City, Province of Cebu, is hereby converted into a regular LTO District Office.1âwphi1

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Transportation (DOTr) shall immediately include in the Department’s program the operationalization of the LTO Extension Office in Lapu-Lapu City, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

The Secretary of the DOTr shall issue the necessary rules and regulations within ninety (90) days from the effectivity of this Act.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
