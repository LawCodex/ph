# Act No. 3416

**An Act to Provide for the Organization of the Municipal District of San Mariano, Province of Isabela, as a Regular Municipality.**

[ Act No. 3416, December 07, 1927 ]

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ORGANIZATION OF THE MUNICIPAL DISTRICT OF SAN MARIANO, PROVINCE OF ISABELA, AS A REGULAR MUNICIPALITY.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The municipal district of San Mariano, Province of Isabela, shall be converted into a regular municipality governed by the provisions of chapter fifty-seven of the Administrative Code.

## Section 2

Upon the approval of this Act, the present president, vice-president, and councilors of said district shall continue in office until their successors shall be duly elected in the general election of nineteen hundred and twenty-eight.

## Section 3

The municipal district of San Mariano hereby converted into a regular municipality shall retain the name of San Mariano and have the same territory and boundaries as heretofore.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 7, 1927.
