# Republic Act No. 6761

**An Act Naming the Camp at Tayug, Pangasinan, now Housing the 151st Pc Company/1st Police District, as Camp Narciso R. Ramos**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6761 September 25, 1989**

AN ACT NAMING THE CAMP AT TAYUG, PANGASINAN, NOW HOUSING THE 151ST PC COMPANY/1ST POLICE DISTRICT, AS CAMP NARCISO R. RAMOS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The camp at Tayug, Pangasinan, now housing the 151st PC Company/1st Police District, is hereby named as Camp Narciso R. Ramos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **September 25, 1989**
