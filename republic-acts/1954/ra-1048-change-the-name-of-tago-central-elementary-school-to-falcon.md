# Republic Act No. 1048

**An Act to Change the Name of Tago Central Elementary School to Falcon Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tago, Province of Surigao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1048**

**An Act to Change the Name of Tago Central Elementary School to Falcon Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tago, Province of Surigao**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

In recognition of the philanthropic services of Bernardo Falcon to the municipality of Tago, Province Surigao, the name of Tago Central Elementary School is hereby changed to Falcon Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
