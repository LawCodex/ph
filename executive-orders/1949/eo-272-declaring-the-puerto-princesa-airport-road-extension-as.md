# Executive Order No. 272

**Declaring the Puerto Princesa Airport Road Extension as National Road**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 272, September 29, 1949 ]

DECLARING THE PUERTO PRINCESA AIRPORT ROAD EXTENSION AS NATIONAL ROAD

Executive Order No. 180, dated October 2, 1948, is hereby amended so as to include the following road in the classi­fication of national roads:

| Palawan | | From Km. | To Km. | Length Kms. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Puerto Princesa Airport Road Extension | ................ | 2.82 | 4.78 | 1.96 |

Done in the City of Manila, this 29th day of September, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and forty-nine, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fourth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
