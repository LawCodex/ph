# Republic Act No. 2770

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Santa Clara and Cunig in the Municipalities of Gonzaga and Gattaran, Respectively, Province of Cagayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2770**

**An Act Creating the Barrios of Santa Clara and Cunig in the Municipalities of Gonzaga and Gattaran, Respectively, Province of Cagayan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Maguing, Lapag, Mamanghag, Calanutan, Caronuan, Malabbeg, Hudong, Ugued, Annokugen and Marapang in the Municipality of Gonzaga, Province of Cagayan, are hereby constituted into a barrio in the same municipality to be known as the barrio of Santa Clara.

## Section 2

The barrio of Madarag and the sitio of Cunig in the Municipality of Gattaran, same province, are hereby constituted into a barrio in the same municipality to be known as the barrio of Cunig.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
