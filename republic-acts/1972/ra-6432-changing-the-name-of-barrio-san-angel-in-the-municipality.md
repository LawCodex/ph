# Republic Act No. 6432

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Angel in the Municipality of Rosales, Province of Pangasinan, to Silvestre Domingo.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6432, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO SAN ANGEL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ROSALES, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN, TO SILVESTRE DOMINGO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio San Angel in the Municipality of Rosales, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Silvestre Domingo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1972.
