# Republic Act No. 8673

**An Act Converting the Barangay DARat High School in the Municipality of Pinili, Province of Ilocos Norte, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Pinili High School**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8673, June 25, 1998 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE BARANGAY DARAT HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PINILI, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE PINILI HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Barangay Darat High School in the Municipality of Pinili, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby converted into a national high school, to be known as the Pinili National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue the necessary rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
