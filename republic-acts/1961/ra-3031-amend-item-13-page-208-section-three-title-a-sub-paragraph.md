# Republic Act No. 3031

**An Act to Amend Item (13), Page 208, Section Three, Title (A), Sub-Paragraph (c) of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3031**

**An Act to Amend Item (13), Page 208, Section Three, Title (A), Sub-Paragraph (c) of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (13), page 208, Section three, Title (A), sub-paragraph (c) of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred and thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"(13) Pototan Social Hall and Gymnasium ₱100,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 8, 1961.
