# Republic Act No. 6812

**An Act Changing the Name of the Laog Elementary School in Barangay Laog, Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, to Col. Vicente Salvador Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6812 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LAOG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY LAOG, MUNICIPALITY OF ANGAT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO COL. VICENTE SALVADOR ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Laog Elementary School located in Barangay Laog, Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Col. Vicente Salvador Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
