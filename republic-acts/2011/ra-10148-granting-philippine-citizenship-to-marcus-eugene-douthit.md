# Republic Act No. 10148

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Marcus Eugene Douthit**

Fifteenth Congress  
First Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-sixth day of July, two thousand ten.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10148**

AN ACT GRANTING PHILIPPINE CITIZENSHIP TO MARCUS EUGENE DOUTHIT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Marcus Eugene Douthit is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## Section 2

Upon taking and registration of the oath of allegiance to the Republic of the Philippines, Marcus Eugene Douthit shall enter upon the full enjoyment of Philippine citizenship.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives | (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of the Representatives and the Senate on October 4, 2010 and November 8, 2010, respectively.
