# Republic Act No. 3718

**An Act Changing the Name of Cadlan Elementary School in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur, to Rodriguez Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3718**

**An Act Changing the Name of Cadlan Elementary School in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur, to Rodriguez Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cadlan Elementary School in the Municipality of Pili, Province of Camarines Sur, is changed to Rodriguez Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
