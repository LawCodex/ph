# Republic Act No. 1433

**An Act to Amend Items Nineteen and Twenty, aa. La Union, Paragraph I, Title B, of Section One of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1433**

**An Act to Amend Items Nineteen and Twenty, aa. La Union, Paragraph I, Title B, of Section One of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Items nineteen and twenty, aa. La Union, paragraph I, title B, of section one Republic Act Numbered One thousand two hundred, are amended to read as follows:

| "(19) Leones-Macalva Tubao-Agoo-Completion | ₱8,000.00 |
|---|---|
| "(20) Sta. Teresa Road, Tubao-Completion | 5,000.00" |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
