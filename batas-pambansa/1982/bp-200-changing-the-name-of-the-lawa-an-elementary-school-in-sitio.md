# Batas Pambansa Blg. 200

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lawa-An Elementary School in Sitio Lawa-An, Municipality of Pantukan, Province of Davao Del Norte, to Doroteo De Castro Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 200**

An Act Changing the Name of the Lawa-An Elementary School in Sitio Lawa-An, Municipality of Pantukan, Province of Davao Del Norte, to Doroteo De Castro Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Lawa-an Elementary School situated in Sitio Lawa-an, Municipality of Pantukan, Province of Davao del Norte, is hereby changed to Doroteo de Castro Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 16, 1982
