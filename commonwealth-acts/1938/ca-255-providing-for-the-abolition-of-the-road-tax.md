# Commonwealth Act No. 255

**An Act Providing for the Abolition of the Road Tax.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 255, March 09, 1938 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING FOR THE ABOLITION OF THE ROAD TAX.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The road tax imposed by section twenty-one hundred and fifty-eight of the Administrative Code is abolished, and all delinquencies by reason thereof are condoned.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, March 9, 1938.
