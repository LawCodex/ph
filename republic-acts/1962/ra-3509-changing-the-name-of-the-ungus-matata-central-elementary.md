# Republic Act No. 3509

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ungus Matata Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tandubas, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Biteng Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3509**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ungus Matata Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tandubas, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Biteng Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Ungus Matata Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tandubas, Province of Sulu, is changed to Panglima Biteng Central Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
