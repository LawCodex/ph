# Republic Act No. 6206

**An Act Changing the Name of San Joaquin High School in the Municipality of San Joaquin, Province of Iloilo, to Don Felix Serra Memorial High School.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6206, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SAN JOAQUIN HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN JOAQUIN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO DON FELIX SERRA MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Joaquin High School in the Municipality of San Joaquin, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Don Felix Serra Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
