# Republic Act No. 2173

**An Act to Change the Name of the Manambong Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Asuncion P. Guevara Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2173**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Manambong Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Asuncion P. Guevara Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Manambong Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Asuncion P. Guevara Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 26, 1959.
