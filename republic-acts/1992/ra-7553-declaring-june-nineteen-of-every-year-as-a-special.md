# Republic Act No. 7553

**An Act Declaring June Nineteen of Every Year As A Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Surigao Del Norte, To Be Known As "Adlaw Nan Surigao Del Norte"**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7553, May 22, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE NINETEEN OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL NORTE, TO BE KNOWN AS "ADLAW NAN SURIGAO DEL NORTE"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

June Nineteen of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Surigao del Norte, to be known as "Adlaw Nan Surigao Del Norte."1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days following its publication in two (2) national newspapers of general circulation in the country.
