# Republic Act No. 4283

**An Act Changing the Name of Washington Street in Iligan City to General Emilio Aguinaldo Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4283**

An Act Changing the Name of Washington Street in Iligan City to General Emilio Aguinaldo Street

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Washington Street in Iligan City shall hereafter be known and referred to as General Emilio Aguinaldo Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
