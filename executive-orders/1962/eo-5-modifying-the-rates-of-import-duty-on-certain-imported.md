# Executive Order No. 5

**Modifying the Rates of Import Duty on Certain Imported Articles as Provided Under Republic Act Number Nineteen Hundred Thirty-Seven, Otherwise Known as the Tariff and Customs Code of the Philippines**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 5, January 21, 1962 ]

**MODIFYING THE RATES OF IMPORT DUTY ON CERTAIN IMPORTED ARTICLES AS PROVIDED UNDER REPUBLIC ACT NUMBER NINETEEN HUNDRED THIRTY-SEVEN, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE TARIFF AND CUSTOMS CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES**

Pursuant to the powers vested in me by section.401 of Republic Act Number Nineteen hundred Thirty-Seven, **I, DIOSDADO P. MACAPAGAL,** President of the Philippines, do hereby direct and order:
