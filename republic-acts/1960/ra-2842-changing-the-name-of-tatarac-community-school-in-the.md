# Republic Act No. 2842

**An Act Changing the Name of Tatarac Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Apalen-Tatarac Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2842**

**An Act Changing the Name of Tatarac Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, to Apalen-Tatarac Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tatarac Community School in the Municipality of Bayambang, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Apalen-Tatarac Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
