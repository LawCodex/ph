# Republic Act No. 8906

**An Act Re-Establishing the Cotabato City National High School**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8906, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT RE-ESTABLISHING THE COTABATO CITY NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby re-established the Cotabato City National High School in Cotabato City.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This law shall take effect immediately upon approval.
