# Batas Pambansa Blg. 215

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dibua Elementary School in Barangay Dibua, City of Laoag, to Don Galicano R. Rafales Memorial Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 215**

An Act Changing the Name of the Dibua Elementary School in Barangay Dibua, City of Laoag, to Don Galicano R. Rafales Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Dibua Elementary School in Barangay Dibua, City of Laoag, is hereby changed to Don Galicano R. Rafales Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
