# Executive Order No. 380

**Reconstituting the Coconut Coordinating Council Created Under Executive Order No. 334 Dated August 30, 1971**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 380 March 1, 1972**

RECONSTITUTING THE COCONUT COORDINATING COUNCIL CREATED UNDER EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 334 DATED AUGUST 30, 1971

Pursuant to the authority vested in me by law, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby reconstitute the composition of the Coconut Coordinating Council created under Executive Order No. 334 dated August 30, 1971, as follows:

| The Secretary of Agriculture and Natural Resources | Chairman | |---|---| | The Chairman-General Manager of PHILCOA | Vice-Chairman | | The Director of the Bureau of Plant Industry | Member | | The Director of the Philippine Coconut Research Institute(PHILCORIN) | Member | | The Commissioner of the Agricultural Productivity Commission (APC) | Member | | The General Manager of the Coconut Investment Company | Member | | The President of the United Coconut Association of the Philippines (UCAP) | Member | | The President, Philippine Federation of Coconut Producers | Member | | The Chairman, Committee on Agriculture, House of Representatives | Member | | The Chairman, Committee on Agriculture, Senate | Member |

Done in the City of Manila, this 1st day of March, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-two.
