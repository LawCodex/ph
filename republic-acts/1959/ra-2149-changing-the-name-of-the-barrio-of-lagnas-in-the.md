# Republic Act No. 2149

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Lagnas in the Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, to San Pascual**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2149**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Lagnas in the Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, to San Pascual**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Lagnas in the Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is changed to San Pascual.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
