# Republic Act No. 7513

**An Act Changing the Name of the Anabo Elementary School in Barangay Anabo, Municipality of Lemery, Province of Iloilo, To Benjamin Aguilar Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7513, May 21, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ANABO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY ANABO, MUNICIPALITY OF LEMERY, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO BENJAMIN AGUILAR ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Anabo Elementary School in Barangay Anabo, Municipality of Lemery, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Benjamin Aguilar Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
