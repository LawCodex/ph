# Republic Act No. 2772

**An Act Changing the Name of Gatmaitan Street in Lipa City to Teodoro M. Kalaw Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2772**

**An Act Changing the Name of Gatmaitan Street in Lipa City to Teodoro M. Kalaw Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Gatmaitan Street in Lipa City is hereby changed to Teodoro M. Kalaw Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
