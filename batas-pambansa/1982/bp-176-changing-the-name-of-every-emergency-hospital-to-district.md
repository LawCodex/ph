# Batas Pambansa Blg. 176

**An Act Changing the Name of Every Emergency Hospital to District Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 176**

An Act Changing the Name of Every Emergency Hospital to District Hospital

## Section 1

Every emergency hospital shall hereafter be known as district hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 12, 1982
