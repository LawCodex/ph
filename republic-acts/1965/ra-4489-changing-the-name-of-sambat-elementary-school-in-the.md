# Republic Act No. 4489

**An Act Changing the Name of Sambat Elementary School in the Municipality of Santa Teresita, Province of Batangas, to Paaralang Sentral ng Santa Teresita**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4489**

An Act Changing the Name of Sambat Elementary School in the Municipality of Santa Teresita, Province of Batangas, to Paaralang Sentral ng Santa Teresita

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Sambat Elementary School in the Municipality of Santa Teresita, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Paaralang Sentral ng Santa Teresita.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
