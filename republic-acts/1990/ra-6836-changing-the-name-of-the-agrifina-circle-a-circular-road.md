# Republic Act No. 6836

**An Act Changing the Name of the Agrifina Circle, a Circular Road Near the Luneta Park in the City of Manila, to Teodoro F. Valencia Circle**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6836

An Act Changing the Name of the Agrifina Circle, a Circular Road Near the Luneta Park in the City of Manila, to Teodoro F. Valencia Circle

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

. The name of the circular road located within the vicinity of Luneta Park, City of Manila, is hereby changed from Agrifina Circle to Teodoro F. Valencia Circle.

## Section 2

. This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
