# Executive Order No. 66

**Fixing the Ceiling Prices of Lumber and for Other Purposes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 66, June 27, 1947 ]

FIXING THE CEILING PRICES OF LUMBER AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

WHEREAS, the public interest requires that a new and reasonable schedule of ceiling prices be established and fixed for lumber in view of changed conditions and circumstances;

**NOW, THEREFORE, I, MANUEL ROXAS** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution and existing laws of the Philippines do hereby order:

## Section 2

Any retailer or vendor of lumber who refuses to sell any of the wood species specified in section 1 hereof, displayed in his lumber yard or place of business, at the price fixed by this Order shall be punished in accordance with the provisions of section 3 of Commonwealth Act No. 600, as amended.

## Section 3

All retailers or vendors of lumber are hereby required to post in a conspicuous place at the entrance of their lumber yards or places of business, or within said premises, a copy of the schedule of prices provided in this Order.

## Section 4

All Executive Orders and all Emergency Control Administration Orders or any of the provisions thereof which are in conflict or inconsistent with this Order or any of its provisions are hereby repealed.

## Section 5

This Executive Order shall take effect July 15, 1947.

Done at the City of Manila, this 27th day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and forty-seven, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the first.

(Sgd.) **MANUEL ROXAS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(Sgd.) **EMILIO ABELLO**
Chief of the Executive Office
