# Republic Act No. 2567

**An Act Providing that the Public Plaza in the Poblacion of the Municipality of Calatrava, Province of Negros Occidental, Shall Hereafter be Known as Andres Menchaca Park**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2567**

**An Act Providing that the Public Plaza in the Poblacion of the Municipality of Calatrava, Province of Negros Occidental, Shall Hereafter be Known as Andres Menchaca Park**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The public plaza in the poblacion of the Municipality of Calatrava Province of Negros Occidental, shall hereafter be known as Andres Menchaca Park.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
