# Republic Act No. 5647

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Nuling in the Province of Cotabato, to Sultan Kudarat**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5647, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF NULING IN THE PROVINCE OF COTABATO, TO SULTAN KUDARAT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Nuling in the Province of Cotabato is hereby changed to Sultan Kudarat.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
