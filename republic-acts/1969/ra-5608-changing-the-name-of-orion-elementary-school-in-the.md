# Republic Act No. 5608

**An Act Changing the Name of Orion Elementary School in the Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan, to Arellano Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5608, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ORION ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ORION, PROVINCE OF BATAAN, TO ARELLANO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Orion Elementary School in the Municipality of Orion, Province of Bataan, is hereby changed to Arellano Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
