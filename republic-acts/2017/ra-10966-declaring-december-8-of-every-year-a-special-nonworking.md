# Republic Act No. 10966

**An Act Declaring December 8 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Entire Country to Commemorate the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary, the Principal Patroness of the Philippines**

Seventeenth Congress  
Second Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand seventeen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10966**

An Act Declaring December 8 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Entire Country to Commemorate the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary, the Principal Patroness of the Philippines

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

December 8 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the entire country to commemorate the Feast of the Immaculate Conception of Mary, the Principal Patroness of the Philippines.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**AQUILINO "KOKO" PIMENTEL**
President of the Senate

**PANTALEON D. ALVAREZ**
Speaker of the House of Representatives
