# Republic Act No. 11101

**An Act Declaring June 18 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Ifugao, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to Be Known as the Ifugao Foundation Day**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11101**

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 18 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF IFUGAO, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE "IFUGAO FOUNDATION DAY"

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

June 18 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Ifugao, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as the "Ifugao Foundation Day".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate
