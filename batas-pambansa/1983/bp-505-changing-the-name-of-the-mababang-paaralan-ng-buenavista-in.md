# Batas Pambansa Blg. 505

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mababang Paaralan Ng Buenavista in the Municipality of Alabat, Province of Quezon, to Rufina-Tiburcio Camacho Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 505**

An Act Changing the Name of the Mababang Paaralan Ng Buenavista in the Municipality of Alabat, Province of Quezon, to Rufina-Tiburcio Camacho Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Mababang Paaralan ng Buenavista in the Municipality of Alabat, Province of Quezon, is changed to Rufina-Tiburcio Camacho Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1983
