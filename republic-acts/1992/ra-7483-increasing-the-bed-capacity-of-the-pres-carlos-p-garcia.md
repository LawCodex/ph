# Republic Act No. 7483

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Pres. Carlos P. Garcia Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, From Seventy-five (75) To One Hundred (100) Beds, and Relocating the Said Hospital in San Jose, Talibon, Bohol, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7483, May 05, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE PRES. CARLOS P. GARCIA PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALIBON, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, FROM SEVENTY-FIVE (75) TO ONE HUNDRED (100) BEDS, AND RELOCATING THE SAID HOSPITAL IN SAN JOSE, TALIBON, BOHOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Pres. Carlos P. Garcia Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, is hereby increased from seventy-five (75) to one hundred (100) beds, and relocating the said hospital in San Jose, Talibon, Bohol.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
