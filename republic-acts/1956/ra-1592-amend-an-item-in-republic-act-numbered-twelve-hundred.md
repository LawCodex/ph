# Republic Act No. 1592

**An Act to Amend an Item in Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred Pertaining to the Province of Capiz**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1592**

**An Act to Amend an Item in Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred Pertaining to the Province of Capiz**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (17) paragraph (n) for the Province of Capiz, subsection three, Title A, section one of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred, is amended to read as follows:

| "(17) Repair and improvement of the Capiz Grandstand, Roxas City | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
