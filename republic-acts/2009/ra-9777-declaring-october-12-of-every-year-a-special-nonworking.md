# Republic Act No. 9777

**An Act Declaring October 12 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Public Holiday in the Municipality of Getafe, Province of Bohol to Be Known as "The Getafe Foundation Day"**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9777**

AN ACT DECLARING OCTOBER 12 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GETAFE, PROVINCE OF BOHOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE "GETAFE FOUNDATION DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

October 12 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking public holiday in the Municipality of Getafe, Province of Bohol to commemorate its founding anniversary and to be known as the **_"Getafe Foundation Day."_**

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
