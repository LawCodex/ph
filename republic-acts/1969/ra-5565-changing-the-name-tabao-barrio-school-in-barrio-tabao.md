# Republic Act No. 5565

**An Act Changing the Name "Tabao Barrio School" in Barrio Tabao, Municipality of San Enrique, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Espiridion Presbitero Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5565, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME "TABAO BARRIO SCHOOL" IN BARRIO TABAO, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN ENRIQUE, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, TO DON ESPIRIDION PRESBITERO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL,

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name "Tabao Barrio School" in Barrio Tabao, Municipality of San Enrique, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Don Espiridion Presbitero Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
