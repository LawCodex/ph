# Republic Act No. 2106

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Kialeg in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, to Barrio Magsaysay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2106**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Kialeg in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, to Barrio Magsaysay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Kialeg in the Municipality of Bansalan, Province of Davao, is changed to Barrio Magsaysay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 3, 1959.
