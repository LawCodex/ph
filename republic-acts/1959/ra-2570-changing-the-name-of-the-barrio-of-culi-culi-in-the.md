# Republic Act No. 2570

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Culi-Culi in the Municipality of Makati, Province of Rizal, to Pio Del Pilar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2570**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Culi-Culi in the Municipality of Makati, Province of Rizal, to Pio Del Pilar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Culi-Culi in the Municipality of Makati Province of Rizal, is changed to Pio del Pilar.
