# Republic Act No. 7597

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Rizal Memorial District Hospital in Dapitan City From Seventy-five (75) To One Hundred (100) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7597, June 02, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE RIZAL MEMORIAL DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN DAPITAN CITY FROM SEVENTY-FIVE (75) TO ONE HUNDRED (100) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Rizal Memorial District Hospital in Dapitan City is hereby increased from seventy-five (75) to one hundred (100) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
