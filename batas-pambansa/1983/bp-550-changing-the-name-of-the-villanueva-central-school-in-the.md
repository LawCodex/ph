# Batas Pambansa Blg. 550

**An Act Changing the Name of the VIllanueva Central School in the Municipality of VIllanueva, Province of Misamis Oriental, to VIcente N. Chaves Memorial Central School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 550**

An Act Changing the Name of the Villanueva Central School in the Municipality of Villanueva, Province of Misamis Oriental, to Vicente N. Chaves Memorial Central School

## Section 1

The name of the Villanueva Central School in the Municipality of Villanueva, Province of Misamis Oriental, is changed to Vicente N. Chaves Memorial Central School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
