# Republic Act No. 1462

**An Act to Amend Item Ten, ab. Marinduque, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1462**

**An Act to Amend Item Ten, ab. Marinduque, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item ten, ab. Marinduque, section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

| "10. Cabahuan Barrio School Building—Boac, Construction | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|
| "10-a. Binunga Barrio School Building—Boac, Construction | 5,000.00 |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
