# Republic Act No. 9414

**An Act Declaring June 19 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Northern Samar to Commemorate its Foundation Day**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9414 March 24, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE 19 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF NORTHERN SAMAR TO COMMEMORATE ITS FOUNDATION DAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

June 19 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Northern Samar to commemorate its foundation day.

## Section 2

This Act sha1l take effect immediately upon its approval.

Approved,
