# Republic Act No. 4450

**An Act Changing the Name of Balasan High School in the Municipality of Basalan, Province of Iloilo, to Leon Ganzon Memorial High School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4450**

An Act Changing the Name of Balasan High School in the Municipality of Basalan, Province of Iloilo, to Leon Ganzon Memorial High School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Balasan High School in the Municipality of Basalan, Province of Iloilo, is hereby change to Leon Ganzon Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
