# Act No. 3236

**An Act to Make An Additional Appropriation of Fifty thousand Pesos for the Campaign Against Rinderpest and Other Contagions and Infectious Cattle Diseases, By VIrtue of the Provisions of Act Numbered thirty-One Hundred and Sixty-Six, and for Other Purposes**

[ Act No. 3236, November 27, 1925 ]

AN ACT TO MAKE AN ADDITIONAL APPROPRIATION OF FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE CAMPAIGN AGAINST RINDERPEST AND OTHER CONTAGIONS AND INFECTIOUS CATTLE DISEASES, BY VIRTUE OF THE PROVISIONS OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-ONE HUNDRED AND SIXTY-SIX, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

For the purpose of continuing and carrying into effect the campaign for the eradication of rinderpest and other contagious and infectious cattle diseases in the Philippine Islands, initiated by virtue of the provisions of Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and sixty-six, the sum of fifty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to defray the necessary expenses in connection with the campaign against rinderpest and other contagious and infectious cattle diseases, and said sum shall be disbursed in the manner and for the purposes provided for in said Act Numbered Thirty-one hundred and sixty-six: _Provided,_ That any balance of the appropriation hereby made remaining unexpended at the end of each fiscal year shall not revert to the Insular Treasury, but shall be used for the. purposes for which said appropriation is made, until". exhausted.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 27, 1925.
