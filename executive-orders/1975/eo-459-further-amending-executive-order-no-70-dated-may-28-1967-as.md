# Executive Order No. 459

**Further Amending Executive Order No. 70 Dated May 28, 1967, as Amended By Executive Order No. 218 Dated March 21, 1970**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 459 December 23, 1975**

FURTHER AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 70 DATED MAY 28, 1967, AS AMENDED BY EXECUTIVE ORDER  
NO. 218 DATED MARCH 21, 1970

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby further amend Executive Order No. 70 dated May 28, 1967, as amended by Executive Order No. 218 dated March 21, 1970, to read as follows:

"The following functions of the Office of the President are likewise hereby transferred or relinquished to the needs of departments, offices or entities as indicated hereunder, in addition to those enumerated in Executive Order No. 70:

xxx xxx xxx

"22. Classification of Delegated to the roads under Republic Secretary of Act No. 917 Public Highways

Done in the City of Manila, this 23rd day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-five.
