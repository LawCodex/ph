# Republic Act No. 2119

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Dallao in the Municipality of Cordon, Province of Isabela, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Dallao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2119**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Dallao in the Municipality of Cordon, Province of Isabela, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Dallao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Dallao in the Municipality of Cordon, Province of Isabela, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as barrio Dallao.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
