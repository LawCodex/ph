# Republic Act No. 6161

**An Act Changing the Name of Lalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, to Doña Concepcion H. Umali Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6161**

**An Act Changing the Name of Lalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, to Doña Concepcion H. Umali Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Lalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Doña Concepcion H. Umali Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 9, 1970.
