# Republic Act No. 6702

**An Act Declaring September Thirteen as Battle Of&nbsp;pulang Lupa&nbsp;day and a Special Non-Working Holiday in the Province of Marinduque**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6702, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER THIRTEEN AS BATTLE OF PULANG LUPA DAY AND A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF MARINDUQUE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Every September thirteen is hereby declared as Battle of Pulang Lupa Day and a special non-working holiday in the Province of Marinduque to commemorate the battle of Pulang Lupa.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
