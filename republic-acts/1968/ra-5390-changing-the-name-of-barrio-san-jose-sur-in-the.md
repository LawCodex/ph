# Republic Act No. 5390

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Jose Sur in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Barrio Santo Niño**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5390**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Jose Sur in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Barrio Santo Niño

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio San Jose Sur in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, is hereby changed to Barrio Santo Niño.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
