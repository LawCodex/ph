# Republic Act No. 7468

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dibuluan Elementary School in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Isabela, To Jones East Central School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7468, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DIBULUAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF JONES, PROVINCE OF ISABELA, TO JONES EAST CENTRAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Dibuluan Elementary School in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to Jones East Central School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
