# Batas Pambansa Blg. 781

**An Act Changing the Name of the Glan Vocational High School in the Municipality of Glan, Province of South Cotabato, to Glan School of Arts and Trades**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 781**

An Act Changing the Name of the Glan Vocational High School in the Municipality of Glan, Province of South Cotabato, to Glan School of Arts and Trades

## Section 1

The name of the Glan Vocational High School in the Municipality of Glan, Province of South Cotabato, is changed to Glan School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
