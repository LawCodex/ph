# Republic Act No. 2595

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pagsulhogon in the Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2595**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pagsulhogon in the Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pagsulhogon in the Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Pagsulhogon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
