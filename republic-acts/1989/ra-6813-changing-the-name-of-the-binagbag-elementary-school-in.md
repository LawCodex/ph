# Republic Act No. 6813

**An Act Changing the Name of the Binagbag Elementary School in Barangay Binagbag, Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, to Francisco F. Illescas Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6813 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BINAGBAG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BINAGBAG, MUNICIPALITY OF ANGAT, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO FRANCISCO F. ILLESCAS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Binagbag Elementary School located in Binagbag, Municipality of Angat, Province of Bulacan, is hereby renamed as Francisco F. Illescas Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
