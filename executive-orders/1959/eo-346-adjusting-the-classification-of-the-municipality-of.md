# Executive Order No. 346

**Adjusting the Classification of the Municipality of Alitagtag, Province of Batangas, and Amending for This Purpose, Executive Order No. 333, Dated March 28, 1959**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 346, July 29, 1959 ]

ADJUSTING THE CLASSIFICATION OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALITAGTAG, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, AND AMENDING FOR THIS PURPOSE, EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 333, DATED MARCH 28, 1959

**WHEREAS,** upon verification by the General Auditing Office, it was found out that the average annual revenue for the fiscal years 1955-58 of the municipality of Alitagtag was P31,779.37; and

**WHEREAS,** under section 2170 of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended, the municipality of Alitagtag should-have been re-classified as 2nd class instead of 3rd class;

**NOW, THEREFORE, I, CARLOS P. GARCIA** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by law, do hereby adjust the re-classification of the municipality of Alitagtag, province of Batangas, to 2nd class.

This classification takes effect as of July 1, 1958.

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF,** I have hereunto set my hand and caused the seal of the Republic of the Philippines to be affixed.

Done in the City of Manila, this 29th day of July, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-nine, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fourteenth.

(Sgd.) **CARLOS P. GARCIA**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(Sgd.) **JUAN C. PAJO**
Executive Secretary
