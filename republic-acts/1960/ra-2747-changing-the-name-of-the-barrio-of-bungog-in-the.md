# Republic Act No. 2747

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bungog in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to Mabuhay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2747**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Bungog in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to Mabuhay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Bungog in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, is changed to Mabuhay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
