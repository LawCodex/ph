# Batas Pambansa Blg. 479

**An Act Establishing a National High School in the Municipality of Maimbung, Province of Sulu, to Be Known as the Maimbung National High School, and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 479**

An Act Establishing a National High School in the Municipality of Maimbung, Province of Sulu, to be Known as the Maimbung National School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

A national high school is hereby established, in the Municipality of Maimbung, Province of Sulu, to be known as the Maimbung National High School.

## Section 2

Such sum as may necessary for the operation and maintenance of said school shall be provided in subsequent General Appropriations Acts, until which time the operation of the institution as a national high school shall be deferred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1983
