# Republic Act No. 3770

**An Act Changing the Name of the Buluan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Buluan, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Luminog Mangelen Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3770**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Buluan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Buluan, Province of Cotabato, to Datu Luminog Mangelen Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Buluan Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Buluan, Province of Cotabato, is hereby changed to Datu Luminog Mangelen Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
