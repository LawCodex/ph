# Republic Act No. 1045

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pinamalayan Elementary School, in the Municipality of Pinamalayan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Juan Morente, Sr. Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1045**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pinamalayan Elementary School, in the Municipality of Pinamalayan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Juan Morente, Sr. Memorial School**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the Pinamalayan Elementary School, in the Municipality of Pinamalayan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is changed to Juan Morente, Sr. Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
