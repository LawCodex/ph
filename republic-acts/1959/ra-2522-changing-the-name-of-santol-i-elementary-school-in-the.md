# Republic Act No. 2522

**An Act Changing the Name of Santol I Elementary School in the Municipality of Bigaa, Province of Bulacan, to Marciano C. Rivera Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2522**

**An Act Changing the Name of Santol I Elementary School in the Municipality of Bigaa, Province of Bulacan, to Marciano C. Rivera Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Santol I Elementary School in the Municipality of Bigaa, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Marciano C. Rivera Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
