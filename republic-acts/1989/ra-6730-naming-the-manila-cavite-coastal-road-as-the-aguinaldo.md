# Republic Act No. 6730

**An Act Naming the Manila-Cavite Coastal Road as the Aguinaldo Boulevard**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6730 June 14, 1989**

AN ACT NAMING THE MANILA-CAVITE COASTAL ROAD AS THE AGUINALDO BOULEVARD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Manila-Cavite coastal road starting from the corner of Roxas Boulevard and MIA road to Cavite shall hereafter be known as the Aguinaldo Boulevard.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general circulation.

Approved: **June 14, 1989**
