# Batas Pambansa Blg. 754

**An Act Changing the Name of the Northern Antique Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, to Governor Leandro Locsin Fullon General Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 754**

An Act Changing the Name of the Northern Antique Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, to Governor Leandro Locsin Fullon General Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Northern Antique Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, is changed to Governor Leandro Locsin Fullon General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
