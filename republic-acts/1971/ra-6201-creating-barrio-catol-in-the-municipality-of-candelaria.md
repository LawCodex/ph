# Republic Act No. 6201

**An Act Creating Barrio Catol in the Municipality of Candelaria, Province of Zambales.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6201, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO CATOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CANDELARIA, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Catol in the Municipality of Candelaria, Province of Zambales, is hereby converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as Barrio Catol.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
