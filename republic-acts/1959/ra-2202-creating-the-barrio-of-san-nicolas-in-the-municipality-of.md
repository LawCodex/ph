# Republic Act No. 2202

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Nicolas in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2202**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Nicolas in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of San Nicolas, Domonob, Barita, Malobog, Pangi and Lo-ok in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Nicolas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
