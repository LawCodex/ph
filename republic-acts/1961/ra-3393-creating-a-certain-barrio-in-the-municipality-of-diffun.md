# Republic Act No. 3393

**An Act Creating a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Diffun, Province of Nueva Vizcaya**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3393**

**An Act Creating a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Diffun, Province of Nueva Vizcaya**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Amañga, Campamiento, Veniado, Peña, Dumanting Decal-la, and Makati, in the Municipality of Diffun, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio, to be known as the barrio of Magsaysay.1avvphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
