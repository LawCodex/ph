# Republic Act No. 9380

**An Act Declaring May 6 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon to Commemorate the Death Anniversary of Mayor Manuel T. Sia**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9380 March 8, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING MAY 6 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PILAR, PROVINCE OF SORSOGON TO COMMEMORATE THE DEATH ANNIVERSARY OF MAYOR MANUEL T. SIA

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

To commemorate the death anniversary of Mayor Manuel T. Sia, Municipal Mayor of the Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon, May 6 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
