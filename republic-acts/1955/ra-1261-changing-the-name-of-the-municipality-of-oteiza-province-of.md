# Republic Act No. 1261

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Oteiza, Province of Surigao, to Marihatag**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1261**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Oteiza, Province of Surigao, to Marihatag**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Oteiza in the Province of Surigao is hereby changed to Marihatag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
