# Republic Act No. 5340

**An Act Changing the Name of Tolosa Central Community School in the Municipality of Tolosa, Province of Leyte, to Daniel Z. Romualdez Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5340**

An Act Changing the Name of Tolosa Central Community School in the Municipality of Tolosa, Province of Leyte, to Daniel Z. Romualdez Memorial School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tolosa Central Community School in the municipality of Tolosa, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Daniel Z. Romualdez Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
