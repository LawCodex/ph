# Republic Act No. 8320

**An Act Establishing A National High School in Barangay Cabalic, Municipality of Balasan, Province of Iloilo, To Be Known As the Cabalic National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8320, June 30, 1997 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY CABALIC, MUNICIPALITY OF BALASAN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE CABALIC NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national high school in Barangay Cabalic, Municipality of Balasan, Province of Iloilo, to be known as the Cabalic National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on June 30, 1997 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27 (1), Article VI of the Constitution.
