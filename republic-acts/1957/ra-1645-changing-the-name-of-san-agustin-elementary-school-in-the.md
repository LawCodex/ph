# Republic Act No. 1645

**An Act Changing the Name of San Agustin Elementary School in the Barrio of San Agustin, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, to Don Felix De Leon Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1645**

**An Act Changing the Name of San Agustin Elementary School in the Barrio of San Agustin, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, to Don Felix De Leon Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of San Agustin Elementary School in the barrio of San Agustin, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, is changed to Don Felix de Leon Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
