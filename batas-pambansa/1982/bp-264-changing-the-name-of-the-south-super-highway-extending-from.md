# Batas Pambansa Blg. 264

**An Act Changing the Name of the South Super Highway, Extending From the Municipality of Makati, Metropolitan Manila, Up to the Municipality of Calamba, Province of Laguna, to Dr. Jose P. Rizal Highway.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 264**

An Act Changing the Name of the South Super Highway, Extending from the Municipality of Makati, Metropolitan Manila, Up to the Municipality of Calamba, Province of Laguna, to Dr. Jose P. Rizal Highway

## Section 1

The name of the South Super Highway, extending from Kilometer 8.970 at the junction of Epifanio de los Santos Avenue (EDSA) in the Municipality of Makati, Metropolitan Manila, up to Kilometer 52.365 at the junction of Calamba, Province of Laguna, or a total length of 43.395 kilometers, is hereby changed to Dr. Jose P. Rizal Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 13, 1982
