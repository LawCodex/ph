# Republic Act No. 7341

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mercedes Municipal High School in the Municipality of Mercedes, Province of Eastern Samar, to Mercedes High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7341, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MERCEDES MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MERCEDES, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO MERCEDES HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mercedes Municipal High School in the Municipality of Mercedes, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Mercedes High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
