# Republic Act No. 7708

**An Act Changing the Name of the Angelina Dumajel Elementary School in the Municipality of Kalawit, Zamboanga Del Norte, to Batayan Elementary School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7708, May 05, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ANGELINA DUMAJEL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF KALAWIT, ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, TO BATAYAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Angelina Dumajel Elementary School in the Municipality of Kalawit, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Batayan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its publication in at least two (2) national newspapers of general circulation.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved,

Lapsed into law on May 5, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
