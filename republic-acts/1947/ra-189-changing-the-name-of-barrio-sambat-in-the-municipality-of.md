# Republic Act No. 189

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Sambat, in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas, to Lumangbayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 189**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Sambat, in the Municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas, to Lumangbayan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Sambat, in the municipality of Rosario, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Lumangbayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1947.
