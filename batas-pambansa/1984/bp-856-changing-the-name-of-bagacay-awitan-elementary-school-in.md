# Batas Pambansa Blg. 856

**An Act Changing the Name of Bagacay Awitan Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, Into Paaralang Elementaria Ng L. VIllamonte**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 856**

An Act Changing the Name of Bagacay Awitan Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, into Paaralang Elementaria Ng L. Villamonte

## Section 1

The name of Bagacay Awitan Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, shall be changed to Paaralang Elementaria Ng L. Villamonte.

## Section 2

The record of the Ministry of Education, Culture and Sports bearing the name of the school mentioned in Section 1 hereof shall be changed and be named to Paaralang Elementaria Ng L. Villamonte.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
