# Republic Act No. 3787

**An Act Amending Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Two Thousand Seven Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3787**

**An Act Amending Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Two Thousand Seven Hundred and One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 16, page 70, and sub-paragraph 32, for the City of Manila, paragraph c, Title B, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Two thousand seven hundred and one is hereby amended to read as follows:

"16. Governor Forbes Street, Sampaloc, from Lealtad Street to Laon-Laan Street

Improvement of lighting system and equipment 100,000.00"

## Section 2

Item (c) page 481, Group III, sub paragraph 31, for the City of Manila, paragraph c, Section two of the same Act is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(c) Nicanor Reyes, Sampaloc, from Claro M. Recto Avenue to España Street

Improvement of lighting system and equipment 50,000.00"

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
