# Republic Act No. 2223

**An Act Changing Certain Names of Barrios in the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2223**

**An Act Changing Certain Names of Barrios in the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the following barrios in the Municipality of Liloy, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are changed, to wit:

1\. Barrio Liloy Beach to Barrio Bayabay; and

2\. Barrio Liloy Townsite to Barrio Liloy.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
