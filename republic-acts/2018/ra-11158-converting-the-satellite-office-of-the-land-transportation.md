# Republic Act No. 11158

**An Act Converting the Satellite Office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the City of Bayugan, Province of Agusan Del Sur, Into a Regular District Office, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11158**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SATELLITE OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN THE CITY OF BAYUGAN, PROVINCE OF AGUSAN DEL SUR, INTO A REGULAR DISTRICT OFFICE, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Satellite Office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the City of Bayugan, Province of Agusan del Sur, is hereby converted into a regular district office to be known as the LTO District Office.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Transportation shall include in the Department’s program the operationalization of the LTO district office in the City of Bayugan, Province of Agusan del Sur, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
