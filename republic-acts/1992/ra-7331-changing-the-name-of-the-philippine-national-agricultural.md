# Republic Act No. 7331

**An Act Changing the Name of the Philippine National Agricultural School in the Municipality of Sta. Maria, Bulacan, to Fortunato F. Halili National Agricultural School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7331, March 31, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF STA. MARIA, BULACAN, TO FORTUNATO F. HALILI NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Philippine National Agricultural School in Ouyong, Sta. Maria, Bulacan, is hereby changed to Fortunato F. Halili National Agricultural School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
