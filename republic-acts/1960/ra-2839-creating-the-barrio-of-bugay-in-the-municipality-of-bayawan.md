# Republic Act No. 2839

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bugay in the Municipality of Bayawan, Province of Negros Oriental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2839**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bugay in the Municipality of Bayawan, Province of Negros Oriental**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bugay, Bato, Baboy, Manobilan, Manduaw and Bucao in the Municipality of Bayawan, Province of Negros Oriental, are hereby constituted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as Barrio Bugay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
