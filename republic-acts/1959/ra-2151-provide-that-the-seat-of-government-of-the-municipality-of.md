# Republic Act No. 2151

**An Act to Provide that the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Aglipay, Nueva Vizcaya, be Located at Guinalbin**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2151**

**An Act to Provide that the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Aglipay, Nueva Vizcaya, be Located at Guinalbin**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Aglipay, Nueva Vizcaya, shall be located at Guinalbin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
