# Republic Act No. 9429

**An Act Declaring September 18 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the City of Tacurong, Province of Sultan Kudarat, to Commemorate its Charter Day Anniversary**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9429 April 10, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER 18 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF TACURONG, PROVINCE OF SULTAN KUDARAT, TO COMMEMORATE ITS CHARTER DAY ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

September 18 of every year is hereby declared a spcial working holiday in the City of Tacurong, Province of Sultan Kudarat to commemorate its charter day anniversary.

## SEC. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
