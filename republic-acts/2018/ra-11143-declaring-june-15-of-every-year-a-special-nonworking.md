# Republic Act No. 11143

**An Act Declaring June 15 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Opol, Province of Misamis Oriental, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to Be Known as Araw Ng Opol**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11143**

An Act Declaring June 15 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Opol, Province of Misamis Oriental, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, To be Known as "Araw ng Opol".

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

June 15 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Opol, Province of Misamis Oriental, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as "Araw ng Opol".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
