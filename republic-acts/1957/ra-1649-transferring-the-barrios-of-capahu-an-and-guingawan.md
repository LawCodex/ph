# Republic Act No. 1649

**An Act Transferring the Barrios of Capahu-an and Guingawan, Municipality of Dagami, Province of Leyte, to the Municipality of Tabontabon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1649**

**An Act Transferring the Barrios of Capahu-an and Guingawan, Municipality of Dagami, Province of Leyte, to the Municipality of Tabontabon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrios of Capahu-an and Guingawan, Municipality of Dagami, Province of Leyte, are hereby transferred to the Municipality of Tabontabon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
