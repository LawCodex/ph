# Batas Pambansa Blg. 801

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dumeles Elementary School in the Municipality of San Enrique, Province of Iloilo, to Don Pedro M. Fernandez Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 801**

An Act Changing the Name of the Dumeles Elementary School in the Municipality of San Enrique, Province of Iloilo, to Don Pedro M. Fernandez Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Dumeles Elementary School in the Municipality of San Enrique, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Don Pedro M. Fernandez Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
