# Republic Act No. 1739

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Banto-anin, Municipality of Gasan, Province of Marinduque, to Barrio Bukal**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1739**

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Banto-anin, Municipality of Gasan, Province of Marinduque, to Barrio Bukal**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Banto-anin, in the Municipality of Gasan, Province of Marinduque, is hereby changed to Bukal.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
