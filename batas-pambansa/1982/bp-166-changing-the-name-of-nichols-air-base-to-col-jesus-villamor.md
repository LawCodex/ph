# Batas Pambansa Blg. 166

**An Act Changing the Name of Nichols Air Base to Col. Jesus VIllamor Air Base.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 166**

An Act Changing the Name of Nichols Air Base to Col. Jesus Villamor Air Base

## Section 1

Nichols Air Base shall hereafter be known and referred to as Col. Jesus Villamor Air Base.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 8, 1982
