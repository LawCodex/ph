# Executive Order No. 344

**Amending Executive Order No. 318, Dated May 25, 1950, Creating the Integrity Board**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ ADMINISTRATIVE ORDER NO. 344 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 318, DATED MAY 25, 1950, CREATING THE INTEGRITY BOARD

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, **I, ELPIDIO QUIRINO,** President of the Philippines, do hereby amend Executive Order No. 318, dated May 25. 1950, creating the Integrity Board, so as to have said body composed of the following:

| Dr. Jorge C. Bocobo | ................... | Chairman |
|---|---|---|
| Justice Mariano H. de Joya | ................... | Member |
| Justice Mariano Albert | ................... | Member |
| Judge Pompeyo Diaz | ................... | Member |
| Mrs. Pilar Hidalgo-Lim | ................... | Member |
| Mr. Manuel Agudo | ................... | Executive Secretary |

Done in the City of Manila, this 7th day of September, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fifth.

(Sgd.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(Sgd.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
