# Republic Act No. 11124

**An Act Declaring September 7 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the City of Cavite, Province of Cavite, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11124**

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER 7 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF CAVITE, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

September 7 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the City of Cavite, Province of Cavite, in commemoration of its founding anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.1âwphi1
