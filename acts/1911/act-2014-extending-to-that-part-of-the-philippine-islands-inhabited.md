# Act No. 2014

**An Act Extending to that Part of the Philippine Islands Inhabited By Moros or Other Non-Christian Tribes the Provisions of Acts Numbered Two thousand and Eleven and Two thousand and Twelve of the Philippine Legislature.**

[ Act No. 2014, January 18, 1911 ]

AN ACT EXTENDING TO THAT PART OF THE PHILIPPINE ISLANDS INHABITED BY MOROS OR OTHER NON-CHRISTIAN TRIBES THE PROVISIONS OF ACTS NUMBERED TWO THOUSAND AND ELEVEN AND TWO THOUSAND AND TWELVE OF THE PHILIPPINE LEGISLATURE.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

There are hereby extended to and placed in force throughout that part of the Philippine Islands inhabited by Moros or other non-Christian tribes the provisions of the following Acts of the Legislature:

No. 2011; passed January 12, 1911; entitled "An Act amending section thirty-nine of Act Numbered Four hundred and ninety-six, entitled 'The Land Registration Act,' so as to extend to Government irrigation canals and laterals thereof the exemptions established in favor of public highways."

No. 2012; passed January 13, 1911; entitled "An Act to amend section eighteen of Act Numbered Fourteen hundred and fifty-nine. entitled 'The Corporation Law,' by providing for the collection of fees for filing copies of amended articles of incorporation, and for other purposes."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect as to each of said Acts simultaneously with the taking effect of the Act in question.

Enacted, January 18, 1911.
