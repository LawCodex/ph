# Executive Order No. 290

**Amending Executive Order No. 243, Dated July 7, 1970, Creating the National Committee for Mineral Exploration and Survey Operations.**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 290, February 10, 1971 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 243, DATED JULY 7, 1970, CREATING THE NATIONAL COMMITTEE FOR MINERAL EXPLORATION AND SURVEY OPERATIONS

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, **FERDINAND E. MARCOS** , President of the Philippines, do hereby amend Executive Order No. 243, dated July 7, 1970, creating the National Committee for Mineral Exploration and Survey Operations, by including a representative of the Central Bank as a member of said committee.

Done the City of Manila, this 10th day of February, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-one.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **ALEJANDRO MELCHOR**
Executive Secretary
