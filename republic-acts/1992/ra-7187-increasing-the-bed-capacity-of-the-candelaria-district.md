# Republic Act No. 7187

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Candelaria District Hospital in the Municipality of Candelaria, Province of Zambales, From Twenty-five (25) to Fifty (50) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7187, January 30, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE CANDELARIA DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CANDELARIA, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES, FROM TWENTY-FIVE (25) TO FIFTY (50) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Candelaria District Hospital in the Municipality of Candelaria, Province of Zambales, is hereby increased from twenty-five (25) to fifty (50) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.
