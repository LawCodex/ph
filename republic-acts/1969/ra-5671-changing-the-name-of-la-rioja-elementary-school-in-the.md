# Republic Act No. 5671

**An Act Changing the Name of La Rioja Elementary School in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, to Teofista M. Salvani Community School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5671, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF LA RIOJA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PATNONGON, PROVINCE OF ANTIQUE, TO TEOFISTA M. SALVANI COMMUNITY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of La Rioja Elementary School in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Teofista M. Salvani Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
