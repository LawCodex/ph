# Republic Act No. 3565

**An Act Amending Item (a), Page 45, Subparagraph (17) III, for the Province of Cebu, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3565**

**An Act Amending Item (a), Page 45, Subparagraph (17) III, for the Province of Cebu, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (a), page 45, subparagraph (17) III, paragraph f, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(a) Talisay Municipal Hall Construction ₱75,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
