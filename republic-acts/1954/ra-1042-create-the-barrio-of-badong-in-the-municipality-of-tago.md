# Republic Act No. 1042

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Badong in the Municipality of Tago, Province of Surigao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1042**

**An Act to Create the Barrio of Badong in the Municipality of Tago, Province of Surigao**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio in Badong in the municipality of Tago, Province of Surigao, is hereby constituted into a barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Badong.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
