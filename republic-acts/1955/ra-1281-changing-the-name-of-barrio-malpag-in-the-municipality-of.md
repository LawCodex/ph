# Republic Act No. 1281

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Malpag in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Babulak**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1281**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Malpag in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Babulak**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Malpag in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Leyte, shall hereafter be known as barrio Babulak.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1955.
