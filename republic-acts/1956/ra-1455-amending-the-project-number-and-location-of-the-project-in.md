# Republic Act No. 1455

**An Act Amending the Project Number and Location of the Project in Appendix A, Title G, of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1455**

**An Act Amending the Project Number and Location of the Project in Appendix A, Title G, of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The project number and location of the project for Project Numbered G-05.10 in Appendix A, Title G, of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"G-32.1: Camalig-Catmon Irrigation Project, Albay."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
