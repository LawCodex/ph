# Republic Act No. 7350

**An Act Declaring October 12 of Every Year a Special Non-working Holiday in the City of Zamboanga**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7350, April 02, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING OCTOBER 12 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF ZAMBOANGA

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

October 12 of every year is hereby declared a special non-working holiday in the City of Zamboanga.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
