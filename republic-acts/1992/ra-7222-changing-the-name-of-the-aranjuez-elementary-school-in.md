# Republic Act No. 7222

**An Act Changing the Name of the Aranjuez Elementary School in Barangay Aranjuez, Municipality of Balasan, Province of Iloilo, to Pedro Bedez Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7222, March 11, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ARANJUEZ ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY ARANJUEZ, MUNICIPALITY OF BALASAN, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO PEDRO BEDEZ ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Aranjuez Elementary School in Barangay Aranjuez, Municipality of Balasan, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Pedro Bedez Elementary School.
