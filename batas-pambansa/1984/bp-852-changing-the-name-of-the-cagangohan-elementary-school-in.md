# Batas Pambansa Blg. 852

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cagangohan Elementary School in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, to Dondoy Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 852**

An Act Changing the Name of the Cagangohan Elementary School in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, to Dondoy Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Cagangohan Elementary School in the Municipality of Panabo, Province of Davao, is changed to Dondoy Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
