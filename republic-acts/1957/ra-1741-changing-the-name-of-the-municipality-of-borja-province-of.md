# Republic Act No. 1741

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Borja, Province of Bohol, to Sagbayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1741**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Borja, Province of Bohol, to Sagbayan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Borja, Province of Bohol, is changed to Sagbayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
