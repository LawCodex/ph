# Republic Act No. 4434

**An Act Changing the Name of Camp Murphy to Camp General Emilio Aguinaldo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4434**

An Act Changing the Name of Camp Murphy to Camp General Emilio Aguinaldo

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Camp Murphy shall hereafter be known and referred to as Camp General Emilio Aguinaldo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
