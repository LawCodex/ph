# Republic Act No. 1320

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Juan in Maricaban Island, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1320**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Juan in Maricaban Island, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pirasan in the barrio of Payapa, Maricaban Island, Municipality of Bauan, Province of Batangas, is separated from the said barrio of Payapa and constituted into a separate and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of San Juan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
