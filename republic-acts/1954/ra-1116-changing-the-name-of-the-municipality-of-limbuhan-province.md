# Republic Act No. 1116

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Limbuhan, Province of Masbate to Pio V. Corpus**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1116**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Limbuhan, Province of Masbate to Pio V. Corpus**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

In recognition of the eminent and patriotic services rendered by the late Pio V. Corpus, the name of the municipality of Limbuhan, Province of Masbate, is changed to Pio V. Corpus.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1954.
