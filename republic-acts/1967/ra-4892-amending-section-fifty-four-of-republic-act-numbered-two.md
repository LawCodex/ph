# Republic Act No. 4892

**An Act Amending Section Fifty-Four of Republic Act Numbered Two Hundred Ninety-Six, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4892**

An Act Amending Section Fifty-Four of Republic Act Numbered Two Hundred Ninety-Six, as Amended

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The third paragraph of Section fifty-four of Republic Act Numbered Two hundred ninety-six, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

"First Judicial District: At Santo Domingo de Basco, Province of Batanes, on the first Tuesday of May of each year. A special term of court shall be held once a year in the Municipality of Ballesteros, Province of Cagayan."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
