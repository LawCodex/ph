# Republic Act No. 1006

**An Act Creating a Sitio in the Eastern Part of Barrio Tabang, Municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, to be Known as the Sitio of Bagong Sikat**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1006**

**An Act Creating a Sitio in the Eastern Part of Barrio Tabang, Municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, to be Known as the Sitio of Bagong Sikat**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

A sitio in the eastern part of barrio Tabang, municipality of Plaridel, Province of Bulacan, is created to be known as the sitio of Bagong Sikat.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
