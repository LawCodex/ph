# Republic Act No. 2558

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Isabela**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2558**

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Isabela**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Isabela, are constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, as follows:

Sitios Salay and Narien to Barrio Salay.

## Section 2

The following sitios in the Municipality of Tumauini, Province of Isabela, are constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, as follows:

Sitios Sical and Cabal to Barrio Namnama.

## Section 3

The sitio of Puroc No. 6, Salinungan, in the Municipality of San Mateo, Province of Isabela, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Salinungan East.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
