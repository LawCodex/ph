# Republic Act No. 5121

**An Act Changing the Name of Ninth Avenue Elementary School, Maria Clara Street, Caloocan City, to Maria Clara Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5121**

An Act Changing the Name of Ninth Avenue Elementary School, Maria Clara Street, Caloocan City, to Maria Clara Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Ninth Avenue Elementary School, Maria Clara Street, Caloocan City, is hereby changed to Maria Clara Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
