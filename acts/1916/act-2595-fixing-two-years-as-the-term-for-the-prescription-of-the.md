# Act No. 2595

**An Act Fixing Two Years as the Term for the Prescription of the Crime of Libel and of a Civil Action Arising therefrom.**

[ Act No. 2595, February 04, 1916 ]

AN ACT FIXING TWO YEARS AS THE TERM FOR THE PRESCRIPTION OF THE CRIME OF LIBEL AND OF A CIVIL ACTION ARISING THEREFROM.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The crime of libel as well as the civil action arising therefrom shall prescribe two years after the publication of the libel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, February 4, 1916.
