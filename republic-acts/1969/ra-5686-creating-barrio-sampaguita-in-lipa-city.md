# Republic Act No. 5686

**An Act Creating Barrio Sampaguita in Lipa City**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5686, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO SAMPAGUITA IN LIPA CITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Sarapalocan in Barrio Cumba, Lipa City, is hereby separated from said barrio, and converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said city, to be known as Barrio Sampaguita.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
