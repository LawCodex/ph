# Republic Act No. 1141

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Malitam, in the Barrio of Libjo, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1141**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Malitam, in the Barrio of Libjo, Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Malitam, in the barrio of Libjo, municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, is converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Malitam.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1954.
