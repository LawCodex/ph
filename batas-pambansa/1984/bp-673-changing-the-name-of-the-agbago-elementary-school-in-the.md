# Batas Pambansa Blg. 673

**An Act Changing the Name of the Agbago Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Don Mauro S. Tumbocon Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 673**

An Act Changing the Name of the Agbago Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Don Mauro S. Tumbocon Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Agbago Elementary School located in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, is changed to Don Mauro S. Tumbocon Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: Macrh 7, 1984
