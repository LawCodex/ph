# Republic Act No. 2019

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lucban Elementary School in the City of Baguio to Gregorio Del Pilar Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2019**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lucban Elementary School in the City of Baguio to Gregorio Del Pilar Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lucban Elementary School in the City of Baguio is hereby changed to Gregorio del Pilar Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
