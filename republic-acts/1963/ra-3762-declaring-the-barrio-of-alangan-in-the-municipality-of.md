# Republic Act No. 3762

**An Act Declaring the Barrio of Alañgan in the Municipality of Limay, Province of Bataan, a Duly Constituted Barrio Thereof**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3762**

**An Act Declaring the Barrio of Alañgan in the Municipality of Limay, Province of Bataan, a Duly Constituted Barrio Thereof**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Alañgan in the Municipality of Limay, Province of Bataan, is hereby declared a duly constituted barrio of said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
