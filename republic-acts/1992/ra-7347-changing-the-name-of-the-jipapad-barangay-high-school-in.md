# Republic Act No. 7347

**An Act Changing the Name of the Jipapad Barangay High School in the Municipality of Jipapad, Province of Eastern Samar, to Jipapad High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7347, April 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE JIPAPAD BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF JIPAPAD, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO JIPAPAD HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Jipapad Barangay High School in the Municipality of Jipapad, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Jipapad High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
