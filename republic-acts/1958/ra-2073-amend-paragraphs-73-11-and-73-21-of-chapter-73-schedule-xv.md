# Republic Act No. 2073

**An Act to Amend Paragraphs 73.11 and 73.21 of Chapter 73, Schedule XV, of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred Thirty-Seven, Otherwise Known as Tariff and Customs Code of the Philippines, and for Other Purposes**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2073**

AN ACT TO AMEND PARAGRAPHS 73.11 AND 73.21 OF CHAPTER 73, SCHEDULE XV, OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED NINETEEN HUNDRED THIRTY-SEVEN, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS TARIFF AND CUSTOMS CODE OF THE PHILIPPINES, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

## Section 1

Paragraph 73.11 of Chapter 73, Schedule XV, of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred thirty-seven is hereby amended to read as follows:

<table>
<tr>
<td rowspan="4">"73.11</td>
<td colspan="3">Angles, shapes, sections and sheet piling, of iron or steel, hot-rolled, forged or cold-finished, whether or not drilled or punched, but not assembled:</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>A.</td>
<td>Angles of which the maximum cross-sectional dimension does not exceed eighty-eight millimeters and channels of which the maximum cross-section dimension does not exceed seventy-five millimeters</td>
<td>ad val. 25%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>B.</td>
<td>Riveted, welded, punched, or drilled</td>
<td>ad val. 80%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>C.</td>
<td>Other</td>
<td>ad val. 15%."</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Section 2

Paragraph 73.21 of Chapter 73, Schedule XV, of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred thirty-seven, is hereby amended to read as follows:

<table>
<tr>
<td rowspan="4">"73.21</td>
<td>Assembled sheet pilling of cast iron, iron or steel; girders, beams, joists and pillars made from assembled elements; structures and finished parts for assembly into structures (e.g., bridge sections, roofing frameworks, shutters, pylons, balconies, balustrades, verandas, window and frames) of cast iron, iron or steel</td>
<td>ad val. 80%."</td>
</tr>
<tr>
</tr>
</table>

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 13, 1958
