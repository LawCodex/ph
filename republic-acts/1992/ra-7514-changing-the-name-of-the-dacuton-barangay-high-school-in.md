# Republic Act No. 7514

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dacuton Barangay High School in the Municipality of Dumarao, Province of Capiz, To Mayor Ramon A. Benjamin, Sr., Memorial High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7514, May 21, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DACUTON BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DUMARAO, PROVINCE OF CAPIZ, TO MAYOR RAMON A. BENJAMIN, SR., MEMORIAL HLGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Dacuton Barangay High School in the Municipality of Dumarao, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to Mayor Ramon A. Benjamin, Sr., Memorial High School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
