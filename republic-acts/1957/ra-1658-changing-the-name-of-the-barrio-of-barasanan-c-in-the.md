# Republic Act No. 1658

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Barasanan (C) in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Ballescas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1658**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Barasanan (C) in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Ballescas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Barasanan (C) in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Ballescas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
