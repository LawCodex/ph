# Republic Act No. 1111

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Dumaran, Province of Palawan, to Araceli**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1111**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Dumaran, Province of Palawan, to Araceli**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Dumaran, Province of Palawan, is changed to Araceli.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1954.
