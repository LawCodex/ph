# Republic Act No. 2453

**An Act Amending Certain Items of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Hundred and Ninety-Three**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2453**

**An Act Amending Certain Items of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Hundred and Ninety-Three**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item III (f), for the Province of Cebu, page two hundred five of Republic Act Numbered Twenty hundred and ninety-three, is hereby amended to read as follows:

III

"(f) Ocaña Elementary School, Carcar Construction of one additional room ₱6,000.00

"(f) Standard shop building, Noel-type, size: 6.90 X 8.55, Carcar Construction ₱6,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
