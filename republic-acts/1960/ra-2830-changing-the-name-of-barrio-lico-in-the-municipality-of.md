# Republic Act No. 2830

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Lico in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Bilbao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2830**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Lico in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Bilbao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Lico in the Municipality of Naval, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Barrio Bilbao.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
