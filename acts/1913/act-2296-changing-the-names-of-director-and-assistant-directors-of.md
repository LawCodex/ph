# Act No. 2296

**An Act Changing the Names of Director and Assistant Directors of Constabulary to "Chief and Assistant Chiefs of Constabulary."**

[ Act No. 2296, November 13, 1913 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAMES OF DIRECTOR AND ASSISTANT DIRECTORS OF CONSTABULARY TO "CHIEF AND ASSISTANT CHIEFS OF CONSTABULARY."

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The designation of Director and Assistant Directors of the Philippine Insular Constabulary, is hereby substituted by that of "Chief and Assistant Chiefs," respectively.

## Section 2

All provisions of existing laws inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby expressly repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, November 13, 1913.
