# Batas Pambansa Blg. 237

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lower San Raymundo Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Sheik Salim Abubakar Primary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 237**

An Act Changing the Name of the Lower San Raymundo Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Sheik Salim Abubakar Primary School

## Section 1

The Lower San Raymundo Primary School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, shall hereafter be known as Sheik Salim Abubakar Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: September 12, 1982
