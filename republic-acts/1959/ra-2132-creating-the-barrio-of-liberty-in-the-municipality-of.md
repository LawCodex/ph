# Republic Act No. 2132

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Liberty in the Municipality of Palompon, Province of Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2132**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Liberty in the Municipality of Palompon, Province of Leyte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Liberty, Calunasan, Cabulihan, Casagawan, Otap and Cabaliwan in the Municipality of Palompon, Province of Leyte, are constituted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Liberty.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
