# Republic Act No. 8968

**An Act Renaming the Caramoan Peninsula Road in the Province of Camarines Sur to Governor Felix A. Fuentebella National Highway**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8968 October 4, 2000**

AN ACT RENAMING THE CARAMOAN PENINSULA ROAD IN THE PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR TO GOVERNOR FELIX A. FUENTEBELLA NATIONAL HIGHWAY

## SECTION 1

The Caramoan Peninsula road, a national road connecting and comprising the municipalities of Lagonoy, Presentacion, Garchitorena and Caramoan, all in the Province of Camarines Sur and bounded by the following road sections, to wit: (a) the 31.73 km. Lagonoy - Presentacion road; (b) the 18.65 km. Presentacion - Maligaya road; (c) the 31.748 km. Maligaya Junction - Caramoan - Guijalo road; and (d) the 12.075 km. Maligaya - Garchitorena road, is hereby renamed as the Governor Felix A. Fuentebella National Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **October 4, 2000**
