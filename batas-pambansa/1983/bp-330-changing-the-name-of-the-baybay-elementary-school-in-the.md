# Batas Pambansa Blg. 330

**An Act Changing the Name of the Baybay Elementary School in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Governor Santos Capadocia Memorial School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 330**

An Act Changing the Name of the Baybay Elementary School in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, to Governor Santos Capadocia Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Baybay Elementary School in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Antique, is changed to Governor Santos Capadocia Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
