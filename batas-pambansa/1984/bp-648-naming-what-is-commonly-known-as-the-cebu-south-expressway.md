# Batas Pambansa Blg. 648

**An Act Naming What Is Commonly Known as the Cebu South Expressway And/Or the Cebu South Road in Cebu City and Cebu Province as the Natalio B. Bacalso South National Highway**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 648**

An Act Naming What Is Commonly Known as the Cebu South Expressway And/or the Cebu South Road in Cebu City and Cebu Province as the Natalio B. Bacalso South National Highway

## Section 1

The National Highway starting from the corner of Leon Kilat Street, along the bus terminal, the south expressway in Cebu City in what is commonly known as the Cebu South Expressway and/or the Cebu South Road in Cebu City and Province of Cebu up to the Municipality of Santander, Cebu, shall be known as the Natalio B. Bacalso South National Highway.

## Section 2

Any law, ordinance, administrative order or designation or identification shall be considered repealed or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
