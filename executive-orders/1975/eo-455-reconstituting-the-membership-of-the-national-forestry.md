# Executive Order No. 455

**Reconstituting the Membership of the National Forestry Extension Advisory Board Created Under Executive Order No. 321 Dated May 31, 1971**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 455 September 25, 1975**

RECONSTITUTING THE MEMBERSHIP OF THE NATIONAL FORESTRY EXTENSION ADVISORY BOARD CREATED UNDER EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 321 DATED MAY 31, 1971

WHEREAS, there is a need to expand the membership of the National Forestry Extension Advisory Board created under Executive Order No. 321 dated May 31, 1971 in view of the participation of more agencies in the Integrated Forest Conservation Information Program;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the law, do hereby reconstitute the membership in the National Forestry Extension Advisory Board as follows:

| The Dean, UPLB College of Forestry Ex-Officio | Chairman | |---|---| | The Director, Bureau of Forest Development | Member | | The Commissioner, Forest Products Research & Industries Development Commission | Member | | The Director, Forest Research Institute | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Lands | Member | | The Director, National Food and Agriculture Council | Member | | The General Manager, Central Luzon-Cagayan Valley Development Authority | Member | | The Chief, Office for Civil Relations | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Soils | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Animal Industry | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Plant Industry | Member | | The Commissioner, National Air and Water Pollution Control Commission | Member | | The Director, National Media Production Center | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Broadcasts | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Elementary Education | Member | | The General Manager, National Power Corporation | Member | | The Representative, Secretary of the Department of Public Highways | Member | | The Representative, Secretary of the Department of Agriculture | Member | | The Representative, Secretary of the Department of National Defense | Member | | The Representative, Secretary of the Department of Local Government and Community Development | Member | | The Director, Bureau of Posts | Member | | The Director, Philippine Sugar Institute | Member | | The President, Rural Broadcasters' Council | Member | | The President, Rural Information Council | Member | | The Executive Secretary, Boy Scouts of the Philippines | Member | | The Representative, Secretary of the Department of Natural Resources | Member |

This Order shall take effect immediately.

Done in the City of Manila, this 25th day of September, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-five.
