# Batas Pambansa Blg. 714

**An Act Changing the Name of the Talibon Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Congressman Maximino Garcia Memorial Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 714**

An Act Changing the Name of the Talibon Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Congressman Maximino Garcia Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Talibon Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, is changed to Congressman Maximino Garcia Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
