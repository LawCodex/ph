# Republic Act No. 2175

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Limbujan in the Municipality of General Macarthur, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2175**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Limbujan in the Municipality of General Macarthur, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Limbujan in the Municipality of General MacArthur, Province of Samar, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Limbujan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 26, 1959.
