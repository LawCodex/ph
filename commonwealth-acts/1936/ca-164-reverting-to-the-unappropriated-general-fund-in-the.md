# Commonwealth Act No. 164

**An Act Reverting to the Unappropriated General Fund in the Philippine Treasury All Amounts Collected By the Bohol and Cagayan Filipino Colonies Established Under Act Numbered Twenty-Two Hundred and Fifty-Four.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 164, November 09, 1936 ]

AN ACT REVERTING TO THE UNAPPROPRIATED GENERAL FUND IN THE PHILIPPINE TREASURY ALL AMOUNTS COLLECTED BY THE BOHOL AND CAGAYAN FILIPINO COLONIES ESTABLISHED UNDER ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-TWO HUNDRED AND FIFTY-FOUR.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

Any provisions of existing law to the contrary notwithstanding, all amounts collected from time to time by the Bohol and Cagayan Filipino Colonies established under Act Numbered Twenty-two hundred and fifty-four shall revert to the unappropriated general fund in the Philippine Treasury, and shall not be made available for expenditure except by subsequent legislative enactment.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 9, 1936.
