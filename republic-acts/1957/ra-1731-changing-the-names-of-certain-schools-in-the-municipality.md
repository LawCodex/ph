# Republic Act No. 1731

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Padada, Province of Davao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1731**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Padada, Province of Davao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the following schools in the Municipality of Padada, Province of Davao, are changed as follows:

1\. Padada Elementary School to Northern Padada Elementary School, and

2\. Roosevelt Elementary School to Padada Central Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
