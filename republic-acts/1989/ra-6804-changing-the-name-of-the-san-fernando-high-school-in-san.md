# Republic Act No. 6804

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Fernando High School in San Fernando, Masbate, to Andres Clemente, Jr. High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6804 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN FERNANDO HIGH SCHOOL IN SAN FERNANDO, MASBATE, TO ANDRES CLEMENTE, JR. HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Fernando High School in San Fernando, Masbate, is hereby changed to Andres Clemente, Jr. High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
