# Republic Act No. 2529

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pantal-Samat Primary School in the Barrio of Pantal-Samat, Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, to Magsaysay Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2529**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pantal-Samat Primary School in the Barrio of Pantal-Samat, Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, to Magsaysay Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pantal-Samat Primary School in the barrio of Pantal-Samat, Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Magsaysay Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
