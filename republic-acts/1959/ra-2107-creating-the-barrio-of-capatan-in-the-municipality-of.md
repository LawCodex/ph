# Republic Act No. 2107

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Capatan in the Municipality of Tuguegarao, Province of Cagayan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2107**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Capatan in the Municipality of Tuguegarao, Province of Cagayan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Capatan in the barrio of Libag, Municipality of Tuguegarao, Province of Cagayan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Capatan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 3, 1959.
