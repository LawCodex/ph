# Republic Act No. 3722

**An Act Changing the Name of the Inudaran Elementary School in the Municipal District of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Guro Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3722**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Inudaran Elementary School in the Municipal District of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Guro Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Inudaran Elementary School in the Municipal District of Marantao, Province of Lanao del Sur, is changed to Sultan Guro Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
