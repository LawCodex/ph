# Republic Act No. 1734

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Agtabo in the Barrio of Salngan, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1734**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Agtabo in the Barrio of Salngan, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Agtabo in the barrio of Salngan, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, is converted into a barrio of said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
