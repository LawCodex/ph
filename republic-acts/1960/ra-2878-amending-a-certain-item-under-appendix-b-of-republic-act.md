# Republic Act No. 2878

**An Act Amending a Certain Item Under Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2878**

**An Act Amending a Certain Item Under Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Priority No. 39, page 77-B, under "Pangasinan III," Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"39. Sta. Barbara Sonquil Repair and Improvement ₱2,500.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
