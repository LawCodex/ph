# Republic Act No. 2117

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of San Pablo, Province of Isabela, to the Site in the Old Poblacion Commonly Known as Centro**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2117**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of San Pablo, Province of Isabela, to the Site in the Old Poblacion Commonly Known as Centro**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of San Pablo, Province of Isabela, is transferred to the site in the old poblacion commonly known as Centro in the same municipality.

## Section 2

Republic Act Numbered Eleven hundred seventy-seven is hereby repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
