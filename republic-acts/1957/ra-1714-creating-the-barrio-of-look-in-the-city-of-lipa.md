# Republic Act No. 1714

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Look in the City of Lipa**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1714**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Look in the City of Lipa**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Waniwani, Sayatin and Look, barrio of Balete, City of Lipa, are separated from said barrio and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said city, to be known as the barrio of Look.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
