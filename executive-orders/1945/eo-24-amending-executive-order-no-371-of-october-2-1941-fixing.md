# Executive Order No. 24

**Amending Executive Order No. 371 of October 2, 1941, Fixing the Maximum Selling Prices of Certain Articles of Prime Necessity, and Promulgating Rules and Regulations for the Enforcement Thereof.**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ Executive Order No. 24, March 10, 1945 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 371 OF OCTOBER 2, 1941, FIXING THE MAXIMUM SELLING PRICES OF CERTAIN ARTICLES OF PRIME NECESSITY, AND PROMULGATING RULES AND REGULATIONS FOR THE ENFORCEMENT THEREOF.

WHEREAS, in view of the shortage of food, other articles of prime necessity, and services caused by the war, it is essential to the welfare of the people that the fixing of the maximum selling prices of such articles as promulgated on October 2, 1941, be continued with such modifications as circumstances may require.

**NOW, THEREFORE, I, SERGIO OSMEÑA,** President of the Philippines, by virtue of the authority vested in me by the Constitution and the laws of the Philippines, do hereby prescribe and promulgate the following schedule of maximum prices in the province of Leyte.
