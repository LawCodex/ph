# Republic Act No. 1654

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Catanduanes, to Payo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1654**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Catanduanes, to Payo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Jose Panganiban, Province of Catanduanes, is changed to Payo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
