# Republic Act No. 3744

**An Act to Declare Barrio Cabaroan of the Municipal District of San Emilio, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Seat of the Municipal District Government of the Said Municipal District**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3744**

**An Act to Declare Barrio Cabaroan of the Municipal District of San Emilio, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Seat of the Municipal District Government of the Said Municipal District**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Cabaroan of the Municipal District of San Emilio, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby declared as the seat of the municipal district government of the said municipal district.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
