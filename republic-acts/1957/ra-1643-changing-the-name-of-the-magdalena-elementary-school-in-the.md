# Republic Act No. 1643

**An Act Changing the Name of the Magdalena Elementary School in the Municipality of Magdalena, Province of Laguna, to Ananias Laico Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1643**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Magdalena Elementary School in the Municipality of Magdalena, Province of Laguna, to Ananias Laico Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Magdalena Elementary School in the Municipality of Magdalena, Province of Laguna, is hereby changed to Ananias Laico Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
