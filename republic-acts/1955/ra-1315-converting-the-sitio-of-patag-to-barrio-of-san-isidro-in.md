# Republic Act No. 1315

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Patag to Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1315**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Patag to Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Patag in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte, is converted into a barrio to be called San Isidro.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
