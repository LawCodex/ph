# Republic Act No. 1459

**An Act Amending Item Three and Repealing Item Four, Group III, Subsection Aa for the Province of Leyte, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1459**

**An Act Amending Item Three and Repealing Item Four, Group III, Subsection Aa for the Province of Leyte, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item three, Group III, subsection aa for the Province of Leyte, section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is amended to read as follows:

| "3. Pintuyan Old School Building—Reconstruction | ₱15,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

Item four, Group III, subsection aa for the Province of Leyte, section three of the same Act, is repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
