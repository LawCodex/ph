# Republic Act No. 2550

**An Act Dividing the Barrio of Manaring in the Municipality of Ilagan, Province of Isabela, into Two Barrios to be Known as Barrio Manaring Primero and Barrio Manaring Segundo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2550**

**An Act Dividing the Barrio of Manaring in the Municipality of Ilagan, Province of Isabela, into Two Barrios to be Known as Barrio Manaring Primero and Barrio Manaring Segundo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Manaring in the Municipality of Ilagan, Province of Isabela, is hereby divided into two barrios to be known as Barrio Manaring Primero and Barrio Manaring Segundo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
