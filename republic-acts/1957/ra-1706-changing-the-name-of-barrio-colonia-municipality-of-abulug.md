# Republic Act No. 1706

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Colonia, Municipality of Abulug, Province of Cagayan, to Libertad**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1706**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Colonia, Municipality of Abulug, Province of Cagayan, to Libertad**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Colonia, in the Municipality of Abulug, Province of Cagayan, is changed to Libertad.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
