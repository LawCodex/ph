# Republic Act No. 2163

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, to the Site of the Barrio of Pawa**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2163**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, to the Site of the Barrio of Pawa**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Hagonoy, Province of Davao, is hereby transferred to the site of the barrio of Pawa.1aшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
