# Republic Act No. 9811

**An Act Separating the Mount Data National High School - Tipunan Annex in Sitio Tipunan, Barangay Monamon Norte, Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province from the Mount Data National High School, Converting it into an Independent National High School to Be Known as Tipunan National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9811 November 26, 2009**

AN ACT SEPARATING THE MOUNT DATA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL - TIPUNAN ANNEX IN SITIO TIPUNAN, BARANGAY MONAMON NORTE, MUNICIPALITY OF BAUKO, MOUNTAIN PROVINCE FROM THE MOUNT DATA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS TIPUNAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Separation and Conversion into a National High School._ \- The Mount Data National High School - Tipunan Annex in Sitio Tipunan, Barangay Monamon Norte, Municipality of Bauko, Mountain Province is hereby separated from the Mount Data National High School and converted into an independent national high school to be known as Tipunan National High School.

## Section 2

_Transfer of Assets and Liabilities._ \- All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Mount Data National High School - Tipunan Annex are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Tipunan National High School.

## Section 3

_Appropriations._ The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the Tipunan National High School, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year's appropriations of the Mount Data National High School - Tipunan Annex. Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation of the school shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 4

_Implementation._ \- The Secretary of Education shall issue rules and regulations that may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.lawphi1

## Section 5

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._
