# Republic Act No. 6222

**An Act Creating Barrio Naparasan in the Municipality of Gamay, Province of Northern Samar.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6222, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO NAPARASAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GAMAY, PROVINCE OF NORTHERN SAMAR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Naparasan, Cadahonan, Capatagan, Guinawitan, Maticawol, Cabogawan, and Lonoy in the Municipality of Gamay, Province of Northern Samar, are hereby constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Naparasan.
