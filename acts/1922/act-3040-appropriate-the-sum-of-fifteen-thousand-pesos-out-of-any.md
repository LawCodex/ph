# Act No. 3040

**An Act to Appropriate the Sum of Fifteen thousand Pesos, Out of Any Funds in the Insular Treasury Not Otherwise Appropriated, for the Purchase of a Parcel of Land on the Island of Limasawa, Province of Leyte, and the Construction on the Said Land of a Public School to Be Denominated "Magellan Public School,'' and for Other Purposes**

[ Act No. 3040, March 10, 1922 ]

AN ACT TO APPROPRIATE THE SUM OF FIFTEEN THOUSAND PESOS, OUT OF ANY FUNDS IN THE INSULAR TREASURY NOT OTHERWISE APPROPRIATED, FOR THE PURCHASE OF A PARCEL OF LAND ON THE ISLAND OF LIMASAWA, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, AND THE CONSTRUCTION ON THE SAID LAND OF A PUBLIC SCHOOL TO BE DENOMINATED "MAGELLAN PUBLIC SCHOOL,'' AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of fifteen thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purchase of a parcel of land on the Island of Limasawa, Province of Leyte, and the erection thereon of a public school to be denominated "Magellan Public School," as a memorial to that great navigator who brought to these Islands the germ of the Western civilization, now enjoyed by their inhabitants.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 10, 1922.
