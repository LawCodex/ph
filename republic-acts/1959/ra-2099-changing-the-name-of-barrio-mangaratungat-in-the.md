# Republic Act No. 2099

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Mangaratungat in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Isabela, to San Vicente**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2099**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Mangaratungat in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Isabela, to San Vicente**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Mangaratungat in the Municipality of Jones, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to San Vicente.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 28, 1959.
