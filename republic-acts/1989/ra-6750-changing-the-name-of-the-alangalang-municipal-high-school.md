# Republic Act No. 6750

**An Act Changing the Name of the Alangalang Municipal High School in Alangalang, Leyte, to Alangalang National High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6750, August 11, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ALANGALANG MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN ALANGALANG, LEYTE, TO ALANGALANG NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Alangalang Municipal High School in Alangalang, Leyte, is hereby changed to Alangalang National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
