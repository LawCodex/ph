# Executive Order No. 239

**Creating An Executive Committee to Act as Advisory Council to the Fao Seminar on Measures to Accelerate Benefits From Water Use At the Farm Level to Be Held in Quezon City in October 1970**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 239, June 22, 1970 ]

**CREATING AN EXECUTIVE COMMITTEE TO ACT AS ADVISORY COUNCIL TO THE FAO SEMINAR ON MEASURES TO ACCELERATE BENEFITS FROM WATER USE AT THE FARM LEVEL TO BE HELD IN QUEZON CITY IN OCTOBER 1970**

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, **I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS** , President of the Philippines, do hereby create an Executive Committee to act as Advisory Council to the FAO Seminar on Measures to Accelerate Benefits from Water Use at the Farm Level to be held in Quezon City in October 1970 composed of the following:

| The Administration, National Irrigation Administration | Chairman |
|---|---|
| The Executive Director, National food and Agriculture Council | Member |
| The Director,Bureau of Plant Industry | Member |
| The Commisioner,Agricultural Productivity Commission | Member |
| The Director,Bureau of Solis | Member |
| The Administration,Irrigation Service Unit | Member |
| The Head,Water Resources Department, Bureau of Public Works | Member |
| The Dean,U.P. College of Agriculture | Member |
| The President,Central Luzon State University | Member |

The Committee may call upon any department, bureau, office, agency or instrumentality of the government, including government-owned or controlled corporations, for such assistance as it may need and create such sub-committees as may be necessary to ensure the success of the seminar.

Done in the City of Manila, this 22nd day of June, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **PONCIANO G. A. MATHAY**
Acting Executive Secretary
