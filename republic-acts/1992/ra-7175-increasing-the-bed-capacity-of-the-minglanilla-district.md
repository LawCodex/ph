# Republic Act No. 7175

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Minglanilla District Hospital in the Municipality of Minglanilla, Province of Cebu, From Fifty (50) to Seventy-five (75) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7175, January 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE MINGLANILLA DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MINGLANILLA, PROVINCE OF CEBU, FROM FIFTY (50) TO SEVENTY-FIVE (75) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Minglanilla District Hospital in the Municipality of Minglanilla, Province of Cebu, is hereby increased from fifty (50) to seventy-five (75) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.
