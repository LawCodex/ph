# Republic Act No. 7462

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dalakit Elementary School in the Municipality of Catarman, Province of Northern Samar, To Generoso Frigillana Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7462, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE DALAKIT ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CATARMAN, PROVINCE OF NORTHERN SAMAR, TO GENEROSO FRIGILLANA MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Dalakit Elementary School in Barangay Dalakit, Municipality of Catarman, Province of Northern Samar, is hereby changed to Generoso Frigillana Memorial Elementary School.
