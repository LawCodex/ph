# Batas Pambansa Blg. 98

**An Act Changing the Name of Tangub Emergency Hospital in the City of Tangub to Doña Maria D. Tan Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 98**

An Act Changing the Name of Tangub Emergency Hospital in the City of Tangub to Doña Maria D. Tan Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Tangub Emergency Hospital in the City of Tangub is hereby changed to Doña Maria D. Tan Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 27, 1980
