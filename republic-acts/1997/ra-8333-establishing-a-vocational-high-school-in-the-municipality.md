# Republic Act No. 8333

**An Act Establishing A Vocational High School in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Isabela, To Be Known As the Alicia Vocational School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8333, July 05, 1997 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ALICIA, PROVINCE OF ISABELA, TO BE KNOWN AS THE ALICIA VOCATIONAL SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, a vocational high school in the Municipality of Alicia, Province of Isabela, to be known as the Alicia Vocational School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue rules and regulations for the effective implementation of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, Lapsed into law on July 5, 1997, without the signature of the President, in accordance with Article VI, Section 27 (1) of the Constitution.
