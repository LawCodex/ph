# Republic Act No. 6665

**An Act Changing the Name of the Western Cagayan Vocational School in the Municipality of Lasam, Province of Cagayan, to Lasam National Agricultural School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6665, June 22, 1988 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE WESTERN CAGAYAN, TO LASAM, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, TO LASAM NATIONAL AGRICULTURAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Western Cagayan Vocational School the Municipality of Lasam, Province of Cagayan, is hereby renamed to Lasam National Agricultural School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
