# Republic Act No. 7727

**An Act Converting the Tunga Barangay High School Into a National High School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7727, May 19, 1994 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE TUNGA BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Tunga Barangay High School in the Municipality of Tunga, Province of Leyte, is hereby converted into a national high school, to be known as the Tunga National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, May 19, 1994.
