# Republic Act No. 6681

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Wright, Province of Samar, to Municipality of Paranas**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6681 November 4, 1988**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF WRIGHT, PROVINCE OF SAMAR, TO MUNICIPALITY OF PARANAS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Wright, Province of Samar, is hereby changed to Municipality of Paranas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **November 4, 1988.**
