# Batas Pambansa Blg. 745

**An Act Changing the Name of Jose Abad Santos Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Felix G. Almano Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 745**

An Act Changing the Name of Jose Abad Santos Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Felix G. Almano Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Jose Abad Santos Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, shall be changed to Felix G. Almano Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
