# Republic Act No. 4891

**An Act Changing the Name of Ebus Elementary School in Barrio San Vicente, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to San Vicente Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4891**

An Act Changing the Name of Ebus Elementary School in Barrio San Vicente, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, to San Vicente Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Ebus Elementary School in Barrio San Vicente, Municipality of Guagua, Province of Pampanga, is hereby changed to and shall hereafter be known as San Vicente Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
