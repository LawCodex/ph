# Republic Act No. 4019

**An Act Changing the Name of Tagbak Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Mammah Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4019**

**An Act Changing the Name of Tagbak Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Mammah Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Tagbak Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, is changed to Panglima Mammah Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2
