# Republic Act No. 3709

**An Act Changing the Name of Indanan Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Indanan Central Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3709**

**An Act Changing the Name of Indanan Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, to Panglima Indanan Central Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Indanan Elementary School in the Municipality of Indanan, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Panglima Indanan Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
