# Republic Act No. 8662

**An Act Establishing the Las Salinas National High School in Barangay Las Salinas, in the Municipality of Loay, Province of Bohol and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8662, June 25, 1998 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE LAS SALINAS NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY LAS SALINAS, IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LOAY, PROVINCE OF BOHOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a high school in Barangay Las Salinas, Municipality of Loay, Province of Bohol, to be known as Las Salinas High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
