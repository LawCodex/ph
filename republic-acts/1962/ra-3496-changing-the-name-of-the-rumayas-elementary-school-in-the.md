# Republic Act No. 3496

**An Act Changing the Name of the Rumayas Elementary School in the Municipality of Lumba-Bayabao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Dagalangit Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3496**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Rumayas Elementary School in the Municipality of Lumba-Bayabao, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Dagalangit Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Rumayas Elementary School in the Municipality of Lumba-Bayabao, Province of Lanao del Sur, is changed to Dagalangit Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
