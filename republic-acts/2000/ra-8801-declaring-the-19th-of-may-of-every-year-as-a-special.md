# Republic Act No. 8801

**An Act Declaring the 19th of May of Every Year as a Special Working Public Holiday in the City of Butuan**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8801, July 19, 2000 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE 19TH OF MAY OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF BUTUAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

May 19 of every year is hereby declared as a special working holiday in the City of Butuan, in recognition and commemoration of the annual fiesta of the City of Butuan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.ℒαwρhi৷
