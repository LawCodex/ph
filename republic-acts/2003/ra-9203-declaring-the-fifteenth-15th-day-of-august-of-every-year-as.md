# Republic Act No. 9203

**An Act Declaring the Fifteenth (15th) Day of August of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in Municipality of Camiguin, to Be Known as "Catarman Day"**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9203 April 20, 2003**

AN ACT DECLARING THE FIFTEENTH (15TH) DAY OF AUGUST OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN MUNICIPALITY OF CAMIGUIN, TO BE KNOWN AS "CATARMAN DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

August Fifteenth (15th) every year is hereby declared as special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Catarman, Province of Camiguin, to commemorate its foundation day anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
