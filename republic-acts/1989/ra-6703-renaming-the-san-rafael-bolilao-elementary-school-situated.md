# Republic Act No. 6703

**An Act Renaming the San Rafael-Bolilao Elementary School Situated in Barangay San Rafael, Municipality of Mandurriao, City of Iloilo, to Donato Pison Memorial School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6703, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE SAN RAFAEL-BOLILAO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL SITUATED IN BARANGAY SAN RAFAEL, MUNICIPALITY OF MANDURRIAO, CITY OF ILOILO, TO DONATO PISON MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The San Rafael-Bolilao Elementary School in Barangay san Rafael, Mandurriao, Iloilo City, shall hereafter be known as the Donato Pison Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
