# Republic Act No. 3556

**An Act to Increase the Bed Capacity of the Mindoro Oriental Provincial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3556**

**An Act to Increase the Bed Capacity of the Mindoro Oriental Provincial Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Mindoro Oriental Provincial Hospital is hereby increased from seventy-five beds to one hundred beds.

## Section 2

The financing of this hospital shall be in accordance with the Hospital Financing Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
