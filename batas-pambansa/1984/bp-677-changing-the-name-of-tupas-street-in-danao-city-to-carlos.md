# Batas Pambansa Blg. 677

**An Act Changing the Name of Tupas street in Danao City to Carlos Enriquez street**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 677**

An Act Changing the Name of Tupas Street in Davao City to Carlos Enriquez Street

## Section 1

The street in Danao City, in the island of Cebu, Philippines, now known as Tupas Street, is hereby renamed, and shall be known as Carlos Enriquez Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
