# Act No. 2201

**An Act Appropriating Seven thousand and Ninety-Two Pesos for the Improvement of Buildings and Grounds At Baguio, Benguet.**

[ Act No. 2201, December 21, 1912 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING SEVEN THOUSAND AND NINETY-TWO PESOS FOR THE IMPROVEMENT OF BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS AT BAGUIO, BENGUET.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated, out of any Appropriation, funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for improving the sanitary condition of small cottages inhabited by Filipino employees at Baguio; for installing open drains in connection therewith; for providing baths and sinks in such cottages; and for the construction of gravel walks and the provision of adequate lighting at night, seven thousand and ninety-two pesos, which sum shall be expended by the Bureau of Public Works.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, December 21, 1912.
