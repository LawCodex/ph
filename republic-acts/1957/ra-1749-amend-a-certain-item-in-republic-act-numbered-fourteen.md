# Republic Act No. 1749

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1749**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item J-045.4 under Romblon, paragraph d, title J, section five, Appendix A of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is amended to read as follows:

"J-045.3 Ambulong (Sibuyan Island) -Construction of causeway and landing: 130: 50: 50: 50 . . . 1 . . ."

## Section 2

This act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
