# Republic Act No. 6701

**An Act Declaring September One of Every Year, the Death Anniversary of Gregorio Aglipay Y Labayan, as Gregorio L. Aglipay Day and a Special Non-Working Holiday in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6701, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER ONE OF EVERY YEAR, THE DEATH ANNIVERSARY OF GREGORIO AGLIPAY Y LABAYAN, AS GREGORIO L. AGLIPAY DAY AND A SPECIAL NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BATAC, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The first day of the month of September of every year, being the death anniversary of Gregorio L. Aglipay y Labayan, is hereby declared as Gregorio L. Aglipay Day and a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte.

## Section 2

Any provision of law, decree, order, rules or regulations inconsistent with this Act is hereby repealed and/or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
