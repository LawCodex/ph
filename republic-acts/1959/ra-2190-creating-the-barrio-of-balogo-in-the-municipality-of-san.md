# Republic Act No. 2190

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Balogo in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Romblon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2190**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Balogo in the Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Romblon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Balogo in the barrio of Calatrava, Municipality of San Agustin, Province of Romblon, is separated from said barrio and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Balogo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
