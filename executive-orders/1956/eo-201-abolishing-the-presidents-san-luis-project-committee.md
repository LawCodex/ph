# Executive Order No. 201

**Abolishing the President’S San Luis Project Committee**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 201, September 03, 1956 ]

ABOLISHING THE PRESIDENT’S SAN LUIS PROJECT COMMITTEE.

In view of the successful completion of the rural reconstruction project in the municipality of San Luis, province of Pampanga, **I, RAMON MAGSAYSAY** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by law, do hereby abolish the President’s San Luis Project Committee created in Executive Order No. 36 dated May 22, 1954, as amended by Executive Order No. 143 dated December 19, 1955.

Executive Order No. 36 dated May 22, 1954, as amended, is hereby revoked.

Done in the City of Manila, this 3rd day of Sept., in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-six, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the eleventh.

(Sgd.) **RAMON MAGSAYSAY**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(Sgd.) **FORTUNATO DE LEON**
Executive Secretary
