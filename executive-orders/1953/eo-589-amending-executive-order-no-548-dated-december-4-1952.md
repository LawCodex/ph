# Executive Order No. 589

**Amending Executive Order No. 548 Dated December 4, 1952, Reducing the 1952 Taxes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 589, May 05, 1953 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 548 DATED DECEMBER 4, 1952, REDUCING THE 1952 TAXES ON ALL REAL PROPERTIES LOCATED IN THE PROVINCE OF ALBAY.

The public interest so requiring, I, **ELPIDIO QUIRINO** , President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by section 53 of Commonwealth Act No. 470, as amended by Commonwealth Act No. 534, and upon the recommendation of the Secretary of Finance, do hereby remit the 50 per cent of the 1952 taxes on all real properties located in the Province of Albay on condition that all the unpaid taxes and penalties corresponding to 1951 and previous years and the remaining 50 per cent of the 1952 taxes shall be paid on or before June 30, 1953.

Executive Order No. 548, dated December 4, 1952, is hereby amended accordingly.

Done in the City of Manila, this 5th day of May, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-three, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the seventh.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **MARCIANO ROQUE**
Acting Executive Secretary
