# Act No. 3979

**An Act to Amend Act Numbered Twenty-Nine Hundred and thirty-Eight, as Amended By Act Numbered thirty-Six Hundred and Ninety-Five.**

[ Act No. 3979, December 03, 1932 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-NINE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-EIGHT, AS AMENDED BY ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-SIX HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Act Numbered Twenty-nine hundred and thirty-eight, as amended, is hereby further amended by making the words "general manager" and "assistant general manager", wherever they appear in said Act and its amendments, read "president" and "vice-president", respectively, and wherever the words "president" and "vice-president" appear, they shall be read "chairman" and "vice-chairman", except in section eleven of said Act Numbered Twenty-nine hundred and thirty-eight, where the word president shall be retained in the sense in which said word is used in the present Act, for the purpose of signing the bonds.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and thirty-three.

Approved, December 3, 1932.
