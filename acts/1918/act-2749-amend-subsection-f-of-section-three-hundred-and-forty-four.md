# Act No. 2749

**An Act to Amend Subsection (F) of Section three Hundred and Forty-Four of the Administrative Code.**

[ Act No. 2749, February 21, 1918 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SUBSECTION (F) OF SECTION THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY-FOUR OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Subsection (f) of section three hundred and forty-four of the Administrative Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(f) Machinery, which term shall embrace machines, mechanical contrivances, instruments, tools, implements, appliances, and apparatus used for industrial, agricultural, or manufacturing purposes: _Provided,_ That until December thirty-one, nineteen hundred and twenty-two, sheds built in accordance with law and used exclusively for the curing of tobacco shall also be included as property exempt from tax under this subsection."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, February 21, 1918.
