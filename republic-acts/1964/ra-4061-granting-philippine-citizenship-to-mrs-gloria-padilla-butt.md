# Republic Act No. 4061

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Mrs. Gloria Padilla-Butt**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4061**

**An Act Granting Philippine Citizenship to Mrs. Gloria Padilla-Butt**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Mrs. Gloria Padilla-Butt is hereby granted Philippine citizenship with all the rights, privileges and prerogatives appurtenant thereto.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
