# Republic Act No. 6677

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Lower Q-M in Baguio City to Barangay General Emilio F. Aguinaldo**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6677 October 12, 1988**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARANGAY LOWER Q-M IN BAGUIO CITY TO BARANGAY GENERAL EMILIO F. AGUINALDO

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Lower Q-M in Baguio City is hereby changed to Barangay General Emilio F. Aguinaldo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **October 12, 1988.**
