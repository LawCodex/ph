# Batas Pambansa Blg. 851

**An Act Naming the National Road starting From the Boundary of Arayat, Pampanga and Ending At the Boundary of San Isidro, Nueva Ecija, the General Ismael D. Lapus National Highway**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 851**

An Act Naming the National Road Starting from the Boundary of Arayat, Pampanga and Ending at the Boundary of San Isidro, Nueva Ecija, the General Ismael D. Lapus National Highway

## Section 1

The national road from Barangay San Vicente, Municipality of Cabiao up to the boundary of San Isidro, Nueva Ecija, traversing the jurisdiction of the Municipality of Cabiao in the same province, is hereby named the General Ismael D. Lapus National Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
