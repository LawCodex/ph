# Republic Act No. 2768

**An Act to Amend Item (ai), Group I, Subparagraph 43, for the Province of Pangasinan, Page 371, Paragraph (f), Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2768**

**An Act to Amend Item (ai), Group I, Subparagraph 43, for the Province of Pangasinan, Page 371, Paragraph (f), Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (ai), Group I, subparagraph 43, for the Province of Pangasinan, page 371, paragraph (f), Section two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(ai) Quinaoayanan-Tipor barrio road, Bani Construction ₱10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
