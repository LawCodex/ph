# Republic Act No. 6654

**An Act Repealing Section Forty of Presidential Decree Numbered Eight Hundred Seven, otherwise known as the Civil Service Decree**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6654 May 20, 1988**

AN ACT REPEALING SECTION FORTY OF PRESIDENTIAL DECREE NUMBERED EIGHT HUNDRED SEVEN, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE CIVIL SERVICE DECREE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Section Forty of Presidential Decree Numbered Eight Hundred Seven, otherwise known as the Civil Service Decree, is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **May 20, 1988.**
