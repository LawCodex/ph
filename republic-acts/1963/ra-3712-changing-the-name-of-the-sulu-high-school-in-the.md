# Republic Act No. 3712

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu High School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Dayang Hadji Piandao Memorial High School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3712**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sulu High School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, to Dayang Hadji Piandao Memorial High School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sulu High School in the Municipality of Jolo, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Dayang Hadji Piandao Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
