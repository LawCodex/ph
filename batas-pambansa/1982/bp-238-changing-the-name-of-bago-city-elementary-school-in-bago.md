# Batas Pambansa Blg. 238

**An Act Changing the Name of Bago City Elementary School in Bago City, Province of Negros Occidental, to Pang-Alaalang Paaralang Heneral Juan A. Araneta.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 238**

An Act Changing the Name of Bago City Elementary School in Bago City, Province of Negros Occidental, to Pang-Ala-Alang Paaralang Heneral Juan A. Araneta

## Section 1

The name of Bago City Elementary School in Bago City, Province of Negros Occidental, is hereby changed to Pang-alaalang Paaralang Heneral Juan A. Araneta.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: September 12, 1982
