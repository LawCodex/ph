# Republic Act No. 1728

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Bual in the Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon, to Del Rosario**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1728**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Bual in the Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon, to Del Rosario**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Bual in the Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon, is hereby changed to Del Rosario.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
