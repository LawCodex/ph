# Republic Act No. 4628

**An Act Increasing the Salaries of the Judges of the City Court of Manila**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4628**

An Act Increasing the Salaries of the Judges of the City Court of Manila

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Subparagraph (a) of Section one of Republic Act Numbered Nine hundred twenty-four, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:

"(a) Of the City of Manila, seventeen thousand pesos;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
