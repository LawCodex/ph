# Republic Act No. 2547

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ibabang Banot, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, to Gitnang Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2547**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ibabang Banot, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, to Gitnang Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Ibabang Banot, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Gitnang Barrio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
