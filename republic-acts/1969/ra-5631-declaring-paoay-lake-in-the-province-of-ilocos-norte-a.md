# Republic Act No. 5631

**An Act Declaring Paoay Lake in the Province of Ilocos Norte a National Park**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5631, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT DECLARING PAOAY LAKE IN THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE A NATIONAL PARK

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Paoay Lake in the Province of IIocos Norte and its extremities within one kilometer from said lake is hereby declared a. national park.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
