# Republic Act No. 4903

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Otieza, Province of Surigao del Sur, to San Agustin**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4903**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Otieza, Province of Surigao del Sur, to San Agustin

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Otieza, Province of Surigao del Sur is hereby changed to San Agustin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
