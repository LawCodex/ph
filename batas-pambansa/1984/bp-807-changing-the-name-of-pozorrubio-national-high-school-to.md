# Batas Pambansa Blg. 807

**An Act Changing the Name of Pozorrubio National High School to Benigno V. Aldana National Memorial High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 807**

An Act Changing the Name of Pozorrubio National High School to Benigno V. Aldana National Memorial High School

## Section 1

The name of Pozorrubio National High School in the Municipality of Pozorrubio, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Benigno V. Aldana National Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
