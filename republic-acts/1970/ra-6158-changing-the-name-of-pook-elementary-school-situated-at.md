# Republic Act No. 6158

**An Act Changing the Name of Pook Elementary School Situated at Barrio Pook, Municipality of Sta. Rosa, Province of Laguna, to Jose Zavalla Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6158**

**An Act Changing the Name of Pook Elementary School Situated at Barrio Pook, Municipality of Sta. Rosa, Province of Laguna, to Jose Zavalla Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Pook Elementary School situated at Barrio Pook, Municipality of Sta. Rosa, Province of Laguna, is hereby named and is to be known henceforth as Jose Zavalla Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 9, 1970.
