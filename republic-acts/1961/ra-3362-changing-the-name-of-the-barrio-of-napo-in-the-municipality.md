# Republic Act No. 3362

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Napo in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Bonifacio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3362**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Napo in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Bonifacio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Napo in the Municipality of Dapitan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is changed to Bonifacio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
