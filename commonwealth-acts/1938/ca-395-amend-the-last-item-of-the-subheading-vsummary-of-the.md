# Commonwealth Act No. 395

**An Act to Amend the Last Item of the Subheading "V—Summary" of the Appropriation for the National Library Made in Commonwealth Act Numbered Two Hundred and Forty-Five So as to Make the Phrase "Less—Force Savings, From Salaries and Wages" to Read "Less—Forced Savings."**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 395, September 05, 1938 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND THE LAST ITEM OF THE SUBHEADING "V—SUMMARY" OF THE APPROPRIATION FOR THE NATIONAL LIBRARY MADE IN COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED TWO HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIVE SO AS TO MAKE THE PHRASE "LESS—FORCE SAVINGS, FROM SALARIES AND WAGES" TO READ "LESS—FORCED SAVINGS."

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The phrase "Less-forced savings from salaries and wages" appearing under subheading "V-Summary" of the appropriation for the National Library as provided in Commonwealth Act Numbered Two hundred and forty-five is hereby amended to read "Less-forced savings."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect as of January first, nine-teen hundred and thirty-eight.

Approved, September 5, 1938.
