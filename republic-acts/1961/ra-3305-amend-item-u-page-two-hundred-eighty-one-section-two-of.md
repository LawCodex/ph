# Republic Act No. 3305

**An Act to Amend Item (u), Page Two Hundred Eighty-One, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3305**

**An Act to Amend Item (u), Page Two Hundred Eighty-One, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (u), page two hundred eighty-one, paragraph f, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(u) Lambunao Municipal building, construction, improvement and/or repair ₱25,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
