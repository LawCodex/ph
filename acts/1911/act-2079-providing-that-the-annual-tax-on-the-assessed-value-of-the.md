# Act No. 2079

**An Act Providing that the Annual Tax on the Assessed Value of the Real Estate of the City of Manila for the Year Nineteen Hundred and Twelve Shall He One and One-Half Per Centum thereof.**

[ Act No. 2079, November 17, 1911 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING THAT THE ANNUAL TAX ON THE ASSESSED VALUE OF THE REAL ESTATE OF THE CITY OF MANILA FOR THE YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND TWELVE SHALL HE ONE AND ONE-HALF PER CENTUM THEREOF.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The annual tax rate on the assessed value of all the real estate of the city of Manila subject to taxation shall be, for the year nineteen hundred and twelve, one and one-half per centum thereof instead of two per centum thereof, anything contained in section forty-seven of Act Numbered One hundred and eighty-three, as amended, to the contrary notwithstanding: _Provided, however,_ That one per centum of the assessed value of all the said real estate shall be payable on or before the first day of July, nineteen hundred and twelve, and the remaining one-half per centum shall be payable on or before the thirty-first day of December, nineteen hundred and twelve.

Enacted, November 17, 1911.
