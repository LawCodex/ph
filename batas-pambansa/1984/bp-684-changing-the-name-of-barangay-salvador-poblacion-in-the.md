# Batas Pambansa Blg. 684

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Salvador Poblacion in the Municipality of Salvador, Province of Lanao Del Norte, to Barangay Balimbing**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 684**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Salvador Poblacion in the Municipality of Salvador, Province of Lanao Del Norte, to Barangay Balimbing

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Salvador Poblacion in the Municipality of Salvador, Province of Lanao del Norte, is changed to Barangay Balimbing.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
