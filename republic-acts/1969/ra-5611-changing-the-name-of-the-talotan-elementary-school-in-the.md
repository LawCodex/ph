# Republic Act No. 5611

**An Act Changing the Name, of the Talotan Elementary School in the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, to Aloncia Asin Memorial Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5611, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME, OF THE TALOTAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CONCEPCION, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO ALONCIA ASIN MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Talotan Elementary School in the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Aloncia Asin Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
