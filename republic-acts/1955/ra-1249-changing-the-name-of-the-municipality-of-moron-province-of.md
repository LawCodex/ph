# Republic Act No. 1249

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Moron, Province of Bataan, to Morong**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1249**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Moron, Province of Bataan, to Morong**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Moron, Province of Bataan, is hereby changed to Morong.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
