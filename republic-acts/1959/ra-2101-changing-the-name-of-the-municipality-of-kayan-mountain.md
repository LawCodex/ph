# Republic Act No. 2101

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Kayan, Mountain Province, to Tadian Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2101**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Kayan, Mountain Province, to Tadian Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Kayan in the Mountain Province is changed to Municipality of Tadian.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 28, 1959.
