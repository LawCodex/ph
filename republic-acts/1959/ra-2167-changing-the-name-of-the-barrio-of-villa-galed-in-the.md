# Republic Act No. 2167

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Villa Galed in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, to Villa Crespo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2167**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Villa Galed in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, to Villa Crespo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Villa Galed in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Villa Crespo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
