# Republic Act No. 1047

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tarug Barrio School in the Barrio of Tarug, Municipality of Alimodian, Province of Iloilo, to Amaquin Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1047**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tarug Barrio School in the Barrio of Tarug, Municipality of Alimodian, Province of Iloilo, to Amaquin Memorial School**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of the Tarug Barrio School in the Barrio of Tarug, Municipality of Alimodian, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Amaquin Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
