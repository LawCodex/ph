# Batas Pambansa Blg. 275

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Montalban, Province of Rizal, to the Municipality of Rodriguez.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 275**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Montalban, Province of Rizal, to the Municipality of Rodriguez

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Montalban, Province of Rizal, is changed to the Municipality of Rodriguez.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 13, 1982
