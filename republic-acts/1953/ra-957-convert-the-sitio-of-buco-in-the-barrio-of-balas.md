# Republic Act No. 957

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Buco in the Barrio of Balas, Municipality of Talisay, Province of Batangas, Into a Barrio**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 957, June 20, 1953 ]

AN ACT TO CONVERT THE SITIO OF BUCO IN THE BARRIO OF BALAS, MUNICIPALITY OF TALISAY, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, INTO A BARRIO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Buco in the barrio of Balas, municipality of Talisay, Province of Batangas, is hereby separated from said barrio and converted into a new barrio to be known as the barrio of Buco.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 20, 1953.
