# Act No. 3975

**An Act to Amend Subsection (E) of Section One of Act Numbered thirty-Nine Hundred and Twelve, Entitled "An Act Making Appropriations for Public Works."**

[ Act No. 3975, December 03, 1932 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SUBSECTION (E) OF SECTION ONE OF ACT NUMBERED THIRTY-NINE HUNDRED AND TWELVE, ENTITLED "AN ACT MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR PUBLIC WORKS."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature

**assembled and by the authority of the same:**

## Section 1

Subsection (e) of section one of Act Numbered Thirty-nine hundred and twelve, entitled "An Act making appropriations for public works", is hereby amended by making item nine, on page nineteen, under the heading "Occidental Misamis", read as follows:

"9. For the completion of the school of the barrio of Talayron, Oroquieta ............. P4,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 3, 1932.
