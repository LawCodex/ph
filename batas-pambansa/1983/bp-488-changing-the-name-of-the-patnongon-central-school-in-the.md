# Batas Pambansa Blg. 488

**An Act Changing the Name of the Patnoñgon Central School in the Municipality of Patnoñgon, Province of Antique, to Colonel Ruperto Abellon Memorial School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 488**

An Act Changing the Name of the Patnongon Central School in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, to Colonel Ruperto Abellon Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of Patnongon Central School in the Municipality of Patnongon, Province of Antique, is changed to Colonel Ruperto Abellon Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1983
