# Republic Act No. 9613

**An Act Declaring March 21 of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in Victorias City, Province of Negros Occidental**

Fourteenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eighth day of July, two thousand eight.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9613**

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH 21 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN VICTORIAS CITY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

March 21 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in Victorias City, Province of Negros Occidental, to commemorate the day it was converted into a component city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
