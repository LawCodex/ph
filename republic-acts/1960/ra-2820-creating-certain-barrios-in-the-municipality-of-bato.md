# Republic Act No. 2820

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Bato, Province of Catanduanes**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2820**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Bato, Province of Catanduanes**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of Bato, Province of Catanduanes, are converted into barrios therein, to wit:

1\. Sitio Mintay to Barrio Mintay;

2\. Sitio Carorian to Barrio Carorian; and

3\. Sitio Buenavista to Barrio Buenavista.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
