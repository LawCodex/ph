# Batas Pambansa Blg. 319

**An Act Changing the Name of the Davao Regional Medical and Training Center in the City of Davao to Davao Medical Center.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 319**

An Act Changing the Name of the Davao Regional Medical and Training Center in the City of Davao to Davao Medical Center

## Section 1

The name of the Davao Regional Medical and Training Center in the City of Davao is changed to Davao Medical Center.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
