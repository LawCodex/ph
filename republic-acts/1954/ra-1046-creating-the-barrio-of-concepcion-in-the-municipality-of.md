# Republic Act No. 1046

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Concepcion in the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1046**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Concepcion in the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio of Concepcion in the municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, is hereby constituted into a barrio of the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Concepcion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
