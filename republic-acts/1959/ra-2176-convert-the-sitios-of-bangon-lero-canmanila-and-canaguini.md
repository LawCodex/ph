# Republic Act No. 2176

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Bañgon, Lero, Canmanila and Canaguini-An, All in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Bacubac of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2176**

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Bañgon, Lero, Canmanila and Canaguini-An, All in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Bacubac of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bañgon, Lero, Canmanila and Canaguini-an, all in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, are hereby converted to a barrio to be known as barrio Bacubac of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 26, 1959.
