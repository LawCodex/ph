# Batas Pambansa Blg. 839

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Isidro Community School in Barangay San Isidro, Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Pangasinan, to Bensican-San Isidro Community School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 839**

An Act Changing the Name of the San Isidro Community School in Barangay San Isidro, Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Pangasinan, to Bensican-San Isidro Community School

## Section 1

The name of the San Isidro Community School in Barangay San Isidro, Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Bensican-San Isidro Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
