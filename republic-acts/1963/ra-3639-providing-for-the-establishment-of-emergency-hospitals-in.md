# Republic Act No. 3639

**An Act Providing for the Establishment of Emergency Hospitals in the Municipality of Angeles and the Municipality of Macabebe, Province of Pampanga**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3639**

**An Act Providing for the Establishment of Emergency Hospitals in the Municipality of Angeles and the Municipality of Macabebe, Province of Pampanga**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Department of Health, one emergency hospital in each of the municipalities of Angeles and Macabebe in the Province of Pampanga.

## Section 2

The emergency hospitals established under its Act shall be financed in accordance with the provisions of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and thirty-nine.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
