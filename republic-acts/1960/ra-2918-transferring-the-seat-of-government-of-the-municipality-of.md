# Republic Act No. 2918

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Sur, to the Barrio of Luna**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2918**

**An Act Transferring the Seat of Government of the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Sur, to the Barrio of Luna**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The seat of government of the Municipality of Burgos, Province of Ilocos Sur, is transferred from the present site to the barrio of Luna.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
