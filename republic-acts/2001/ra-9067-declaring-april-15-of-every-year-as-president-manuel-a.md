# Republic Act No. 9067

**An Act Declaring April 15 of Every Year as President Manuel A. Roxas Day Which Shall Be Observed as a Special Working Public Holiday in the Province of Capiz and the City of Roxas**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9067 April 08, 2001**

AN ACT DECLARING APRIL 15 OF EVERY YEAR AS PRESIDENT MANUEL A. ROXAS DAY WHICH SHALL BE OBSERVED AS A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF CAPIZ AND THE CITY OF ROXAS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

April 15 of every year is hereby declared as President Manuel A. Roxas Day which shall be observed as a special working public holiday in the Province of Capiz and the City of Roxas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) AQUILINO Q. PIMENTEL, JR. President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE, JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on February 6, 2001 and May 29, 2000, respectively.
