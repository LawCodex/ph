# Act No. 1887

**An Act to Authorize Frederick Garfield Waite and Others to Maintain and Action At Law Against the Government of the Philippine Islands and Authorizing the Attorney-General of the Philippine Islands to Appear therein.**

[ Act No. 1887, May 08, 1909 ]

AN ACT TO AUTHORIZE FREDERICK GARFIELD WAITE AND OTHERS TO MAINTAIN AND ACTION AT LAW AGAINST THE GOVERNMENT OF THE PHILIPPINE ISLANDS AND AUTHORIZING THE ATTORNEY-GENERAL OF THE PHILIPPINE ISLANDS TO APPEAR THEREIN.

**Whereas** an action is pending in the Court of First Instance of the city of Manila between Frederick Garfield Waite, Luengo Martinez as administrator of the estate of Pedro Gruet and Juana Moreno, Jose Alemany y Gruet, Juan Atayde y Gruet, Julio Atayde y Gruet and Pedro Atayde y Gruet, plaintiffs, and the Insular government of the Philippine Islands, Frank A. Branagan, Treasuere of the Philippines Islands, and the Banco Bspañol-Filipino, defendants, to determine the ownership of certain shares of the capital stock of the Banco Español-Filipino, the certificates of which are in the possession of the Insular Treasurer: and

**Whereas** it is desirable that the Government of the Philippine Islands be a party to said action in order that the same may be decided on its merits: Now, therefore,

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

Authority is hereby granted to Frederick Garfield Waite, Luengo Martinez as administrator of the estate of Pedro Gruet and Juana Moreno, Jose Alemany y Gruet, Juan Atayde y Gruet, Julio Atayde y Gruet and Pedro Atayde y Gruel, to maintain an action in the Court of First Instance of the city of Manila against the Government of the Philippine Islands and the Treasurer of the Philippine Islands to determine the ownership of certain shares of the capital stock of the Banco Español-Filipino the certificates of which are in the possession of the said Treasurer, and the Attorney-General of the Philippine Islands and the Treasurer of the Philippine islands and defend said Government and Treasurer in said action.

## Section 2

this Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, May 8, 1909.
