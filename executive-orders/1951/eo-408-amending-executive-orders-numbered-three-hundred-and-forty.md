# Executive Order No. 408

**Amending Executive Orders Numbered Three Hundred and Forty Three, Three Hundred and Fifty-Three, and for Other Purposes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 408, February 03, 1951 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDERS NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY THREE, THREE HUNDRED AND FIFTY-THREE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

## Section 3

Any commodity not included in any previous Executive Order fixing ceiling prices and issued by authority of Republic Act No. 509, or in this Order, the size and specification of which are the same as those of the foregoing, shall have the same ceiling price.

## Section 4

The ceiling prices fixed in this Order include the 7% Sales and 1% Municipal Tax.

## Section 5

This Order shall take effect five days from the date hereof.

Done in the City of Manila, this 3rd day of February, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-one and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fifth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
