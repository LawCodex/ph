# Republic Act No. 1326

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Taniongbobog, Municipality of Brooke’s Point, Province of Palawan, to Saraza**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1326**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Taniongbobog, Municipality of Brooke’s Point, Province of Palawan, to Saraza**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio of Taniongbobog, Municipality of Brooke’s Point, Province of Palawan, is changed to Saraza.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval

Approved: June 16, 1955.
