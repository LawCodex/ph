# Republic Act No. 5001

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Siko in Lipa City**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5001**

An Act Creating the Barrio of Siko in Lipa City

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Siko in the barrio of Tambo, Lipa City, is hereby separated from said barrio, and converted into a distinct and independent barrio of said city, to be known as the barrio of Siko.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
