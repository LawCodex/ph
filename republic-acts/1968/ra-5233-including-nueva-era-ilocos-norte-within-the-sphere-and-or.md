# Republic Act No. 5233

**An Act Including Nueva Era, Ilocos Norte, Within the Sphere and/or Jurisdiction of the Commission on National Integration**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5233**

An Act Including Nueva Era, Ilocos Norte, Within the Sphere and/or Jurisdiction of the Commission on National Integration

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The town of Nueva Era, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby included within the sphere and/or jurisdiction of the Commission on National Integration.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
