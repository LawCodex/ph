# Batas Pambansa Blg. 124

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cumadcad Emergency Hospital in Barangay Cumadcad, Municipality of Castilla, Province of Sorsogon, to VIcente L. Peralta Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 124**

An Act Changing the Name of the Cumadcad Emergency Hospital in Barangay Cumadcad, Municipality of Castilla, Province of Sorsogon, to Vicente L. Peralta Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Cumadcad Emergency Hospital in Barangay Cumadcad, Municipality of Castilla, Province of Sorsogon, is hereby changed to Vicente L. Peralta Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 10, 1981
