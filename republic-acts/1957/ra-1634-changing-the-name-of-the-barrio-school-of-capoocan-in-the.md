# Republic Act No. 1634

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio School of Capoocan in the City of Calbayog, to “Tiburcio Tancinco Memorial School”**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1634**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio School of Capoocan in the City of Calbayog, to "Tiburcio Tancinco Memorial School"**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio school of Capoocan in the City of Calbayog, shall hereafter be known as "Tiburcio Tancinco Memorial School," in honor of the late Tiburcio Tancinco, who was a Representative from the first district of the Province of Samar and later Judge of the Court of First Instance.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
