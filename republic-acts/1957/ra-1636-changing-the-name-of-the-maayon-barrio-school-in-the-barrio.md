**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1636**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Maayon Barrio School in the Barrio of Maayon, Municipality of Pontevedra, Province of Capiz, to Arturo Cantiller Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Maayon Barrio School in the barrio of Maayon, Municipality of Pontevedra, Province of Capiz, is hereby changed to Arturo Cantiller Memorial School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
