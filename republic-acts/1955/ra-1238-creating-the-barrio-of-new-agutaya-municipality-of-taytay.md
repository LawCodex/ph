# Republic Act No. 1238

**An Act Creating the Barrio of New Agutaya, Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1238**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of New Agutaya, Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Nasalogan in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan, is constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, to be known as the barrio of New Agutaya.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 7, 1955.
