# Republic Act No. 15

**An Act Providing that in Any Criminal Proceeding Before a Justice of the Peace No Cost De Oficio Shall be Taxed Against the Municipality Concerned When the Case is Dismissed or the Defendant Acquitted**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 15**

**An Act Providing that in Any Criminal Proceeding Before a Justice of the Peace No Cost De Oficio Shall be Taxed Against the Municipality Concerned When the Case is Dismissed or the Defendant Acquitted**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Any provisions of law to the contrary notwithstanding, in any criminal proceeding before a justice of the peace no cost de oficio shall be taxed against the municipality concerned when the case is dismissed or the defendant acquitted.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: September 18, 1946
