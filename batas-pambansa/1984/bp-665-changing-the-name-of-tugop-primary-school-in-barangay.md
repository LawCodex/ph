# Batas Pambansa Blg. 665

**An Act Changing the Name of Tugop Primary School in Barangay Bangco, Municipality of Titay, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Bangco Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 665**

**An Act Changing the Name of Tugop Primary School in Barangay Bangco, Municipality of Titay, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Bangco Primary School**

## Section 1

The name of Tugop Primary School in Barangay Bangco, Municipality of Titay, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Bangco Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
