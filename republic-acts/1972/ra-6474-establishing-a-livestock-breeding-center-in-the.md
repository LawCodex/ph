# Republic Act No. 6474

**An Act Establishing a Livestock Breeding Center in the Municipality of Coron, Province of Palawan, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6474, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A LIVESTOCK BREEDING CENTER IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CORON, PROVINCE OF PALAWAN, AND AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS THEREFOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby established, under the supervision of the Bureau of Animal Industry, a livestock breeding center in the Municipality of Coron, Province of Palawan, at a site to be selected by the Director of Animal Industry through donations, to be known as Coron Livestock Breeding Center.

## Section 2

The sum of seventy-five thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the construction of an office-laboratory building and animals, and for such other expenses as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.1âшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall be take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 17, 1972.
