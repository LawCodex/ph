# Republic Act No. 3767

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tiaman Community School in the Barrio of Bolinsong, Municipality of Bonifacio, Province of Misamis Occidental, to Epifanio De Los Santos Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3767**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tiaman Community School in the Barrio of Bolinsong, Municipality of Bonifacio, Province of Misamis Occidental, to Epifanio De Los Santos Community School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tiaman Community School in the Barrio of Bolinsong, Municipality of Bonifacio, Province of Misamis Occidental, is hereby changed to Epifanio de Los Santos Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
