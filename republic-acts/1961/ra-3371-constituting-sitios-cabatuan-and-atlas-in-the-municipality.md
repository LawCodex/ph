# Republic Act No. 3371

**An Act Constituting Sitios Cabatuan and Atlas in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Talisay**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3371**

**An Act Constituting Sitios Cabatuan and Atlas in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Talisay**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sitios Cabatuan and Atlas in the Municipality of Bontoc, Province of Leyte, are hereby constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Talisay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
