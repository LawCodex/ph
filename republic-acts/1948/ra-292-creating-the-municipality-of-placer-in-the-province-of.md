# Republic Act No. 292

**An Act Creating the Municipality of Placer in the Province of Masbate**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 292**

An Act Creating the Municipality of Placer in the Province of Masbate

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Placer in the municipality of Cataignan, Province of Masbate, is separated from the municipality and constituted into a new municipality to be known as the municipality of Placer.

## Section 2

The elective officials of the new municipality shall be appointed by the President of the Philippines and shall hold office until their successors shall have been elected and qualified.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1948.
