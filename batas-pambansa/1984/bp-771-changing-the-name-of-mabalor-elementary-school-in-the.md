# Batas Pambansa Blg. 771

**An Act Changing the Name of Mabalor Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibaan, Province of Batangas, to Mabalor-Catandala Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 771**

An Act Changing the Name of Mabalor Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibaan, Province of Batangas, to Mabalor-Catandala Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Mabalor Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibaan, Province of Batangas, is changed to Mabalor-Catandala Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
