# Republic Act No. 2537

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Calansayan in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, to Barrio Concepcion**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2537**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Calansayan in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, to Barrio Concepcion**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Calansayan in the Municipality of Batangas, Province of Batangas, is changed to Barrio Concepcion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
