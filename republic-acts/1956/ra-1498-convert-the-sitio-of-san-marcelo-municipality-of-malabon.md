# Republic Act No. 1498

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of San Marcelo, Municipality of Malabon, Province of Rizal, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Acacia of the Same Municipality and Separating it from the Barrio of Tugatog of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1498**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of San Marcelo, Municipality of Malabon, Province of Rizal, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Acacia of the Same Municipality and Separating it from the Barrio of Tugatog of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of San Marcelo in the Municipality of Malabon, Province of Rizal, is hereby separated from the barrio of Tugatog and converted into a distinct barrio to be known as barrio Acacia of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
