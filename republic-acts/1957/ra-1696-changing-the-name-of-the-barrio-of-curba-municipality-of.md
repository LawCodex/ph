# Republic Act No. 1696

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Curba, Municipality of Santa Fe, Province of Leyte, to Barrio San Roque**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1696**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Curba, Municipality of Santa Fe, Province of Leyte, to Barrio San Roque**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Curba, Municipality of Santa Fe, Province of Leyte, is changed to barrio San Roque.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
