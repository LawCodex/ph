# Batas Pambansa Blg. 338

**An Act Changing the Name of the sta. Cruz Elementary School in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, to VIdal Gileto Memorial School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 338**

An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Cruz Elementary School in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, to Vidal Gileto Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Cruz Elementary School in the Municipality of Pandan, Province of Antique, is changed to Vidal Gileto Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 10, 1983
