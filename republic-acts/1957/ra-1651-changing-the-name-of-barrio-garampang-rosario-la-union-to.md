# Republic Act No. 1651

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Garampang, Rosario, La Union, to Barrio San Jose, Rosario, La Union**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1651**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Garampang, Rosario, La Union, to Barrio San Jose, Rosario, La Union**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The barrio of Garampang in the Municipality of Rosario, La Union, shall be renamed barrio San Jose, Rosario, La Union.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
