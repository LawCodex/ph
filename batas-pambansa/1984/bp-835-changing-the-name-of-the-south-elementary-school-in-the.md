# Batas Pambansa Blg. 835

**An Act Changing the Name of the South Elementary School in the City of San Carlos to Congressman VIcente Gustilo, Sr. Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 835**

An Act Changing the Name of the South Elementary School in the City of San Carlos to Congressman Vicente Gustilo, Sr. Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the South Elementary School in the City of San Carlos, Negros Occidental, is changed to Congressman Vicente Gustilo, Sr. Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
