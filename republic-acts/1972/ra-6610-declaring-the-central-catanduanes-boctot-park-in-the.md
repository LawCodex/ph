# Republic Act No. 6610

**An Act Declaring the Central Catanduanes (Boctot) Park in the Province of Catanduanes as the Alberto National Memorial Park.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6610, October 23, 1972 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE CENTRAL CATANDUANES (BOCTOT) PARK IN THE PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES AS THE ALBERTO NATIONAL MEMORIAL PARK.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Central Catanduanes (Boctot) Park in the Province of Catanduanes is hereby declared as Alberto National Memorial Park.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved.
