# Republic Act No. 1257

**An Act Changing the Name of San Fernando Diversion Road in the Municipality of San Fernando, Province of Pampanga, to Jose Abad Santos Boulevard**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1257**

**An Act Changing the Name of San Fernando Diversion Road in the Municipality of San Fernando, Province of Pampanga, to Jose Abad Santos Boulevard**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of San Fernando Diversion Road in the Municipality of San Fernando, Province of Pampanga, is hereby changed to Jose Abad Santos Boulevard.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
