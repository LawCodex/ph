# Republic Act No. 8299

**An Act Changing the Name of Sibulan National High School To Polillo National High School in the Municipality of Polillo, Province of Quezon**

Tenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8299, June 06, 1997 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SIBULAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO POLILLO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF POLILLO, PROVINCE OF QUEZON

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sibulan National High School in the Municipality of Polillo, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Polillo National High School.1aшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on June 6, 1997 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
