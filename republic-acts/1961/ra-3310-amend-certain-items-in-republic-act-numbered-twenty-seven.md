# Republic Act No. 3310

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3310**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 20, page 119, subparagraph 54, for the Province of Tarlac, paragraph c, Title B, Section one Republic Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred one, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"20. San Felipe-Mangandingay Road, San Manuel, Tarlac 5,000.00"

## Section 2

Item (db), page 663, Group I, subparagraph 53, for the Province of Tarlac, paragraph c, Section two of the same Act, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(db) Camposanto 2nd Barrio School, Moncada, Tarlac Construction, repair and/or improvement 5,000.00"

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
