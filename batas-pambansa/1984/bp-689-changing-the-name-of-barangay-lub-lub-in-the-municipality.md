# Batas Pambansa Blg. 689

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Lub-Lub in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Barangay Monfort Norte**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 689**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Lub-Lub in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Barangay Monfort Norte

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Lub-lub in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Barangay Monfort Norte.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
