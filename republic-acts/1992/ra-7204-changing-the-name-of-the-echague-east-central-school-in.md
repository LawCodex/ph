# Republic Act No. 7204

**An Act Changing the Name of the Echague East Central School in Echague, Isabela, to Tuguegarao-sto. Domingo Elementary School**

Fifth Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, nineteen hundred and ninety-one.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7204, March 02, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ECHAGUE EAST CENTRAL SCHOOL IN ECHAGUE, ISABELA, TO TUGUEGARAO-STO. DOMINGO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Echague East Central School in Echague, lsabela, is hereby changed to Tuguegarao-Sto. Domingo Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
