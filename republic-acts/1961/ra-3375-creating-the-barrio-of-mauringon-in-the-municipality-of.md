# Republic Act No. 3375

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Mauringon in the Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3375**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Mauringon in the Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Mauringon in the Municipality of Araceli, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Mauringon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
