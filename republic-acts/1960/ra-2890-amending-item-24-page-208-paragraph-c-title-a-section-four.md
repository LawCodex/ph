# Republic Act No. 2890

**An Act Amending Item (24), Page 208, Paragraph c, Title A, Section Four of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2890**

**An Act Amending Item (24), Page 208, Paragraph c, Title A, Section Four of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred and Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (24), page 208, paragraph c, Title A, Section four of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred and thirteen, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(24) Januiay Town Hall, Iloilo—Completion ₱50,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
