# Republic Act No. 2549

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ipil Pequeno in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to Barrio Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2549**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Ipil Pequeno in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, to Barrio Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Ipil Pequeno in the Municipality of Echague, Province of Isabela, is hereby changed to Barrio Quezon.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
