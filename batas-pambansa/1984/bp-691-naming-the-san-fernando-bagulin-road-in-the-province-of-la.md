# Batas Pambansa Blg. 691

**An Act Naming the San Fernando-Bagulin Road in the Province of La Union to Francisco I. Ortega Highway**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 691**

An Act Naming the San Fernando-Bagulin Road in the Province of La Union to Francisco I. Ortega Highway

## Section 1

The road from Quezon Boulevard in the Municipality of San Fernando to the poblacion in the Municipality of Bagulin, Province of La Union, which is referred to as San Fernando-Bagulin Road, is hereby named Francisco I. Ortega Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
