# Batas Pambansa Blg. 472

**An Act Creating a Secondary Vocational School in the Municipality of sto. Niño, Province of South Cotabato, to Be Known as the sto. Niño National School of Arts and Trades and Providing Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 472**

An Act Creating a Secondary Vocational School in the Municipality of Sto. Niño, Province of South Cotabato, to be Known as the Sto. Niño National School of Arts and Trades and Providing Funds Therefor

## Section 1

There shall be established a secondary vocational school in the Municipality of Sto. Niño, Province of South Cotabato, to be known as the Sto. Niño National School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

The school shall offer secondary education curriculum for vocational trade school courses.

## Section 3

Such sums as may necessary for the operation and maintenance of said school shall be provided in subsequent General Appropriations Acts, until which time the operation of the institution as a national high school shall be deferred.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1983
