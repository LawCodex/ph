# Republic Act No. 2749

**An Act Amending Section Four of Republic Act Numbered Two Thousand Twenty-One Relative to the Salary of the Lieutenant-Governor of the Subprovince of Camiguin, Province of Oriental Misamis**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2749**

AN ACT AMENDING SECTION FOUR OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED TWO THOUSAND TWENTY-ONE RELATIVE TO THE SALARY OF THE LIEUTENANT-GOVERNOR OF THE SUBPROVINCE OF CAMIGUIN, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MISAMIS.

## Section 1

Section four of Republic Act Numbered Two thousand twenty-one is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Sec. 4. The lieutenant-governor of the Subprovince of Camiguin shall receive a compensation of six thousand pesos _per annum._ "

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 19, 1960.
