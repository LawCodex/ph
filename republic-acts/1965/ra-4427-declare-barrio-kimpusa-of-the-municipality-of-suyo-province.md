# Republic Act No. 4427

**An Act to Declare Barrio Kimpusa of the Municipality of Suyo, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Poblacion and the Seat of the Municipal Government of the Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4427**

An Act to Declare Barrio Kimpusa of the Municipality of Suyo, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Poblacion and the Seat of the Municipal Government of the Said Municipality

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Kimpusa of the Municipality of Suyo, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby declared as the poblacion and the seat of the municipal government of the said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
