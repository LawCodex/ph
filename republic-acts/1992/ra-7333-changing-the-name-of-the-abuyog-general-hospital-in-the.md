# Republic Act No. 7333

**An Act Changing the Name of the Abuyog General Hospital in the Municipality of Abuyog, Province of Leyte, to Octavio Traya, Sr., Memorial Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7333, March 31, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ABUYOG GENERAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ABUYOG, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, TO OCTAVIO TRAYA, SR., MEMORIAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Abuyog General Hospital in the Municipality of Abuyog, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Octavio Traya, Sr., Memorial Hospital.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
