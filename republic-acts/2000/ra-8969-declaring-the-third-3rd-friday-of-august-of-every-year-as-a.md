# Republic Act No. 8969

**An Act Declaring the Third (3rd) Friday of August of Every Year as a Special Working Public Holiday for the City of Davao**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8969 October 27, 2000**

AN ACT DECLARING THE THIRD (3RD) FRIDAY OF AUGUST OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY FOR THE CITY OF DAVAO

## SECTION 1

The third (3rd) Friday of the month of August of every year is hereby declared as a special working public holiday for the City of Davao.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **October 27, 2000**
