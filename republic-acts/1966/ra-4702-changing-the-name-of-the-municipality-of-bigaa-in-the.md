# Republic Act No. 4702

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bigaa in the Province of Bulacan to the Municipality of Balagtas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4702**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bigaa in the Province of Bulacan to the Municipality of Balagtas

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Bigaa in the Province of Bulacan is hereby changed to the Municipality of Balagtas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
