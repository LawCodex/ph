# Republic Act No. 5559

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Bantayan in the Municipality of Lingayen, Province of Pangasinan, Into a Barrio of Said Municipality, to Be Known as the Barrio of Bantayan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5559, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIO OF BANTAYAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LINGAYEN, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN, INTO A BARRIO OF SAID MUNICIPALITY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE BARRIO OF BANTAYAN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bantayan in the Municipality of Lingayen, Province of Pangasinan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Bantayan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
