# Batas Pambansa Blg. 713

**An Act Changing the Name of Basilan City Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Lamitan, Province of Basilan, to Lamitan District Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 713**

An Act Changing the Name of Basilan City Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Lamitan, Province of Basilan, to Lamitan District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Basilan City Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Lamitan, Province of Basilan, is hereby changed of Lamitan District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
