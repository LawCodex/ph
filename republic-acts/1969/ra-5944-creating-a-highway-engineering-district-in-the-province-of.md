# Republic Act No. 5944

**An Act Creating a Highway Engineering District in the Province of Bohol to Comprise the Third Congressional District**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5944, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING A HIGHWAY ENGINEERING DISTRICT IN THE PROVINCE OF BOHOL TO COMPRISE THE THIRD CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Any provision of law to the contrary notwithstanding, the Third Congressional District of Bohol shall be constituted into one highway engineering district comprising the towns of Valencia, Garcia-Hernandez, Jagna, Duero, Guindulman, Candijay, Mabini, Alicia, Sierra-Bullones, San Miguel, Ubay, Danao, Trinidad, Talibon, Jetafe Buenavista, Anda and Pilar, which shall be known as Second Highway Engineering District of Bohol, with headquarters in Guindulman, Province of Bohol,

## Section 2

All existing laws or parts thereof, executive orders and administrative regulations contrary to the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, **June 21, 1969.**
