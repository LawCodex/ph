# Batas Pambansa Blg. 785

**An Act Changing the Name of Tandang Sora Elementary School in Barangay Talipapa, Quezon City, to Placido Del Mundo Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 785**

An Act Changing the Name of Tandang Sora Elementary School in Barangay Talipapa, Quezon City, to Placido Del Mundo Memorial School

## Section 1

Tandang Sora Elementary School in Barangay Talipapa in Quezon City is hereby changed to Placido del Mundo Memorial School in honor of Mr. Placido del Mundo, the philanthropist and civic-minded person of the said barangay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
