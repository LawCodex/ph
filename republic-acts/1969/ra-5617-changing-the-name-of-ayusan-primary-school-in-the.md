# Republic Act No. 5617

**An Act Changing the Name of Ayusan Primary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, to Bienvenida and Asinas Primary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5617, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF AYUSAN PRIMARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TIAONG, PROVINCE OF QUEZON, TO BIENVENIDA AND ASINAS PRIMARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Ayusan Primary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to "Bienvenida and Asinas Primary School."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
