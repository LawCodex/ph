# Republic Act No. 2147

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Cogon in the Municipality of Dipolog, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2147**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Cogon in the Municipality of Dipolog, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cogon in the Municipality of Dipolog, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Cogon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
