# Batas Pambansa Blg. 758

**An Act Changing the Name of Talagutong Elementary School in the Municipality of Don Marcelino, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Don Marcelino Maruya Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 758**

An Act Changing the Name of Talagutong Elementary School in the Municipality of Don Marcelino, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Don Marcelino Maruya Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Talagutong Elementary School in the Municipality of Don Marcelino, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Don Marcelino Maruya Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
