# Republic Act No. 5572

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Calamundingan in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Barrio Lucio Laurel**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5572, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO CALAMUNDINGAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GLORIA, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO BARRIO LUCIO LAUREL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Calamundingan in the Municipality of Gloria, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Barrio Lucio Laurel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
