# Republic Act No. 5569

**An Act Constituting Sitios Calunyan, Ihawan, Kagsilihan, Monterico and Tagbinonga in the Municipality of Mahaplag, Province of Leyte, Into a Barrio of Said Municipality, to Be Known as Barrio San Juan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5569, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONSTITUTING SITIOS CALUNYAN, IHAWAN, KAGSILIHAN, MONTERICO AND TAGBINONGA IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAHAPLAG, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, INTO A BARRIO OF SAID MUNICIPALITY, TO BE KNOWN AS BARRIO SAN JUAN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Sitios Calunyan, Ihawan, Kagsilihan, Monterico and Tagbinonga in the Municipality of Mahaplag, Province of Leyte, are hereby constituted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as Barrio San Juan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
