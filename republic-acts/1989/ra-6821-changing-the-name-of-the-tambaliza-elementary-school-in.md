# Republic Act No. 6821

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tambaliza Elementary School in Barangay Tambaliza, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, to Fernando A. Arlos Memorial School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6821 December 18, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE TAMBALIZA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TAMBALIZA, MUNICIPALITY OF CONCEPCION, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO FERNANDO A. ARLOS MEMORIAL SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tambaliza Elementary School in Barangay Tambaliza, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Fernando A. Arlos Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 18, 1989**
