# Republic Act No. 6232

**An Act Creating Barrio Peñafrancia in the City of Naga**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6232, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO PEÑAFRANCIA IN THE CITY OF NAGA

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Sitio Peñafrancia, City of Naga, is hereby constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Peñafrancia.

## Section 2

The Barrio of Peñafrancia shall be bounded on the North by the Catholic Cemetery; on the East, by Naga River; on the South, by Santoja Street; and on the West, by Jacob Street.1âшphi1

## Section 3

The new barrio officials shall be appointed by the City Mayor, and they shall hold office until their successors are elected in the next regular barrio elections.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
