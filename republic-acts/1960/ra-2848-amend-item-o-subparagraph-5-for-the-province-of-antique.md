# Republic Act No. 2848

**An Act to Amend Item (o), Subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, Page 234, Paragraph a, Title B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2848**

**An Act to Amend Item (o), Subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, Page 234, Paragraph a, Title B, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred and One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (o), subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, page 234, paragraph a, Title B, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and one is hereby amended to read as follows:

(o) For the asphalting of Felicidad and Rizal streets, Dao ₱10,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
