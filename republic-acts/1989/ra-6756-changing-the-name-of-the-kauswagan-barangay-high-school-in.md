# Republic Act No. 6756

**An Act Changing the Name of the Kauswagan Barangay High School in Palo, Leyte, to Kauswagan National High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6756 August 11, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE KAUSWAGAN BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN PALO, LEYTE, TO KAUSWAGAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Kauswagan Barangay High School in Palo, Leyte, is hereby changed to Kauswagan National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **August 11, 1989.**
