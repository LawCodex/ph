# Republic Act No. 7479

**An Act Naming the Airfield At the Sangley Point Naval and Air Training Station To Major Danilo Atienza Airfield, Sangley Point**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7479, May 05, 1992 ]

AN ACT NAMING THE AIRFIELD AT THE SANGLEY POINT NAVAL AND AIR TRAINING STATION TO MAJOR DANILO ATIENZA AIRFIELD, SANGLEY POINT

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The airfield at the Sangley Point Naval and Air Training Station is hereby named as Major Danilo Atienza Airfield.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
