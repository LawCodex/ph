# Act No. 4154

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Fifty thousand Pesos for the Aid of the Unemployed and Other Persons Seeking Relief.**

[ Acts No. 4154, December 01, 1934 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE AID OF THE UNEMPLOYED AND OTHER PERSONS SEEKING RELIEF.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of fifty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, to relieve and succor persons who are in distress due to unemployment, and other persons found deserving of material aid.

## Section 2

This amount shall be expended by the Director of Public Welfare or any relief agency under the supervision of the Bureau of Public Welfare, subject to such rules and regulations as the Secretary of Public Instruction may approve.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 1, 1934.
