# Republic Act No. 936

**An Act to Convert Sitio Pili in the Municipality of Despujols, Province of Romblon, Into a Barrio With the Name of Dona Trinidad Roxas**

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 936, June 20, 1953 ]

AN ACT TO CONVERT SITIO PILI IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DESPUJOLS, PROVINCE OF ROMBLON, INTO A BARRIO WITH THE NAME OF DONA TRINIDAD ROXAS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Sitio Pili in the municipality of Despujols, Province of Romblon, is hereby converted into a barrio with the name of Dona Trinidad Roxas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 20, 1953.
