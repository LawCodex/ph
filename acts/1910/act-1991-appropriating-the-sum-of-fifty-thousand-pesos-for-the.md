# Act No. 1991

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Fifty thousand Pesos for the Relief of Sufferers From Public Calamities.**

[ Act No. 1991, April 19, 1910 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE RELIEF OF SUFFERERS FROM PUBLIC CALAMITIES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Relief of sufferers Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The sum of fifty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the purposes set forth in Act Numbered Eighteen hundred and nine of the Philippine Legislature and its amendments.

## Section 2

The said sum of fifty thousand pesos shall be available until totally expended, and shall be disbursed in accordance with the provisions of the aforesaid Act Numbered Eighteen hundred and nine and its amendments.

## Section 3

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the same shall take effect on its passage, in accordance with section one of Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and forty-five of the Philippine Legislature.

Enacted, April 19, 1910.
