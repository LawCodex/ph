# Republic Act No. 5022

**An Act Changing the Name of Bagoñgon Elementary School in the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, to Narciso Centeno Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5022**

An Act Changing the Name of Bagoñgon Elementary School in the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, to Narciso Centeno Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bagoñgon Elementary School in the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Narciso Centeno Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
