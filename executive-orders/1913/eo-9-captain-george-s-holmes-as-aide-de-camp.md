# Executive Order No. 9

**Captain George S. Holmes, as Aide-de-Camp**

**EXECUTVE ORDER No. 9**

Captain George S. Holmes, Philippine Constabulary, is announced as aide-de-camp to the Governor-General of the Philippine Islands, to date from January twentieth, nineteen hundred and thirteen.

**W. CAMERON FORBES**
_Governor-General_
