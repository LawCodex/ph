# Republic Act No. 6945

**An Act Changing the Name of the Amai-Pakpak General Hospital in Marawi City to Amai-Pakpak Provincial Hospital to Ensure a More Efficient and Effective Delivery of Services to the People**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6945 April 5, 1990**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE AMAI-PAKPAK GENERAL HOSPITAL IN MARAWI CITY TO AMAI-PAKPAK PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL TO ENSURE A MORE EFFICIENT AND EFFECTIVE DELIVERY OF SERVICES TO THE PEOPLE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Amai-Pakpak General Hospital in Marawi City is hereby changed to Amai-Pakpak Provincial Hospital.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Health is hereby directed to implement the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **April 5, 1990**
