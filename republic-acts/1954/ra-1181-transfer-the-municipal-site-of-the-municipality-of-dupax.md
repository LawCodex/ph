# Republic Act No. 1181

**An Act to Transfer the Municipal Site of the Municipality of Dupax, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, from its Present Location to the Barrio of Malasin of the Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1181**

**An Act to Transfer the Municipal Site of the Municipality of Dupax, Province of Nueva Vizcaya, from its Present Location to the Barrio of Malasin of the Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The municipal site of the municipality of Dupax, province of Nueva Vizcaya, is transferred from its present location to the barrio of Malasin of the said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1954.
