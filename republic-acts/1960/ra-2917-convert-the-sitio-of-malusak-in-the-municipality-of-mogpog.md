# Republic Act No. 2917

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Malusak in the Municipality of Mogpog, Province of Marinduque, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2917**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Malusak in the Municipality of Mogpog, Province of Marinduque, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Malusak in the Municipality of Mogpog, Province of Marinduque, is converted into a barrio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
