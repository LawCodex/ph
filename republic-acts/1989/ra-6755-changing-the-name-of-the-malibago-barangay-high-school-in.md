# Republic Act No. 6755

**An Act Changing the Name of the Malibago Barangay High School in Babatngon, Leyte to Malibago National High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6755 August 11, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MALIBAGO BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN BABATNGON, LEYTE TO MALIBAGO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Malibago Barangay High School in Babatngon Leyte, is hereby changed to Malibago National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **August 11, 1989.**
