# Republic Act No. 1486

**An Act Amending Item (b), Group I, Paragraph Seventeen, for the Province of Cebu, Subsection “f,” Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1486**

**An Act Amending Item (b), Group I, Paragraph Seventeen, for the Province of Cebu, Subsection "f," Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (b), Group I, paragraph seventeen, for the Province of Cebu, subsection "f," section two of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven is amended to read as follows:

| "(b) Barrio Schools to be designated by the Municipal Mayor, Danao Construction and/or repair | ₱18,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
