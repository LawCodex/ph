# Republic Act No. 7604

**An Act Changing the Name of the Canauili Barangay High School in Barangay Canauili, Municipality of Janiuay, Province of Iloilo, To Manuel A. Aaron Memorial Barangay High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7604, June 03, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE CANAUILI BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY CANAUILI, MUNICIPALITY OF JANIUAY, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, TO MANUEL A. AARON MEMORIAL BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Canauili Barangay High School in Barangay Canauili, Municipality of Janiuay, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Manuel A. Aaron Memorial Barangay High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
