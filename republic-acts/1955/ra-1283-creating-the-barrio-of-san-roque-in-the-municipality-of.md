# Republic Act No. 1283

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Roque in the Municipality of Unisan, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1283**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Roque in the Municipality of Unisan, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of San Roque in the barrio of Tagumpay, Municipality of Unisan, Province of Quezon, is hereby separated from the barrio of Tagumpay and constituted into a separate and independent barrio of the same municipality, to be known as barrio San Roque.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1955.
