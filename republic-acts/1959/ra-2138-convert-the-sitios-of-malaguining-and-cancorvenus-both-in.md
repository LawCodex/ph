# Republic Act No. 2138

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Malaguining and Cancorvenus, Both in the Municipality of Talalora, Province of Samar, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Francisco of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2138**

**An Act to Convert the Sitios of Malaguining and Cancorvenus, Both in the Municipality of Talalora, Province of Samar, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio San Francisco of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Malaguining and Cancorvenus, both in the Municipality of Talalora, Province of Samar, are hereby converted to a barrio to be known as Barrio San Francisco of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
