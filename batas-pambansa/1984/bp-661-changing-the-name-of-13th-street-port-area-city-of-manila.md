# Batas Pambansa Blg. 661

**An Act Changing the Name of 13th street, Port Area, City of Manila, to Roberto S. Oca, Sr. street**

**BATAS PAMBANSA BLG. 661**

**An Act Changing the Name of 13Th Street, Port Area, City of Manila, to Roberto S. Oca, Sr. Street**

## Section 1

It is hereby provided that 13th Street, Port Area, City of Manila, be renamed to Roberto S. Oca, Sr. Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
