# Act No. 4146

**An Act to Consider the Late Ponciano Reyes and Lucio Diuquillo, Retired Under Act Numbered Twenty-Five Hundred and Eighty-Nine, as Amended.**

[ Acts No. 4146, November 26, 1934 ]

AN ACT TO CONSIDER THE LATE PONCIANO REYES AND LUCIO DIUQUILLO, RETIRED UNDER ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-FIVE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-NINE, AS AMENDED.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The late Ponciano Reyes, judge of the Court of First Instance of Zamboanga, and Lucio Diuquillo, provincial treasurer of Masbate, who died in the performance of their official duties, are hereby considered retired in accordance with the provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-five hundred and eighty-nine, and for the purpose hereof, said retirement shall be as effective as if an application therefor had been filed before June thirty, nineteen hundred and sixteen, and as if the same had been acted upon favorably prior to the dates of their death.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 26, 1934.
