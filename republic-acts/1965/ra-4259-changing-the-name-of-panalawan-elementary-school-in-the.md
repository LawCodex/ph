# Republic Act No. 4259

**An Act Changing the Name of Panalawan Elementary School in the Municipal District of Bubong, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Mariga Alonto Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4259**

An Act Changing the Name of Panalawan Elementary School in the Municipal District of Bubong, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Mariga Alonto Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Panalawan Elementary School in the Municipal District of Bubong, Province of Lanao del Sur, is changed to Mariga Alonto Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
