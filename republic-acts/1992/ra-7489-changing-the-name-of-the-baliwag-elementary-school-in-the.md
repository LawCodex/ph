# Republic Act No. 7489

**An Act Changing the Name of the Baliwag Elementary School in the Municipality of Magallanes, Province of Cavite, To Pacifico Villacarlos Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7489, May 13, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BALIWAG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAGALLANES, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, TO PACIFICO VILLACARLOS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Baliwag elementary School in the Municipality of Magallanes, Provinc of Cavite, is hereby changed to Pacifico Villacarlos Elementary School.LaW㏗iL

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, May 13, 1992.
