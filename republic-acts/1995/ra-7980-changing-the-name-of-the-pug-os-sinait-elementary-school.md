# Republic Act No. 7980

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pug-Os (SINAIT) Elementary School Located At Barangay Pug-os, Municipality of Sinait, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Dean Leopoldo Yabes Memorial Elementary School**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 7980, April 22, 1995 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PUG-OS (SINAIT) ELEMENTARY SCHOOL LOCATED AT BARANGAY PUG-OS, MUNICIPALITY OF SINAIT, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR, TO DEAN LEOPOLDO YABES MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pug-os (Sinait) Elementary School located at Barangay Pug-os, Municipality of Sinait, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Dean Leopoldo Yabes Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1aшphi1

Lapsed into law on April 22,1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
