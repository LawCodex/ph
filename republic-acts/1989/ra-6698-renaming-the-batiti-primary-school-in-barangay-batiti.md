# Republic Act No. 6698

**An Act Renaming the Batiti Primary School in Barangay Batiti, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, as the Borres-Posadas Primary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6698, February 10, 1989 ]

AN ACT RENAMING THE BATITI PRIMARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BATITI, MUNICIPALITY OF CONCEPCION, PROVINCE OF ILOILO, AS THE BORRES-POSADAS PRIMARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Batiti Primary School in Barangay Batiti, Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Borres-Posadas Primary School.

## Section 2
