# Republic Act No. 6800

**An Act Changing the Name of the Labasan Elementary School in the Municipality of Bongabong, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Moises Abante Memorial Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6800 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LABASAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BONGABONG, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO MOISES ABANTE MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Labasan Elementary School in the Municipality of Bongabong, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is hereby changed to Moises Abante Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
