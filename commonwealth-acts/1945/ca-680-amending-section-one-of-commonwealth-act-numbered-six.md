# Commonwealth Act No. 680

**An Act Amending Section One of Commonwealth Act Numbered Six Hundred and Seventy-Four Entitled "An Act Appropriating Funds for the Operation of the Government of the Commonwealth of the Philippines Beginning July First, Nineteen Hundred and Forty-Five Until the General Appropriation Act for the Fiscal Year Ending June thirtieth, Nineteen Hundred and Forty-Six Approved."**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 680, September 18, 1945 ]

AN ACT AMENDING SECTION ONE OF COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED SIX HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-FOUR ENTITLED "AN ACT APPROPRIATING FUNDS FOR THE OPERATION OF THE GOVERNMENT OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF THE PHILIPPINES BEGINNING JULY FIRST, NINETEEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-FIVE UNTIL THE GENERAL APPROPRIATION ACT FOR THE FISCAL YEAR ENDING JUNE THIRTIETH, NINETEEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-SIX APPROVED."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The first sentence, section one, of Commonwealth Act Numbered Six hundred and seventy-four, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"**Section 1.** Pending approval of the General Appropriation Act for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and forty-six, Commonwealth Act Numbered Six hundred and fifty-four, Administrative Order Numbered Five, dated May twenty-five, nineteen hundred and forty-five, and Executive Order Numbered Eighteen-W, dated August thirty-first, nineteen hundred and forty-four, shall continue in force for the operation of the Government of the Commonwealth of the Philippines for a period of three months beginning July first, nineteen hundred and forty-five, and for this purpose, there are hereby appropriated from any fund in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sums equal to one-fourth of the various items of appropriations authorized in said Act and Administrative and executive orders."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on July first, nineteen hundred and forty-five.

Approved, September 18, 1945.
