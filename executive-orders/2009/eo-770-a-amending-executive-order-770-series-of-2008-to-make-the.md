# Executive Order No. 770-A

**Amending Executive Order 770, Series of 2008, to Make the Leagues of Provinces, Cities and Municipalities as Regular Members of the Neda Board-National Land Use Committee (Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo)**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 770-A, September 30, 2009 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER 770, SERIES OF 2008, TO MAKE THE LEAGUES OF PROVINCES, CITIES AND MUNICIPALITIES AS REGULAR MEMBERS OF THE NEDA BOARD-NATIONAL LAND USE COMMITTEE

WHEREAS, Executive Order (EO) 770 entitled: “Amending Letter of Instruction 1350 otherwise known as ‘Providing the Institutional Framework for National Physical Planning'” reconstituted the National Land Use Committee (NLUC) into a NEDA Board (NB) committee;

**WHEREAS,** Section 2 of EO 770 lists down the composition of the Committee and states that “The NB-NLUC shall invite the respective Presidents of the Leagues of Provinces, Municipalities and Cities to its meetings”;

**WHEREAS,** the membership of the LGU Leagues in the NB-NLUC is necessary to ensure that local inputs and responses to land use policies that affect local governance are considered by the Committee.

**NOW, THEREFORE, I, GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO** , President of the Republic of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by law, do hereby order:

## Section 1

Section 2 of EO 770 is hereby amended to read as follows:

“**Section 2.** _Composition._ The NB-NLUC shall be composed of the following:

Chairperson :

Secretary of Socio-Economic Planning

Members :

Secretaries of Environment and Natural Resources; Agriculture; Agrarian Reform; Trade and Industry; Public Works and Highways; Transportation and Communications; Tourism; Interior and Local Government; Justice; Science and Technology; and Energy.Chairpersons of the Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council/Housing and Land Use Regulatory Board; and the National Commission on Indigenous People.

Presidents of the League of Provinces, Municipalities and Cities.

Two private sector representatives."

## Section 2

All executive orders and executive issuances or portions thereof which are inconsistent with this Executive Order are hereby revoked, amended or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Executive Order shall take effect immediately.

DONE in the City of Manila, this 30th day of September, in the year of Our Lord, Two Thousand Nine.

(SGD.) **GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**

By the President:

(SGD.) **EDUARDO R. ERMITA**
Executive Secretary
