# Republic Act No. 6218

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Anao-Aon, Province of Surigao Del Norte, to San Francisco.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6218, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF ANAO-AON, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL NORTE, TO SAN FRANCISCO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Anao-aon in the Province of Surigao del Norte, is hereby changed to San Francisco,

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
