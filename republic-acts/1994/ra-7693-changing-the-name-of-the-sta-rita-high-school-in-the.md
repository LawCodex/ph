# Republic Act No. 7693

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Rita High School in the Municipality of Guiguinto, Province of Bulacan, to Felizardo C. Lipana Memorial High School**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Phillipines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7693, April 09, 1994 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE STA. RITA HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GUIGUINTO, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO FELIZARDO C. LIPANA MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Rita High School, located in the Municipality of Guiguinto, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Felizardo C. Lipana Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

Lapsed into law without the signature of the President on April 9, 1994, in accordance with Article VI, Section 27 (1) of the Constitution.1a⍵⍴h!1
