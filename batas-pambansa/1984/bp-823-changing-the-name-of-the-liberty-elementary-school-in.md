# Batas Pambansa Blg. 823

**An Act Changing the Name of the Liberty Elementary School in Barangay VIsayan VIllage, in the Municipality of Tagum, Province of Davao, to VIsayan VIllage Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 823**

An Act Changing the Name of the Liberty Elementary School in Barangay Visayan Village, in the Municipality of Tagum, Province of Davao, to Visayan Village Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Liberty Elementary School in the Visayan Village, in the Municipality of Tagum, Province of Davao, is hereby changed to Visayan Village Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
