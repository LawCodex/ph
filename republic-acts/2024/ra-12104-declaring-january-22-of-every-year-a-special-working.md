# Republic Act No. 12104

**An Act Declaring January 22 of Every Year A Special Working Holiday in the Municipality of Guiguinto, Province of Bulacan, in Celebration of the Halamanan Festival ra2024/pdra_12104_2024.pdf**

Nineteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12104, December 13, 2024 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY 22 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GUIGUINTO, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, IN CELEBRATION OF HALAMANAN FESTIVAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

January 22 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the Municipality of Guiguinto, Province of Bulacan, in celebration of the Halamanan Festival. Upon proclamation of the President, January of every year shall be a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Guiguinto, Province of Bulacan.ℒαwρhi৷

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
