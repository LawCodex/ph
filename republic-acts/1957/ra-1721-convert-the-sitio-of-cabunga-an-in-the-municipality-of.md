# Republic Act No. 1721

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Cabunga-an in the Municipality of Tarangnan, Province of Samar, into a Regular Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1721**

**An Act to Convert the Sitio of Cabunga-an in the Municipality of Tarangnan, Province of Samar, into a Regular Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cabunga-an in the Municipality of Tarangnan, Province of Samar, is hereby converted into a regular barrio to be called henceforth as the barrio of Cabunga-an.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
