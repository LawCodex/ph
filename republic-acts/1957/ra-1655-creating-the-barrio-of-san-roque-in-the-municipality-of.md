# Republic Act No. 1655

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Roque in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1655**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Roque in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cabugao in the Municipality of Marabut, Province of Samar, is hereby constituted into a barrio of the said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Roque.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
