# Batas Pambansa Blg. 674

**An Act Changing the Name of the Calimbajan Elementary School in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, to Assemblyman Manuel T. Terencio, Sr. Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 674**

An Act Changing the Name of the Calimbajan Elementary School in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, to Assemblyman Manuel T. Terencio, Sr. Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Calimbajan Elementary School located in the Municipality of Makato, Province of Aklan, is changed to Assemblyman Manuel T. Terencio, Sr. Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
