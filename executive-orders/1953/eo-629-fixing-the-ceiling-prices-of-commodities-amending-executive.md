# Executive Order No. 629

**Fixing the Ceiling Prices of Commodities, Amending Executive Order Numbered Four Hundred and Forty-Two, Executive Order Numbered Three Hundred and Thirty-One, and for Other Purposes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 629, October 14, 1953 ]

FIXING THE CEILING PRICES OF COMMODITIES, AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED FOUR HUNDRED AND FORTY-TWO, EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-ONE, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

## Section 3

Section 1 of Executive Order No. 331, dated July 7, 1950, insofar as it sets up ceiling prices for locally manufactured canned pineapples, is hereby repealed inasmuch as there is an abundant supply of said commodity.

## Section 4

The ceiling prices fixed in this Order include the 7 per cent sales tax and 1 per cent municipal tax.

## Section 5

This Order shall take effect five days after the date hereof.

Done in the City of Manila, this 14th day of October, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-three, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the eighth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **MARCIANO ROQUE**
