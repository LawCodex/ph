# Republic Act No. 1317

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Lidong, Taba-Taba, Manmontoy, Borabod, Taisan, Hinampacan, Manlimonsito, Polantuna, and Oliva in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte, into Barrios of Said Municipality with the Same Names**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1317**

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Lidong, Taba-Taba, Manmontoy, Borabod, Taisan, Hinampacan, Manlimonsito, Polantuna, and Oliva in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte, into Barrios of Said Municipality with the Same Names**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Lidong, Taba-taba, Manmontoy, Borabod, Taisan, Hinampacan, Manlimonsito, Polantuna, and Oliva in the Municipality of Basud, Province of Camarines Norte, are converted into barrios of said municipality with the same names.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
