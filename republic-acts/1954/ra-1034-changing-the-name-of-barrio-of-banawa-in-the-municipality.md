# Republic Act No. 1034

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio of Banawa, in the Municipality of Kabagan, Province of Cotabato, to Banawag**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1034**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio of Banawa, in the Municipality of Kabagan, Province of Cotabato, to Banawag**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of Barrio of Banawa, in the Municipality of Kabagan, Province of Cotabato, is hereby changed to Banawag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
