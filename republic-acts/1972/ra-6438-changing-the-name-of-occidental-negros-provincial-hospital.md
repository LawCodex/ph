# Republic Act No. 6438

**An Act Changing the Name of Occidental Negros Provincial Hospital in the City of Bacolod to Corazon Locsin-Montelibano Memorial Hospital.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6438, June 17, 1972 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF OCCIDENTAL NEGROS PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL IN THE CITY OF BACOLOD TO CORAZON LOCSIN-MONTELIBANO MEMORIAL HOSPITAL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Occidental Negros Provincial Hospital, situated in the City of Bacolod Philippines, is hereby changed and, henceforth, shall be named Corazon Locsin-Montelibano Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 17, 1972.
