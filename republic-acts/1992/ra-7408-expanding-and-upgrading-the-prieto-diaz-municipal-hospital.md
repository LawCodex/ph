# Republic Act No. 7408

**An Act Expanding and Upgrading the Prieto-diaz Municipal Hospital From A Ten-bed Capacity Municipal Hospital Into A Twenty-five-bed Capacity District Hospital, To Be Known As the Prieto-diaz District Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7408, April 14, 1992 ]

AN ACT EXPANDING AND UPGRADING THE PRIETO-DIAZ MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL FROM A TEN-BED CAPACITY MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL INTO A TWENTY-FIVE-BED CAPACITY DISTRICT HOSPITAL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE PRIETO-DIAZ DISTRICT HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Prieto-Diaz Municipal Hospital in the Province of Sorsogon, is hereby expanded and upgraded from a ten-bed capacity municipal hospital into a twenty-five-bed capacity district hospital, to be known as the Prieto-Diaz District Hospital.1âшphi1

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
