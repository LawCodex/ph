# Act No. 1485

**An Act Extending Until August thirty-First, Nineteen Hundred and Six, the Time for the Payment, Without Penalty, of the Cedula Tax for the Year Nineteen Hundred and Six By the Non-Christian Inhabitants of the Province of Ilocos Sur and the Subprovince of Abra, Except those in the Settlements of Tineg, Danac, and Mayabo in the Subprovince of Abra.**

[ Act No. 1485, May 11, 1906 ]

AN ACT EXTENDING UNTIL AUGUST THIRTY-FIRST, NINETEEN HUNDRED AND SIX, THE TIME FOR THE PAYMENT, WITHOUT PENALTY, OF THE CEDULA TAX FOR THE YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND SIX BY THE NON-CHRISTIAN INHABITANTS OF THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR AND THE SUBPROVINCE OF ABRA, EXCEPT THOSE IN THE SETTLEMENTS OF TINEG, DANAC, AND MAYABO IN THE SUBPROVINCE OF ABRA.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The period for the payment without penalty of the cedula tax for the year nineteen hundred and six by the non-Christian inhabitants of the Province of Ilocos Sur and the subprovince of Abra, except those in the settlements of Tineg, Danac, and Mayabo, in the subprovinee of Abra. is hereby extended until August thirty-first, nineteen hundred and six.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This Act shall be retroactive and take effect as of April twenty-eighth, nineteen hundred and six.

Enacted, May 11, 1906.
