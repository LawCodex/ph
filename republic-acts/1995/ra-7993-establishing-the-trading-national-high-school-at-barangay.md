# Republic Act No. 7993

**An Act Establishing the Trading National High School At Barangay 37-d, Quezon Boulevard, Davao City, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 7993, April 22, 1995 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE TRADING NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AT BARANGAY 37-D, QUEZON BOULEVARD, DAVAO CITY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, a high school at Barangay 37-D, Quezon Boulevard, Davao City, to be known as Trading National High School.

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to implement the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on April 22, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
