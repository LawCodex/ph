# Act No. 3705

**An Act Providing that the Municipality of Delgado, Leyte, Shall Be Called "Anahawan."**

[ Act No. 3705, November 20, 1930 ]

AN ACT PROVIDING THAT THE MUNICIPALITY OF DELGADO, LEYTE, SHALL BE CALLED "ANAHAWAN."

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The municipality of Delgado, Province of Leyte, shall hereafter be called municipality of "Anahawan."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and thirty-one.

Approved, November 20, 1930.
