# Republic Act No. 4865

**An Act Naming the Portion of the Pan-Philippine Highway from Barrio Guintiguian, Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Pinamono-an, Municipality of Mahaplag of the Same Province as the Daniel Z. Romualdez Highway**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4865**

An Act Naming the Portion of the Pan-Philippine Highway from Barrio Guintiguian, Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Pinamono-an, Municipality of Mahaplag of the Same Province as the Daniel Z. Romualdez Highway

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The portion of the Pan-Philippine Highway from Barrio Guintiguian, Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte, to Barrio Pinamono-an, Municipality of Mahaplag of the same Province shall hereafter be known and referred to as the Daniel Z. Romualdez Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 26, 1967.
