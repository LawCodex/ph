# Republic Act No. 5535

**An Act Creating Additional Positions of Assistant City Fiscal for the City of Baguio, By Further Amending Certain Sections of the Charter of Said City**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5535, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING ADDITIONAL POSITIONS OF ASSISTANT CITY FISCAL FOR THE CITY OF BAGUIO, BY FURTHER AMENDING CERTAIN SECTIONS OF THE CHARTER OF SAID CITY.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Section two thousand five hundred sixty-one of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended is further amended to read as follows:

"Section 2561. _The Assistant City Fiscals._ —There shall be seven assistant city fiscals who shall assist the city fiscal as he shall direct. The assistant city fiscals shall receive the following compensation:

"(a) First Assistant, nine thousand pesos _per annum_.

"(b) Second assistant, seven thousand four hundred pesos _per annum_.

"(c) Third assistant, five thousand four hundred pesos _per annum_.

"(d) Fourth assistant, eight thousand four hundred pesos _per annum_.

"(e) Three fifth assistants, seven thousand two hundred pesos _per annum_ each."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
