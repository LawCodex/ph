# Act No. 2998

**An Act to Amend Section three of Act Numbered Twenty-Nine Hundred and Seventy-Two, Entitled "An Act to Provide in What Language Account Books Shall Be Kept, and to Establish Penalties for Its VIolation," Authorizing the Suspension of the Effects of Said Act.**

[ Act No. 2998, January 26, 1922 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SECTION THREE OF ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-NINE HUNDRED AND SEVENTY-TWO, ENTITLED "AN ACT TO PROVIDE IN WHAT LANGUAGE ACCOUNT BOOKS SHALL BE KEPT, AND TO ESTABLISH PENALTIES FOR ITS VIOLATION," AUTHORIZING THE SUSPENSION OF THE EFFECTS OF SAID ACT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Section three of Act Numbered Twenty-nine hundred and seventy-two is hereby amennded to read as follows:

"**Section 3.** This Act shall take effect on November first, nineteen hundred and twenty-one: _Provided, however,_ That the Governor-General may, by proclamation, suspend the effects of this Act for a period not to extend beyond January first, nineteen hundred and twenty-three."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, January 26, 1922.
