# Republic Act No. 7990

**An Act Establishing a National High School in Barangay Dikapinisan, Municipality of San Luis, Province of Aurora, to Be Known as the Dikapinisan National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 7990, April 22, 1995 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY DIKAPINISAN, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN LUIS, PROVINCE OF AURORA, TO BE KNOWN AS THE DIKAPINISAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, a national high school in Barangay Dikapinisan, Municipality of San Luis, Province of Aurora, to be known as the Dikapinisan National High School.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to implement the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.1aшphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on April 22, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
