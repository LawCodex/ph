# Batas Pambansa Blg. 383

**An Act Naming the Highway Connecting the Municipalities of San Remigio and Tabuelan in the Province of Cebu as Antonio Y. De Pio Highway.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 383**

An Act Naming the Highway Connecting the Municipalities of San Remigio and Tabuelan in the Province of Cebu as Antonio Y. De Pio Highway

## Section 1

The highway connecting the Municipalities of San Remigio and Tabuelan in the Province of Cebu shall be known as Antonio Y. de Pio Highway.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 8, 1983
