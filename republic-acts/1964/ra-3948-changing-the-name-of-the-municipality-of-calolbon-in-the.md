# Republic Act No. 3948

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Calolbon in the Province of Catanduanes to San Andres**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3948**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Calolbon in the Province of Catanduanes to San Andres**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Calolbon in the Province of Catanduanes is hereby changed to San Andres.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.(awÞhi(
