# Republic Act No. 2486

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Icogan in the Municipality of Looc, Province of Romblon, to San Isidro**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2486**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Icogan in the Municipality of Looc, Province of Romblon, to San Isidro**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Icogan in the Municipality of Looc, Province of Romblon, is changed to San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
