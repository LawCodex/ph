# Batas Pambansa Blg. 774

**An Act Changing the Name of the Alegrea Primary School in the Municipality of Marihatag, Province of Surigao Del Sur, to Lingaya Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 774**

An Act Changing the Name of the Alegrea Primary School in the Municipality of Marihatag, Province of Surigao Del Sur, to Lingaya Primary School

## Section 1

The name of the Alegrea Primary School in the Municipality of Marihatag, Province of Surigao del Sur, is changed to Lingaya Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
