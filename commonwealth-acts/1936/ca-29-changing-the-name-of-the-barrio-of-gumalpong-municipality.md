# Commonwealth Act No. 29

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Gumalpong. Municipality of Baco, Province of Mindoro, to that of Santa Rosa.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 29, September 08, 1936 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BARRIO OF GUMALPONG. MUNICIPALITY OF BACO, PROVINCE OF MINDORO, TO THAT OF SANTA ROSA.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Gumalpong, municipality of Baco, Province of Mindoro, is hereby changed to that of Santa Rosa.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted, without Executive approval, September 8, 1936.
