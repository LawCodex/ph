# Act No. 2348

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Five thousand Pesos to Enable the Philippine Islands to Be Represented At the Far Eastern Athletic Association Meet At Shanghai, China.**

[ Act No. 2348, February 28, 1914 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIVE THOUSAND PESOS TO ENABLE THE PHILIPPINE ISLANDS TO BE REPRESENTED AT THE FAR EASTERN ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION MEET AT SHANGHAI, CHINA.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The sum of five thousand pesos, is hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the payment of traveling expenses, subsistence and incidental expenses of the representatives of the Philippine Islands at the meet of the Far Eastern Athletic Association to be held at Shanghai, China, in October, nineteen hundred and fourteen. Such representatives shall be selected by the Philippine Amateur Athletic Federation subject to approval of the Secretary of Public Instruction.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, February 28, 1914.
