# Republic Act No. 1029

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Balayang, Mabini, Pangasinan, to Barrio Luna**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1029**

**An Act to Change the Name of Barrio Balayang, Mabini, Pangasinan, to Barrio Luna**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The name of barrio Balayang, Mabini, Pangasinan, is hereby changed to barrio Luna.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
