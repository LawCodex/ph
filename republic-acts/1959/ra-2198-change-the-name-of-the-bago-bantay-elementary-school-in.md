# Republic Act No. 2198

**An Act to Change the Name of the Bago Bantay Elementary School in Quezon City to Esteban Abada Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2198**

**An Act to Change the Name of the Bago Bantay Elementary School in Quezon City to Esteban Abada Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bago Bantay Elementary School in Quezon City is hereby changed to Esteban Abada Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
