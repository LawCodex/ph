# Republic Act No. 9069

**An Act Declaring May 14 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday for Obando, Bulacan**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9069 April 08, 2001**

AN ACT DECLARING MAY 14 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY FOR OBANDO, BULACAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

It is hereby declared that May 14 of every year, being the Foundation Day of Obando, Bulacan, shall be a special working holiday for the same.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of national circulation and at least in two (2) local newspapers and/or community newsletters.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) AQUILINO Q. PIMENTEL, JR. President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE, JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on February 6, 2001 and October 2, 2000, respectively.
