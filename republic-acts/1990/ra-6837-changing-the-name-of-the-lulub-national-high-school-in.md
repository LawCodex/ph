# Republic Act No. 6837

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lulub National High School in Barangay Purificacion D. Manfort, Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Purificacion D. Monfort National School**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6837

An Act Changing the Name of the Lulub National High School in Barangay Purificacion D. Manfort, Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Purificacion D. Monfort National School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Lublub National High School in Barangay Purificacion D. Manfort, Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, is hereby changed to Purificacion D. Monfort National School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
