# Commonwealth Act No. 229

**An Act Authorizing the Transfer of Any Amount From One Provincial Fund to Another, Whenever the Public Interests So Require.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 229, November 30, 1936 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE TRANSFER OF ANY AMOUNT FROM ONE PROVINCIAL FUND TO ANOTHER, WHENEVER THE PUBLIC INTERESTS SO REQUIRE.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

Whenever the public interests so require, the provincial board may, with the approval of the Department Head, transfer any amount from the existing balances of the different provincial funds to another fund in order to cover deficiencies in the latter.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted, without Executive approval, November 30, 1936.
