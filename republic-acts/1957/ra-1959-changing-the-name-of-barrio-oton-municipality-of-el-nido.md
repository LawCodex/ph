# Republic Act No. 1959

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Oton, Municipality of El Nido, Province of Palawan, to Mabini**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1959**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Oton, Municipality of El Nido, Province of Palawan, to Mabini**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Oton, Municipality of El Nido, Province of Palawan, is changed to Mabini.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
