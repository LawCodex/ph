# Republic Act No. 1705

**An Act Creating Certain, Barrios in the Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1705**

**An Act Creating Certain, Barrios in the Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Lubigan, Laoang, Lawac-Amulag, Labney and San Juan, Municipality of Tarlac, Province of Tarlac, are converted into barrios of said municipality to be known as the barrios of Lubigan, Laoang, Lawac-Amulag, Labney and San Juan, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
