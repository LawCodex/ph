# Act No. 4210

**An Act to Change the Name of the Yawa Bridge, in the Municipality of Legaspi, Province of Albay, to Antonio De Las Alas Bridge.**

[ Acts No. 4210, August 02, 1935 ]

AN ACT TO CHANGE THE NAME OF THE YAWA BRIDGE, IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LEGASPI, PROVINCE OF ALBAY, TO ANTONIO DE LAS ALAS BRIDGE.

Be it enacted, by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The bridge called Yawa Bridge, on the river of the same name in the municipality of Legaspi, Province of Albay shall hereafter be called Antonio de las Alas Bridge.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, August 2, 1935.
