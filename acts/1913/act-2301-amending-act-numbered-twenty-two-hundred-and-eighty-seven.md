# Act No. 2301

**An Act Amending Act Numbered Twenty-Two Hundred and Eighty-Seven By Exempting From the Provisions of Said Act those Persons Who At the Time of the Passage thereof Held Legal Licenses for the Sale of the Intoxicating Liquors Described therein.**

[ Act No. 2301, December 09, 1913 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-SEVEN BY EXEMPTING FROM THE PROVISIONS OF SAID ACT THOSE PERSONS WHO AT THE TIME OF THE PASSAGE THEREOF HELD LEGAL LICENSES FOR THE SALE OF THE INTOXICATING LIQUORS DESCRIBED THEREIN.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-two hundred and eighty-seven prohibiting the sale of intoxicating liquors, with certain exceptions, in the Mountain Province, Nueva Vizcaya, and Agusan, except in certain districts, are hereby amended so as to exempt from the prohibitions and penalties of said Act all those persons who, at the time of the passage of said Act, held legal licenses for the sale of ardent spirits, ale, beer, wine, or intoxicating liquors of any kind, in said districts, for and during the term of said licenses.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, December 9, 1913.
