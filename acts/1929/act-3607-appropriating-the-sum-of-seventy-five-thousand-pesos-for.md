# Act No. 3607

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Seventy-Five thousand Pesos for Additional Public Land Inspectors to Expedite the Inspection Work of the Bureau of Lands, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Act No. 3607, December 02, 1929 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF SEVENTY-FIVE THOUSAND PESOS FOR ADDITIONAL PUBLIC LAND INSPECTORS TO EXPEDITE THE INSPECTION WORK OF THE BUREAU OF LANDS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, the sum of seventy-five thousand pesos for the salaries and traveling expenses of the following additional personnel for the Bureau of Lands:

| 30 public land inspectors at P1,200 | P36,000.00 |
|---|---|
| 50 assistant public land inspectors at P600 | P30,000.00 |
| Traveling expenses and per diems | 9,000.00 |
| | --------------- |
| Total | 75,000.00 |

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and thirty.

Approved, December 2, 1929.
