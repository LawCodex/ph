# Republic Act No. 6783

**An Act Changing the Name of the Upi Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Upi, Province of Maguindanao, to Datu Blah T. Sinsuat District Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6783, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE UPI EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF UPI, PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO, TO DATU BLAH T. SINSUAT DISTRICT HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Upi Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Upi, Province of Maguindanao, is hereby changed to Datu Blah T. Sinsuat District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
