# Republic Act No. 3745

**An Act to Declare Barrio Nalasin of the Municipality of Lidlidda, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Seat of the Municipal Government of the Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3745**

**An Act to Declare Barrio Nalasin of the Municipality of Lidlidda, Province of Ilocos Sur, as the Seat of the Municipal Government of the Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Barrio Nalasin of the Municipality of Lidlidda, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby declared as the seat of the municipal government of the said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
