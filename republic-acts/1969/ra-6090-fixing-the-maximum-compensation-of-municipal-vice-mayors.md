# Republic Act No. 6090

**An Act Fixing the Maximum Compensation of Municipal Vice-Mayors and Councilors**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6090, August 04, 1969 ]

AN ACT FIXING THE MAXIMUM COMPENSATION OF MUNICIPAL VICE-MAYORS AND COUNCILORS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

In lieu of _per diem_ s, the municipal council may grant to each Vice-Mayor and councilor an annual salary not to exceed the following:

| Class of Municipality | Salary per annum |
|---|---|
| First Class—1 | ₱4,800.00 |
| First Class—2 | 4,500.00 |
| First Class—3 | 4,200.00 |
| First Class—4 | 3,900.00 |
| First Class—5 | 3,600.00 |
| First Class—6 | 3,300.00 |
| Second Class | 3,000.00 |
| Third Class | 2,400.00 |

## Section 2

Municipalities of the fourth, fifth, sixth and seventh classes shall not be covered by the provisions of this Act: _Provided, however,_ That the Municipal Councils of the municipalities belonging to these classes may grant to each Vice-Mayor and Councilor a _per diem_ not exceeding fifteen pesos for every day of regular or special session of the Council actually attended by them: _Provided,_ finally, That no _per diem_ may be granted for more than two special sessions a month.

## Section 3

All Acts, executive orders and administrative orders, or parts thereof, which are inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, August 4, 1969.
