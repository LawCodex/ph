# Presidential Decree No. 787

**Renaming the Batangas School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Province of Batangas, the Apolinario R. Apacible School of Fisheries**

MALACAÑANG  
M a n i l a

**PRESIDENTIAL DECREE No. 787 August 30, 1975**

RENAMING THE BATANGAS SCHOOL OF FISHERIES IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF NASUGBU, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, THE APOLINARIO R. APACIBLE SCHOOL OF FISHERIES

WHEREAS, the Parent Teachers Association of the Batangas School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Batangas, strongly recommended the renaming of the school to the Apolinario R. Apacible School of Fisheries;

WHEREAS, it is the earnest desire of the people in the service area of the school to perpetuate the memory of the late Congressman Apolinario R. Apacible, whose dedicated concern for public education made possible the establishment of the school 1956;

WHEREAS, the most fitting and proper way to acknowledge such a debt of gratitude is for the school to be named after him and thus keep his memory alive;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution, do hereby order and decree:

## Section 1

The Batangas School of Fisheries at Nasugbu, Batangas is hereby named the APOLINARIO R. APACIBLE SCHOOL OF FISHERIES.

## Section 2

This Decree shall take effect upon its promulgation.

Done in the City of Manila, this 30th day of August, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-five.
