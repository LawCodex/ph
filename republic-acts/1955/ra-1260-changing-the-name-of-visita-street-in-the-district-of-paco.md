# Republic Act No. 1260

**An Act Changing the Name of Visita Street in the District of Paco, City of Manila to Burgos Street**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1260**

**An Act Changing the Name of Visita Street in the District of Paco, City of Manila to Burgos Street**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Visita Street in the District of Paco, City of Manila, is hereby changed to Burgos Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
