# Republic Act No. 7603

**An Act Changing the Name of the Malaguit Elementary School in Barangay Malaguit, Municipality of Paracale, Province of Camarines Norte, To Pedro V. Moreno Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7603, June 03, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MALAGUIT ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MALAGUIT, MUNICIPALITY OF PARACALE, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO PEDRO V. MORENO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Malaguit Elementary School in Barangay Malaguit, Municipality of Paracale, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Pedro V. Moreno Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
