# Republic Act No. 1717

**An Act Creating a Certain Barrio in the Municipality of Gingoog, Province of Misamis Oriental**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1717**

AN ACT CREATING A CERTAIN BARRIO IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GINGOOG, PROVINCE OF MISAMIS ORIENTAL.

## Section 1

The sitio of Malibod, Municipality of Gingoog, Province of Misamis Oriental, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Malibod.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957
