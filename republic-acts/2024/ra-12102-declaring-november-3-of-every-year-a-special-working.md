# Republic Act No. 12102

**An Act Declaring November 3 of Every Year A Special Working Holiday in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, To Be Known As the “Kalibo Foundation Day” ra2024/pdra_12102_2024.pdf**

Nineteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 12102, December 13, 2024 ]

AN ACT DECLARING NOVEMBER 3 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF KALIBO, PROVINCE OF AKLAN, TO BE KNOWN AS THE "KALIBO FOUNDATION DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

November 3 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, to be known as the "Kalibo Foundation Day". Upon proclamation of the President, November 3 of every year shall be a special non-working holiday in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan.ℒαwρhi৷

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
