# Republic Act No. 11135

**An Act Declaring October 28 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Davao Occidental in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11135**

AN ACT DECLARING OCTOBER 28 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF DAVAO OCCIDENTAL IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

October 28 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Davao Occidental in commemoration of its founding anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

**GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
Speaker of the House of Representatives

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on May 15, 2017 and September 24, 2018, respectively.
