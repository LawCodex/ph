# Republic Act No. 2520

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Libog, Province of Albay, to Municipality of Sto. Domingo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2520**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Libog, Province of Albay, to Municipality of Sto. Domingo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Municipality of Libog, Province of Albay, shall hereafter be known as the Municipality of Sto. Domingo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
