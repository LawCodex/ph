# Batas Pambansa Blg. 268

**An Act Converting the Belison Barangay High School in the Municipality of Belison, Province of Antique, Into a National School, to Be Known as the Belison National School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 268**

An Act Converting the Belison Barangay High School in the Municipality of Belison, Province of Antique, into a National School, to be Known as the Belison National School

## Section 1

The Belison Barangay High School in the Municipality of Belison, Province of Antique, is converted into a national school, to be known as the Belison National School.

## Section 2

The amount of one hundred thousand pesos for the operation of the school shall be funded out of the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 131 for the Ministry of Education and Culture intended for new national schools. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 13, 1982
