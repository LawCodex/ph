# Act No. 2434

**An Act to Return From the Friar Lands Sinking Fund to the General Unappropriated Surplus of the Insular Government the Sum of One Million three Hundred thousand Pesos as a Partial Reimbursement of the Amounts Appropriated From Said Surplus for Said Sinking Fund.**

[ Act No. 2434, December 23, 1914 ]

AN ACT TO RETURN FROM THE FRIAR LANDS SINKING FUND TO THE GENERAL UNAPPROPRIATED SURPLUS OF THE INSULAR GOVERNMENT THE SUM OF ONE MILLION THREE HUNDRED THOUSAND PESOS AS A PARTIAL REIMBURSEMENT OF THE AMOUNTS APPROPRIATED FROM SAID SURPLUS FOR SAID SINKING FUND.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The sum of one million three hundred thousand pesos is hereby returned from the friar lands sinking fund to the general unappropriated surplus of the Insular Government, as a partial reimbursement of the amounts heretofore appropriated for said sinking fund from said general unappropriated surplus.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, December 23, 1914.
