# Republic Act No. 5286

**An Act Creating Four Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal for the Province of Occidental Misamis, Amending for the Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5286**

An Act Creating Four Additional Positions of Assistant Provincial Fiscal for the Province of Occidental Misamis, Amending for the Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Administrative Code

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The provisions of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Administrative Code, as amended, relative to the number of assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Occidental Misamis, is further amended to read as follows:

"Occidental Misamis, six assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
