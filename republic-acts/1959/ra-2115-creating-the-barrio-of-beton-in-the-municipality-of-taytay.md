# Republic Act No. 2115

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Beton in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2115**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Beton in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Island of Icaduang Banua in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan, is constituted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Beton.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
