# Act No. 3398

**An Act Giving the Name of Florentino Torres to a street of Manila.**

[ Act No. 3398, December 05, 1927 ]

AN ACT GIVING THE NAME OF FLORENTINO TORRES TO A STREET OF MANILA.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the rent authority of the same:

## Section 1

In order to perpetuate the memory of the illustrious magistrate Florentino Torres, the street now called Almanza, a name which bears no recollections for the Filipinos, shall henceforth be known as Florentino Torres Street.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 5, 1927.
