# Republic Act No. 7554

**An Act To Honor and Perpetuate the Memory of the Late Rafael M. Salas By Naming the 7.9-km. Strip of the National Highway, Commencing At Sitio Camates, Barangay Sampinit, Situated At Km. 18 + 400, Bago City, Up To the Boundary of the Adjacent Municipality of Pulupandan, Situated At Km. 26 + 300, Province of Negros Occidental, As the Rafael M. Salas Drive - "The Gateway To Southern Negros"**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7554, May 26, 1992 ]

AN ACT TO HONOR AND PERPETUATE THE MEMORY OF THE LATE RAFAEL M. SALAS BY NAMING THE 7.9-KM. STRIP OF THE NATIONAL HIGHWAY, COMMENCING AT SITIO CAMATES, BARANGAY SAMPINIT, SITUATED AT KM. 18 + 400, BAGO CITY, UP TO THE BOUNDARY OF THE ADJACENT MUNICIPALITY OF PULUPANDAN, SITUATED AT KM. 26 + 300, PROVINCE OF NEGROS OCCIDENTAL, AS THE RAFAEL M. SALAS DRIVE - "THE GATEWAY TO SOUTHERN NEGROS"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The 7.9-km. strip of the national highway, commencing at Km. 18 + 400, Sitio Camates, Barangay Sampinit, Bago City, up to the boundary of the adjacent Municipality of Pulupandan, situated at Km. 26 + 300, Province of Negros Occidental, shall be known as the Rafael M. Salas Drive "The Gateway to Southern Negros."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
