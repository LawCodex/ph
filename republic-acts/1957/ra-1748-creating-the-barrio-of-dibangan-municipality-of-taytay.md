# Republic Act No. 1748

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Dibangan, Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1748**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Dibangan, Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Island of Dibangan is constituted into a barrio in the Municipality of Taytay, Province of Palawan, to be known as the barrio of Dibangan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
