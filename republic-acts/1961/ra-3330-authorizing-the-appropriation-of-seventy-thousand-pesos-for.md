# Republic Act No. 3330

**An Act Authorizing the Appropriation of Seventy Thousand Pesos for the Acquisition of a School Site for the Tanauan School of Fisheries and for the Improvement Thereof**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3330**

**An Act Authorizing the Appropriation of Seventy Thousand Pesos for the Acquisition of a School Site for the Tanauan School of Fisheries and for the Improvement Thereof**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sum of seventy thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the acquisition of a school site for the Tanauan School of Fisheries in Ambulong, Tanauan, Province of Batangas, established under Republic Act Numbered Fifteen hundred fifty-nine, and for the improvement thereof.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
