# Republic Act No. 2748

**An Act Amending Item (5), Under “d. Bataan,” Paragraph 2, Title G, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2748**

**An Act Amending Item (5), Under "d. Bataan," Paragraph 2, Title G, Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (5) under "d. Bataan," paragraph 2, Title G, Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(5) Abucay Communal Irrigation System, (Labangan River)—Construction of diversion works ₱15,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
