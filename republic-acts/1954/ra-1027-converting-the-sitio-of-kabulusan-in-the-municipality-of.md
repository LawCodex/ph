# Republic Act No. 1027

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kabulusan in the Municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Kabulusan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1027**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Kabulusan in the Municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, to a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Kabulusan**

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:_

## Section 1

The sitio of Kabulusan in the municipality of Pakil, Province of Laguna, is hereby converted to a barrio to be known as barrio Kabulusan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 12, 1954.
