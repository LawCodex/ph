# Batas Pambansa Blg. 326

**An Act Changing the Name of Nagsangalan Elementary School in Barangay Talogtog, Municipality of Dolores, Province of Abra, to Don Rosalio Eduarte Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 326**

An Act Changing the Name of Nagsangalan Elementary School in Barangay Talogtog, Municipality of Dolores, Province of Abra, to Don Rosalio Eduarte Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Nagsangalan Elementary School in Barangay Talogtog, Municipality of Dolores, Province of Abra, is changed to Don Rosalio Eduarte Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
