# Batas Pambansa Blg. 756

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 756**

An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan

## Section 1

The names of the San Antonio Elementary School and the Minanga Elementary School, both in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan, are changed to Aparri North Central School and Aparri South Central School, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
