# Republic Act No. 5599

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pundaquit in Barrio San Miguel, Municipality of San Antonio, Province of Zambales, Into a Barrio**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5599, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIO OF PUNDAQUIT IN BARRIO SAN MIGUEL, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN ANTONIO, PROVINCE OF ZAMBALES, INTO A BARRIO.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pundaquit in Barrio San Miguel, Municipality of San Antonio, Province of Zambales is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Pundaquit.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
