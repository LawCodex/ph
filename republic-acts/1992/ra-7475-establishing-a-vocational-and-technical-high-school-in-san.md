# Republic Act No. 7475

**An Act Establishing A Vocational and Technical High School in San Aquilino, Municipality of Roxas, Province of Oriental Mindoro, To Be Known As the Marcelo I. Cabrera Vocational High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Fifth Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, nineteen hundred and ninety-one..

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7475, May 05, 1992 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A VOCATIONAL AND TECHNICAL HIGH SCHOOL IN SAN AQUILINO, MUNICIPALITY OF ROXAS, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE MARCELO I. CABRERA VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a vocational and technical high school in San Aquilino, Municipality of Roxas, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to be known as the Marcelo I. Cabrera Vocational High School.
