# Republic Act No. 5658

**An Act Declaring the Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, a Permanent Subport**

>

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5658, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT DECLARING THE MUNICIPALITY OF MAUBAN, PROVINCE OF QUEZON, A PERMANENT SUBPORT.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, is hereby declared a permanent subport.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
