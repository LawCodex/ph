# Commonwealth Act No. 200

**An Act Granting Sick Leave With Pay to Members of the Crew of Any Vessel of the Bureau of Coast and Geodetic Survey.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 200, November 18, 1936 ]

AN ACT GRANTING SICK LEAVE WITH PAY TO MEMBERS OF THE CREW OF ANY VESSEL OF THE BUREAU OF COAST AND GEODETIC SURVEY.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

Any provision of existing law to the contrary notwithstanding, the members of the crew of any vessel of the Bureau of Coast and Geodetic Survey shall be 1 entitled to pay for not more than sixty days when they are sick in a hospital, in accordance with the United States regulations for Coast and Geodetic Survey vessels.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on January first, nineteen hundred and thirty-seven.

Approved, November 18, 1936.
