# Republic Act No. 2813

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Toril in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2813**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Toril in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Toril in the Municipality of Maribojoc, Province of Bohol, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Toril.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
