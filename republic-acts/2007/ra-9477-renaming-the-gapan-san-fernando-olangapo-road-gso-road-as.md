# Republic Act No. 9477

**An Act Renaming the Gapan - San Fernando - Olangapo Road (GSO Road) as Jose Abad Santos Avenue (JASA)**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9477 May 22, 2007**

AN ACT RENAMING THE GAPAN - SAN FERNANDO - OLONGAPO ROAD (GSO ROAD) AS JOSE ABAD SANTOS AVENUE (JASA)

_Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled_ :

## SECTION 1

The Gapan - San Fernando- Olongapo Road (GSO Road) is hereby renamed as Jose Abad Santos Avenue (JASA) .

## SEC. 2

All laws, decrees, orders and issuances or parts thereof inconsistent herewith are hereby repealed or modified accordingly.

## SEC. 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
