# Republic Act No. 1736

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Santa Rosa, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, to Santo Tomas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1736**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Santa Rosa, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, to Santo Tomas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Santa Rosa, Municipality of Passi, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Santo Tomas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
