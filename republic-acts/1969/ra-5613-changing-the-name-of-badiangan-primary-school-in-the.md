# Republic Act No. 5613

**An Act Changing the Name of Badiangan Primary School in the Municipality of Patnogon, Province of Antique, to Telesforo De La Cruz Community School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5613, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BADIANGAN PRIMARY SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PATNOGON, PROVINCE OF ANTIQUE, TO TELESFORO DE LA CRUZ COMMUNITY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Badiangan Primary School in the Municipality of Patnogon, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Telesforo de la Cruz; Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved, June 21, 1969.
