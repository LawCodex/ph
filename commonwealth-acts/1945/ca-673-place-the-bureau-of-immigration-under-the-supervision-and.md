# Commonwealth Act No. 673

**An Act to Place the Bureau of Immigration Under the Supervision and Control of the Department of Labor.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 673, July 24, 1945 ]

AN ACT TO PLACE THE BUREAU OF IMMIGRATION UNDER THE SUPERVISION AND CONTROL OF THE DEPARTMENT OF LABOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Bureau of Immigration shall be under the supervision and control of the Department of Labor.

## Section 2

This Act repeals Executive Ordered Numbered Three hundred of the President of the Philippines, dated September three, nineteen hundred and forty, and all other orders ort acts inconsistent herewith.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, July 24, 1945.
