# Republic Act No. 2095

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Balud in the Municipality of Salcedo, Province of Samar, into a Barrio, to be Known as the Barrio of Balud**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2095**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Balud in the Municipality of Salcedo, Province of Samar, into a Barrio, to be Known as the Barrio of Balud**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Balud in the Municipality of Salcedo, Province of Samar, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Balud.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 22, 1959.
