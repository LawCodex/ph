# Republic Act No. 4394

**An Act to Amend Item 17, Page 213, Paragraph 29, Leyte, Title D of Republic Act Numbered Thirty-One Hundred One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4394**

An Act to Amend Item 17, Page 213, Paragraph 29, Leyte, Title D of Republic Act Numbered Thirty-One Hundred One

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 17, page 213, paragraph 29, Leyte, Title D of Republic Act Thirty-one hundred one is amended to read as follows:

"17. Barrio Mada-o Waterworks, Kawayan 6,000’

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
