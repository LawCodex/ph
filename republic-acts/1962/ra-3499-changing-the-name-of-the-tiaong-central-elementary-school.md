# Republic Act No. 3499

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tiaong Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, to Claro M. Recto Memorial Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3499**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tiaong Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, to Claro M. Recto Memorial Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tiaong Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Tiaong, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Claro M. Recto Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
