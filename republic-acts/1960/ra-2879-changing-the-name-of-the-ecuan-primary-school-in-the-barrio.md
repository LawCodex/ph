# Republic Act No. 2879

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ecuan Primary School in the Barrio of Golictop, Municipality of Dumingag, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to Golictop Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2879**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Ecuan Primary School in the Barrio of Golictop, Municipality of Dumingag, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, to Golictop Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Ecuan Primary School in the barrio of Golictop, Municipality of Dumingag, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Golitop Primay School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
