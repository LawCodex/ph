# Republic Act No. 1692

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tubog in the Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, to San Gabriel**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1692**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Tubog in the Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, to San Gabriel**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Tubog in the Municipality of Mauban, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to San Gabriel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
