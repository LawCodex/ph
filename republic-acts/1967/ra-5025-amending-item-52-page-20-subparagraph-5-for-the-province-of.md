# Republic Act No. 5025

**An Act Amending Item (52), Page 20, Subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, Paragraph (c), Title A, Section Two, of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Three Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5025**

An Act Amending Item (52), Page 20, Subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, Paragraph (c), Title A, Section Two, of Republic Act Numbered Twenty Three Hundred and One

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (52), Page 20, Subparagraph 5, for the Province of Antique, Paragraph (c), Title A, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and one is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(52) San Francisco Elementary School Home Economics Building, Aniniy ₱5,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
