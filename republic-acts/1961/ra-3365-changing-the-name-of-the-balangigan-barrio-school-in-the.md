# Republic Act No. 3365

**An Act Changing the Name of the Balangigan Barrio School in the Municipality of Duenas, Province of Iloilo, to Biba Barrio School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3365**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Balangigan Barrio School in the Municipality of Duenas, Province of Iloilo, to Biba Barrio School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Balangigan Barrio School in the Municipality of Duenas, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Biba Barrio School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
