# Batas Pambansa Blg. 117

**An Act Naming the Court of First Instance Building in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, the Godofredo P. Ramos Hall of Justice.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 117**

An Act Naming the Court of First Instance Building in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, the Godofredo P. Ramos Hall of Justice

## Section 1

The Court of First Instance Building in the Municipality of Kalibo, Province of Aklan, is hereby named the Godofredo P. Ramos Hall of Justice.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 21, 1981
