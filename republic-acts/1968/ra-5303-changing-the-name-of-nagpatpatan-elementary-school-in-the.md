# Republic Act No. 5303

**An Act Changing the Name of Nagpatpatan Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Hilario Valdez Memorial Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5303**

An Act Changing the Name of Nagpatpatan Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Hilario Valdez Memorial Community School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Nagpatpatan Elementary School in the Municipality of Solsona, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Hilario Valdez Memorial Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
