# Republic Act No. 7495

**An Act Changing the Name of the Namuac Elementary School in Barangay San Andres, Municipality of Sanchez Mira, Province of Cagayan, To Namuac-san Andres Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7495, May 13, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE NAMUAC ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SAN ANDRES, MUNICIPALITY OF SANCHEZ MIRA, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, TO NAMUAC-SAN ANDRES ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Namuac Elementary School in Barangay San Andres, Municipality of Sanchez, Mira, Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to Namuac-San Andres Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
