# Republic Act No. 2760

**An Act to Amend Item (67), Page 820, Subparagraph (av), for the Province of Sorsogon, Paragraph b, Title A, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2760**

**An Act to Amend Item (67), Page 820, Subparagraph (av), for the Province of Sorsogon, Paragraph b, Title A, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (67), page 820, subparagraph (av), for the Province of Sorsogon, paragraph b, Title A, Section three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(67) Construction of, fencing and improvement of site for, Industrial Arts Bldg., Basud Barrio School, Sorsogon ₱10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
