# Executive Order No. 501

**Further Amending Executive Order No. 318, Dated May 25, 1950, Creating the Integrity Board**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 501, May 07, 1952 ]

FURTHER AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 318, DATED MAY 25, 1950, CREATING THE INTEGRITY BOARD.

By virtue of the powers vested in me by law, I, ELPIDIO QUIRINO, President of the Philippines, do hereby further amend Executive Order No. 318, dated May 25, 1950, creating the Integrity Board, as amended so as to have said body composed of the following:

| Justice Luis P. Torres | Chairman |
|---|---|
| Justice Mariano H. de Joya | Member |
| Honorable Jose P. Bengzon | Member |
| Judge Pompeyo Diaz | Member |
| Mrs. Pilar Hidalgo Lim | Member |
| Dr. Jose A. de Jesus Executive | Secretary |

Done in the City of Manila, this 7th day of May, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-two, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the sixth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **MARCIANO ROQUE**
Acting Executive Secretary
