# Batas Pambansa Blg. 737

**An Act Changing the Name of Urdaneta General Hospital in the Municipality of Urdaneta, Province of Pangasinan, to Amadeo J. Perez, Sr. Memorial General Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 737**

An Act Changing the Name of Urdaneta General Hospital in the Municipality of Urdaneta, Province of Pangasinan, to Amadeo J. Perez, Sr. Memorial General Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Urdaneta General Hospital in the Municipality of Urdaneta, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Amadeo J. Perez, Sr. Memorial General Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
