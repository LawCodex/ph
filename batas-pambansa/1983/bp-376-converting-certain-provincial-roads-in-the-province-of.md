# Batas Pambansa Blg. 376

**An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Iloilo Into National Roads.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 376**

An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Iloilo into National Roads

## Section 1

The following provincial roads in the province of Iloilo are converted into and declared as national roads:

a) Concepcion-San Dionisio Road

b) Ajuy-Fuente Bunglas-Jamulawon Road

c) Passi-San Enrique-Garita Road

## Section 2

Any provision of law, decree, order, rule or regulation inconsistent with this Act is repealed or modified accordingly.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1983
