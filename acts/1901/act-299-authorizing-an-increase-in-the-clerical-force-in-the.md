# Act No. 299

**An Act Authorizing An Increase in the Clerical Force in the Offices of the Sheriff and City Attorney of the City of Manila.**

[ Act No. 299, November 11, 1901 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING AN INCREASE IN THE CLERICAL FORCE IN THE OFFICES OF THE SHERIFF AND CITY ATTORNEY OF THE CITY OF MANILA.

By authority of the President of the United States, be it enacted by the United States Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The following additional employee's are hereby authorized in the office of the sheriff of the city of Manila: One clerk of Class D, two deputies to net as bailiffs for the courts of justices of the peace, at a monthly salary of fifteen dollars each, and two bailiffs of Class K, to attend upon the Courts of First Instance.

## Section 2

The following additional employees are hereby authorized in the office of the City Attorney of the city of Manila: One clerk of class nine and one clerk of Class C.

## Section 3

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, November 11, 1901.
