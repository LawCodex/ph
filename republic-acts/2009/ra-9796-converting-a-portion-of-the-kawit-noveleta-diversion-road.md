# Republic Act No. 9796

**An Act Converting a Portion of the Kawit-Noveleta Diversion Road, Province of Cavite into a National Road**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9796**

AN ACT CONVERTING A PORTION OF THE KAWIT-NOVELETA DIVERSION ROAD, PROVINCE OF CAVITE INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

A portion of the Kawit-Noveleta Diversion Road servicing the Cavite Economic Zone from kilometer 23+400 to kilometer 27+225 with a total length of 3.825 kilometers in the Province of Cavite is hereby converted into a national road.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.1avvphi1

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.
