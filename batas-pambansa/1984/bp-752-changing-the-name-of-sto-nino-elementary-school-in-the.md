# Batas Pambansa Blg. 752

**An Act Changing the Name of sto. Niño Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Badal Yuba Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 752**

An Act Changing the Name of Sto. Niño Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Badal Yuba Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Sto. Niño Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Badal Yuba Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
