# Republic Act No. 2676

**An Act Making the Suribao River the Boundary Between the Municipalities of Borongan and Maydolong, Province of Samar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2676**

**An Act Making the Suribao River the Boundary Between the Municipalities of Borongan and Maydolong, Province of Samar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Suribao River is hereby made the boundary between the municipalities of Borongan and Maydolong, Province of Samar.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1960.
