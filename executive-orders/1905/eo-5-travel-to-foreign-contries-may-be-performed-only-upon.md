# Executive Order No. 5

**Travel to Foreign Contries may be performed only upon authority of the Governor-General**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER No. 5**

Hereafter, unless otherwise provided by law, travel to foreign countries and the United States on official business or on special assignments in connection with investigations or study which may be advantage to the Government, may be performed only upon authority of the Governor-General.

**NEWTON W. GILBERT**
_Acting Governor-General_
