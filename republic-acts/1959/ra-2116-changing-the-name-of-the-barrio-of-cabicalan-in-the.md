# Republic Act No. 2116

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cabicalan in the Municipal District of Potia, Ifugao, Mountain Province, to Barrio San Domingo**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2116**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cabicalan in the Municipal District of Potia, Ifugao, Mountain Province, to Barrio San Domingo**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Cabicalan in the Municipal District of Potia, Ifugao, Mountain Province, is changed to barrio San Domingo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 5, 1959.
