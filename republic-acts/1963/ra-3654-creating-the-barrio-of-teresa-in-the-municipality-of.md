# Republic Act No. 3654

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Teresa in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3654**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Teresa in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Catubangan, Bato-Bato and Pinagdogoan in the Municipality of Aborlan, Province of Palawan, are constituted into a district and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the Barrio of Teresa.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
