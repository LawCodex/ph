# Republic Act No. 6584

**An Act Establishing An Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Victoria, Province of Tarlac, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6584, September 30, 1972 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN EMERGENCY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF VICTORIA, PROVINCE OF TARLAC, AND AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS THEREFOR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Director of Medical Services, an emergency hospital in the Municipality of Victoria, Province of Tarlac.

## Section 2

The sum of four hundred thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the establishment, operation and maintenance of said hospital during the fiscal year nineteen hundred seventy-two. Thereafter, such sums as may be needed for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without executive approval, September 30, 1972.
