# Republic Act No. 5623

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cogon Elementary School in Barrio San Isidro, Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Davao Bel Sur, San Isidro Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5623, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE COGON ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARRIO SAN ISIDRO, MUNICIPALITY OF SANTA MARIA, PROVINCE OF DAVAO BEL SUR, SAN ISIDRO ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cogon Elementary School in Barrio San Isidro, Municipality of Santa Maria, Province of Davao del Sur, is hereby changed to San Isidro Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
