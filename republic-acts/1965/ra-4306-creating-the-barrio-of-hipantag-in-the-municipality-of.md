# Republic Act No. 4306

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Hipantag in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4306**

An Act Creating the Barrio of Hipantag in the Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Hipantag in the barrio of Benit, Municipality of Sogod, Province of Southern Leyte, is separated from said barrio and constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Hipantag.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
