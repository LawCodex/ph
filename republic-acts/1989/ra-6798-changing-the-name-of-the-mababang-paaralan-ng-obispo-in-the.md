# Republic Act No. 6798

**An Act Changing the Name of the Mababang Paaralan Ng Obispo in the Municipality of Tuy, Province of Batangas, to Mababang Paaralang Gregorio Agoncillo**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MABABANG PAARALAN NG OBISPO IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TUY, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS, TO MABABANG PAARALANG GREGORIO AGONCILLO

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Mababang Paaralan ng Obispo in the Municipality of Tuy, Province of Batangas, is hereby changed to Mababang Paaralang Gregorio Agoncillo.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
