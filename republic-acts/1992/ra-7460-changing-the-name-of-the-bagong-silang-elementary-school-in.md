# Republic Act No. 7460

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bagong Silang Elementary School in Barangay Bagong Silang, Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, To Felix Asis Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7460, April 28, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BAGONG SILANG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BAGONG SILANG, MUNICIPALITY OF LABO, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES NORTE, TO FELIX ASIS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

**Section 1. 1âшphi1** The name of the Bagong Silang Elementary School in Barangay Bagong Silang, Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Felix Asis Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
