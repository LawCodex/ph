# Republic Act No. 7286

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Provincial Hospital of South Cotabato From One Hundred (100) to One Hundred Twenty-five (125) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7286, March 24, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL OF SOUTH COTABATO FROM ONE HUNDRED (100) TO ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-FIVE (125) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The provincial hospital of South Cotabato located in the capital town of Koronadal is hereby expanded from one hundred (100) to one hundred twenty-five (125) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
