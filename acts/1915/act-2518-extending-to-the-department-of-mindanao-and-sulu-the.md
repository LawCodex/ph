# Act No. 2518

**An Act Extending- to the Department of Mindanao and Sulu the Provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-Four Hundred and Ninety-Five, Entitled "An Act to Amend Certain Sections of Act Numbered Twenty-three Hundred and Forty-Seven, Entitled 'An Act to Provide for the Reorganization of the Courts of First Instance and of the Court of Land Registration,' By Changing the Dates of the Terms, the Seats, and the Jurisdiction of Certain Courts, and for Other Purposes."**

[ Act No. 2518, February 26, 1915 ]

AN ACT EXTENDING- TO THE DEPARTMENT OF MINDANAO AND SULU THE PROVISIONS OF ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-FOUR HUNDRED AND NINETY-FIVE, ENTITLED "AN ACT TO AMEND CERTAIN SECTIONS OF ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN, ENTITLED 'AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE REORGANIZATION OF THE COURTS OF FIRST INSTANCE AND OF THE COURT OF LAND REGISTRATION,' BY CHANGING THE DATES OF THE TERMS, THE SEATS, AND THE JURISDICTION OF CERTAIN COURTS, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES."

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-four hundred and ninety-five, entitled "An Act to amend certain sections of Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and forty-seven, entitled 'An Act to provide for the reorganization of the Courts of First Instance and of the Court of Land Registration,' by changing the dates of the terms, the seats, and the jurisdiction of certain courts, and for other purposes," are hereby made applicable to the Department of Mindanao and Sulu.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, February 26, 1915.
