# Republic Act No. 6841

**An Act Changing the Name of the Manggahan Elementary School Unit I in the Municipality of Pasig, Metro Manila, to San Lorenzo Ruiz Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6841

An Act Changing the Name of the Manggahan Elementary School Unit I in the Municipality of Pasig, Metro Manila, to San Lorenzo Ruiz Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Manggahan Elementary School Unit I in the Municipality of Pasig, Metro Manila, is hereby changed to San Lorenzo Ruiz Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
