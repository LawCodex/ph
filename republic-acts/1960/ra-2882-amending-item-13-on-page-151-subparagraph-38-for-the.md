# Republic Act No. 2882

**An Act Amending Item (13) on Page 151, Subparagraph 38 for the Province of Negros Oriental, Under Paragraph c, Title a of Section One of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2882**

AN ACT AMENDING ITEM (13) ON PAGE 151, SUBPARAGRAPH 38 FOR THE PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL, UNDER PARAGRAPH C, TITLE A OF SECTION ONE OF REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-THREE HUNDRED AND ONE.

## Section 1

Item (13) on page 151, subparagraph 38 for the Province of Negros Oriental, under paragraph c, Title A of Section one of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and one is amended to read as follows:

"(13) Pacuan Elementary School Building,

La Libertad P10,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval, June 19, 1960.
