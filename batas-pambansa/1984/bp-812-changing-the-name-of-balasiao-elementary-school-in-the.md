# Batas Pambansa Blg. 812

**An Act Changing the Name of Balasiao Elementary School in the Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Isabelo Calma, Sr. Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 812**

An Act Changing the Name of Balasiao Elementary School in the Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Isabelo Calma, Sr. Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Balasiao Elementary School, in Barangay Balasiao, Municipality of Kiblawan, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Isabelo Calma, Sr. Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
