# Republic Act No. 1638

**An Act Changing the Name of Daculang-Bolo Barrio School in the Barrio of Daculang-Bolo, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, to Salvacion Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1638**

**An Act Changing the Name of Daculang-Bolo Barrio School in the Barrio of Daculang-Bolo, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, to Salvacion Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Daculang-bolo Barrio School in the barrio of Daculang-bolo, Municipality of Daet, Province of Camarines Norte, is changed to Salvacion Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
