# Act No. 601

**An Act Authorizing the Provincial Board of Cavite to Divert the Sum of Four thousand Four Hundred Dollars, United states Currency, From the Funds By Law Devoted to the Improvement of Roads and Bridges, for General Provincial Expenses.**

[ Act No. 601, January 27, 1903 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE PROVINCIAL BOARD OF CAVITE TO DIVERT THE SUM OF FOUR THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED DOLLARS, UNITED STATES CURRENCY, FROM THE FUNDS BY LAW DEVOTED TO THE IMPROVEMENT OF ROADS AND BRIDGES, FOR GENERAL PROVINCIAL EXPENSES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The provincial board of the Province of Cavite is hereby authorized to divert from funds required by law to be devoted to the improvement of roads and bridges a sum not exceeding four thousand four hundred dollars, United States currency, to be spent for general provincial purposes, anything in Act Numbered Three hundred and eighty-one to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, January 27, 1903.
