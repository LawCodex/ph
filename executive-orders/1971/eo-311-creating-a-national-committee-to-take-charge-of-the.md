# Executive Order No. 311

**Creating a National Committee to Take Charge of the Celebrations of Philippine Independence Day on June 12, 1971**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 311, April 23, 1971 ]

CREATING A NATIONAL COMMITTEE TO TAKE CHARGE OF THE CELEBRATIONS OF PHILIPPINE INDEPENDENCE DAY ON JUNE 12, 1971

Pursuant to the powers vested in me by law, I, **FERDINAND E. MARCOS** , President of the Philippines, do hereby create a national committee to take charge of the celebration of Philippine Independence Day on June 12, 1971.

The Committee shall be composed of the following:

| The Secretary of Labor | Chairman |
|---|---|
| The Secretary of Education | Vice-Chairman |
| The Commissioner of the Budget | Member |
| The Press Secretary | Member |
| The Commissioner of the National Integration Commission | Member |
| The Undersecretary of Public Works | Member |
| Undersecretary of National Defense | Member |
| The Undersecretary of Commerce | Member |
| The Assistant Executive Secretary (Local Governments) | Member |
| The Deputy Administrator of Economic Coordination | Member |
| The Mayor of Manila | Member |
| The Chairman, National Historical Commission | Member |
| The President, Philippine Association of Colleges and Universities | Member |
| The President, Veterans Federation of the Philippines | Member |
| The President, Civic Assembly of Woman of the Philippines (CAWP) | Member |
| The President, Chamber of Commerce of the Philippines | Member |
| Representative from Youth Group | Member |
| The Presidential Protocol Officer | Member-Secretary |

The Committee shall meet at the call of the Chairman and for the purpose of discharging its functions, may create such sub-committees as may be necessary.

The Committee is hereby authorized to call upon any department, bureau, office, agency or instrumentality of the government, including government-owned or controlled corporations, for such assistance as it may need in the discharge of its duties and functions.

Done in the City of Manila, this 23rd day of April, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-one.

(SGD.) **FERDINAND E. MARCOS**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **FLORES A. BAYOT**
Acting Executive Secretary
