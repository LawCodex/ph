# Act No. 3863

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Talingting, Subprovince of Siquijor, Province of Oriental Negros, to that of Enrique VIllanueva.**

[ Act No. 3863, November 13, 1931 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF TALINGTING, SUBPROVINCE OF SIQUIJOR, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL NEGROS, TO THAT OF ENRIQUE VILLANUEVA.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The name of the municipality of Talingting, Subprovince of Siquijor, Province of Oriental Negros, is hereby changed to that of Enrique Villanueva.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 13, 1931.
