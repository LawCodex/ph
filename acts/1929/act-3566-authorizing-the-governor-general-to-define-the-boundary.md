# Act No. 3566

**An Act Authorizing the Governor-General to Define the Boundary Between the Mountain Province and the Province of Cagayan in Pursuance of Act Twenty-Seven Hundred and Eleven, Known as the Administrative Code, as Amended By Act Numbered Twenty-Nine Hundred and Twenty-Nine.**

[ Act No. 3566, November 26, 1929 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE GOVERNOR-GENERAL TO DEFINE THE BOUNDARY BETWEEN THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE AND THE PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN IN PURSUANCE OF ACT TWENTY-SEVEN HUNDRED AND ELEVEN, KNOWN AS THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE, AS AMENDED BY ACT NUMBERED TWENTY-NINE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

For the purpose of settling the long standing territorial dispute between the Mountain Province and the Province of Cagayan and in pursuance of section sixty-eight of Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and eleven, known as the Administrative Code, as amended by Act Numbered Twenty-nine hundred and twenty-nine, the Governor-General is hereby authorized to fix and define the boundary between said provinces in accordance with the mutual agreement between the respective provincial boards of said provinces.

## Section 2

All acts or parts of acts inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 26, 1929.
