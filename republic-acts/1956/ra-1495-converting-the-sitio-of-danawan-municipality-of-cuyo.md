# Republic Act No. 1495

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Danawan, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1495**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Danawan, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Danawan, Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Danawan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1956.
