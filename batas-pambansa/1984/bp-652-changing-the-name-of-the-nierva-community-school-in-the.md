# Batas Pambansa Blg. 652

**An Act Changing the Name of the Nierva Community School in the Municipality of Nabua, Province of Camarines Sur, to Cosme O. Follosco Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 652**

An Act Changing the Name of the Nierva Community School in the Municipality of Nabua, Province of Camarines Sur, to Cosme O. Follosco Memorial School

## Section 1

The Nierva Community School in the Municipality of Nabua, Province of Camarines Sur, shall hereafter be known as the Cosme O. Follosco Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
