# Republic Act No. 4786

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Numancia, Province of Surigao del Norte, to Del Carmen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4786**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Numancia, Province of Surigao del Norte, to Del Carmen

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Numancia in the Province of Surigao del Norte, is hereby changed to Del Carmen.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
