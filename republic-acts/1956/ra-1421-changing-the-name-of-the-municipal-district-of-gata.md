# Republic Act No. 1421

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Gata, Province of Lanao, to Municipal District of Poona Bayabao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1421**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipal District of Gata, Province of Lanao, to Municipal District of Poona Bayabao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipal District of Gata, Province of Lanao, is changed to Municipal District of Poona Bayabao.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1956.
