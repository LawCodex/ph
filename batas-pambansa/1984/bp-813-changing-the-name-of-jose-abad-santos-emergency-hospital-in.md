# Batas Pambansa Blg. 813

**An Act Changing the Name of Jose Abad Santos Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Tomas Lachica District Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 813**

An Act Changing the Name of Jose Abad Santos Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Tomas Lachica District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of Jose Abad Santos Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, is hereby changed to Tomas Lachica District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
