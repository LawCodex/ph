# Republic Act No. 4494

**An Act Creating Barrio Abaroan in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4494**

An Act Creating Barrio Abaroan in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sitios Baututo and Egruroan in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan are hereby constituted into a district and independent barrio of said municipality, to be known as Barrio Abaroan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
