# Act No. 4159

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Twenty thousand Pesos for the Purchase of Law Books, Legal Works, Publications, Etc., and of Library Equipment for the Department of Justice.**

[ Acts No. 4159, December 01, 1934 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TWENTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE PURCHASE OF LAW BOOKS, LEGAL WORKS, PUBLICATIONS, ETC., AND OF LIBRARY EQUIPMENT FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of twenty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury, not otherwise appropriated, to be spent at the discretion of the Secretary of Justice, for the purchase of law books, legal works, publications, etc., and of library equipment for the Department of Justice.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 1, 1934.
