# Republic Act No. 2020

**An Act Changing the Name of the Loakan Elementary School in the City of Baguio to Malvar Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2020**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Loakan Elementary School in the City of Baguio to Malvar Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Loakan Elementary School in the City of Baguio is hereby changed to Malvar Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
