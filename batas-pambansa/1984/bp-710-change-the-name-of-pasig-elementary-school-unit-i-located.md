# Batas Pambansa Blg. 710

**An Act to Change the Name of Pasig Elementary School Unit I Located At Kapitan Ato street, Municipality of Pasig, Metropolitan Manila, to Liberato Damian Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 710**

An Act to Change the Name of Pasig Elementary School Unit I Located at Kapitan Ato Street, Municipality of Pasig, Metropolitan Manila, to Liberato Damian Elementary School

## Section 1

Pasig Elementary School Unit I is located at Kapitan Ato Street, Municipality of Pasig, Metropolitan Manila, is hereby named Liberato Damian Elementary School in honor Liberato Damian, the "Cabeza de Barangay" of the area during the Spanish regime and early American occupation.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
