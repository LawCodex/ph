# Executive Order No. 333

**Reclassifying All Municipalities in the Philippines**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 333, May 28, 1959 ]

RECLASSIFYING ALL MUNICIPALITIES IN THE PHILIPPINES
