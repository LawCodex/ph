# Republic Act No. 11789

**An Act Converting the Extension Office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the Second District of the Province of Bohol Into a "class C" LTO District Office and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eighteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-sixth day of July, two thousand twenty one.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 11789, May 29, 2022 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE EXTENSION OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN THE SECOND DISTRICT OF THE PROVINCE OF BOHOL INTO A "CLASS C" LTO DISTRICT OFFICE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The existing Land Transportation Office (LTO) Extension Office in the Second District of the Province of Bohol is hereby converted into a "Class C" LTO District Office.

## Section 2

The Secretary of Transportation shall include in the Department's program the operationalization of a "Class C" LTO district office in the Second District of the Province of Bohol, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the Extension Office located in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for the continued operation of the "Class C" LTO district office shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.ℒαwρhi৷
