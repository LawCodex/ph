# Republic Act No. 2792

**An Act to Amend Item (aq), Subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, Page Two Hundred Fourteen, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2792**

**An Act to Amend Item (aq), Subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, Page Two Hundred Fourteen, Paragraph f, Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (aq), subparagraph (6), for the Province of Bataan, page two hundred fourteen, paragraph f, Section two of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(aq) Gumitna-Eskina road, Samal—Improvement ₱3,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1aшphi1

Approved: June 19, 1960.
