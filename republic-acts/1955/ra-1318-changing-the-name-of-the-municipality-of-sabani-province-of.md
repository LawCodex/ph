# Republic Act No. 1318

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Sabani, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Gabaldon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1318**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Sabani, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Gabaldon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Sabani, Province of Nueva Ecija, is hereby changed to Gabaldon.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
