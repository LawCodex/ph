# Republic Act No. 7542

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Hinunangan Community Hospital in the Municipality of Hinunangan, Province of Southern Leyte, From Ten (10) To Twenty-five (25) Beds, Upgrading It Into A District Hospital To Be Known As the Hinunangan District Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7542, May 22, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE HINUNANGAN COMMUNITY HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF HINUNANGAN, PROVINCE OF SOUTHERN LEYTE, FROM TEN (10) TO TWENTY-FIVE (25) BEDS, UPGRADING IT INTO A DISTRICT HOSPITAL TO BE KNOWN AS THE HINUNANGAN DISTRICT HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Hinunangan Community Hospital in the Municipality of Hinunangan, Province of Southern Leyte, is hereby increased from ten (10) to twenty-Five (25) beds and upgrading it into a district hospital to be known as the Hinunangan District Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
