# Republic Act No. 3687

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Catalina Elementary School in the Municipality of Lubao, Province of Pampanga, to Graciano Paule Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3687**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Sta. Catalina Elementary School in the Municipality of Lubao, Province of Pampanga, to Graciano Paule Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Sta. Catalina Elementary School in the Municipality of Lubao, Province of Pampanga, is changed to Graciano Paule Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
