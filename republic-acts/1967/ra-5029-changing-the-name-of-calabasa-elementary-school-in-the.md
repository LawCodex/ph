# Republic Act No. 5029

**An Act Changing the Name of Calabasa Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, to Paaralang Bayan ng Eugenio Dasco, Sr.**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5029**

An Act Changing the Name of Calabasa Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, to Paaralang Bayan ng Eugenio Dasco, Sr.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Calabasa Elementary School in the Municipality of Labo, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Paaralang Bayan ng Eugenio Dasco Sr.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
