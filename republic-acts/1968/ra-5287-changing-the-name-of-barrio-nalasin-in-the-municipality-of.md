# Republic Act No. 5287

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Nalasin in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to General Artemio Ricarte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5287**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Nalasin in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, to General Artemio Ricarte

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Nalasin in the Municipality of Batac, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to General Artemio Ricarte.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
