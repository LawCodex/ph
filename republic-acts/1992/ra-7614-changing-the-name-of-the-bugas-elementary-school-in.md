# Republic Act No. 7614

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bugas Elementary School in Barangay Manduyong, Municipality of Badian, Province of Cebu, To Manduyong Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7614, June 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BUGAS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MANDUYONG, MUNICIPALITY OF BADIAN, PROVINCE OF CEBU, TO MANDUYONG ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Bugas Elementary School in Barangay Manduyong, Municipality of Badian, Province of Cebu, is hereby changed to Manduyong Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
