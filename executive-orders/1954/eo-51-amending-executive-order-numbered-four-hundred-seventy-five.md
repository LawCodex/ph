# Executive Order No. 51

**Amending Executive Order Numbered Four Hundred Seventy-Five and for Other Purposes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 51, August 10, 1954 ]

AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED FOUR HUNDRED SEVENTY-FIVE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.
