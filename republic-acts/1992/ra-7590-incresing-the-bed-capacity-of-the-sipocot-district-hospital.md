# Republic Act No. 7590

**An Act Incresing the Bed Capacity of the Sipocot District Hospital in the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur, From Twenty-five (25) To Fifty (50) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7590, June 01, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCRESING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE SIPOCOT DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SIPOCOT, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, FROM TWENTY-FIVE (25) TO FIFTY (50) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative s of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Sipocot District Hospital in the Municipality of Sipocot, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby increased from twenty-five (25) to fifty (50) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriation of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 1, 1992
