# Batas Pambansa Blg. 887

**An Act Changing the Name of Pangpang Bridge Along the Antique-Aklan Road in the Province of Antique to Marcos Yee-Jonathan Gallo Memorial Bridge**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 887**

An Act Changing the Name of Pangpang Bridge Along the Antique-Aklan Road in the Province of Antique to Marcos Yee-Jonathan Gallo Memorial Bridge

## Section 1

The name of Pangpang Bridge along the Antique-Aklan Road in the Province of Antique is hereby changed to Marcos Yee-Jonathan Gallo Memorial Bridge.

## Section 2

The Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: December 3, 1985
