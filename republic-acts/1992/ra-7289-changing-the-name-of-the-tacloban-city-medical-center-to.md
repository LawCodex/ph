# Republic Act No. 7289

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tacloban City Medical Center to Eastern Visayas Regional Medical Center**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7289, March 24, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE TACLOBAN CITY MEDICAL CENTER TO EASTERN VISAYAS REGIONAL MEDICAL CENTER

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tacloban City Medical Center in Tacloban City is hereby changed to Eastern Visayas Regional Medical Center.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
