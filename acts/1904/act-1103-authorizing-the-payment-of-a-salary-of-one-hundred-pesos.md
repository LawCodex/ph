# Act No. 1103

**An Act Authorizing; the Payment of a Salary of One Hundred Pesos Per Month to the President of the Municipal Board of Health of Daet, Province of Ambos Camarines.**

[ Act No. 1103, April 04, 1904 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING; THE PAYMENT OF A SALARY OF ONE HUNDRED PESOS PER MONTH TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE MUNICIPAL BOARD OF HEALTH OF DAET, PROVINCE OF AMBOS CAMARINES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The payment of a salary of one hundred pesos per month to the president of the municipal, board of health of Daet, Province of Ambos Camarines, is hereby authorized, the provisions of Act Numbered Three hundred and eight to the contrary notwithstanding.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the passage of the same is hereby expedited in accordance; with section two of "An Act prescribing the order of procedure by the Commission in the enactment of laws," passed September twenty-sixth, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, April 4, 1904.
