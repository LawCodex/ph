# Commonwealth Act No. 339

**An Act Appropriating the Amount of One thousand Pesos for the Construction of the Isiri Elementary School, Calbiga, Samar.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 339, June 21, 1938 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE AMOUNT OF ONE THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF THE ISIRI ELEMENTARY SCHOOL, CALBIGA, SAMAR.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The amount of one thousand pesos appro­priated under paragraph A-(c) ; Samar, item numbered thirty-seven of section one of Act Numbered Forty-one hundred and eighty of the Philippine Legislature is reap-propriated for the construction of the Biri Elementary School, Calbiga, Samar.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1938.
