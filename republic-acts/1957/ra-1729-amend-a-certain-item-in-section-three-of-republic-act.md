# Republic Act No. 1729

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1729**

**An Act to Amend a Certain Item in Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item one, Group V, under q. Cebu, page three hundred seventy-four, section three of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, is amended to read as follows:

| "1. Tumalog Barrio School Building, Oslob Construction | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
