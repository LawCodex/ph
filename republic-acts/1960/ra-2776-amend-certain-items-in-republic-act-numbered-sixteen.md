# Republic Act No. 2776

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2776**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Project Numbered B-221.25, for the Province of Iloilo, page 464-A, Title B-Highways (Feeder and Secondary Roads), Appendix A of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen is amended to read as follows:

"B-221.25 Guimbal-Igbaras Road. Construction of bridges GR 8.00 8.00 8.00 4"

## Section 2

Priority Numbered 19, Group I, for the province of Iloilo, page 42-B, Appendix B of the same Act is amended to read as follows:

"19 Miagao—Pungtod-Cavite Construction 5,000.00."

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
