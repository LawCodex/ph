# Batas Pambansa Blg. 735

**An Act Changing the Name of the Banga Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Banga, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Jose A. Urquiola Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 735**

An Act Changing the Name of the Banga Central Elementary School in the Municipality of Banga, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Jose A. Urquiola Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Banga Central Elementary School located in the Municipality of Banga, Province of Aklan, is hereby changed to Honorable Jose A. Urquiola Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
