# Republic Act No. 1475

**An Act Amending Title F of Section One of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1475**

**An Act Amending Title F of Section One of Republic Act Numbered One Thousand Two Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Title F of section one of Republic Act numbered One thousand two hundred is hereby amended by transferring sub-item H (5) of item "af. Negros Occidental" to item "n. Capiz" as sub-item (4) thereof.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 14, 1956.
