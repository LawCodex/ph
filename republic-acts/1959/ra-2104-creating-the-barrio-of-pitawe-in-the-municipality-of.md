# Republic Act No. 2104

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pitawe in the Municipality of Labason, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2104**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pitawe in the Municipality of Labason, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Bacong, Gabong, Pitawe and Banga-an in the Municipality of Labason, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, are constituted into a distinct and independent barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Pitawe.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 3, 1959.
