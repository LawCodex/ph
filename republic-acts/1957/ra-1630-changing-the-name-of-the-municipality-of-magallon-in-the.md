# Republic Act No. 1630

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Magallon in the Province of Negros Occidental to Municipality of Moises Padilla**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1630**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Magallon in the Province of Negros Occidental to Municipality of Moises Padilla**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Municipality of Magallon in the Province of Negros Occidental is hereby renamed Municipality of Moises Padilla.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
