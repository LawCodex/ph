# Republic Act No. 7441

**An Act Authorizing the Leyte State School of Agriculture in Alangalang, Leyte, To Offer the Bachelor of Science in Agriculture and Bachelor of Science in Agri-business and Other Agriculture-related Programs**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7441, April 27, 1992 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE LEYTE STATE SCHOOL OF AGRICULTURE IN ALANGALANG, LEYTE, TO OFFER THE BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN AGRICULTURE AND BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN AGRI-BUSINESS AND OTHER AGRICULTURE-RELATED PROGRAMS

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Leyte State School of Agriculture in Alangalang, Leyte, is hereby authorized to offer the Bachelor of Science in Agriculture and Bachelor of Science in Agri-business and other agriculture-related programs.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
