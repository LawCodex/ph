# Republic Act No. 5294

**An Act Changing the Name of the Maternity and Children's Hospital to Dr. Jose Fabella Memorial Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5294**

An Act Changing the Name of the Maternity and Children’s Hospital to Dr. Jose Fabella Memorial Hospital

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Maternity and Children's Hospital in the City of Manila under the Department of Health is hereby changed to Dr. Jose Fabella Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
