# Republic Act No. 2853

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Añgarian in the Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality, to be Known as the Barrio of Añgarian**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2853**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Añgarian in the Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, into a Barrio of Said Municipality, to be Known as the Barrio of Añgarian**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Añgarian in the Municipality of Bugallon, Province of Pangasinan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Añgarian.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
