# Batas Pambansa Blg. 767

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barotac VIejo Central School in the Municipality of Barotac VIejo, Province of Iloilo, to Raul O. V. Causing Memorial Central School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 767**

An Act Changing the Name of the Barotac Viejo Central School in the Municipality of Barotac Viejo, Province of Iloilo, to Raul O. V. Causing Memorial Central School

## Section 1

The name of the Barotac Viejo Central School in the Municipality of Barotac Viejo, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Raul O. V. Causing Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
