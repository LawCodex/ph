# Republic Act No. 1206

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Trinidad, Province of Davao, to Jose Abad Santos**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1206**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Trinidad, Province of Davao, to Jose Abad Santos**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Trinidad, Province of Davao, is changed to Jose Abad Santos.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 20, 1955.
