# Republic Act No. 1687

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pulang-Bato in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1687**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Pulang-Bato in the Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pulang-Bato is separated from the barrio of Alas-as, Municipality of San Nicolas, Province of Batangas, and constituted into a barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Pulang-Bato.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
