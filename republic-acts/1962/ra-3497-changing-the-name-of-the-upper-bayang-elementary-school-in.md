# Republic Act No. 3497

**An Act Changing the Name of the Upper Bayang Elementary School in the Municipality of Bayang, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Pandapatan Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3497**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Upper Bayang Elementary School in the Municipality of Bayang, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Sultan Pandapatan Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Upper Bayang Elementary School in the Municipality of Bayang, Province of Lanao del Sur, is changed to Sultan Pandapatan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1962.
