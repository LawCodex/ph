# Republic Act No. 2561

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Batangas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2561**

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Batangas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of Nasugbu, Province of Batangas, are hereby converted into barrios of the said municipality, as follows:

Sitio Lumbangan to Barrio Lumbangan; Sitio Balaytigui to Barrio Balaytigui; Sitio Malapad na Bato to Barrio Malapad na Bato; Sitio Aga to Barrio Aga; Sitio Kayrilao to Barrio Kayrilao; and Sitio Mataas na Pulo to Barrio Mataas na Pulo.

## Section 2

The sitios of Sambal in the Municipality of Lemery, Province of Batangas, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Sambal.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
