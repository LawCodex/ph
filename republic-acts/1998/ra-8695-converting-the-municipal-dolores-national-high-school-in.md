# Republic Act No. 8695

**An Act Converting the Municipal Dolores National High School in Barangay Talogtog, Municipality of Dolores, Province of Abra, Into a National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8695, July 09, 1998 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE MUNICIPAL DOLORES NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TALOGTOG, MUNICIPALITY OF DOLORES, PROVINCE OF ABRA, INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The municipal government-funded Dolores National High School in Barangay Talogtog, Municipality of Dolores, Province of Abra, is hereby converted, under the direct supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, into a nationally funded high school, to be known as the Rosalio Eduarte National High School.Ꮮαwρhi৷

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
