# Batas Pambansa Blg. 849

**An Act Changing the Name of the Tinambacan National School of Fisheries in the City of Calbayog to Rafael Lentejas Memorial School of Fisheries**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 849**

An Act Changing the Name of the Tinambacan National School of Fisheries in the City of Calbayog to Rafael Lentejas Memorial School of Fisheries

## Section 1

The name of the Tinambacan National School of Fisheries in the City of Calbayog is changed to Rafael Lentejas Memorial School of Fisheries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
