# Republic Act No. 2479

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cadamortisan in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Don Montano**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2479**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cadamortisan in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Don Montano**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio Cadamortisan in the Municipality of Umingan, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Don Montano.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
