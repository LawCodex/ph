# Republic Act No. 1722

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Campitic in the Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Campitic**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1722**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Campitic in the Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of Campitic**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Campitic in the Municipality of Palo, Province of Leyte, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of Campitic.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
