# Republic Act No. 5489

**An Act Dividing the Province of Ilocos Norte Into Two Highway Engineering Districts**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5489, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT DIVIDING THE PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE INTO TWO HIGHWAY ENGINEERING DISTRICTS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Any provision of law to the contrary notwithstanding, the Province of Ilocos Norte shall be divided into two highway engineering districts: the first engineering district to comprise the First Representative District; and the second engineering district to comprise the Second Representative District, with one highway district engineer for each engineering district.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
