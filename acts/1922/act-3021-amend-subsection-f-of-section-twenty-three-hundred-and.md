# Act No. 3021

**An Act to Amend Subsection (F) of Section Twenty-three Hundred and Seven of the Administrative Code**

[ Act No. 3021, March 08, 1922 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND SUBSECTION (F) OF SECTION TWENTY-THREE HUNDRED AND SEVEN OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE CODE

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Subsection (f) of section twenty-three hundred and seven of the Administrative Code is hereby amended to read as follows:

(f) Hawkers, peddlers, hucksters, not including hucksters or peddlers who sell only native vegetables, fruits, or foods personally carried by them: _Provided, however,_ That hawkers, peddlers, and hucksters who have secured licenses at the rates fixed by ordinance in any municipality shall not be required to take out licenses in other municipalities through which they may travel occasionally, unless the term for the renewal of such licenses shall have expired."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 8, 1922.
