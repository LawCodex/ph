# Republic Act No. 3708

**An Act Changing the Name of Sitangkai Central School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, to Datu Halun Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3708**

**An Act Changing the Name of Sitangkai Central School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, to Datu Halun Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Sitangkai Central School in the Municipality of Parang, Province of Sulu, is hereby changed to Datu Halun Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
