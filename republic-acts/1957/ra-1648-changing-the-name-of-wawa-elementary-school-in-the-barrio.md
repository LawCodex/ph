# Republic Act No. 1648

**An Act Changing the Name of Wawa Elementary School in the Barrio of Wawa, Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, to David Jimenez Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1648**

**An Act Changing the Name of Wawa Elementary School in the Barrio of Wawa, Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, to David Jimenez Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Wawa Elementary School in the barrio of Wawa, Municipality of Rosario, Province of Cavite, is changed to David Jimenez Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
