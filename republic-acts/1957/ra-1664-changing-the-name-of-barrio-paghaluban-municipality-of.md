# Republic Act No. 1664

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Paghaluban, Municipality of Barcelona, Province of Sorsogon, to Peña Francia**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1664**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Paghaluban, Municipality of Barcelona, Province of Sorsogon, to Peña Francia**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Paghaluban, Municipality of Barcelona, Province of Sorsogon, is changed to Peña Francia.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
