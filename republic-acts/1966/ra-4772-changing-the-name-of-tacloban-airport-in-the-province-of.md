# Republic Act No. 4772

**An Act Changing the Name of Tacloban Airport in the Province of Leyte to Speaker Daniel Z. Romualdez Airport**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4772**

An Act Changing the Name of Tacloban Airport in the Province of Leyte to Speaker Daniel Z. Romualdez Airport

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Tacloban Airport in the Province of Leyte is hereby changed to Speaker Daniel Z. Romualdez Airport.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
