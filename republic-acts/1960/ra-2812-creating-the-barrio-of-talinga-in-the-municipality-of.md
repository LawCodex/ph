# Republic Act No. 2812

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Talinga in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2812**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Talinga in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Talinga in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Talinga.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
