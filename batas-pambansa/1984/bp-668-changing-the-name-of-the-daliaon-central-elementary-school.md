# Batas Pambansa Blg. 668

**An Act Changing the Name of the Daliaon Central Elementary School in the City of Davao to Don Juan Dela Cruz Central Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 668**

An Act Changing the Name of the Daliaon Central Elementary School in the City of Davao to Don Juan Dela Cruz Central Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Daliaon Central Elementary School in the City of Davao is changed to Don Juan dela Cruz Central Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
