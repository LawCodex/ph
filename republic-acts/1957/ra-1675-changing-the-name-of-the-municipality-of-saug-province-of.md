# Republic Act No. 1675

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, to Asuncion**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1675**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, to Asuncion**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Saug, Province of Davao, is changed to Asuncion.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
