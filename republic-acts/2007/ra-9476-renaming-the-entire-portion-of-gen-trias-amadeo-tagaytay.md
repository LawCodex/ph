# Republic Act No. 9476

**An Act Renaming the Entire Portion of Gen. Trias - Amadeo - Tagaytay Road From Governor's Drive to Tagaytay City in the Province of Cavite to Crisanto M. de Los Reyes Avenue**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9476 May 22, 2007**

AN ACT RENAMING THE ENTIRE PORTION OF GEN. TRIAS - AMADEO - TAGAYTAY ROAD FROM GOVERNOR’S DRIVE TO TAGAYTAY CITY IN THE PROVINCE OF CAVITE TO CRISANTO M. DE LOS REYES AVENUE

_Be it enacted by the Senate and the House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled_ :

## SECTION 1

In due recognition of the generous contributions and unending support of Crisanto M. De Los Reyes in the patriotic cause of our Filipino soldiers in Cavite during the Spanish occupation, the Gen. Trias - Amadeo - Tagaytay Road from Governor’s Drive to Tagaytay City is hereby renamed as Crisanto M. De Los Reyes Avenue.

## SEC. 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
