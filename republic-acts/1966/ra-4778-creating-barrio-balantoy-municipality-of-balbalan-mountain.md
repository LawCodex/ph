# Republic Act No. 4778

**An Act Creating Barrio Balantoy, Municipality of Balbalan, Mountain Province**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4778**

An Act Creating Barrio Balantoy, Municipality of Balbalan, Mountain Province

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sitios Balantoy, Ligayan, Kilayon, Bolo and Nawoy, Municipality of Balbalan, Mountain Province, are constituted into a barrio, to be known as Barrio Balantoy of said municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1966.
