# Republic Act No. 6216

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Santo Tomas in the Municipality of Pinili, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Barrio Arcangel.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6216, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARRIO SANTO TOMAS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PINILI, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE, TO BARRIO ARCANGEL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Santo Tomas in the Municipality of Pinili, Province of Ilocos Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Arcangel.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
