# Republic Act No. 1726

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Mat-i in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, to President Roxas**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1726**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Mat-i in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, to President Roxas**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of barrio, Mat-i in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to President Roxas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
