# Republic Act No. 2477

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Calolbon, Province of Catanduanes**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2477**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Calolbon, Province of Catanduanes**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of San Isidro in the Municipality of Calolbon, Province of Catanduanes, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Isidro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
