# Republic Act No. 4365

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bugho in the Province of Leyte, to the Municipality of Javier**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4365**

An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bugho in the Province of Leyte, to the Municipality of Javier

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Bugho in the Province of Leyte is hereby changed to the Municipality of Javier.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1965.
