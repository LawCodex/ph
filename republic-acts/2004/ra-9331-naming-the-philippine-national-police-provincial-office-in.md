# Republic Act No. 9331

**An Act Naming the Philippine National Police Provincial Office in the Municipality of Lagawe, Province of Ifugao as Camp Colonel Joaquin P. Dunuan**

Twelfth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eight day of July, two thousand and three.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9331**

AN ACT NAMING THE PHILIPPINE NATIONAL POLICE PROVINCIAL OFFICE IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LAGAWE, PROVINCE OF IFUGAO AS CAMP COLONEL JOAQUIN P. DUNUAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Philippine National Police (PNP) Provincial Office in the Municipality of Lagawe, Province of Ifugao is hereby named Camp Col Joaquin P. Dunuan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
