# Executive Order No. 466

**Reclassifying All Municipalities in the Philippines**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 466, August 22, 1951 ]

RECLASSIFYING ALL MUNICIPALITIES IN THE PHILIPPINES
