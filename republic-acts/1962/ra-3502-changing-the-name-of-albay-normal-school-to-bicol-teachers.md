# Republic Act No. 3502

**An Act Changing the Name of Albay Normal School to Bicol Teachers College**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3502**

**An Act Changing the Name of Albay Normal School to Bicol Teachers College**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Albay Normal School is hereby changed to Bicol Teachers College.
