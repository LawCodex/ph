# Republic Act No. 6782

**An Act Changing the Name of the Cagayan Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Tuguegarao, Province of Cagayan, to Cagayan Valley Regional Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6782, November 29, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE CAGAYAN PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF TUGUEGARAO, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN, TO CAGAYAN VALLEY REGIONAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Cagayan Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Tuguegarao, Province of Cagayan, is hereby changed to Cagayan Valley Regional Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1
