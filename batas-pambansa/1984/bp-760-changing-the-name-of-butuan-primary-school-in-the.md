# Batas Pambansa Blg. 760

**An Act Changing the Name of Butuan Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Sofronio Castro Primary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 760**

An Act Changing the Name of Butuan Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Sofronio Castro Primary School

## Section 1

The name of Butuan Primary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, is changed to Sofronio Castro Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
