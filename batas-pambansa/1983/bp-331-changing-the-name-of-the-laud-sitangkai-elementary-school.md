# Batas Pambansa Blg. 331

**An Act Changing the Name of the Laud Sitangkai Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Panglima Alare Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 331**

An Act Changing the Name of the Laud Sitangkai Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Panglima Alare Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Laud Sitangkai Elementary School in the Municipality of Sitangkai, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is changed to Panglima Alare Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
