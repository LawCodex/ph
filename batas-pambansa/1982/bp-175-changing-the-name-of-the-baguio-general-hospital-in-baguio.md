# Batas Pambansa Blg. 175

**An Act Changing the Name of the Baguio General Hospital in Baguio City to Dr. Efrain C. Montemayor Medical Center.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 175**

An Act Changing the Name of the Baguio General Hospital in Baguio City to Dr. Efrain C. Montemayor Medical Center

## Section 1

The name of the Baguio General Hospital located in Baguio City is hereby changed to Dr. Efrain Montemayor Medical Center.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: February 12, 1982
