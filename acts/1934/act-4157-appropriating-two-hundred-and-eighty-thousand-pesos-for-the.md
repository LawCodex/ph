# Act No. 4157

**An Act Appropriating Two Hundred and Eighty thousand Pesos for the Construction of Additional Buildings in the Insular Psychopathic Hospital At Mandaluyong, Province of Rizal, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Acts No. 4157, December 01, 1934 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING TWO HUNDRED AND EIGHTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR THE CONSTRUCTION OF ADDITIONAL BUILDINGS IN THE INSULAR PSYCHOPATHIC HOSPITAL AT MANDALUYONG, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines  
in Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sum of two hundred and eighty thousand pesos is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the construction of additional buildings in the Insular Psychopathic Hospital at Mandaluyong, Rizal.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 1, 1934.
