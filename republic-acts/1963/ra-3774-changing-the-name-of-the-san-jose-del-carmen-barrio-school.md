# Republic Act No. 3774

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Jose Del Carmen Barrio School in the Barrio of San Jose, Municipality of Talisay, Province of Camarines Norte, to Manuel Cacho Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3774**

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Jose Del Carmen Barrio School in the Barrio of San Jose, Municipality of Talisay, Province of Camarines Norte, to Manuel Cacho Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Jose-Del Carmen Barrio School in the barrio of San Jose, Municipality of Talisay, Province of Camarines Norte, is hereby changed to Manuel Cacho Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1963.
