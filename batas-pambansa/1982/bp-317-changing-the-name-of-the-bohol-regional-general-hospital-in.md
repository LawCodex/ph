# Batas Pambansa Blg. 317

**An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol Regional General Hospital in the City of Tagbilaran to Governor Celestino Gallares Memorial Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 317**

An Act Changing the Name of the Bohol Regional General Hospital in the City of Tagbilaran to Governor Celestino Gallares Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Bohol Regional General Hospital in the City of Tagbilaran is changed to Governor Celestino Gallares Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
