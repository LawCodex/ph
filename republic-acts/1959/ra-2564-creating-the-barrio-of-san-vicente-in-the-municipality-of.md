# Republic Act No. 2564

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Vicente in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2564**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of San Vicente in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Malamnang in the Municipality of Roxas, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of San Vicente.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
