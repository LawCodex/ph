# Republic Act No. 10508

**An Act Changing the Name of Malalim Elementary School in Barangay Malalim, City of Batangas, Province of Batangas to Eulalia Talban Ebora Memorial Elementary School**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand twelve.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 10508**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF MALALIM ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MALALIM, CITY OF BATANGAS, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS TO EULALIA TALBAN EBORA MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

_Renaming of an Elementary School._ – The name of Malalim Elementary School in Barangay Malalim, City of Batangas, Province of Batangas is hereby changed to Eulalia Talban Ebora Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

_Location of the Main Campus._ – The main campus of the Eulalia Talban Ebora Memorial Elementary School shall continue to remain in its present location in Barangay Malalim, City of Batangas, Province of Batangas.

## Section 3

_Effectivity._ – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on September 5, 2012 and January 28, 2013, respectively.
