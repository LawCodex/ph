# Republic Act No. 1725

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pamoblaran in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of San Antonio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1725**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Pamoblaran in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio to be Known as the Barrio of San Antonio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Pamoblaran in the Municipality of Dulag, Province of Leyte, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as the barrio of San Antonio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
