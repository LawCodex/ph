# Batas Pambansa Blg. 201

**An Act Converting the Prenza Barangay High School in the Municipality of Marilao, Province of Bulacan, Into a National High School to Be Known as the Prenza National High School, and Appropriating Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 201**

An Act Converting the Prenza Barangay High School in the Municipality of Marilao, Province of Bulacan, into a National High School to be Known as the Prenza National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor

## Section 1

The Prenza Barangay High School in the Municipality of Marilao, Province of Bulacan, is converted into a national high school to be known as the Prenza National High School.

## Section 2

The amount of two hundred thousand pesos for the operation of the school shall be charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 80 for the Ministry of Education and Culture intended for new national schools: _Provided,_ That the local government unit concerned shall continue to provide its share in the cost of operating and maintaining said school: _Provided, further,_ That thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 16, 1982
