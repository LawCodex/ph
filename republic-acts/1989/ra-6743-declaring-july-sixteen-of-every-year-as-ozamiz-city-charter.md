# Republic Act No. 6743

**An Act Declaring July Sixteen of Every Year as Ozamiz City Charter Day and a Special Public Non-working Holiday in the City of Ozamiz**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6743, August 09, 1989 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JULY SIXTEEN OF EVERY YEAR AS OZAMIZ CITY CHARTER DAY AND A SPECIAL PUBLIC NON-WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF OZAMIZ

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

July Sixteen of every year is hereby declared as Ozamiz City Charter Day and a special public non-working holiday in the City of Ozamiz.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
