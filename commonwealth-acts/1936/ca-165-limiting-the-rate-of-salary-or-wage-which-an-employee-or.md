# Commonwealth Act No. 165

**An Act Limiting the Rate of Salary or Wage Which An Employee or Laborer of the Government Marine Railway and Repair Shops Employed Under Act Numbered Forty- One Hundred and Eleven May Receive.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 165, November 09, 1936 ]

AN ACT LIMITING THE RATE OF SALARY OR WAGE WHICH AN EMPLOYEE OR LABORER OF THE GOVERNMENT MARINE RAILWAY AND REPAIR SHOPS EMPLOYED UNDER ACT NUMBERED FORTY- ONE HUNDRED AND ELEVEN MAY RECEIVE.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

Unless otherwise specifically provided in the annual Appropriation Act, no employee or laborer of the Government Marine Railway and Repair Shops employed under Act Numbered Forty-one hundred and eleven shall be paid more than ten pesos per day or more than two hundred and fifty pesos per month.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, November 9, 1936.
