# Republic Act No. 2156

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Balite in the Barrio of Banoyo, Municipality of San Luis, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2156**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Balite in the Barrio of Banoyo, Municipality of San Luis, Province of Batangas, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Balite in the barrio of Banoyo Municipality of San Luis, Province of Batangas, is hereby separated from the said barrio and converted into a new barrio to be known as the barrio of Balite.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
