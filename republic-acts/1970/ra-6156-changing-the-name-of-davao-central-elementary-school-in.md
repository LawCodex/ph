# Republic Act No. 6156

**An Act Changing the Name of Davao Central Elementary School in Davao City to Capitan Tomas Monteverde, Sr. Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6156**

**An Act Changing the Name of Davao Central Elementary School in Davao City to Capitan Tomas Monteverde, Sr. Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Davao Central Elementary School in Davao City is hereby changed to Capitan Tomas Monteverde, St. Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 9, 1970.
