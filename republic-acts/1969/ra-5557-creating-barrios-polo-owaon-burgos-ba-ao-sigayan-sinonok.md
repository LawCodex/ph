# Republic Act No. 5557

**An Act Creating Barrios Polo, Owaon, Burgos, Ba-Ao, Sigayan, Sinonok, Canlucani, Guimputlan, Silinog and Aliguay in the City of Dapitan**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5557, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIOS POLO, OWAON, BURGOS, BA-AO, SIGAYAN, SINONOK, CANLUCANI, GUIMPUTLAN, SILINOG AND ALIGUAY IN THE CITY OF DAPITAN.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There is hereby created in the City of Dapitan, Barrios Polo, Owaon, Burgos, Ba-ao, Sigayan, Sinonok, Canlucani, Guimputlan, Silinog and Aliguay, their respective boundaries to be determined by the city council with the approval of the city mayor within six months after the approval of this Act.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
