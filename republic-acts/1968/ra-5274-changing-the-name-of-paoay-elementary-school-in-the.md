# Republic Act No. 5274

**An Act Changing the Name of Paoay Elementary School in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Celo Haight Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5274**

An Act Changing the Name of Paoay Elementary School in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, to Celo Haight Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Paoay Elementary School located in the Municipality of Atok, Province of Benguet, is hereby changed to Celo Haight Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
