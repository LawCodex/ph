# Republic Act No. 7525

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Vigan Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Vigan, Province of Ilocos Sur, From One Hundred (100) To One Hundred Twenty-five (125) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7525, May 21, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE VIGAN PROVINCIAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF VIGAN, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR, FROM ONE HUNDRED (100) TO ONE HUNDRED TWENTY-FIVE (125) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Vigan Provincial Hospital in the Municipality of Vigan, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby increased from one hundred (100) to one hundred twenty-five (125) beds.

## Section 2

The Department of Health shall take the necessary steps for the immediate implementation of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
