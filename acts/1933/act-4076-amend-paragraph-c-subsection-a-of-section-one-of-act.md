# Act No. 4076

**An Act to Amend Paragraph (C), Subsection a of Section One of Act Numbered Four thousand and Fifty-Nine.**

[ Acts No. 4076, October 27, 1933 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND PARAGRAPH (C), SUBSECTION A OF SECTION ONE OF ACT NUMBERED FOUR THOUSAND AND FIFTY-NINE.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

Paragraph (c), subsection A of section one of Act Numbered Four thousand and fifty-nine, is hereby amended so that item three, on page thirty, under the caption "Marinduque", shall read as follows:

<table>
<tr>
<td rowspan="4"><nobr>" 3.</nobr></td>
<td>(a)</td>
<td>For the shop building, Poblacion, Santa Cruz</td>
<td>P2,500.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(b)</td>
<td>For the completion of the Kawit Barrio School, Boac</td>
<td>1,000.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(c)</td>
<td>For the repair and reconstruction of the Santa Cruz Intermediate Building, Santa Cruz</td>
<td>1,000.00</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>(d)</td>
<td>For the repair and reconstruction of the Jupi School, Santa Cruz</td>
<td>500.00"</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

Approved, October 27, 1933.
