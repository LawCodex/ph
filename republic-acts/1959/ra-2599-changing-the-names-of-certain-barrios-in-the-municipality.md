# Republic Act No. 2599

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Goa, Province of Camarines Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2599**

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Barrios in the Municipality of Goa, Province of Camarines Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The names of the barrios of Balaynan and Aroro in the Municipality of Goa, Province of Camarines Sur, are changed to San Rafael and San Pedro, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
