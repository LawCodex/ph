# Republic Act No. 2823

**An Act Converting Sitio Caligangan, Municipality of Liloan, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2823**

**An Act Converting Sitio Caligangan, Municipality of Liloan, Province of Leyte, into a Barrio**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sitio Caligangan, Municipality of Liloan, Province of Leyte, is hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Caligangan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
