# Republic Act No. 6943

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Santol, Municipality of Balagtas, Province of Bulacan, to Barangay Dr. Nemesio G. Garcia, Sr.**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6943 April 5, 1990**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARANGAY SANTOL, MUNICIPALITY OF BALAGTAS, PROVINCE OF BULACAN, TO BARANGAY DR. NEMESIO G. GARCIA, SR.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Santol, Municipality of Balagtas, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed to Barangay Dr. Nemesio G. Garcia, Sr..

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its publication in at least two national newspapers of general circulation.

Approved: **April 5, 1990**
