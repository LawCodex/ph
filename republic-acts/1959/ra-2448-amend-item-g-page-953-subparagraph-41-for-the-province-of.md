# Republic Act No. 2448

**An Act to Amend Item (g), Page 953, Subparagraph 41, for the Province of Sorsogon, Paragraph a, Title G, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2448**

**An Act to Amend Item (g), Page 953, Subparagraph 41, for the Province of Sorsogon, Paragraph a, Title G, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (g), page 953, subparagraph 41, for the Province of Sorsogon, paragraph a, Title G, section three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(g) Tuba-Malapoc Communal Irrigation Project, Donsol ₱25,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
