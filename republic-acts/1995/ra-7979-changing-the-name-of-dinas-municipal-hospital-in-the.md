# Republic Act No. 7979

**An Act Changing the Name of Dinas Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Dinas, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Don Santiago A. Nahial, Sr. Memorial Hospital**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 7979, April 22, 1995 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF DINAS MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DINAS, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR, TO DON SANTIAGO A. NAHIAL, SR. MEMORIAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name Dinas Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Dinas, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Don Santiago A. Nahial, Sr. Memorial Hospital.1aшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on April 22,1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
