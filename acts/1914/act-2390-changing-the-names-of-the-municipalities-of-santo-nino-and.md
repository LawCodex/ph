# Act No. 2390

**An Act Changing; the Names of the Municipalities of Santo Nino and Mawanan, Province of Cagayan; San Isidro Labrador and San Isidro De Potot, Province of Pangasinan; San Francisco De Malabon and Santa Cruz De Malabon, Province of Cavite; Nagpartian and San Miguel, Province of Ilocos Norte; Langaran, Province of Misamis; San Pedro Tunasan, Province of Laguna; Cabagan Nuevo, Province of Isabela; Nueva Caceres, Province of Ambos Camarines; San Pedro Macati, Province of Rizal; San Juan De Bocboc, Province of Batangas; San Juan, Province of Nueva Ecija; Township of Barrit-Luluno, Province of Ilocos Sur, and of the Barrios of Tublijon and Gibigaan, Municipality of Sorsogon, Province of Sorsogon.**

[ Act No. 2390, February 28, 1914 ]

AN ACT CHANGING; THE NAMES OF THE MUNICIPALITIES OF SANTO NINO AND MAWANAN, PROVINCE OF CAGAYAN; SAN ISIDRO LABRADOR AND SAN ISIDRO DE POTOT, PROVINCE OF PANGASINAN; SAN FRANCISCO DE MALABON AND SANTA CRUZ DE MALABON, PROVINCE OF CAVITE; NAGPARTIAN AND SAN MIGUEL, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS NORTE; LANGARAN, PROVINCE OF MISAMIS; SAN PEDRO TUNASAN, PROVINCE OF LAGUNA; CABAGAN NUEVO, PROVINCE OF ISABELA; NUEVA CACERES, PROVINCE OF AMBOS CAMARINES; SAN PEDRO MACATI, PROVINCE OF RIZAL; SAN JUAN DE BOCBOC, PROVINCE OF BATANGAS; SAN JUAN, PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECIJA; TOWNSHIP OF BARRIT-LULUNO, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR, AND OF THE BARRIOS OF TUBLIJON AND GIBIGAAN, MUNICIPALITY OF SORSOGON, PROVINCE OF SORSOGON.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philijipine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

The names of the municipality of Santo Nino and Manawan, Province of Cagayan, are changed to Faire and Rizal, respectively; those of the municipalities of San Isidro Labrador and San Isidro de Potot, Province of Pangasinan, to Labrador and Burgos, respectively; those of the municipalities of San Francisco de Malabon and Santa Cruz de Malabon, Province of Cavite, to Malabon and Tanza, respectively; those of Nagpartian and San Miguel, Province of Ilocos Norte, to Burgos and Sarrat, respectively; the name of the municipality of Langaran, Province of Misa-mis, to Plaridel; that of San Pedro de Tunasan, Province of Laguna, to San Pedro; that of Cabagan Nuevo, Province of Isabela, to Cabagan; that of Nueva Caceres, Province of Ambos Camarines, to Naga; that of San Pedro Macati, Province of Rizal, to Makati; that of San Juan de Bocboc, Province of Batangas, to Bolbok; that of San Juan de Guimba, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Guimba; that of the township of Barrit-Luluno, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Luba; and those of the barrios of Tublijon and Gibigaan, municipality of Sorsogon, Province of Sorsogon, to Rizal and Buenavista, respectively.

## Section 2

The public good requiring the speedy enactment of this bill, the same shall take effect on its passage, in accordance with section one of Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and forty-five of the Philippine Legislature.

Enacted, February 28, 1914.
