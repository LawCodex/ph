# Republic Act No. 1796

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Panadtaran Elementary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Mayor Avelino Chagas Elementary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1796**

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Panadtaran Elementary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Mayor Avelino Chagas Elementary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The school in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at present known as Panadtaran Elementary School, shall hereafter be known as Mayor Avelino Chagas Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
