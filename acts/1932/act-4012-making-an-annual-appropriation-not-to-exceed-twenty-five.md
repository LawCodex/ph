# Act No. 4012

**An Act Making An Annual Appropriation Not to Exceed Twenty-Five thousand Pesos, From the Income Derived From the Penal Colonies Under the Bureau of Prisons, to Constitute a Special Fund for the Promotion and Development of Agriculture and Industry in Said Colonies and for Encouraging and Promoting Vocational Training Among the Colonists.**

[ Acts No. 4012, December 05, 1932 ]

AN ACT MAKING AN ANNUAL APPROPRIATION NOT TO EXCEED TWENTY-FIVE THOUSAND PESOS, FROM THE INCOME DERIVED FROM THE PENAL COLONIES UNDER THE BUREAU OF PRISONS, TO CONSTITUTE A SPECIAL FUND FOR THE PROMOTION AND DEVELOPMENT OF AGRICULTURE AND INDUSTRY IN SAID COLONIES AND FOR ENCOURAGING AND PROMOTING VOCATIONAL TRAINING AMONG THE COLONISTS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

A sum not to exceed twenty-five thousand pesos is hereby set aside annually from the income of the penal colonies, to constitute a special fund which shall be disbursed, subject to the approval of the Philippine Legislature, for the promotion and development of agriculture, and industry in the penal colonies and for encouraging and promoting vocational training among the colonists.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, December 5, 1932.
