# Republic Act No. 1476

**An Act Repealing Sections Two Hundred Ninety-Six, Two Hundred Ninety-Seven, Two Hundred Ninety-Eight, Two Hundred Ninety-Nine, and Three Hundred of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four Hundred Sixty-Six, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1476**

**An Act Repealing Sections Two Hundred Ninety-Six, Two Hundred Ninety-Seven, Two Hundred Ninety-Eight, Two Hundred Ninety-Nine, and Three Hundred of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four Hundred Sixty-Six, as Amended**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Sections two hundred ninety-six, two hundred ninety-seven, two hundred ninety-eight, two hundred ninety-nine and three hundred, Chapter Eight, of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred sixty-six, as amended, are hereby repealed: _Provided,_ That all fees heretofore collected under the above-named sections of Commonwealth Act Numbered Four hundred sixty-six, as amended, shall continue to be disbursed in accordance with the provisions of the said Act.

## Section 2

All fees uncollected under the above-specified provisions of said Act are hereby condoned.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1956.
