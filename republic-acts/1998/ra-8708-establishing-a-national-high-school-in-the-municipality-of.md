# Republic Act No. 8708

**An Act Establishing a National High School in the Municipality of Dimiao, Province of Bohol, to Be Known as the Dimiao National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8708, July 16, 1998 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DIMIAO, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE DIMIAO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national high school in the Municipality of Dimiao, Province of Bohol, to be known as the Dimiao National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the implementation of this Act.Ꮮαwρhi৷

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on July 16, 1998 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
