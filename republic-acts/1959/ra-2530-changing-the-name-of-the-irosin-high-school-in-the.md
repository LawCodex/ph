# Republic Act No. 2530

**An Act Changing the Name of the Irosin High School in the Municipality of Irosin, Province of Sorsogon, to Gallanosa High School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2530**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Irosin High School in the Municipality of Irosin, Province of Sorsogon, to Gallanosa High School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Irosin High School in the Municipality of Irosin Province of Sorsogon, is hereby changed to Gallanosa High School.(awÞhi(

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
