# Republic Act No. 2559

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Pangasinan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2559**

**An Act Creating Barrios in the Province of Pangasinan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios in the Municipality of Labrador, Province of Pangasinan, are constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, as follows:

Sitios of Tokoc, Polowen and the adjoining part of Barrio Bolo bordering Silag to Barrio Magsaysay.

## Section 2

The following sitios in the Municipality of Mabini, Province of Pangasinan, are constituted into a barrio of the said municipality, as follows:

Sitios Loboc, Lalasoran, Libsong and Biga to Barrio Kaabiangan.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
