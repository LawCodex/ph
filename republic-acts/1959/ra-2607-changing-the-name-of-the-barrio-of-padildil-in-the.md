# Republic Act No. 2607

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Padildil in the Municipality of San Quintin, Province of Abra, to Villa Mercedes**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2607**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Padildil in the Municipality of San Quintin, Province of Abra, to Villa Mercedes**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Padildil in the Municipality of San Quintin, Province of Abra, is changed to Villa Mercedes.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
