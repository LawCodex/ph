# Batas Pambansa Blg. 672

**An Act Changing the Name of Ondoy Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Teodolfo S. Suñer Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 672**

An Act Changing the Name of Ondoy Elementary School in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, to Honorable Teodolfo S. Suñer Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Ondoy Elementary School located in the Municipality of Ibajay, Province of Aklan, is hereby changed to Honorable Teodolfo S. Suñer Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
