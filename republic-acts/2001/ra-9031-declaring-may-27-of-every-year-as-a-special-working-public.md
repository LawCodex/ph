# Republic Act No. 9031

**An Act Declaring May 27 of Every Year as a Special Working Public Holiday in the Province of Camarines Sur and in the Cities of Naga and Iriga in Commemoration of Its Annual Foundation Anniversary**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9031 March 5, 2001**

AN ACT DECLARING MAY 27 OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR AND IN THE CITIES OF NAGA AND IRIGA IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS ANNUAL FOUNDATION ANNIVERSARY

## SECTION 1

May 27 of every year is hereby declared as a Special Working Public Holiday in the entire Province of Camarines Sur including the cities of Naga and Iriga in commemoration of its foundation anniversary as a political unit

## SECTION 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **March 5, 2001**
