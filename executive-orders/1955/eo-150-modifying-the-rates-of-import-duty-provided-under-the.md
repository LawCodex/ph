# Executive Order No. 150

**Modifying the Rates of Import Duty Provided Under the Philippine Tariff Act of 1909, as Amended**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 150, December 31, 1955 ]

MODIFYING THE RATES OF IMPORT DUTY PROVIDED UNDER THE PHILIPPINE TARIFF ACT OF 1909, AS AMENDED

Pursuant to the powers vested in me by Republic Act Number Nine hundred and eleven, as amended by Republic Act Number Eleven hundred and ninety-six, **I, RAMON MAGSAYSAY** , President of the Philippines, do hereby direct and order:

## Section 2

All articles dutiable under the Philippine Tariff Act of 1909, as amended, not specifically included in the foregoing list shall pay, in addition to the existing rates of import duty thereon, an incremental duty amounting to thirty (30) _per centum_ of such rates.

## Section 3

"United States articles" shall be subject to the percentage rates of those prescribed in sections 1 and 2 above in accordance with the revised trade agreement between the Philippines and the United States made effective pursuant to Proclamation No. 216 dated November 28, 1955.

## Section 4

The articles for which rates of import duty are established herein shall also be subject to the general rules of the Philippine Tariff Act of 1909, as amended.

## Section 5

This Order shall take effect after 12:00 o'clock midnight of December 31, 1955. All dutiable articles entered, or withdrawn from warehouse, in the Philippines, for consumption after 12:00 o'clock midnight of the above-mentioned date shall pay the rates of duty herein prescribed.
