# Batas Pambansa Blg. 207

**An Act Changing the Name of Sulu Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Tawi-Tawi Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 207**

An Act Changing the Name of Sulu Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to Tawi-Tawi Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries

## Section 1

The name of Sulu Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries, located at Sanga-Sanga in the Municipality of Bongao, Province of Tawi-Tawi, is hereby changed to Tawi-Tawi Pilot Opportunity School of Fisheries.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
