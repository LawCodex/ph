# Batas Pambansa Blg. 769

**An Act to Change the Name of Eulogio Rodriguez Elementary School Unit 1 Located At the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, to Andres Bonifacio Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 769**

An Act to Change the Name of Eulogio Rodriguez Elementary School Unit 1 Located at the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, to Andres Bonifacio Elementary School

## Section 1

Eulogio Rodriguez Elementary School Unit 1 located at the Municipality of Mandaluyong, Metro Manila, is hereby renamed Andres Bonifacio Elementary School in honor of one of the heroes of the Philippine Revolution, Andres Bonifacio.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
