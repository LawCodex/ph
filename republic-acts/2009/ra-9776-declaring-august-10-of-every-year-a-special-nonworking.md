# Republic Act No. 9776

**An Act Declaring August 10 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in San Jose City, Province of Nueva Ecija**

Fourteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand nine.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9776**

AN ACT DECLARING AUGUST 10 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN SAN JOSE CITY, PROVINCE OF NUEVA ECIJA

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

August 10 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the City of San Jose, Province of Nueva Ecija to commemorate its creation as a city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
