# Republic Act No. 8571

**An Act Establishing a Barangay High School in the Barangay of Tambongan, Municipality of Candijay, Province of Bohol, to Be Known as Tambongan National High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Tenth Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8571, February 27, 1998 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE BARANGAY OF TAMBONGAN, MUNICIPALITY OF CANDIJAY, PROVINCE OF BOHOL, TO BE KNOWN AS TAMBONGAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national high school in the Barangay of Tambongan, Municipality of Candijay, Province of Bohol, to be known as the Tambongan National High School.

## Section 2

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary for the implementation of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
