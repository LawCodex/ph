# Republic Act No. 3367

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Quinale in the Municipality of Calabanga, Province of Camarines Sur**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3367**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Quinale in the Municipality of Calabanga, Province of Camarines Sur**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Quinale in the Municipality of Calabanga, Province of Camarines Sur, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Quinale.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
