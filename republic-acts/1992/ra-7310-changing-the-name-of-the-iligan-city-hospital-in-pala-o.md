# Republic Act No. 7310

**An Act Changing the Name of the Iligan City Hospital in Pala-o, Iligan City, Province of Lanao Del Norte, to Gregorio T. Lluch, Sr., Memorial Hospital**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7310, March 30, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE ILIGAN CITY HOSPITAL IN PALA-O, ILIGAN CITY, PROVINCE OF LANAO DEL NORTE, TO GREGORIO T. LLUCH, SR., MEMORIAL HOSPITAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Iligan City Hospital in Pala-o, Iligan City, Province of Lanao del Norte, is hereby changed to Gregorio T. Lluch, Sr., Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
