# Republic Act No. 1663

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven, Regarding Public Works Projects for the Province of Masbate**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1663**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Fourteen Hundred Eleven, Regarding Public Works Projects for the Province of Masbate**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Items (f), (g), (h) and (i), paragraph thirty, page eighty-eight of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, for the Province of Masbate, are amended to read as follows:

| "(f) Placer Municipal Building Construction | ₱5,000.00 |
|---|---|
| "(g) Uson Municipal Building Construction | ₱5,000.00 |
| "(h) Uson Municipal Building Construction | ₱5,000.00 |
| "(i) Uson Municipal Building Construction | ₱5,000.00." |

## Section 2

Item seven, page two hundred ten of Republic Act Numbered Fourteen hundred eleven, for the Province of Masbate, is amended to read as follows:

| ""7. Municipal Building, Placer | ₱5,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
