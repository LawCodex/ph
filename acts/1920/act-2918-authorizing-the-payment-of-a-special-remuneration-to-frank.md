# Act No. 2918

**An Act Authorizing the Payment of a Special Remuneration to Frank W. Carpenter, Governor of the Department of Mindanao and Sulu.**

[ Act No. 2918, March 24, 1920 ]

AN ACT AUTHORIZING THE PAYMENT OF A SPECIAL REMUNERATION TO FRANK W. CARPENTER, GOVERNOR OF THE DEPARTMENT OF MINDANAO AND SULU.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The Secretary of the Interior is hereby authorized to pay, out of any items of expense appropriated for the Bureau of Non-Christian Tribes by Act Numbered Twenty-eight hundred and seventy-five, the sum of fifty thousand pesos to Frank W. Carpenter, Governor of the Department of Mindanao and Sulu, in recognition of the extraordinary services rendered by him to the Government of the Philippine Islands. This special remuneration shall be paid without prejudice to any priveleges or rights granted by existing law.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 24, 1920.
