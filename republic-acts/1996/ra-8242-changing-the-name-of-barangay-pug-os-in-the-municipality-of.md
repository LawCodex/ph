# Republic Act No. 8242

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Pug-os in the Municipality of Sinait, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Barangay Dean Leopoldo Yabes**

Tenth Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8242, December 20, 1996 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BARANGAY PUG-OS IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SINAIT, PROVINCE OF ILOCOS SUR, TO BARANGAY DEAN LEOPOLDO YABES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Pug-os in the Municipality of Sinait, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Barangay Dean Leopoldo Yabes.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 20, 1996.
