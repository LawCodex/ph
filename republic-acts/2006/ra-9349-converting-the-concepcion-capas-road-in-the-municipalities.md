# Republic Act No. 9349

**An Act Converting the Concepcion – Capas Road in the Municipalities of Concepcion and Capas, Province of Tarlac into a National Road**

Thirteenth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fifth day of July, two thousand five.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9349 August 4, 2006**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE CONCEPCION – CAPAS ROAD IN THE MUNICIPALITIES OF CONCEPCION AND CAPAS, PROVINCE OF TARLAC INTO A NATIONAL ROAD

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

The Concepcion – Capas Road in the municipalities of Concepcion and Capas, Province of Tarlac, consisting of 7.5 kilometers, more or less, is hereby converted into a national road.

## SEC. 2

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

## SEC. 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
