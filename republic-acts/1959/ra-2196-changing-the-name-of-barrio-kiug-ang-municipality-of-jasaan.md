# Republic Act No. 2196

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Kiug-Ang, Municipality of Jasaan, Province of Misamis Oriental, to Luz**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2196**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Kiug-Ang, Municipality of Jasaan, Province of Misamis Oriental, to Luz**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Kiug-ang in the Municipality of Jasaan, Province of Misamis Oriental is changed to Luz, to wit: Barrio Luz, Municipality of Jasaan, Province to Misamis Oriental.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 7, 1959.
