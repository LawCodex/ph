# Republic Act No. 9463

**An Act Naming the San Nicolas-Solsona portion of the Ilocos Norte-Apayao Road as the Josefa Llanes Escoda National Highway**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9463 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT NAMING THE SAN NICOLAS-SOLSONA PORTION OF THE ILOCOS NORTE-APAYAO ROAD AS THE JOSEFA LLANES ESCODA NATIONAL HIGHWAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

This Act shall be known as the **"_Josefa Llanes Escoda National Highway Act_ "**.

## Sec. 2

The San Nicolas - Solsona portion of the Ilocos Norte-Apayao Road starting from Km. Post 484+000 to Km. Post 524+000 in the Province of Ilocos Norte is hereby named as the Josefa Llanes Escoda National Highway.

## Sec. 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
