# Republic Act No. 2877

**An Act to Amend Item (3), Subparagraph (ab), for the Province of Leyte, Page 700, Paragraph b, Title A, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2877**

**An Act to Amend Item (3), Subparagraph (ab), for the Province of Leyte, Page 700, Paragraph b, Title A, Section Three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (3), subparagraph (ab), for the Province of Leyte, page 700, paragraph b, Title A, Section three of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(3) Construction of 2-room building including painting, Campokpok Barrio School, Tabango ₱12,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
