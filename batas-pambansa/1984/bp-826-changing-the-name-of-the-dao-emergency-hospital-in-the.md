# Batas Pambansa Blg. 826

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Capiz, to Senator Gerardo M. Roxas Memorial District Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 826**

An Act Changing the Name of the Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Capiz, to Senator Gerardo M. Roxas Memorial District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Dao, Province of Capiz, is changed to Senator Gerardo M. Roxas Memorial District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
