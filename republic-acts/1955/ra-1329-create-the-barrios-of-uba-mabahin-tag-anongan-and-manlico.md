# Republic Act No. 1329

**An Act to Create the Barrios of Uba, Mabahin, Tag-Anongan and Manlico, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Surigao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1329**

**An Act to Create the Barrios of Uba, Mabahin, Tag-Anongan and Manlico, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Surigao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Uba, Mabahin, Tag-anongan and Manlico, in the Municipality of Cortes, Province of Surigao, are hereby constituted into barrios in the same municipality, to be known as Uba, Mabahin, Tag-anongan and Manlico, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 16, 1955.
