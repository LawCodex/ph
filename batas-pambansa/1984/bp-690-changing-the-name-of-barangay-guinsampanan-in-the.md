# Batas Pambansa Blg. 690

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Guinsampanan in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Barangay Monfort Sur**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 690**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Guinsampanan in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, to Barangay Monfort Sur

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Guinsampanan in the Municipality of Dumangas, Province of Iloilo, is changed to Barangay Monfort Sur.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
