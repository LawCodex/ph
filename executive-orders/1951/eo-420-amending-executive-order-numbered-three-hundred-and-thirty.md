# Executive Order No. 420

**Amending Executive Order Numbered Three Hundred and Thirty-One, Executive Order Numbered Three Hundred and Forty-Three and for Other Purposes**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 420, March 08, 1951 ]

**AMENDING EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED AND THIRTY-ONE, EXECUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED THREE HUNDRED AND FORTY-THREE AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.**

By virtue of the powers vested in me by section 3 of Republic Act No. 509, entitled “An Act declaring national policy, authorizing the President, of the Philippines for a, limited period to fix the ceiling prices of commodities and to. promulgate rules and regulations regarding prices of commodities to effectuate such policy, and authorizing the appropriation of a certain sum for the, purpose,” and upon the recommendation of the Price Administration Board, I, **ELPIDIO QUIRINO** , President of the. Philippines, do hereby order:

## Section 1

Section one of Executive Order No. 331, dated July 7, 1950, is hereby amended by increasing and setting up new ceiling prices for Crescent and Maxwell House Coffee as follows:

## Section 3

The ceiling prices herein above fixed include the 7 per cent sales tax and 1 per cent municipal tax.

## Section 4

This Order shall take effect immediately.

Done in the City of Manila, this 8th day of March, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and fifty-one, and of the Independence of the Philippines, the fifth.

(SGD.) **ELPIDIO QUIRINO**
President of the Philippines

By the President:

(SGD.) **TEODORO EVANGELISTA**
Executive Secretary
