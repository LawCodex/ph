# Batas Pambansa Blg. 859

**An Act Changing the Name of the Lupao Barangay High School in the Municipality of Lupao, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Doña Juana Chioco Barangay High School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 859**

An Act Changing the Name of the Lupao Barangay High School in the Municipality of Lupao, Province of Nueva Ecija, to Doña Juana Chioco Barangay High School

## Section 1

The name of the Lupao Barangay High School in the Municipality of Lupao, Province of Nueva Ecija, is changed to Doña Juana Chioco Barangay High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
