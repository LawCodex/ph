# Republic Act No. 7233

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Sergio Osmeña, Sr., Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Sergio Osmeña, Sr., Province of Zamboanga Del Norte, From Ten (10) to Twenty-five (25) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7233, March 19, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE SERGIO OSMEÑA, SR., MUNICIPAL HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SERGIO OSMEÑA, SR., PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL NORTE, FROM TEN (10) TO TWENTY-FIVE (25) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Sergio Osmeña, Sr., Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Sergio Osmeña, Sr., Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby increased from ten (10) to twenty-five (25) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
