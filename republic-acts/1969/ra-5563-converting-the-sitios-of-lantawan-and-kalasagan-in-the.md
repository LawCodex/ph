# Republic Act No. 5563

**An Act Converting the Sitios of Lantawan and Kalasagan in the Municipality of Lupon, Province of Davao, Into a Barrio, to Be Known as the Barrio of Lantawan of the Same Municipality**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5563, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SITIOS OF LANTAWAN AND KALASAGAN IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LUPON, PROVINCE OF DAVAO, INTO A BARRIO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE BARRIO OF LANTAWAN OF THE SAME MUNICIPALITY.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Lantawan and Kalasagan in the Municipality of Lupon, Province of Davao, are hereby converted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Lantawan of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
