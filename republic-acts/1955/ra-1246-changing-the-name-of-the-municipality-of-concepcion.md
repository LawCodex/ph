# Republic Act No. 1246

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Gregorio Del Pilar**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1246**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Ilocos Sur, to Gregorio Del Pilar**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Concepcion, Province of Ilocos Sur, is hereby changed to Gregorio del Pilar.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 10, 1955.
