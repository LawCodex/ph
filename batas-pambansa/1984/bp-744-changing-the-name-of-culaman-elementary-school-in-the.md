# Batas Pambansa Blg. 744

**An Act Changing the Name of Culaman Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Harden Joyce Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 744**

An Act Changing the Name of Culaman Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao Del Sur, to Harden Joyce Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Culaman Elementary School in the Municipality of Jose Abad Santos, Province of Davao del Sur, shall be changed to Harden Joyce Elementary School.

## Section 2

The record of the Ministry of Education, Culture and Sports bearing the name of the school mentioned in Section 1 hereof shall be changed and be substituted by the name Harden Joyce Elementary School.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect immediately upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
