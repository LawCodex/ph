# Republic Act No. 9411

**An Act Declaring January 22 of Each Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the City of Vigan, Ilocos Sur to Commemorate Its Anniversary as a City**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-fourth day of July, two thousand six.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9411 March 24, 2007**

AN ACT DECLARING JANUARY 22 OF EACH YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF VIGAN, ILOCOS SUR TO COMMEMORATE ITS ANNIVERSARY AS A CITY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

January 22 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the City of Vigan, Ilocos Sur to commemorate its anniversary as a city.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days from its publication in at least two newspapers of general circulation in the Philippines.
