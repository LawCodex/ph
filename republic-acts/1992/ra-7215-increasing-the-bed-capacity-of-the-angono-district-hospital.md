# Republic Act No. 7215

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of the Angono District Hospital in the Municipality of Angono, Province of Rizal, From Fifty (50) to One Hundred (100) Beds, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, nineteen hundred and ninety-one.

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7215, March 06, 1992 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE ANGONO DISTRICT HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ANGONO, PROVINCE OF RIZAL, FROM FIFTY (50) TO ONE HUNDRED (100) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR.1âшphi1

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of the Angono District Hospital in the Municipality of Angono, Province of Rizal, is hereby increased from fifty (50) to one hundred (100) beds.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
