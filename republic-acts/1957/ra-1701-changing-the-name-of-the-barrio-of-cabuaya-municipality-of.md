# Republic Act No. 1701

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cabuaya, Municipality of Mati, Province of Davao, to Barrio Dawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1701**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Cabuaya, Municipality of Mati, Province of Davao, to Barrio Dawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Cabuaya, Municipality of Mati, Province of Davao, is changed to barrio Dawan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
