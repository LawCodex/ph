# Republic Act No. 1763

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Puangi in the Municipality of Dipaculao, Province of Quezon**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1763**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Puangi in the Municipality of Dipaculao, Province of Quezon**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Puangi in the Municipality of Dipaculao, Province of Quezon, is hereby constituted into a new barrio in the said municipality to be known as the barrio of Puangi.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
