# Republic Act No. 5399

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Dinokot in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Barrio Ricardo G. Macias**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5399**

An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Dinokot in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Barrio Ricardo G. Macias

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Dinokot in the Municipality of Sindangan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is hereby changed to Barrio Ricardo G. Macias.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
