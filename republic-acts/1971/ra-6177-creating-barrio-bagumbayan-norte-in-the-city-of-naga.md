# Republic Act No. 6177

**An Act Creating Barrio Bagumbayan Norte in the City of Naga**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6177, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO BAGUMBAYAN NORTE IN THE CITY OF NAGA

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Sitio Bagumbayan Norte, City of Naga, is hereby constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Bagumbayan Norte.

## Section 2

The Barrio of Bagumbayan Norte shall be bounded on the North by the boundary line between the Municipality of Canaman and the City of Naga; on the east, by Barrio Calawag; on the south, by the culvert beside De Jesus' residence; on the west, by the boundary line between the Municipality of Canaman and the City of Naga.1âшphi1

## Section 3

The new barrio officials shall be appointed by the City Mayor, and they shall hold office until their successors are elected in the next regular barrio elections.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
