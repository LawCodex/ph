# Republic Act No. 8986

**An Act Declaring March Twenty-One, a Special Working Holiday in the City of Calapan, Province of Oriental Mindoro**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8986 December 9, 2000**

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH TWENTY-ONE, A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF CALAPAN, PROVINCE OF ORIENTAL MINDORO

## Section 1

March twenty-one of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the City of Calapan, Province of Oriental Mindoro to commemorate the creation/birth of the said City.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **December 9, 2000**
