# Republic Act No. 4971

**An Act Amending Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Four Thousand Six Hundred and Five**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4971**

An Act Amending Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Four Thousand Six Hundred and Five

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section Two of Republic Act Numbered Four thousand six hundred and five is hereby amended to read as follows:

## Section 2

The grantee is hereby authorized to manufacture and supply one hundred tons of ice a day.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
