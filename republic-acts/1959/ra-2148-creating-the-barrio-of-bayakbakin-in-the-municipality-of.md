# Republic Act No. 2148

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bayakbakin in the Municipality of Torrijos, Province of Marinduque**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2148**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Bayakbakin in the Municipality of Torrijos, Province of Marinduque**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Bayakbakin in the Municipality of Torrijos, Province of Marinduque, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Bayakbakin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 24, 1959.
