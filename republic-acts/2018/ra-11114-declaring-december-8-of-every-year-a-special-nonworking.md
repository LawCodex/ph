# Republic Act No. 11114

**An Act Declaring December 8 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Agoo, Province of La Union, to Be Known as Agoo Day**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11114**

AN ACT DECLARING DECEMBER 8 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF AGOO, PROVINCE OF LA UNION, TO BE KNOWN AS "AGOO DAY’

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Decembers of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Agoo, Province of La Union, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as "Agoo Day". **Section 2.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

**VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

**GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
Speaker of the House of Representatives

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on May 2, 2017 and September 10, 2018, respectively.
