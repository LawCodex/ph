# Republic Act No. 8011

**An Act Changing the Name of the Leyte State School of Arts and Trades in Tanauan, Leyte, to Leyte College of Arts and Trades**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8011, May 25, 1995 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE LEYTE STATE SCHOOL OF ARTS AND TRADES IN TANAUAN, LEYTE, TO LEYTE COLLEGE OF ARTS AND TRADES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Leyte State School of Arts and Trades in Tanauan, Leyte, is hereby changed to Leyte College of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

The Leyte College of Arts and Trades shall be under the supervision of the Commission on Higher Education.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on May 25, 1995 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
