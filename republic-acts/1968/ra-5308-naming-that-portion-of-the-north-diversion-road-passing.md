# Republic Act No. 5308

**An Act Naming that Portion of the North Diversion Road Passing Through the Province of Bulacan "Marcelo H. Del Pilar Highway"**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5308**

An Act Naming that Portion of the North Diversion Road Passing Through the Province of Bulacan "Marcelo H. Del Pilar Highway"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

That portion of the North Diversion Road passing through the territorial jurisdiction of the Province of Bulacan is hereby named "Marcelo H. del Pilar Highway."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
