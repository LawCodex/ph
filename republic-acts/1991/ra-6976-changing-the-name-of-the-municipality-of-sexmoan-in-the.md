# Republic Act No. 6976

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Sexmoan in the Province of Pampanga to Municipality of Sasmuan**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6976 January 15, 1991**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF SEXMOAN IN THE PROVINCE OF PAMPANGA TO MUNICIPALITY OF SASMUAN

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Sexmoan in the Province of Pampanga changed to Municipality of Sasmuan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **January 15, 1991**.
