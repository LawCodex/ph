# Republic Act No. 9455

**An Act Changing the Name of Odiongan Elementary School in Barangay San Jose, City of Tanjay, Province of Negros Oriental to Egmidio V. Manzo Memorial Elementary School**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9455 May 15, 2007**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF ODIONGAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN BARANGAY SAN JOSE, CITY OF TANJAY, PROVINCE OF NEGROS ORIENTAL TO EGMIDIO V. MANZO MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## SECTION 1

_Renaming of Elementary School._ \- The name of Odiongan Elementary School in Barangay San Jose, City of Tanjay, Province of Negros Oriental is hereby changed to Egmidio V. Manzo Memorial Elementary School.

## SEC. 2

_Effectivity._ \- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette._
