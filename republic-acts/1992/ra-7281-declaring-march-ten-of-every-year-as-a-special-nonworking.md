# Republic Act No. 7281

**An Act Declaring March Ten of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Province of Abra to Be Known as Abra Day**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7281, March 24, 1992 ]

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH TEN OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF ABRA TO BE KNOWN AS ABRA DAY

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

March Ten of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Abra to commemorate the creation of the Province, to be known as Abra Day.
