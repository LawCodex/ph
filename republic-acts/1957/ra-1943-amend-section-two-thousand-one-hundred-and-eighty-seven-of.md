# Republic Act No. 1943

**An Act to Amend Section Two Thousand One Hundred and Eighty-Seven of the Revised Administrative Code**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1943**

**An Act to Amend Section Two Thousand One Hundred and Eighty-Seven of the Revised Administrative Code**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Paragraph one of section two thousand one hundred eighty-seven of the Revised Administrative Code is hereby amended so as to read as follows:

"Sec. 2187. _Compensation of vice-mayor and councilors—Full pay for sick mayor._ -The municipal council may grant to the vice-mayor and each councilor a per diem not to exceed ten pesos in the municipalities of the first class; eight pesos in municipalities of the second class; six pesos in municipalities of the third class; and four pesos in the fourth and fifth class municipalities, for each day of regular or special session of the council actually attended by them: _Provided, However_ , That no per diems may be granted for more than four special sessions a month. When, by reason of absence, suspension, or other disability, the mayor ceases to discharge the duties of his office, the vice-mayor or councilor acting as mayor shall receive compensation equivalent to the salary of the mayor during the period of such service."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 22, 1957.
