# Batas Pambansa Blg. 299

**An Act Establishing a Ten-Bed Capacity Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Surigao Del Norte, to Be Known as Socorro Emergency Hospital, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds therefor.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 299**

An Act Establishing a Ten-Bed Capacity Municipal Hospital in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Surigao Del Norte, to Known as Socorro Emergency Hospital, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor

## Section 1

There shall be established under the direct supervision of the Office of the Minister (Ministry of Health), a ten-bed capacity municipal hospital in the Municipality of Socorro, Province of Surigao del Norte, to be known as the Socorro Emergency Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount needed for the operation of the hospital shall be determined in accordance with Section 40 of Presidential Decree No. 1177 and charged to the appropriations authorized by Batas Pambansa Blg. 131 for the Ministry of Health intended for new hospitals. Thereafter, such sum as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The Minister of Health shall determine the manner by which said hospital shall be made operational.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
