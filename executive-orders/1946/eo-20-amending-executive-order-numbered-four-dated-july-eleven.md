# Executive Order No. 20

**Amending Executive Order Numbered Four, Dated July Eleven, Nineteen Hundred and Forty-Six Entitled “Fixing the Salaries and Allowances of Officers and Enlisted Men of the Philippine Army.”**

MALACAÑAN PALACE  
MANILA

BY THE PRESIDENT OF THE PHILIPPINES

[ EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 20, October 15, 1946 ]

AMENDING EXEUTIVE ORDER NUMBERED FOUR, DATED JULYELEVEN, NINETEEN HUUNDRED AND FORTY-SIX ENTITLED “FIXING THE SALARIES AND ALLOWANCES OF OFFICERS AND ENLISTED MEN OF THE PHILIPPINE ARMY.”
