# Republic Act No. 2446

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Section One, Republic Act Numbered Two Thousand Ninety-Three**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2446**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Section One, Republic Act Numbered Two Thousand Ninety-Three**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Items (m) and (n), for the Province of Leyte, sub-paragraph twenty-three, paragraph a, Title D, section one of Republic Act Numbered Two thousand ninety-three, is hereby amended to read as follows:

"(m) San Roque Waterworks, Macrohon ₱5,000.00

"(n) San Isidro Waterworks, Malitbog 20,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
