# Republic Act No. 5603

**An Act Changing the Name of Sibaguan Elementary School in the City of Roxas to Juan Fuentes Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5603, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF SIBAGUAN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN THE CITY OF ROXAS TO JUAN FUENTES ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Sibaguan Elementary School in the City of Roxas is hereby changed, to Juan Fuentes Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
