# Batas Pambansa Blg. 854

**An Act Changing the Name of Taal (Pulilan) Elementary School to Don Francisco Castro Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 854**

An Act Changing the Name of Taal (Pulilan) Elementary School to Don Francisco Castro Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Taal (Pulilan) Elementary School, situated at Barangay Taal, Pulilan, Bulacan, is hereby changed to Don Francisco Castro Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
