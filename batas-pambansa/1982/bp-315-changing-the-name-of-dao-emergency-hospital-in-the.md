# Batas Pambansa Blg. 315

**An Act Changing the Name of Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Tobias Fornier, Province of Antique, to Governor Mamerto Portillo Memorial District Hospital.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 315**

An Act Changing the Name of Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Tobias Fornier, Province of Antique, to Governor Mamerto Portillo Memorial District Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Dao Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Tobias Fornier, Province of Antique, is changed to Governor Mamerto Portillo Memorial District Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 14, 1982
