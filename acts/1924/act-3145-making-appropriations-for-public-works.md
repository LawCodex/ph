# Act No. 3145

**An Act Making Appropriations for Public Works.**

[ Act No. 3145, March 09, 1924 ]

AN ACT MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR PUBLIC WORKS.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The following sums, or so much thereof as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated, out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, to be at the disposal of the Director of Public Works for the purposes mentioned hereunder, after allotment by the Secretary of Commerce and Communications and approval by a Joint Committee to be appointed by the Senate or the President thereof and the House of Representatives or the Steering Committee, and presided over by the presiding officers of both Houses of the Legislature: _Provided,_ That the appropriations made in paragraphs (b), (g), (h), (i), (k), (l), (m), (o), and (u) of this Act, as hereinafter set forth in detail, shall be made available immediately by the Director of Public Works:

## Section 2

All balances of the several appropriations made by this Act the destination of which has not yet been detemined by the proper authority on June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and twenty-four, and all balances of appropriations which remain unexpended on June thirtieth, nineteen hundred and twenty-five, shall revert to the unappropriated funds in the Insular Treasury, and thereafter shall not be available for expenditure except upon appropriation by the Legislature.

## Section 3

The Secretary of Commerce and Communications, with the approval of the Joint Committee to be appointed by the Senate or the President thereof and the House of Representatives or the Steering Committee, may, for the purposes of allotment, transfer unexpended portions of any item of appropriation under this Act to any other item hereunder.

## Section 4

In the construction of school houses for which appropriations are made in this Act, mixed materials shall be used when the funds provided for this purpose amount to less than five thousand pesos.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 9, 1924, with the exception of items (c), (d), (e), (j), (k), (I), (m), (n), and (y), which have been vetoed.
