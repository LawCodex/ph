# Republic Act No. 1417

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Longos, Province of Laguna, to Municipality of Kalayaan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1417**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Longos, Province of Laguna, to Municipality of Kalayaan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Longos, Province of Laguna, is changed to Municipality of Kalayaan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 17, 1956.
