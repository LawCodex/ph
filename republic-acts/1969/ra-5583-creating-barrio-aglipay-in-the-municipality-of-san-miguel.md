# Republic Act No. 5583

**An Act Creating Barrio Aglipay in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bohol**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5583, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CREATING BARRIO AGLIPAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN MIGUEL, PROVINCE OF BOHOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Sitio Aglipay in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bohol, is hereby created and constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Aglipay.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
