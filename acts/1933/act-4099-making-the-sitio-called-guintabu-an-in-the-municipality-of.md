# Act No. 4099

**An Act Making the Sitio Called Guintabu-An, in the Municipality of Saravia, Province of Occidental Negros, a National Park.**

[ Acts No. 4099, November 29, 1933 ]

AN ACT MAKING THE SITIO CALLED GUINTABU-AN, IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SARAVIA, PROVINCE OF OCCIDENTAL NEGROS, A NATIONAL PARK.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The sitio called Guintabu-an, in the municipality of Saravia, Province of Occidental Negros, is hereby made a national park and a committee is created, composed of the president of Saravia and two other members designated by the municipal council of said municipality, which committee shall locate said sitio of Guintabu-an and endeavor to obtain from its owners the donation of an area of not less than four hectares thereof. This committee shall submit its report to the Legislature not later than ten clays after the inauguration of the Tenth Legislature.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect in accordance with section eleven of the Administrative Code.

Approved, November 29, 1933.
