# Republic Act No. 2846

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of New Piñan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Piñan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2846**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of New Piñan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte, to Piñan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of New Piñan in the Province of Zamboanga del Norte, is changed to Piñan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
