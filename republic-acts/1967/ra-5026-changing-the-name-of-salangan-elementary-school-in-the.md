# Republic Act No. 5026

**An Act Changing the Name of Salangan Elementary School in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, into Mababang Paaralang Pang-Alaala Kay Dr. Juan Pascual**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5026**

An Act Changing the Name of Salangan Elementary School in the Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, into Mababang Paaralang Pang-Alaala Kay Dr. Juan Pascual

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Salangan Elementary School in Barrio Salangan, Municipality of San Miguel, Province of Bulacan, is hereby changed into Mababang Paaralang Pang-alaala kay Dr. Juan Pascual.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
