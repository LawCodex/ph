# Republic Act No. 6839

**An Act Renaming the Ilijan Elementary School to Pedro S. Tolentino Memorial Elementary School**

Eighth Congress

REPUBLIC ACT No. 6839

An Act Renaming the Ilijan Elementary School to Pedro S. Tolentino Memorial Elementary School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Ilijan Elementary School located in Barangay Ilijan, City of Batangas, is hereby renamed as the Pedro S. Tolentino Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 5, 1990.
