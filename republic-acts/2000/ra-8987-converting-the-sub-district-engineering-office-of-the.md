# Republic Act No. 8987

**An Act Converting the Sub-District Engineering Office of the Department of Public Works and Highways in the City of Butuan into a Full-Fledged Engineering District to be known as the Butuan City Engineering District and appropriating funds therefor**

Eleventh Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 8987 December 12, 2000**

AN ACT CONVERTING THE SUB-DISTRICT ENGINEERING OFFICE OF THE DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND HIGHWAYS IN THE CITY OF BUTUAN INTO A FULL-FLEDGED ENGINEERING DISTRICT TO BE KNOWN AS THE BUTUAN CITY ENGINEERING DISTRICT AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

## Section 1

The Sub-District Engineering Office of the Department of Public Works and Highways in the City of Butuan is hereby converted into a full-fledged engineering district to be known as the Butuan City Engineering District.

## Section 2

The said engineering district shall comprise only the City of Butuan.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

The Secretary of the Department of Public Works and Highways shall promulgate the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provisions of this Act.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, **December 12, 2000**
