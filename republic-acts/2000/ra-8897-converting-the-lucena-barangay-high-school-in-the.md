# Republic Act No. 8897

**An Act Converting the Lucena Barangay High School in the Municipality of Prosperidad, Province of Agusan Del Sur Into a National Government-Funded Secondary School to Be Known as the Lucena National High School and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8897, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT CONVERTING THE LUCENA BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PROSPERIDAD, PROVINCE OF AGUSAN DEL SUR INTO A NATIONAL GOVERNMENT-FUNDED SECONDARY SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE LUCENA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Lucena Barangay High School in the Municipality of Prosperidad, Province of Agusan del Sur is hereby converted into a regular secondary school under the control and supervision of the national government through the Department of Education, Culture and Sports.

## Section 2

This school shall be known officially as the Lucena National High School.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
