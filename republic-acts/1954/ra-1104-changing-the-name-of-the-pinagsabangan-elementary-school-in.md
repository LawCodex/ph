# Republic Act No. 1104

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pinagsabangan Elementary School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Juan L. Luna Memorial School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1104**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pinagsabangan Elementary School in the Municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, to Juan L. Luna Memorial School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pinagsabangan Elementary School in the municipality of Naujan, Province of Oriental Mindoro, is changed to Juan L. Luna Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1954.
