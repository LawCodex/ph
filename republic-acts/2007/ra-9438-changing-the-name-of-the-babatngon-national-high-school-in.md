# Republic Act No. 9438

**An Act Changing the Name of the Babatngon National High School in the Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte to Juan S. Tismo National High School**

Thirteenth Congress  
Third Special Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the nineteenth day of February, two thousand seven.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 9438 April 27, 2007**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE BABATNGON NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF BABATNGON, PROVINCE OF LEYTE TO JUAN S. TISMO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

**Section 1._Renaming a National High School._** – The name of the Babatngon National High School in the Municipality of Babatngon, Province of Leyte is hereby changed to Juan S. Tismo National High School.

**Sec. 2._Effectivity._** – This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette.

Approved,
