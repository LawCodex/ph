# Republic Act No. 5484

**An Act Changing the Name of Batac Central School to Mariano Marcos Memorial Elementary School**

REPUBLIC ACT NO. 5484, June 21, 1969 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF BATAC CENTRAL SCHOOL TO MARIANO MARCOS MEMORIAL ELEMENTARY SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Batac Central School is hereby change to Mariano Marcos Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 21, 1969.
