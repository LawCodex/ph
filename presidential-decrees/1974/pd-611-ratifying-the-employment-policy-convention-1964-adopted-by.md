# Presidential Decree No. 611

**Ratifying the Employment Policy Convention, 1964, Adopted By the International Labour Organization**

MALACAÑANG  
M a n i l a

**PRESIDENTIAL DECREE No. 611 December 18, 1974**

RATIFYING THE EMPLOYMENT POLICY CONVENTION, 1964, ADOPTED BY THE INTERNATIONAL LABOUR ORGANIZATION

I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, by virtue of the powers vested in me by the Constitution, do hereby decree and order that the Employment Policy Convention, 1964 (Convention No. 122), adopted by the International Labour Organization on July 9, 1964, at its Forty-eight session held at Geneva, Switzerland, shall be, as it is, hereby ratified.

Let a copy of this Decree be transmitted to the Director-General, International Labor Office pursuant to its rules.

Done in the City of Manila, this 18th day of December, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-four.
