# Republic Act No. 2128

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of New Piñan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2128**

**An Act Creating Certain Barrios in the Municipality of New Piñan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The following sitios are constituted into barrios in the Municipality of New Piñan, Province of Zamboanga del Norte:

1\. The sitios of Desin, Upper Disoy, San Fernando, San Vicente, Santa Fe, Sikitan, Dewacat, Dilawa and Lambog to be known as the barrio of Villarico; and

2\. The sitios of Upper Boboringan, Lower Boboringan, Penacio, Bagong Silang and Situbo to be known as the barrio of Teresita.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 7, 1959.
