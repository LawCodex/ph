# Republic Act No. 2598

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Kaga-Olas in the Municipality of Matanao, Province of Davao, to Swerte**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2598**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Kaga-Olas in the Municipality of Matanao, Province of Davao, to Swerte**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Kaga-olas in the Municipality of Matanao, Province of Davao, is changed to Swerte.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
