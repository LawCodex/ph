# Republic Act No. 2789

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bulalacao in the Province of Oriental Mindoro to San Pedro**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2789**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Bulalacao in the Province of Oriental Mindoro to San Pedro**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Bulalacao in the Province of Oriental Mindoro is changed to San Pedro.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
