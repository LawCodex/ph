# Act No. 31

**An Act Fixing the Salary of the Chief Statistician.**

[ Act No. 31, October 24, 1900 ]

AN ACT FIXING THE SALARY OF THE CHIEF STATISTICIAN.

By authority of the President of the United States, be it enacted by the United States Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The animal salary of the chief statistician shall be three thousand five hundred dollars in money of the United States.

## Section 2

This Act shall relate to the first day of October, nineteen hundred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect on its passage.

Enacted, October 24, 1900.
