# Republic Act No. 8139

**An Act Establishing a National Science High School in the Capital Town of Tandag, Province of Surigao Del Sur, to Be Known as the Tandag National Science High School, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Nineth Congress  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT No. 8139, July 16, 1995 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL IN THE CAPITAL TOWN OF TANDAG, PROVINCE OF SURIGAO DEL SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE TANDAG NATIONAL SCIENCE HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national science high school in the capital town of Tandag, Province of Surigao del Sur, to be known as the Tandag National Science High School.

## Section 2

The school shall be situated within the eighth-hectare campus of the Surigao del Sur Polytechnic College in Barangay Governor Adela Serra Ty, Municipality of Tago, Surigao del Sur.

## Section 3

The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.

## Section 4

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,
