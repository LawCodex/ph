# Executive Order No. 378

**Creatingthe Philippine Tourist Board**

**EXECUTIVE ORDER NO. 378 February 29, 1972**

CREATING THE PHILIPPINE TOURIST BOARD

WHEREAS, there is need to coordinate efforts in the development and promotion of tourism in the Philippines, and to involve all entities directly or indirectly connected with tourism so as to provide a wholesome atmosphere for tourists in the Philippines;

NOW, THEREFORE, I, FERDINAND E. MARCOS, President of the Philippines, do hereby create the Philippine Tourist Board composed of the following:

| Commissioner of Tourism | Chairman | |---|---| | President, Philippine Tourist & Travel Association | Vice-Chairman | | Commissioner of Immigration | Member | | Commissioner of Customs | Member | | Director, Bureau of Quarantine | Member | | Director, Civil Aeronautics Administration | Member | | Director, Civil Aeronautics Board | Member | | Manager, Manila International Airport | Member | | Director, Parks and Wildlife Office | Member | | Administrator, NACIDA | Member | | Chief, METROCOM | Member | | President, Philippine Chamber of Tourism | Member | | President, Associated Hotels of the Philippines | Member | | President, Hotels & Restaurant Association of the Philippines | Member | | President, Advertising Council of the Philippines | Member | | President, Philippine Press Institute | Member | | President, Association of Tour Operators of the Philippines | Member | | President, Philippine Society of Travel Agencies | Member | | President, Taxicab Operators of the Philippines | Member | | President, Bus Operators Association of the Philippines | Member | | President, Hunting and Fishing Association | Member | | President, Chamber of Cottage Industries | Member | | President, Airlines Operators of the Philippines | Member | | President of the Jaycees, Rotary and Lions Clubs in the Philippines | Member | | Representative of the Mayor, Manila | Member | | Representative of the Mayor, Pasay | Member | | Representative of the Mayor, Makati | Member | | Representative of the Mayor, Quezon City | Member |

The Board shall serve as an advisory body to establish the proper direction, the long-term and short-term objectives, the standards, and to recommend the necessary legislation, towards the advancement of tourism in the Philippines.

Done in the City of Manila, this 29th day of February, in the year of Our Lord, nineteen hundred and seventy-two.
