# Republic Act No. 7221

**An Act Changing the Name of the Wawa Elementary School in San Agustin Village (Formerly Sitio Wawa-bukid) in Barangay Moonwalk, Municipality of Parañaque, Metro Manila, to San Agustin Elementary School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7221, March 11, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE WAWA ELEMENTARY SCHOOL IN SAN AGUSTIN VILLAGE (FORMERLY SITIO WAWA-BUKID) IN BARANGAY MOONWALK, MUNICIPALITY OF PARAÑAQUE, METRO MANILA, TO SAN AGUSTIN ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Wawa Elementary School in San Agustin Village (formerly Sitio Wawa-Bukid) in Barangay Moonwalk, Municipality of Parañaque, Metro Manila, is hereby changed to San Agustin Elementary School.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
