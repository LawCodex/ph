# Batas Pambansa Blg. 808

**An Act Changing the Name of the Balangiga Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Balangiga, Eastern Samar, Into Albino M. Duran Memorial Hospital**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 808**

An Act Changing the Name of the Balangiga Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Balangiga, Eastern Samar, into Albino M. Duran Memorial Hospital

## Section 1

The name of the Balangiga Emergency Hospital in the Municipality of Balangiga, Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Albino M. Duran Memorial Hospital.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
