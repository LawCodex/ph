# Batas Pambansa Blg. 818

**An Act Changing the Name of the Nabali-An Elementary School in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Marcelo Vasquez Memorial School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 818**

An Act Changing the Name of the Nabali-An Elementary School in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, to Don Marcelo Vasquez Memorial School

## Section 1

The name of the Nabali-an Elementary School in the Municipality of Himamaylan, Province of Negros Occidental, is changed to Don Marcelo Vasquez Memorial School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
