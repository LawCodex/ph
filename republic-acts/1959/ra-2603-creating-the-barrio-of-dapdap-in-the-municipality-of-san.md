# Republic Act No. 2603

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Dapdap in the Municipality of San Remigio, Province of Cebu**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2603**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Dapdap in the Municipality of San Remigio, Province of Cebu**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Dapdap in the Municipality of San Remigio, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of the said Municipality to be known as the barrio of Dapdap.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
