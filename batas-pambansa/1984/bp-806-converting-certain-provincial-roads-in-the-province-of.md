# Batas Pambansa Blg. 806

**An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Romblon Into National Roads**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 806**

An Act Converting Certain Provincial Roads in the Province of Romblon into National Roads

## Section 1

The Ferrol-Buenavista Looc Provincial Road, the Linawan-Mablaran Falls Provincial Road both in the Island of Tablas and the Magdiwang-Agtiwa-San Fernando Provincial Road in the Island of Sibuyan, all in the Province of Romblon, are converted into national roads.

## Section 2

The amount necessary for the improvement and maintenance of said roads shall continue to be funded out of the lump sum appropriation authorized under Batas Pambansa Blg. 640 for the repair and maintenance of local roads until such time that the maintenance funds for said roads shall have been provided for in the succeeding appropriations acts.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
