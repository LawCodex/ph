# Batas Pambansa Blg. 542

**An Act Establishing a National Vocational High School in the City of Toledo to Be Known as the Toledo City National Vocational High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 542**

An Act Establishing a National Vocational High School in the City of Toledo to be Known as the Toledo City National Vocational High School

## Section 1

There shall be established a vocational high school in the City of Toledo to be known as the Toledo, City National Vocational High School.

## Section 2

Such sum as may be necessary for the operation and maintenance of said school shall be provided in subsequent General Appropriations Acts until which time the operation of the institution as a national high school shall be deferred.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
