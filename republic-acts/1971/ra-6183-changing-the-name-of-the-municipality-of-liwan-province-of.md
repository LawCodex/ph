# Republic Act No. 6183

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Liwan, Province of Kalinga-Apayao, to the Municipality of Rizal.**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6183, June 19, 1971 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF LIWAN, PROVINCE OF KALINGA-APAYAO, TO THE MUNICIPALITY OF RIZAL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Liwan in the Province of Kalinga-Apayao is changed to the Municipality of Rizal.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, June 19, 1971.
