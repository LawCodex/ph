# Republic Act No. 3561

**An Act Creating One Additional Position of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Iloilo, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3561**

**An Act Creating One Additional Position of Assistant Provincial Fiscal in the Province of Iloilo, Amending for This Purpose Section Sixteen Hundred Seventy-Four of the Revised Administrative Code, as Amended**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The provision of Section sixteen hundred seventy-four of the Revised Administrative Code, as amended, relative to assistant provincial fiscals in the Province of Iloilo, is further amended to read as follows:

"Iloilo, nine assistant provincial fiscals;"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1963.
