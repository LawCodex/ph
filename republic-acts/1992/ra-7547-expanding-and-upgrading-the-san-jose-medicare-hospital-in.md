# Republic Act No. 7547

**An Act Expanding and Upgrading the San Jose Medicare Hospital in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Camarines Sur, From A Fifteen-bed Capacity Medicare Hospital Into A Twenty-five-bed Capacity District Hospital, To Be Known As the Partido District Hospital, and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7547, May 22, 1992 ]

AN ACT EXPANDING AND UPGRADING THE SAN JOSE MEDICARE HOSPITAL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF SAN JOSE, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, FROM A FIFTEEN-BED CAPACITY MEDICARE HOSPITAL INTO A TWENTY-FIVE-BED CAPACITY DISTRICT HOSPITAL, TO BE KNOWN AS THE PARTIDO DISTRICT HOSPITAL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The San Jose Medicare Hospital in the Municipality of San Jose, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby expanded and upgraded into a twenty-five-bed capacity district hospital, to be known as the Partido District Hospital.

## Section 2

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
