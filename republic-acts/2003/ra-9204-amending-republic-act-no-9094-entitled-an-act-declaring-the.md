# Republic Act No. 9204

**An Act Amending Republic Act No. 9094, Entitled "An Act Declaring the Seventh (7th) Day of January of Every Year as a Special Working Public Holiday in the Province of Camiguin to Be Known as 'Araw Ng Camiguin'"**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9204 April 20, 2003**

AN ACT AMENDING REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9094, ENTITLED "AN ACT DECLARING THE SEVENTH (7TH) DAY OF JANUARY OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF CAMIGUIN TO BE KNOWN AS 'ARAW NG CAMIGUIN'"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Act no. 9094, otherwise known as "An Act Declaring the Seventh (7th) Day of January of Every Year as a Special Working Public Holiday in the Province of Camiguin to be known as 'Araw ng Camiguin'", is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Section 1. January seven of every year is hereby declared as a special nonworking holiday in the Province of Camiguin to commemorate its charter day anniversary.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
