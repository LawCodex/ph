# Batas Pambansa Blg. 824

**An Act Changing the Name of the Magsaysay Elementary School to Lamisahan Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 824**

An Act Changing the Name of the Magsaysay Elementary School to Lamisahan Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Magsaysay Elementary School in Barangay Lamisahan, City of Zamboanga, is hereby changed to Lamisahan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 27, 1984
