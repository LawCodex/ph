# Republic Act No. 6752

**An Act Changing the Name of the San Miguel, Municipal High School in San Miguel, Leyte, to San Miguel National High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6752, August 11, 1989 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE SAN MIGUEL, MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN SAN MIGUEL, LEYTE, TO SAN MIGUEL NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the San Miguel Municipal High School in San Miguel, Leyte, is hereby changed to San Miguel National High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.1a⍵⍴h!1

Approved: August 11, 1989.
