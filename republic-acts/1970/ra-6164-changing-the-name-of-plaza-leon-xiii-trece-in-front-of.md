# Republic Act No. 6164

**An Act Changing the Name of Plaza Leon XIII (Trece) in Front of Tondo Church, Manila, to Ka Amado Hernandez**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6164**

**An Act Changing the Name of Plaza Leon XIII (Trece) in Front of Tondo Church, Manila, to Ka Amado Hernandez**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Plaza Leon XIII (Trece) situated in front of Tondo Church, Manila, is hereby changed to Ka Amado Hernandez.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: November 9, 1970.
