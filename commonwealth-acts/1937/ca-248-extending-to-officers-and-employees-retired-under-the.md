# Commonwealth Act No. 248

**An Act Extending to Officers and Employees Retired Under the Provisions of Art Numbered Twenty-Fire Hundred and Eighty-Nine the Privileges Granted By Commonwealth Act Numbered Two Hundred and Six to Officers and Employees Retired Under Act Numbered Four thousand and Fifty-One.**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 248, December 17, 1937 ]

AN ACT EXTENDING TO OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIRED UNDER THE PROVISIONS OF ART NUMBERED TWENTY-FIRE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY-NINE THE PRIVILEGES GRANTED BY COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED TWO HUNDRED AND SIX TO OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES RETIRED UNDER ACT NUMBERED FOUR THOUSAND AND FIFTY-ONE.

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The privileges granted by Commonwealth Act Numbered Two hundred and six to persons retired under the provisions of Act Numbered Four thousand and fifty-one of the Philippine Legislature, and reappointed to any position in the Government, are extended to persons retired under the provisions of Act Numbered Twenty-five hundred and eighty-nine of the Philippine Legislature, who are reappointed to any Government position.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, .December 17, 1937.
