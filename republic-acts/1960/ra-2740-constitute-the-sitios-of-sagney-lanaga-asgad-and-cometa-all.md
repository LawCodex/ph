# Republic Act No. 2740

**An Act to Constitute the Sitios of Sagney, Lanaga, Asgad, and Cometa, All in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Guirang of the Same Municipality**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2740**

**An Act to Constitute the Sitios of Sagney, Lanaga, Asgad, and Cometa, All in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, into a Barrio to be Known as Barrio Guirang of the Same Municipality**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitios of Sagney, Lanaga, Asgad and Cometa, all in the Municipality of Basey, Province of Samar, are hereby constituted into a barrio to be known as Barrio Guirang of the same municipality.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
