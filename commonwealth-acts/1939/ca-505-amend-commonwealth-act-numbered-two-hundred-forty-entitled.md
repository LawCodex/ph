# Commonwealth Act No. 505

**An Act to Amend Commonwealth Act Numbered Two Hundred Forty Entitled " An Act Appropriating the Sum of Five Million Fifty thousand Pesos for Elementary School Buildings."**

[ Commonwealth Act No. 505, October 27, 1939 ]

AN ACT TO AMEND COMMONWEALTH ACT NUMBERED TWO HUNDRED FORTY ENTITLED " AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF FIVE MILLION FIFTY THOUSAND PESOS FOR ELEMENTARY SCHOOL BUILDINGS."

Be it enacted by the National Assembly of the Philippines:

## Section 1

The following items of appropriation under section one, paragraph A, of Commonwealth Act Numbered Two hundred forty are amended so as to read as follows:

"ALBAY

| 32 | For the Oas Home Economics building | P4, 000.00 |
|---|---|---|

"CAGAYAN

| 128 | For the Namungaran barrio school, Rizal | 500.00 |
|---|---|---|

"MOUNTAIN PROVINCE

| 61 | For the construction of Amduntog school, Kiangan | 2,000.00 |
|---|---|---|

"SORSOGON

| 42 | For the High School building, Sorsogon | 500.00 |
|---|---|---|

## Section 2

Item 33 under Albay of section one, subsection A of Commonwealth Act Numbered Two hundred forty is suppressed.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, October 27, 1939, with the exception of the following item: Section 1: Cagayan, Item 128.
