# Republic Act No. 7828

**An Act Establishing a Vocational-technical School in the Municipality of Caramoan, Province of Camarines Sur, to Be Known as the Caramoan Vocational-technical School and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Third Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7828, December 02, 1994 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING A VOCATIONAL-TECHNICAL SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF CARAMOAN, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE CARAMOAN VOCATIONAL-TECHNICAL SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the supervision of the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA), a vocational-technical school in the Municipality of Caramoan, Province of Camarines Sur, to be known as the Caramoan Vocational-Technical School, hereinafter referred to as the School.

## Section 2

The School shall offer vocational and technical courses as may be authorized by the TESDA.

## Section 3

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports, and the TESDA shall take the necessary steps for the establishment and operation of the School.

## Section 4

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 5

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved, December 2, 1994.
