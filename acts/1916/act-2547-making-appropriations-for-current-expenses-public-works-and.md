# Act No. 2547

**An Act Making Appropriations for Current Expenses, Public Works and Permanent Improvements, and for the Advancement in Civilization of the Non-Christian Peoples in the Mountain Province and Nueva VIzcaya for the Fiscal Year Nineteen Hundred and Sixteen, and for Other Purposes.**

[ Act No. 2547, January 14, 1916 ]

AN ACT MAKING APPROPRIATIONS FOR CURRENT EXPENSES, PUBLIC WORKS AND PERMANENT IMPROVEMENTS, AND FOR THE ADVANCEMENT IN CIVILIZATION OF THE NON-CHRISTIAN PEOPLES IN THE MOUNTAIN PROVINCE AND NUEVA VIZCAYA FOR THE FISCAL YEAR NINETEEN HUNDRED AND SIXTEEN, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Commission, that:

## Section 1

The following sums, or so much thereof as may be necessary, are hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated for the fiscal year ending December thirty-first, nineteen hundred and sixteen:

(a) For Insular aid to the Mountain Province, for salaries and wages; contingent expenses; public works and permanent improvements; sanitary purposes; aid to townships; for the agricultural, educational, economic and industrial advancement of its inhabitants, and for other purposes ...... .. P187,000.00

(b) For Insular aid to the Province of Nueva Vizcaya, for salaries and wages; contingent expenses; public orks and permanent improvements; sanitary purposes; aid to townships; for the agricultural, educational, economic and industrial advancement of its inhabitants, and for other purposes 73,000.00

_Provided,_ That detailed statements of the expenditures of the Insular aid appropriated by this section shall be furnished by the Mountain Province and Nueva Vizcaya, to the Secretary of the Interior, and submitted to the Philippine Commission, on or before March first, nineteen hundred and sixteen for its approval.

(c) For salary of the Delegate of the Secretary of the Interior, at eight thousand pesos, and accrued leave for nineteen hundred and fourteen and nineteen hundred and fifteen, one thousand three hundred and eleven pesos and eleven centavos 9,311.11

Total of appropriations for all purposes 269,311.11

## Section 2

Subject to the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, the provincial boards of Nueva Vizcaya and the Mountain Province may expend upon the construction, improvement and maintenance of roads and public works, including irrigation ditches and provincial gardens, the funds accruing from the road tax and the road and bridge fund.

## Section 3

All public works projects in the Mountain Province and in the Province of Nueva Vizcaya requiring appropriation from provincial funds of more than one thousand pesos shall be carried out under the supervision of the Bureau of Public Works unless otherwise directed by the Secretary of Commerce and Police: _Provided,_ That the compensation due to employees used temporarily on any special project shall be paid only from the funds of such special project.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect as of January first, nineteen hundred and sixteen.

Enacted, January 14, 1916.
