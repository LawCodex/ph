# Republic Act No. 10357

**An Act Declaring September 5 of Every Year a Special Working Public Holiday in the City of Naga Province of Cebu to be Known as the "Charter Day of the City of Naga"**

Fifteenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand twelve.

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 10357**

AN ACT DECLARING SEPTEMBER 5 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF NAGA PROVINCE OF CEBU TO BE KNOWN AS THE "CHARTER DAY OF THE CITY OF NAGA"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

September 5 of every year is hereby declared to be a special working public holiday in the City of Naga, Province of Cebu to commemorate its founding day and to be known as the "Charter Day of the City of Naga".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved,

| (Sgd.) JUAN PONCE ENRILE President of the Senate | (Sgd.) FELICIANO BELMONTE JR. Speaker of the House of Representatives |
|---|---|

This Act which originated in the House of Representatives was finally passed by the House of Representatives and the Senate on November 13, 2012 and October 15, 2012, respectively.
