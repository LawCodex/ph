# Republic Act No. 2033

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Panaytayon Primary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Jose Boja Primary School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2033**

**An Act Providing that the School in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at Present Known as Panaytayon Primary School, Shall Hereafter be Known as Jose Boja Primary School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The school in the Municipality of Tubigon, Province of Bohol, at present known as Panaytayon Primary School, shall hereafter be known as Jose Boja Primary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 23, 1957.
