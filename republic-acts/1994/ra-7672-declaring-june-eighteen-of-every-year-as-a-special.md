# Republic Act No. 7672

**An Act Declaring June Eighteen of Every Year as a Special Nonworking Public Holiday in the Province of Benguet**

Republic of the Philippines  
Congress of the Philippines  
Second Regular Session

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7672, January 10, 1994 ]

AN ACT DECLARING JUNE EIGHTEEN OF EVERY YEAR AS A SPECIAL NONWORKING PUBLIC HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF BENGUET

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The Province of Benguet was created by virtue of Republic Act No. 4695 which was enacted into law on June 18, 1966.1a⍵⍴h!1

## Section 2

It is hereby declared that June Eighteen of every year be a nonworking public holiday in the Province of Benguet.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Lapsed into law on January 10, 1994 without the President's signature, pursuant to Sec. 27(1), Article VI of the Constitution.
