# Republic Act No. 2434

**An Act to Establish a National Regional Agricultural School in the Municipality of Borongan, Province of Samar, to be Known as the Eastern Samar, National Regional Agricultural School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2434**

**An Act to Establish a National Regional Agricultural School in the Municipality of Borongan, Province of Samar, to be Known as the Eastern Samar, National Regional Agricultural School**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Director of Public Schools, a national regional agricultural school of the Negros Occidental National Agricultural School type in the Municipality of Borongan, Province of Samar, at a suitable site to be selected by said Director, to be known as the Eastern Samar National Regional Agricultural School.

## Section 2

Upon the recommendation of the Director of Public Schools, the President shall set aside and reserve such portion of the public lands located within the Municipality of Borongan of said province, as may be necessary and convenient for the establishment of the said school and its farm site.

## Section 3

The sum of three hundred thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, for the establishment, operation and maintenance of the said school during the fiscal year ending June thirty, nineteen hundred sixty. Such sums as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance in subsequent years shall be included in the annual General Appropriation Acts.

## Section 4

Thi s Act shall take effect upon the date the funds authorized herein to be appropriated shall have been provided for.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
