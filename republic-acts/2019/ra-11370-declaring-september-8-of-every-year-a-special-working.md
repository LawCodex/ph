# Republic Act No. 11370

**An Act Declaring September 8 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Entire Country to Commemorate the Feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session

Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11370**

**An Act Declaring September 8 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the Entire Country to Commemorate the Feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress assembled:

## Section 1

September 8 of every year is hereby declared a special working holiday in the entire country to commemorate the Feast of the Nativity of the Blessed Virgin Mary.1âшphi1

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,

(Sgd) **VICENTE C. SOTTO III**
President of the Senate

(Sgd) **GLORIA MACAPAGAL-ARROYO**
