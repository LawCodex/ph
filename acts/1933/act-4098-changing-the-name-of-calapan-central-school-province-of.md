# Act No. 4098

**An Act Changing the Name of Calapan Central School, Province of Mindoro, to that of Adriatico Memorial School.**

[ Acts No. 4098, November 29, 1933 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF CALAPAN CENTRAL SCHOOL, PROVINCE OF MINDORO, TO THAT OF ADRIATICO MEMORIAL SCHOOL.

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

The name of Calapan Central School, Province of Mindoro, is hereby changed to that of Adriatico Memorial School, to perpetuate the memory of the late Honorable Macario Adriatico, who has been colonel of the Revolutionary Army, a member of the Royal Spanish Academy, a member of the Philippine Assembly for three consecutive terms, a member of the Code Committee, and Director of the National Library and Museum until his death.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, November 29, 1933.
