# Republic Act No. 4002

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tiltilan in the Municipality of Tinambac, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Jose**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4002**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio Tiltilan in the Municipality of Tinambac, Province of Camarines Sur, to San Jose**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio Tiltilan in the Municipality of Tinambac, Province of Camarines Sur, is hereby changed to San Jose.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1964.
