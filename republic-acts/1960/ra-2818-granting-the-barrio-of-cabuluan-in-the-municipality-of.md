# Republic Act No. 2818

**An Act Granting the Barrio of Cabuluan in the Municipality of Victoria, Province of Tarlac**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2818**

**An Act Granting the Barrio of Cabuluan in the Municipality of Victoria, Province of Tarlac**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Cabuluan in the Municipality of Victoria, Province of Tarlac, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Cabuluan.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
