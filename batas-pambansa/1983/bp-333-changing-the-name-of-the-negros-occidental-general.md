# Batas Pambansa Blg. 333

**An Act Changing the Name of the Negros Occidental General Comprehensive High School in Bacolod City to Negros Occidental High School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 333**

An Act Changing the Name of the Negros Occidental General Comprehensive High School in Bacolod City to Negros Occidental High School

## Section 1

The name of the Negros Occidental General Comprehensive High School in Bacolod City is changed to Negros Occidental High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: January 19, 1983
