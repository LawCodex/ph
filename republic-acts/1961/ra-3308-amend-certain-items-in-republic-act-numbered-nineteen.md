# Republic Act No. 3308

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3308**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Nineteen Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (b), paragraph II, page 218, Section 2-f, of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(b) Orense Park, Bauan Construction and/or improvement ₱10,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
