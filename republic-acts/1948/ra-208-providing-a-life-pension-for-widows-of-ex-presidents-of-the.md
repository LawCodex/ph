# Republic Act No. 208

**An Act Providing a Life Pension for Widows of Ex-Presidents of the Philippines**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 208**

An Act Providing a Life Pension for Widows of Ex-Presidents of the Philippines

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The widow of an ex-President of the Philippines, or of a President who dies in office shall be entitled to a life pension of twelve thousand pesos per year payable to her monthly by the Treasury of the Philippines.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect on April fifteen, nineteen hundred and forty-eight.1aшphi1

Approved: June 1, 1948.
