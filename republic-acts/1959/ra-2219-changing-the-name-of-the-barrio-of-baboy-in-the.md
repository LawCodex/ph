# Republic Act No. 2219

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baboy in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Barrio San Agustin**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2219**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Baboy in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, to Barrio San Agustin**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Baboy in the Municipality of Talibon, Province of Bohol, is changed to Barrio San Agustin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: May 16, 1959.
