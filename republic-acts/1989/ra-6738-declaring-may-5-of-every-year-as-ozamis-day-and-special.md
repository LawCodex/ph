# Republic Act No. 6738

**An Act Declaring May 5 of Every Year as Ozamis Day and Special Working Holiday in the Province of Misamis Occidental and Its Component Cities in Honor of the Late Senator Jose F. Ozamis**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6738, August 04, 1989 ]

AN ACT DECLARING MAY 5 OF EVERY YEAR AS OZAMIS DAY AND SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE PROVINCE OF MISAMIS OCCIDENTAL AND ITS COMPONENT CITIES IN HONOR OF THE LATE SENATOR JOSE F. OZAMIS.
