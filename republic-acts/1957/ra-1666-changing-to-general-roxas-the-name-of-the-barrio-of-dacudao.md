# Republic Act No. 1666

**An Act Changing to General Roxas the Name of the Barrio of Dacudao in the City of Davao**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1666**

**An Act Changing to General Roxas the Name of the Barrio of Dacudao in the City of Davao**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Dacudao in the City of Davao is hereby changed to General Roxas.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 20, 1957.
