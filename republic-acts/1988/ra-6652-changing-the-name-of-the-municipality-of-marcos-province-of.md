# Republic Act No. 6652

**An Act Changing the Name of the Municipality of Marcos, Province of Palawan, to Municipality of Dr. Jose P. Rizal**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6652, April 17, 1988 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MUNICIPALITY OF MARCOS, PROVINCE OF PALAWAN, TO MUNICIPALITY OF DR. JOSE P. RIZAL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Municipality of Marcos in the Province of Palawan is hereby changed to Municipality of Dr. Jose P. Rizal.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
