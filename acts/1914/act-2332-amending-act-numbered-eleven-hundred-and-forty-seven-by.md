# Act No. 2332

**An Act Amending Act Numbered Eleven Hundred and Forty-Seven By Providing for the Free Registration and Branding of Large Cattle Under Two Years of Age Under Certain Conditions.**

[ Act No. 2332, February 26, 1914 ]

AN ACT AMENDING ACT NUMBERED ELEVEN HUNDRED AND FORTY-SEVEN BY PROVIDING FOR THE FREE REGISTRATION AND BRANDING OF LARGE CATTLE UNDER TWO YEARS OF AGE UNDER CERTAIN CONDITIONS.

By authority of the United States, be it enacted by the Philippine Legislature, that:

## Section 1

Section twenty-two of Act Numbered Eleven hundred and forty-seven is hereby amended to read as follows:

"**Section 22.** No transfer of large cattle of any age shall be valid unless such large cattle shall have first been branded and originally registered in the manner prescribed in sections six and eight hereof: _Provided,_ , That large cattle under two years of age may be registered and branded gratis for the purpose of effecting a valid transfer, if the registration and transfer are made at the same time."

Enacted, February 26, 1914.
