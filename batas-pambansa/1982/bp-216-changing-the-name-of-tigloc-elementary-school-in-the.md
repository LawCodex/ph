# Batas Pambansa Blg. 216

**An Act Changing the Name of Tigloc Elementary School in the Municipality of Tungawan, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Lower Tungawan Elementary School.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 216**

An Act Changing the Name of Tigloc Elementary School in the Municipality of Tungawan, Province of Zamboanga Del Sur, to Lower Tungawan Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of Tigloc Elementary School in the Municipality of Tungawan, Province of Zamboanga del Sur, is hereby changed to Lower Tungawan Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 25, 1982
