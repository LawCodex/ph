# Republic Act No. 4895

**An Act Changing the Name of Banago Elementary School in the Municipality of Balabagan, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Balabagan Elementary Community School**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4895**

An Act Changing the Name of Banago Elementary School in the Municipality of Balabagan, Province of Lanao del Sur, to Balabagan Elementary Community School

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Banago Elementary School in the Municipality of Balabagan, Province of Lanao del Sur, is hereby changed to Balabagan Elementary Community School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
