# Republic Act No. 2909

**An Act Reappropriating Certain Sums Under Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2909**

**An Act Reappropriating Certain Sums Under Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Three Hundred One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item 14 (b), page 530 of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-three hundred and one, is amended to read as follows:

"14. (b) Bario school building, Sitio Saay, Barrio Can-asujan, Carcar—Construction of one room ₱4,000.00."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
