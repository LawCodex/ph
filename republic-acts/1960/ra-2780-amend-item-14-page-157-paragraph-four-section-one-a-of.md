# Republic Act No. 2780

**An Act to Amend Item (14), Page 157, Paragraph Four, Section One-A of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2780**

**An Act to Amend Item (14), Page 157, Paragraph Four, Section One-A of Republic Act Numbered Twelve Hundred**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item (14), page 157, paragraph four, Section One-a of Republic Act Numbered Twelve hundred is amended to read as follows:

"(14) New municipal building, Tanauan, Batangas—Construction and/or improvement ₱20,000.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
