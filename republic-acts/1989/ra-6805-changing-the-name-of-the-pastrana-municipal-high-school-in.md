# Republic Act No. 6805

**An Act Changing the Name of the Pastrana Municipal High School in the Municipality of Pastrana, Province of Leyte, to Juan Villablanca Memorial High School**

Eighth Congress

**Republic Act No. 6805 December 12, 1989**

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE PASTRANA MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF PASTRANA, PROVINCE OF LEYTE, TO JUAN VILLABLANCA MEMORIAL HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Pastrana Municipal High School in the Municipality of Pastrana, Province of Leyte, is hereby changed to Juan Villablanca Memorial High School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **December 12, 1989**
