# Republic Act No. 6961

**An Act Repealing Republic Act Numbered Three Thousand Eight Hundred Eighty-Four**

Eighth Congress

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 6961 August 17, 1990**

AN ACT REPEALING REPUBLIC ACT NUMBERED THREE THOUSAND EIGHT HUNDRED EIGHTY-FOUR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

Republic Act No. 3884 entitled: "An Act establishing a normal college in Dipolog, Zamboanga del Norte, and authorizing the appropriation of funds therefor" is hereby repealed.

## Section 2

This repealing Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: **August 17, 1990**
