# Republic Act No. 2476

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Manlungay of the Barrio of Canaan, Municipality of Rizal, Province of Nueva Ecija, into a Barrio of the Said Municipality to be Known as Barrio Villa Paraiso**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2476**

**An Act Converting the Sitio of Manlungay of the Barrio of Canaan, Municipality of Rizal, Province of Nueva Ecija, into a Barrio of the Said Municipality to be Known as Barrio Villa Paraiso**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Manlungay of the barrio of Canaan, Municipality of Rizal, Province of Nueva Ecija, is separated from said barrio of Canaan and constituted into a barrio of the said municipality to be known as Barrio Villa Paraiso.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
