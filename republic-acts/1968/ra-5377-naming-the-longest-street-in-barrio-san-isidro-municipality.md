# Republic Act No. 5377

**An Act Naming the Longest Street in Barrio San Isidro, Municipality of San Francisco, Province of Cebu, the Quirino Maningo Road**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5377**

An Act Naming the Longest Street in Barrio San Isidro, Municipality of San Francisco, Province of Cebu, the Quirino Maningo Road

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The longest street in Barrio San Isidro, Municipality of San Francisco, Province of Cebu, is hereby named Quirino Maningo Road.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 15, 1968.
