# Act No. 3137

**An Act Appropriating the Sum of Twenty thousand Pesos, to Be Used in the Cementation, Repair, and Reconstruction of Corrals, Etc., At the Sisiman Matadero and Quarantine station, and Authorizing the Director of Agriculture to Disburse the Same, and F**

[ Act No. 3137, March 06, 1924 ]

AN ACT APPROPRIATING THE SUM OF TWENTY THOUSAND PESOS, TO BE USED IN THE CEMENTATION, REPAIR, AND RECONSTRUCTION OF CORRALS, ETC., AT THE SISIMAN MATADERO AND QUARANTINE STATION, AND AUTHORIZING THE DIRECTOR OF AGRICULTURE TO DISBURSE THE SAME, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in  
Legislature assembled and by the authority of the same:

## Section 1

There is hereby appropriated out of any funds in the Insular Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of twenty thousand pesos to be used in the cementation, repair, and reconstruction of corrals, pier, sheds, inclosures, and other buildings or structures at the Sisiman Matadero and Quarantine Station.

## Section 2

The money appropriated by this Act, shall be taken from and covered by the money collected as slaughter fees from said Sisiman Matadero and Quarantine Station.

## Section 3

The Director of Agriculture is hereby authorized to undertake the work outlined in section one of this Act, or may in his discretion cause the same to be undertaken, and to disburse the money appropriated herein.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect on its approval.

Approved, March 6, 1924.
