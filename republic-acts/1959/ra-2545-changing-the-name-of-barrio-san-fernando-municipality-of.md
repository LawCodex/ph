# Republic Act No. 2545

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Fernando, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, to Camohaguin**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2545**

**An Act Changing the Name of Barrio San Fernando, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, to Camohaguin**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Barrio San Fernando, Municipality of Gumaca, Province of Quezon, is hereby changed to Camohaguin.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1959.
