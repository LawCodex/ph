# Republic Act No. 3303

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred and One**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3303**

**An Act to Amend Certain Items in Republic Act Numbered Twenty-Seven Hundred and One**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Items (p) and (r) under Group III, for the Province of Cebu, page 340, paragraph (c), Section two of Republic Act Numbered Twenty-seven hundred and one are hereby amended to read as follows:

"(p) Carcar Municipal Hall, Carcar Construction ₱50,000.00"

"(r) Tungkop Elementary School Building, Minglanilla Repair and remodelling 7,500.00"

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 18, 1961.
