# Batas Pambansa Blg. 779

**An Act Changing the Name of the Dalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Balayan, Province of Batangas, to Doña Margarita Toribio Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 779**

An Act Changing the Name of the Dalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Balayan, Province of Batangas, to Doña Margarita Toribio Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Dalig Elementary School in the Municipality of Balayan, Province of Batangas, is changed to Doña Margarita Toribio Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
