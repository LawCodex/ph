# Republic Act No. 2874

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Patonga in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2874**

**An Act Creating the Barrio of Patonga in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The sitio of Patonga in the Municipality of Cuyo, Province of Palawan, is converted into a barrio of said municipality, to be known as the barrio of Patonga.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 19, 1960.
