# Batas Pambansa Blg. 772

**An Act Changing the Name of the Eastern Pangasinan/Asingan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Asingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Luciano Millan Memorial School of Arts and Trades**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 772**

An Act Changing the Name of the Eastern Pangasinan/Asingan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Asingan, Province of Pangasinan, to Luciano Millan Memorial School of Arts and Trades

## Section 1

The name of the Eastern Pangasinan/Asingan School of Arts and Trades in the Municipality of Asingan, Province of Pangasinan, is hereby changed to Luciano Millan Memorial School of Arts and Trades.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
