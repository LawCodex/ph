# Republic Act No. 3155

**An Act Converting the Dr. Gregorio B. Tan Memorial Hospital in the Municipality of Laoang, Province of Samar, into a General Hospital to be Known as the Dr. Gregorio B. Tan General Hospital**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 3155**

**An Act Converting the Dr. Gregorio B. Tan Memorial Hospital in the Municipality of Laoang, Province of Samar, into a General Hospital to be Known as the Dr. Gregorio B. Tan General Hospital**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The Dr. Gregorio B. Tan Memorial Hospital in the Municipality of Laoang, Province of Samar, is converted into a general hospital to be known as the Dr. Gregorio B. Tan General Hospital.

## Section 2

The operation and maintenance of this hospital shall be governed by the provisions of Republic Act Numbered Nineteen hundred and thirty-nine, otherwise known as the Hospital Financing Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1961.
