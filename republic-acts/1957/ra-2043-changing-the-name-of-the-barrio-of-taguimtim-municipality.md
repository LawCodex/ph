# Republic Act No. 2043

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Taguimtim, Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Paciencia**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 2043**

**An Act Changing the Name of the Barrio of Taguimtim, Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, to Paciencia**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the barrio of Taguimtim, Municipality of Dao, Province of Antique, is hereby changed to Paciencia.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 23, 1957.
