# Republic Act No. 7390

**An Act Changing the Name of the Malabag Municipal High School in Barangay Malabag, Municipality of Giporlos, Province of Eastern Samar, to Malabag High School**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 7390, April 10, 1992 ]

AN ACT CHANGING THE NAME OF THE MALABAG MUNICIPAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY MALABAG, MUNICIPALITY OF GIPORLOS, PROVINCE OF EASTERN SAMAR, TO MALABAG HIGH SCHOOL

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The name of the Malabag Municipal High School in Barangay Malabag, Municipality of Giporlos, Province of Eastern Samar, is hereby changed to Malabag High School.
