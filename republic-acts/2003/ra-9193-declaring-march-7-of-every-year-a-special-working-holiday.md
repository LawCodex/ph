# Republic Act No. 9193

**An Act Declaring March 7 of Every Year a Special Working Holiday in the City of Tagum, Province of Davao Del Norte**

Twelfth Congress  
Second Regular Session

Begun held in Metro Manila on Monday, the twenty-second day of July, two thousand two

**REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9193 March 06, 2003**

AN ACT DECLARING MARCH 7 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL WORKING HOLIDAY IN THE CITY OF TAGUM, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL NORTE

_Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:_

## Section 1

It is hereby declared that March 7 of every year be a special working public holiday in the City of Tagum to enable its residents to fittingly commemorate and celebrate its foundation day.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
