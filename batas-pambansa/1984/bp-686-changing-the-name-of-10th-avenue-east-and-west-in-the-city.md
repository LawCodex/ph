# Batas Pambansa Blg. 686

**An Act Changing the Name of 10th Avenue (East and West) in the City of Caloocan, Metropolitan Manila, to Daang Macario B. Asistio, Sr.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 686**

An Act Changing the Name of 10Th Avenue (East and West) in the City of Caloocan, Metropolitan Manila, to Daang Macario B. Asistio, Sr.

## Section 1

The name of 10th Avenue (East and West) in the City of Caloocan, Metropolitan Manila, is changed to Daang Macario B. Asistio, Sr.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
