# Republic Act No. 11145

**An Act Declaring April 21 of Every Year a Special Nonworking Holiday in the Municipality of Orani, Province of Bataan, in Commemoration of its Founding Anniversary, to Be Known as Orani Foundation Day**

Seventeenth Congress  
Third Regular Session  
Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-third day of July, two thousand eighteen.

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 11145**

AN ACT DECLARING APRIL 21 OF EVERY YEAR A SPECIAL NONWORKING HOLIDAY IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF ORANI, PROVINCE OF BATAAN, IN COMMEMORATION OF ITS FOUNDING ANNIVERSARY, TO BE KNOWN AS "ORANI FOUNDATION DAY"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

April 21 of every year is hereby declared a special nonworking holiday in the Municipality of Orani, Province of Bataan, in commemoration of its founding anniversary, to be known as "Orani Foundation Day".

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the _Official Gazette_ or in a newspaper of general circulation.

Approved,
