# Republic Act No. 6564

**An Act Increasing the Bed Capacity of Zamboanga General Hospital in the City of Zamboanga, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor**

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 6564, September 30, 1972 ]

AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF ZAMBOANGA GENERAL HOSPITAL IN THE CITY OF ZAMBOANGA, AND AUTHORIZING THE APPROPRIATION OF FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

The bed capacity of Zamboanga General Hospital in the City of Zamboanga, is hereby increased from one hundred fiftty beds to two hundred.

## Section 2

The sum of fifty thousand pesos is hereby authorized to be appropriated, out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, in addition to its existing appropriations, to carry out the provision of this Act for the fiscal year nineteen hundred and seventy-two. Thereafter, such sums as may be necessary for its operation and maintenance shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

## Section 3

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Enacted without Executive approval on September 30, 1972.
