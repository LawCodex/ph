# Republic Act No. 8858

**An Act Establishing the Tubod (San Fernando) National High School in Barangay Tubod, Municipality of San Fernando, Province of Cebu and Appropriating Funds Therefor**

Eleventh Congress

[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8858, August 22, 2000 ]

AN ACT ESTABLISHING THE TUBOD (SAN FERNANDO) NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TUBOD, MUNICIPALITY OF SAN FERNANDO, PROVINCE OF CEBU AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

## Section 1

There shall be established a national high school in Barangay Tubod, Municipality of San Fernando, Province of Cebu, to be known as the Tubod (San Fernando) National High School.

## Section 2

The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

## Section 4

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
