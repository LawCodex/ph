# Batas Pambansa Blg. 755

**An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 755**

An Act Changing the Names of Certain Schools in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan

## Section 1

The names of the Maura Elementary School and the Punta Elementary School, both in the Municipality of Aparri, Province of Cagayan, are changed to Aparri East Central School and Aparri West Central School, respectively.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: April 13, 1984
