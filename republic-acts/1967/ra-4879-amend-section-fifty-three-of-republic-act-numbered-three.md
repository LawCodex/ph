# Republic Act No. 4879

**An Act to Amend Section Fifty-Three of Republic Act Numbered Three Hundred Thirty-Seven, Otherwise Known as "The General Banking Act"**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 4879**

An Act to Amend Section Fifty-Three of Republic Act Numbered Three Hundred Thirty-Seven, Otherwise Known as "The General Banking Act"

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Section fifty-three of Republic Act Numbered Three hundred thirty-seven is hereby amended to read as follows:

"Sec. 53. Mutual building and loan associations may purchase, hold, and convey real estate under the same conditions as those specified with reference to commercial banks in Section twenty-five of this Act. They may also acquire real estate for subdivision into residential lots, on each of which it must construct and erect a residential house for disposition by sales or lease exclusively to their members: _Provided,_ That each association shall not invest more than twenty-five (25%) percent of its paid-in capital and surplus in the acquisition of real estate for subdivision purposes: _Provided, further,_ That a member of any building and loan association shall acquire not more than one residential lot, subdivided under this Section: _Provided,_ finally that the acquisition and resale or lease of real estate as provided for in this section shall be subject to the approval of two-thirds of all the members of the Board of Directors of the associations."

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
