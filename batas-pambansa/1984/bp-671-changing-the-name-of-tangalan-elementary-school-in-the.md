# Batas Pambansa Blg. 671

**An Act Changing the Name of Tangalan Elementary School in the Municipality of Tangalan, Province of Aklan, to Justice Jose Hontiveros Memorial Elementary School**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 671**

An Act Changing the Name of Tangalan Elementary School in the Municipality of Tangalan, Province of Aklan, to Justice Jose Hontiveros Memorial Elementary School

## Section 1

The name of the Tangalan Elementary School in the Municipality of Tangalan, Province of Aklan, is changed to Justice Hontiveros Memorial Elementary School.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: March 7, 1984
