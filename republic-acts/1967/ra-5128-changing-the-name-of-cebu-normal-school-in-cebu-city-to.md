# Republic Act No. 5128

**An Act Changing the Name of Cebu Normal School in Cebu City to Cebu Normal College**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 5128**

An Act Changing the Name of Cebu Normal School in Cebu City to Cebu Normal College

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

The name of Cebu Normal School in Cebu City is hereby changed to Cebu Normal College.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 17, 1967.
