# Republic Act No. 1776

**An Act Amending Item Numbered Eighty-Six, Under “Camarines Sur II,” Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

**REPUBLIC ACT No. 1776**

**An Act Amending Item Numbered Eighty-Six, Under "Camarines Sur II," Appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen Hundred Thirteen**

Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:

## Section 1

Item numbered eighty-six, under "Camarines Sur II," appendix B of Republic Act Numbered Sixteen hundred thirteen, is amended to read as follows:

| "86. Tandoc, Siruma Construction of one room | ₱4,000.00" |
|---|---|

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 21, 1957.
