# Batas Pambansa Blg. 602

**An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Ventinilla East in the Municipality of sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, to Barangay Primicias.**

**BATAS PAMBANSA Blg. 602**

An Act Changing the Name of Barangay Ventinilla East in the Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, to Barangay Primicias

## Section 1

The name of Barangay Ventinilla East in the Municipality of Sta. Barbara, Province of Pangasinan, is changed to Barangay Primicias.

## Section 2

This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

Approved: June 24, 1983
